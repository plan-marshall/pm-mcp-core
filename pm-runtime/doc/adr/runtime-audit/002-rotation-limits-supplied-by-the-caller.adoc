= ADR 002: The Rotation Limits Are Supplied by the Caller
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-14

== Context

The specification rotates `logs/audit.jsonl` when either threshold of `audit_rotation` in `machine-config.json` is
reached first: `max_bytes` (default 64 MiB) and `max_days` (default 30). No store of `machine-config.json` exists in
the code yet. The operator decided how the rotation gets its limits as decision q4 of the work package.

== Decision

* `AuditRotation.Limits(maxBytes, maxAge)` is supplied by the caller through the constructor of `AuditHead`.
* `Limits.DEFAULTS` are 64 MiB (`DEFAULT_MAX_BYTES`) and 30 days (`DEFAULT_MAX_AGE`), the defaults of the
  specification; a limit that is not positive is refused.
* No `machine-config.json` store is built in this package.

Rationale not recorded.

== Alternatives Considered

Alternatives not recorded.

== Consequences

* The assembly passes `Limits.DEFAULTS` until it reads `audit_rotation` of `machine-config.json`, and then passes the
  values it read.
* The age of a log is the age of its first record, so a log is rotated by age at the first append after the limit.
* `retain_days`, the purge of rotated files by `pm-operator sweep --machine`, is not part of the rotation and stays
  with that verb.
