= ADR 012: The Jobs Part of Recovery Step 3 Is Real, Its Worker Part a Seam
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-17

== Context

Step 3 of the per-project recovery, `JOBS_AND_WORKERS`, settles what a previous runtime instance left of its jobs and
workers. Until this work package it was a named seam (ADR 006). The job record store and the job runner now exist; the
launch of workers, with the fence of a worker's generation, its job token, its task lease and its job-private directory,
does not. The step also has to get into the recovery: `ProjectRecovery.withEnrolment(store, check, enrolments,
lockManager, steps, executor)` builds the two enrolment steps from the enrolment store and takes every other step from
the caller's list, and the assembly `pm-mcp-server` in `plan-marshall-mcp` calls it today with a seam for step 3. That
repository builds against the deployed `SNAPSHOT` of this one.

== Decision

* `JobRecoveryStep` is the jobs part of `JOBS_AND_WORKERS`: every job left `queued` or `running` in the project's job
  records is settled `ended`/`killed` by outcome row 4, never adopted and never executed again. A queued job is not
  signalled; a running one is settled by the first matching rule: (a) an in-process operation, not signalled and no
  orphan note; (b) spawned before the boot time of the system, not signalled; (c) no recorded PID, not signalled, with
  the audit record `job_recovered` noting `orphan unidentifiable`, which the step builds itself
  (`JobRecoveryStep.orphanNote`); (d) otherwise the group is probed with `kill(-pgid, 0)` and a live group terminated with
  steps 1 to 3 of the termination protocol.
* The settlement runs through `JobSettlement`: the fate first, then the slot the caller's `JobRecoveryStep.Slots` names;
  `Slots.NONE` names none. The boot time and the slots are the caller's.
* The worker part of the step stays a seam: a worker's record is settled like every other job's, and the report of the
  step names the worker part `not implemented in this runtime` with the number of workers it settled. It is filled by the
  work package that launches workers.
* `ProjectRecovery.withEnrolment` keeps its signature unchanged and source-compatible: it still takes the step of
  `JOBS_AND_WORKERS` from the list, a seam included. An additive overload takes a `JobRecoveryStep` and places it as
  step 3 in place of any step of that name in the list. No coordinated release of `plan-marshall-mcp` is needed; the
  assembly switches to the overload in a follow-up there (Q-Gate finding 3f46fc of PLAN-20).
* The runner's `DrainParticipant`, `JobDrainParticipant`, joins the drain through the seam ADR 006 left for it.

== Alternatives Considered

1. *Change the signature of `withEnrolment` to require the jobs step.*
_Rejected_: the assembly in `plan-marshall-mcp` builds against the `SNAPSHOT` and would break until a coordinated change
there (finding 3f46fc); further reasons not recorded.

== Consequences

* A project whose previous runtime left jobs unsettled gets them settled before its recovery finishes; a step that cannot
  read the records, hand over an orphan note or record a fate fails, and the recovery of that project stops there.
* A worker left running by a previous runtime keeps its fence, token, task lease and job-private directory until the
  worker part is filled.
* `JobRecoveryKilledRuntimeIT` proves the step against a runtime killed at each point of a job's life.
