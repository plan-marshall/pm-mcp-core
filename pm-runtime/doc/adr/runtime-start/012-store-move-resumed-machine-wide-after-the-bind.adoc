= ADR 012: The Store Move Is Resumed Machine-Wide After the Bind
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-17

== Context

A move between the credential stores cut by a stop is resumed after the next start from its record
`state/credential-store-move.json`. The start of the runtime has the startup sequence up to the bind and, after it,
the per-project recovery with its eleven `RecoveryStep`s. The move concerns the machine, not a project, and the work
package had to decide where its resume runs (question q1 of the work package).

== Decision

* The resume is the machine-wide class `CredentialStoreMoveResume` of `runtime.start`, run once after the bind and
  wired by the assembly. It calls `CredentialStoreMove.resume()`: in phase `copying` with the old store recorded the
  copies are deleted from the target and the move runs again from the first entry; in phase `switched`, or in phase
  `copying` with the target recorded (the stop fell between the switch and the rewrite of the record), the record is
  marked `switched` and the deletion from the old store is finished; without a record nothing happens. A resumed
  move is logged as `PM_RUNTIME-29`.
* `StartupSequence`, `RecoveryStep` and `ProjectRecovery` are unchanged.

Rationale not recorded beyond the move being machine-wide.

== Alternatives Considered

* A `RecoveryStep` of the per-project recovery: not taken; the move concerns the machine, while the recovery runs per
  project. Further rationale not recorded.

== Consequences

* The assembly calls the resume once after the bind; until it does, an open move stays open, and no other writer
  records a store while its record exists.
* The recovery steps keep their number and order.
