= ADR 005: A Remote Endpoint's Enrolment Records Its Origin and Credential Key
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-28

== Context

Client Profiles § Remote Form states that the remote form of the protocol `system_one` has no serving program, so its
profile's `executable`, `identity` and `update_evidence` are `null`, and that the harness enrolment records the origin and
the credential key. Store Schemas § Harness Enrolment Store required, for every entry of `harnesses.json`,
`executable_path`, `sha256` and `version`, and its closed entry object had no member for an origin or a credential key.
The two specification parts contradicted each other for the remote form, and the shipped `HarnessEnrolment` record is
built to the store's program shape, which no remote endpoint could satisfy.

The package had to decide where the remote form is enrolled and in which shape.

== Decision

`harnesses.json` carries two exclusive entry forms. A serving program's entry keeps `executable_path`, `sha256` and
`version`. A remote endpoint's entry carries exactly one member, `endpoint`, with `origin` (an `https` origin, the
profile's `base_url`) and `credential_key` (the Token Lane entry the runtime reads), and it carries none of the program
members. `HarnessEnrolment` holds the second form as its `Binding` and is created through
`HarnessEnrolment.endpoint(...)`; `HarnessEnrolmentStore` writes the form the entry has and refuses a document that mixes
the forms or lacks the members of its own form (`harness_store_unreadable`), so the unreadable-store rule still holds.
Store Schemas § Harness Enrolment Store states the two forms.

== Alternatives Considered

Recording the remote endpoint outside `harnesses.json` was considered and not taken: Client Profiles § Remote Form names
the harness enrolment as the record, and the enrolment is the one place the runtime reads a harness from. Filling the
program members with sentinel values was considered and not taken: a remote endpoint has no executable, and a fake digest
would make the owner check of the loopback form look applicable to it. Adding a separate store was considered and not
taken: it would be a second machine-wide file with its own sidecar, lock and re-baseline rules for one entry form.

== Consequences

- A reader of `harnesses.json` decides the form by the presence of `endpoint`, and an entry of either form is refused as a
  whole when the members of its form are missing.
- A remote endpoint is never self-updated: `HarnessEnrolment.updated(...)` refuses the endpoint form, because there is no
  executable whose version could change.
- A future harness kind without a serving program adds its own form to the store schema and to `HarnessEnrolment`, not a
  program-shaped entry with empty members.
