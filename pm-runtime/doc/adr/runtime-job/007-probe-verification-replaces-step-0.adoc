= ADR 007: A Probe Is Verified Against Its Candidate Record Under a Fixed Budget
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-17

== Context

A probe (kind `probe`, `job_id` prefix `v`) runs a program a scan of the machine or the detection of a project's
onboarding found and that is not enrolled yet: the version, models or login probe of a harness or host tool, or a
script of a checkout. Step 0 of the verify-not-resolve pipeline is the enrolment check, which such a program cannot
pass. The scan wrote a candidate record of what it found: the executable and its interpreter by path and digest, the
declared arguments, the exit codes the probe declares as its success, the owning scope and the working directory. The
specification gives each probe kind a fixed budget, without learning, and lets a probe run only after the operator's
consent of its kind.

== Decision

* `ProbeVerification` is step 0 of a probe in place of the enrolment check: the digests of the executable and of a
  script's interpreter (`executable_digest_mismatch`), the working directory, the canonical git top-level of the checkout
  or the probe's own job-private `cwd/` (`path_traversal_rejected`), and a requested argument vector against the
  declared one (`argument_rejected`). The first check that refuses decides. Nothing is resolved at launch: the vector
  starts with the absolute path the candidate record names.
* The runner launches the declared vector of the candidate by construction: `startProbe` hands the candidate's own
  arguments to the verification as the requested ones, so `argument_rejected` can fire only for a caller of
  `ProbeVerification.verify` that supplies a different vector. It is a property of the verification, kept with its unit
  tests, and not a control of the runner path; the engine port is not widened to carry a requested vector.
* `SubprocessJobRunner.startProbe` refuses a probe without the consent of its kind with `consent_required` and records
  nothing. Otherwise the allocation of the `v-` id, the verification and the first record are one transaction under the
  lock of the probe's scope, the machine queue lock or the entry lock; a refusal is recorded as the fate `refused`.
* `ProbeBudget` holds the budgets as compiled constants, `probe:version` 15, `probe:models` 30, `probe:login` 120 and
  `probe:script` 600 seconds, recorded with the derivation `floor_default`; they are never learned or escalated.
* Rows 1 to 4 of the outcome derivation and an exit code the probe declares decide its fate; any other end leaves the
  exit code and the output to the step that asked, and the runner's output parser does not read a probe's log. The
  runner never starts a probe again, also not one that ended `timed_out`.

== Alternatives Considered

Alternatives not recorded.

== Consequences

* A probe of a program whose file changed after the scan is refused and recorded, and nothing is spawned.
* The record and the log of a probe lie in the store of its scope, `machine/jobs/` or `onboardings/<entry_id>/jobs/`.
* `floor_default` is the derivation of the job record closest to a compiled constant; the schema has no derivation of
  its own for a probe budget.
