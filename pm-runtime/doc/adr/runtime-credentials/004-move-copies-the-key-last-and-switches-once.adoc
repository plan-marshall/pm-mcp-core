= ADR 004: The Move Copies the Key Last and Switches Once
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-17

== Context

The specification orders the move: record it, copy and read back every Token Lane entry, copy the integrity key last
and verify every sidecar under it in the target, switch `credentials.store` in one write, delete every entry from the
old store, end the record; a move cut by a stop is resumed from its record. It named no file for the record; the work
package corrected the store schema to name `state/credential-store-move.json`. A credential write arriving during the
move could land in the old store after it was copied, and a stop may cut the move between any two steps.

== Decision

* `CredentialStoreMove` holds `locks/credentials.lock`, the lock every credential write takes, for the whole move, and
  re-reads its record inside the lock before every phase. It takes `locks/machine-config.lock` only inside it, for the
  switch.
* The record `state/credential-store-move.json` (`CredentialStoreMoveRecord`: `from`, `to`, `started_at`, `phase`) is
  written under the credentials lock in phase `copying`, rewritten once in phase `switched`, and deleted when the
  move ends.
* While the record exists only the move records `credentials.store`: `CredentialStoreChoice` refuses every other
  writer, the credential-store aspect included, with `aspect_owned`, and checks the record under the credentials lock
  before it takes `locks/machine-config.lock`, so no writer records a store between the check and its write.
  Conversely the writer `STORE_MOVE` records only while the record exists: a caller that names itself the store move
  outside a move is refused with an `IllegalStateException` and writes nothing.
* `credentials.store` is switched before the record is marked `switched`, and only after every entry and the key were
  copied and verified. A stop between the two leaves a record in phase `copying` while the target is recorded; since
  no other writer records a store while the record exists, that state is the move's own switch, and the resume
  decides the phase from the recorded store as well as from the record, treats that move as switched and finishes the
  deletion from the old store. A stop before the switch leaves the old store recorded and holding every entry, from
  which the resume copies again; a stop never deletes the only copy of an entry.
* A move into a target that holds a Token Lane entry already is refused with `credential_store_failed` before the
  record is written, and nothing is written or deleted in either store. A secret never exists in two stores, so such
  a target is inconsistent; the refusal makes sure that the copy never overwrites an entry the target held, and that
  the clean-up of a failed move, which deletes from the target every account the old store holds, deletes only copies
  of this move. The integrity key is no Token Lane entry; a key a failed deletion after a switch left in both stores
  is handled by `IntegrityKeyCarry`.
* A failure before the switch fails with `credential_store_failed` and leaves the old store recorded and untouched;
  it deletes the copies only while the old store is recorded, so the clean-up never deletes from the recorded store.
  The record stays exactly while the next start has a clean-up left that it may do: it is deleted where every copy
  was removed, and kept where a copy could not be removed (the resume removes the copies and moves again from the
  first entry) or the machine configuration cannot be read (the resume runs once it reads again). Where the target is
  recorded at the failure, which the lock discipline excludes, the copies stay, since the target is the recorded
  store, and the record is deleted, since a resume would read that state as the move's own switch and delete the
  entries from the old store.
* The resume finishes a record in phase `switched` only while `credentials.store` names its target; otherwise it is
  refused, deletes nothing from either store and keeps the record.
* The `operator` audit record of the completed move is appended before the record is deleted, so a stop or a failing
  audit sink leaves the record, and the next start finishes the move again and appends the audit record then: the
  audit record is delivered at least once, as `AuditSink` requires of a producer.
* `EnrolmentKey` gained the public `write(SecretKey)` and `delete()` for the copy and the deletion of the key.
* The `operator` audit record `credential_store_moved` (`from`, `to`, `entries`) is built directly as an `AuditEvent`;
  no factory was added to `AuditEvent` of `pm-core`.

Rationale not recorded beyond the order the specification gives and the stop that must never lose an entry.

== Alternatives Considered

* Marking the record `switched` before switching the store: not taken, since a stop between the two would let the
  resume delete from the old store entries that the recorded store does not yet name.
* Letting the aspect record a store while a move is open: not taken, since a store the aspect recorded after the copy
  of the entries reads to the resume as the move's own switch, and the deletion from the old store would then remove
  the only copies; the clean-up of a failed move had the same window between reading the recorded store and deleting
  the copies.
* Deleting the record after every failure before the switch: not taken (review of PR 90), since a copy the clean-up
  could not remove would then stay in the target with no record that a later start cleans it up, and a later move
  into that target would treat it as an entry of its own.
* Deleting the record before the audit record is appended: not taken (review of PR 90), since a stop or a failing
  sink between the two would lose the audit record of a completed move for good.

== Consequences

* No credential is written while a move runs; a writer waits for the credentials lock.
* No store is recorded while a move record exists, also after a stop until the resume ends the move; the aspect is
  refused with `aspect_owned` until then.
* The move is the only change of a recorded store: the aspect records a store the first time only, and with a store
  recorded it is refused with `aspect_owned` for the other store, also after the move ended, and is a no-op for the
  recorded one (ADR 006). An aspect that waited for the credentials lock of a running move therefore never writes over
  the move's switch.
* `resume()` decides on the phase of the record and the recorded store: `copying` with the old store recorded starts
  again from the first entry; `switched` with the target recorded, or `copying` with the target recorded, finishes the
  deletion; `switched` with another or no store recorded is refused.
* A move needs an empty target: a target that holds a Token Lane entry is cleaned up by the operator before the move.
* A failing audit sink after the switch leaves a record that refuses the aspect and another move until a start
  appends the audit record; the audit log may then hold the record of the move twice.
* A later change keeps the lock order: credentials before the enrolment locks, `harnesses` and `machine-config`.
