== Purpose
_Requirements: link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14], link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3]_

The package `de.planmarshall.runtime.launch` holds the launch-side coordination of the runtime's workers. This guide
covers its first part, the machine model slots: the admission that bounds how many workers run on the machine and which
of them wait.

The slots are the machine-wide half of the worker launch; the worker's own launch contract — the composed argument
vector, the job token, the harness enrolment and the prompt — is the work package `worker-launch` (PLAN-29) and will be
added here beside this admission. The store the slots live in is `de.planmarshall.core.store.ModelSlotStore`, described
in link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-store.adoc[`core.store` Developer Guide].

== The Model Slots
_Requirements: link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14]_

Every running worker, warm or fresh, holds one of the machine's `model_slots` slots from its spawn to its exit; a warm
worker holds its slot for its whole generation, idle or not. The cap is `model_slots` of `machine-config.json`, its
compiled default `2` and its bounds `1` to `16` (Timeouts & Budgets § Model slot lease).

[horizontal]
`ModelSlotScheduler`:: The admission. Its `request` answers `Granted` when the worker holds a slot, `Queued` when it
waits in the FIFO, and `Deferred` when its plan already has a `writer` worker that holds a slot or waits for one. Its
`release(jobId)` and `releasePlan(planId)` free what a settled worker or a paused plan held and return the leases the
freed slots admitted, so the caller knows which workers to start.
`ModelSlotScheduler.Liveness`:: The seam that decides whether the runtime a slot records still runs. `processes()` is
the production probe: the PID must exist and its process start instant must lie within one second of the recorded one.
A runtime the probe cannot decide is never reaped.
`ModelSlotStore`:: The document `state/model-slots.json`. It decides nothing itself: `update` runs one read, change and
write under the lock `LockKey.modelSlots`, which is what makes an admission atomic.

[source]
----
caller ──► ModelSlotScheduler.request(request)
              └─ ModelSlotStore.update(document -> …)         one transaction on locks/model-slots.lock
                   1. reap: drop every slot and waiting worker whose runtime is not alive
                   2. already granted or waiting? answer that place
                   3. writer of a plan that holds or waits? Deferred("writer_holds_plan")
                   4. append the waiting entry (with its form) and admit from the head while slots < cap
                   5. return Granted, Queued or Deferred
----

=== Invariants

* *The cap holds under concurrent claims*: every admission reads the slots and writes the one it adds in the same
  transaction, so no two claims see the same free slot and the document never holds more than `model_slots` leases.
* *FIFO*: a request is appended to `waiting` and admission takes from its head only; a release or a pause admits the
  head first, and never a worker behind it.
* *One writer per plan*: at most one `writer` worker of a plan holds a slot or waits for one. A second writer is
  `Deferred` and its task stays `queued`; `read_only` and `zero_tool` workers of the plan run beside it within the cap.
* *Warm holds the slot*: a slot is released by its worker's fate, not by an idle wait; `releasePlan` is the one act that
  releases a live plan's slots early, and it drops the plan's waiting workers too.
* *Stale slots are reaped first*: a slot or waiting entry whose runtime died is dropped in the admission transaction,
  before the cap is applied, so a killed daemon never keeps a worker out.
* *The budget starts at the spawn*: the time a worker waits for a slot is outside its budget and is recorded apart as
  `queued_ms` of its metrics row.

=== Failure Modes

[cols="2,3"]
|===
|Failure |Behaviour

|`model-slots.json` is of another format version or unreadable
|`StoreRefusal` naming the file; nothing is admitted or released.

|The machine's cap lies outside `1` to `16`
|The scheduler's constructor throws `IllegalArgumentException`; no admission runs.

|A writer request for a plan that already has a writer
|`Deferred("writer_holds_plan")`; the task stays `queued`, nothing is written for it.

|The runtime of a slot is dead
|The slot is reaped in the next admission transaction and the freed slot is admitted to the head of the FIFO.

|A pause
|`releasePlan` frees every slot of the plan and drops its waiting workers in one transaction.
|===

Tested by `ModelSlotSchedulerTest`: the FIFO with a cap of one, eight concurrent claims against a cap of two (exactly
the cap granted, never more in the store), the one-writer rule beside a `read_only` worker, a stale slot reaped, a
paused plan releasing both of its held slots at once and a second test dropping the plan's waiting worker, and the
bounds of the cap.
