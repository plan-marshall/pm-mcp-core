_Requirements: link:{req}/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-10[PM-WF-10], link:{req}/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-4[PM-ARCH-4]_

`PendingEntryDirectoryReader` is the adapter of the engine port
`de.planmarshall.core.spi.PendingEntryReader` over the pending entry directory `pending/<entry_id>/` of the workspace
scope (`{spec}/store-schemas/01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Store Schemas § Pending Entry]`). The
engine addresses a pending plan or epic bootstrap as an entity of the workspace scope by its `entry_id` and resolves it
from the persisted record `entry.json`, never from the memory of a running engine; this package is that read, outside
the engine packages (PM-IMPL-5), as `runtime.question` is the read of a question record.

The read is tri-state, the port's own: `Live` with the
link:../../../pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/store/PendingEntryRecord.java[`Kind`] wire name of the record
(`plan` or `epic`), `Deleted` for an absent record, and `Unreadable` with the refusal for a record that does not parse,
of another `format_version`, or stating another `entry_id` than its directory.

*Trust assumptions* (link:../adr/runtime-entry/001-the-entry-reader-is-lock-free.adoc[ADR runtime-entry/001]):

* The read takes no lock, writes nothing and repairs nothing. The record is replaced by an atomic rename under the
  workspace lock, so the read sees one complete version; a torn record is evidence of an edit outside a transaction.
* The read judges the record alone, never the entry's source directory, question or decision store, or its transcript.
* The read does not verify the digest sidecar, because a lock-free read cannot decide an edit outside a transaction.
  Integrity is decided by `PendingEntryStore.verify` under the workspace lock, whose `Tampered` answer names the entry
  and its store; the runtime raises the entry's own integrity hold from that answer.
* An absent record is `Deleted`. The directory a removal a stop cut off left behind is the periodic sweep's residue, and
  the reader never removes it.

Tested by `PendingEntryDirectoryReaderTest`: a plan and an epic read live with their kinds, an absent entry and the
directory of a cut-off removal read deleted, a torn and a mismatching record are unreadable, a tampered record still
reads live (the reader never decides the edit), and a project id that is no identifier is refused at construction.
