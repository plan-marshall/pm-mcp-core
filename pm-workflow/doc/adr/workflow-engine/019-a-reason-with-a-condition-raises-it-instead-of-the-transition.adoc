= ADR workflow-engine 019: A Reason with a Condition Raises It Instead of the Transition
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-11-20

== Context

A primitive's outcome may carry a reason, and a reason answers at most one condition kind from the closed table of
link:../../../../doc/specification/workflow-dsl/05-primitives.adoc#outcomes-and-reasons[Workflow DSL § Outcomes and Reasons]
(`footprint_violation`, `precondition_unmet`). Until `PLAN-V2-19` the engine ignored the reason: a result whose reason
named a boundary breach took the transition of its outcome like any other, so the condition the table promised was never
raised, and the cause was lost once the scope moved on. The question was where the condition takes effect: beside the
transition, after it, or in its place.

== Decision

* `Run.runPrimitive` checks the reason after the outcome and returns a `PrimitiveRun`: the target of the transition of the
  outcome, or the condition kind with its facts. A reason whose `conditionKind()` is present raises that condition
  instead of the transition: the scope stays at its state, phase and call stack, and the condition overlay records it
  (`EngineStep.ConditionRaised`).
* The outcome itself is unchanged: the unit still routes it once, whatever its reason (Rule 2), and a reason that maps to
  no condition takes that transition as an outcome without a reason does.
* A reason the outcome does not declare is the internal fault `UNDECLARED_OUTCOME`, naming the primitive, the state and
  the reason.
* An `auto` state raises from the chain; a resumed composite job state and an operator link raise over the status the
  event's advance started from, so the job stays bound and a `fills` slot is not written.

== Alternatives Considered

* *Take the transition and raise the condition at its target.* Not taken: the target is a state the unit chose for the
  outcome, not for the breach, and the condition would rest at a position the unit never meant to rest at.
* *Take the transition and only record the reason.* Not taken: the table of the specification maps a reason to a
  condition, and a condition that is never raised makes the table dead text.
* *Route the reason as an outcome of its own.* Not taken: a reason is data only
  (link:../../../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-spi/011-reasons-are-data-only-and-never-routable.adoc[ADR core-spi/011]).

== Consequences

* The condition's self-remedy re-runs the primitive once the condition clears; a second advance of the raised status
  returns it unchanged, so the primitive runs again only after the clear.
* A raise writes no `left` history line and merges no fact of the result
  (link:022-a-reason-stays-out-of-the-sub-workflow-result-record.adoc[ADR 022]); its facts are bounded text
  (link:020-the-facts-of-a-raised-reason-are-bounded-text.adoc[ADR 020]).
* `ReasonConditionTest` pins every case, from an `auto` state, a sub-workflow frame, a resumed composite job and an
  operator link.
