= ADR workflow-engine 021: The Reason Table Belongs to the Primitive
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-11-20

== Context

The specification states a closed table from reasons to conditions
(link:../../../../doc/specification/workflow-dsl/05-primitives.adoc#outcomes-and-reasons[Workflow DSL § Outcomes and Reasons]).
Each reason constant answers its own `conditionKind()`, and `PrimitiveRegistry` refuses at registration a kind outside
`OutcomeReason.MAPPABLE_CONDITION_KINDS` (link:../../../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-spi/010-the-reason-to-condition-table-is-total-and-closed.adoc[ADR core-spi/010]).
The engine needs the kind at the moment a result arrives; the question was whether it keeps a table of its own and who
else may raise the condition a reason maps to.

== Decision

* Only the engine raises the condition of a reason, and it holds no table: it reads `conditionKind()` of the reason the
  result carries and raises that kind, or takes the transition when the answer is empty.
* No primitive raises a condition itself and no unit names one; the primitive declares the mapping on its reason enum,
  and the registry has already checked it.

== Alternatives Considered

* *A table of reasons in the engine.* Not taken: it would repeat the mapping the reason enum states, and every new reason
  of a primitive would need an engine change; two copies of one table drift.
* *A primitive raises the condition itself.* Not taken: a primitive returns a result and has no access to the status or
  the overlay; raising is the engine's act on the scope.

== Consequences

* A new reason with a mappable kind takes effect without a change of `pm-workflow`.
* The set of kinds a primitive can originate stays the registry's concern; the engine trusts a registered primitive and
  checks only that the reason pairs with its outcome.
