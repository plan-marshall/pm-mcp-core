= ADR workflow-engine 020: The Facts of a Raised Reason Are Bounded Text
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-11-20

== Context

A condition overlay carries `facts`, which the status store persists and both clients show with the hold
(link:../../../../doc/specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#condition-overlays[Workflow DSL § Condition Overlays]).
When a reason raises a condition (link:019-a-reason-with-a-condition-raises-it-instead-of-the-transition.adoc[ADR 019]),
the result the primitive returned holds `updatedFacts` of any type and any size: a list of offending paths may run to
thousands of entries, and a text may carry the content of a file. Persisting them as they are would let one primitive
grow the status document without bound and copy content into a store an operator reads on another device.

== Decision

* The facts of a raised condition are, in this order, `primitive`, `outcome` and `reason` (the wire names), then the facts
  of the result in their order; a result fact named like one of the three is dropped.
* Every value is rendered as text: a collection by its elements joined with `,`, at most `MAX_FACT_ELEMENTS` (50) of them
  and then `,…`; every other value by `String.valueOf`. The text is cut to `MAX_FACT_LENGTH` (256) characters, the last
  of them `…`, never inside a surrogate pair.
* The bounds are private constants of `WorkflowEngine`: a bound on size and on what a fact can disclose, not a setting.

== Alternatives Considered

* *Persist the result facts as JSON values.* Not taken: no bound on size, and the overlay's `facts` would depend on the
  type a primitive happens to return.
* *Persist only the three wire names.* Not taken: the self-remedy and the operator need the offending files or values the
  primitive found, which only the result facts name.
* *A configurable bound.* Not taken: rationale not recorded beyond the plan's statement that the bound is a privacy and
  size bound of the engine.

== Consequences

* A fact the hold shows may be cut; the marker `…` says so, and the primitive's own record keeps the whole value where it
  has one.
* `ReasonConditionTest` (group *The bounds of the facts*) pins the order, the dropped shadowing fact, the collection cut
  at 50 elements, the short collection joined whole, the text cut at 256 characters and the rendering of a number and a
  boolean.
