= ADR 005: The Frontmatter Is Read by a Line Parser of Its Own
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-18

== Context

Every `.pmwf` file opens with a frontmatter between two lines `---`: the unit's name, description and type, its
entries and exits, and its origin. The specification makes it a closed, line-oriented subset of YAML, read by the
runtime's own parser and never by a general YAML loader, so that no tag, anchor, alias or merge key is ever resolved.
The keys `entry` and `exit` repeat once per name, which a YAML mapping cannot express. The frontmatter must be readable
without the body, for the lists that show a unit, and every violation is reported with its file, line and column.

The work package also left open where the frontmatter of the two units that are not read from one file comes from: the
merged plan unit, built from seven phase files, and a composed plan instance version, built from the merged template.

== Decision

* `UnitFrontmatterReader` in `workflow.dsl` reads the frontmatter line by line with a parser of its own and imports no
  YAML library. It is a plain final class with a private constructor, because `pm-workflow` does not use Lombok. It
  reads up to the closing `---` and nothing after it, so it serves a caller that wants the frontmatter alone and does
  not depend on the body being parseable.
* A refusal is a value: `read` returns the sealed `UnitFrontmatterReader.Result`, `Read` with the `UnitFrontmatter` and
  the line the body starts at, or `Refused` with a `Diagnostic` of the new `RefusalRule` member `FRONTMATTER`, the
  line and the column. The first refusal wins, as in the parser of the body. `WorkflowParser` calls the reader first and
  returns its refusal as its own, then reads the body from the reported line.
* The day of validation, after which no `modification_date` may lie, is taken in UTC
  (`LocalDate.now(ZoneOffset.UTC)`), so the result does not depend on the time zone of the machine.
* A composed plan instance version takes the frontmatter of its template with the type `instance`
  (`PlanInstanceComposer`); the plan unit and each phase sub-workflow keep their own frontmatter, since no file is
  merged any more (link:../workflow-unit/007-the-plan-unit-calls-its-phases.adoc[ADR workflow-unit 007]).

Rationale not recorded beyond the specification's requirement of a parser of its own.

== Alternatives Considered

1. **A YAML library with a safe loader**:
*Rejected*: the specification forbids a general YAML loader for the frontmatter; a loader resolves tags, anchors,
aliases and merge keys unless each is switched off, folds the repeated `entry` and `exit` keys into one value, and
accepts forms the closed format refuses.

2. **Reading the frontmatter inside the body parser**:
*Rejected*: the frontmatter must be readable without the body, and its format is not the line grammar of the body.

== Consequences

* Every unit text, every fixture and every inline unit of the tests opens with a frontmatter; a text without one is
  refused with `FRONTMATTER` at line 1.
* A refusing fixture of the body states its expected line below the frontmatter, and its expected line moves with every
  line the frontmatter gains.
* A unit whose `modification_date` is today in UTC is accepted on every machine; one dated tomorrow in UTC is refused
  even where the local date has already turned.
* The frontmatter of the merged plan unit and of a composed instance names the release and author of the first part or
  the template, not of the merge or the composition; a later change that gives them a frontmatter of their own changes
  `PlanUnitMerger` and `PlanInstanceComposer`.
