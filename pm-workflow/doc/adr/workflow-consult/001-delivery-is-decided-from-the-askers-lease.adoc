= ADR 001: Delivery Is Decided from the Asker's Lease When the Consultation Closes
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted.

== Date

2026-10-24

== Context

A consultation closes while the asking task is in `consulting`. The specification requires the answer to be delivered
exactly once, to the asking generation's next wait, or — when the asking generation was fenced while the consultation
was open or its answer undelivered — to the successor's offer through the task's facts
(`model-work/05-consultation.adoc#consult-answer`). The queue task record carries the delivery as `delivered_at` and
`delivered_to` (`wait` or `facts`) on the asking task's `consultations[]` entry
(`store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc`), and it keeps the current lease of the asking task.

The delivery could be decided when the answer arrives, when the asking task is next waited for, or by an outbox of its
own.

== Decision

`ConsultationService.settleAsker` decides the place in the same `QueueStore.updateAll` transaction that closes the
consultation task: `wait` when the asking task's lease still names the generation that asked (`asked_by`), `facts`
otherwise. On `wait` the asking task returns to `executing`, so the task protocol's next wait ends the `consulting`
attempt as `waited_again` and re-offers the task with the answer in its facts; on `facts` the asking task stays
`queued`, or keeps the successor's `executing` status when the successor already holds it, and the successor's offer
carries the answer. The entry's `delivered_at` and `delivered_to` are written when the consultation closes, and a second
answer is refused `stale_entity`. `ConsultationService.reconcile` may later rewrite `delivered_to` from `wait` to
`facts` when the asking generation no longer holds the task, and never back.
<p>
A fence can land after the answer was recorded `wait` and before the asking generation's next wait. That delivery is
treated as a delivery to the successor when the task is represented: `ConsultationService.links` suppresses the role
when the recorded delivery is `facts`, or is `wait` and the current lease no longer names the asking generation.

== Alternatives Considered

1. *Decide the place on the asking task's next wait.*
   _Rejected_: a fenced generation never waits again, so the answer would never reach the successor, and the decision
   would have to read the fence after the consultation closed.
2. *Keep a delivery outbox beside the queue.*
   _Rejected_: it is a second source of truth for a fact the queue task record already carries, and it would have to be
   reconciled after every restart.
3. *Record the answer only in the consultation task and read it from there on the next wait.*
   _Rejected_: the asking task's `consultations[]` is the specified home of the answer and of its delivery
   (`store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc`), and a successor offer needs it without a second read of another store.

== Consequences

* The delivery and the close are one journaled write on the queue lock; no window exists in which a closed
  consultation has no delivery place.
* A lost consultation is delivered the same way with the status `unavailable` and no answer; the asker continues on its
  own judgment.
* The asking task's status after an answer is `executing` (delivered to its wait) or `queued` (delivered to the
  successor). A later change must keep `holdsLease` consistent with the status, which `QueueTaskRecord.validate`
  enforces.
