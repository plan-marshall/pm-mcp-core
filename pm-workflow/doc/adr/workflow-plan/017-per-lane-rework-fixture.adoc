= ADR 017: A Fixture Whose Per-Lane Call States Prove the Rework Routes
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-21

== Context

ADR link:012-per-lane-routes-and-other-lane-pruning.adoc[012] gives each lane its own rework and replan routes: the
rework of a phase call re-enters the authoring entry of the calling lane, `outline.deep` for `deep` and
`outline.light-regate` for `light` (link:../../../../doc/specification/state-catalogue/18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-plan-unit[State
Catalogue § Plan Unit], the `phases.<phase>` call states).

The composer tests proved those routes over the merged phase files of `unit-fixtures/defaults/`. That fixture models the
phases as states of one unit, and its Phases 4 and 5 are shared after `outline.gate`, so its rework enters
`outline.deep` from both lanes — the shape ADR
link:013-default-plan-unit-in-the-specification-and-test-fixtures.adoc[013] gives the fixture units. The plan unit of
the specification instead calls the phases through *per-lane* call states, `phases.plan` and `phases.execute` for the
`deep` lane and `phases.plan-light` and `phases.execute-light` for the `light` lane, and each of them routes its rework
to its own lane's authoring entry. No fixture held that shape, so a regression that routed a light call's `fix_requested`
into `outline.deep`, or a deep call's into `outline.light-regate`, passed every test, and so did a fixture whose light
call states stayed in a deep version.

== Decision

* Add the plan unit `src/test/resources/plan/per-lane-rework.pmwf`, a complete unit of the type `workflow`, the id
  `plan` and the scope `plan` in one file: the two lane entries `refine.baseline-reconcile` and `init.light-gate` at the
  split of `init.ready`; a call state per lane for the phases `plan` and `execute`; `fix_requested` and `loop_back` of the deep call states
  re-entering `outline.deep`, those of the light call states `outline.light-regate`; a light review job whose failure
  re-enters `outline.light-regate`; no transition from a deep state into the light route; and an awaited job on every
  rework cycle (Rule 11b).
* Add `PlanFixtures.perLaneComposer()` and `PlanFixtures.perLaneRequest(event, facts, previous, position)` over that
  file, with the registries of `InvariantFixtures`, and the phase call targets `phase-plan` and `phase-execute` written
  in `PlanFixtures` itself: the fixture must be one file, and a called sub-workflow needs declared entries and exits.
* Add the nested class `PerLaneRework` to `PlanInstanceComposerTest`: it composes the fixture at `start` and then at
  `outline` for `Lane.DEEP` and `Lane.LIGHT` and asserts the `other_lane` prune of the light call states and of the
  light outline route in the deep version, the deep calls' rework on `outline.deep`, the light version keeping both
  routes with the light calls' rework on `outline.light-regate`, and an empty `HeaderSchemaCheck` in both versions.
* The fixture lives beside `plan-template-fixture.pmwf` under `plan/`, not under `unit-fixtures/defaults/`: it declares
  the unit id `plan`, so it would collide with `plan.pmwf` of the loaded default population, and `PlanFixtures` reads it
  by explicit path.

== Alternatives Considered

1. *Extend the merged phase files of `unit-fixtures/defaults/` with per-lane call states.* _Not taken_: those phases
   are shared after `outline.gate`, so a call state with its own rework route cannot be added without changing what every
   other composer and invariant test composes; and the fixture's light route must stay prunable, which a shared phase
   cannot be.
2. *Compose a call-only plan unit, without the phase states.* _Not taken_: the phase invariants run over the composed
   unit. Most of their checks are reference-relative — `requirePresent` reports a missing state only when the reference
   unit carries it, and a unit that is its own default supplies no reference target — but `INV-FIN-1` is absolute: its
   mandatory step completions come from the finalize roster, not from the reference unit. A unit with call states only
   satisfies the call contract but carries none of the phase states that check names.
3. *Assert the routing on the merged fixtures alone.* _Not taken_: that is the state that let the defect through; the
   merged fixture has no light call state to route.

== Consequences

* A light call state that routes its rework into the deep authoring entry, a deep one that routes into the light route,
  and a deep version that keeps the light call states each fail `PlanInstanceComposerTest` `PerLaneRework`.
* The lane entry ids are declared once in `LaneEntryIds` (link:../workflow-verify/030-lane-entry-ids-in-one-holder.adoc[ADR
  workflow-verify 030]); `InstancePruner` and the invariant checks read them from there. The fixture
  `per-lane-rework.pmwf` and `PerLaneRework.deepPrunesLightCalls` repeat them as text. Renaming only the constants
  leaves the fixture unchanged, so `InstancePruner.otherLane` prunes nothing and `deepPrunesLightCalls` fails.
* *For the assembly in `plan-marshall-mcp`*: test resources and tests of `workflow.plan` only; no production type
  changes.
* The fixture is written for these tests and never copied from the shipped content, as ADR
  link:013-default-plan-unit-in-the-specification-and-test-fixtures.adoc[013] requires of the fixture units.
