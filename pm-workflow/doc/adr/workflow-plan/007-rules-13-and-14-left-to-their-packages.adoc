= ADR 007: Rules 13 and 14 Are Left to the Packages That Own Roles and Skills
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-14

== Context

The specification verifies every composed version against Rules 1-14; Rules 13 and 14 resolve roles and skills at composition (`role_undefined`, `class_mismatch`, `skill_unresolved`).
The role sets and the skill catalogue they resolve against do not exist in this repository yet; they are the work packages `profile-loader` and `role-sets-and-model-caller`.

== Decision

* `PlanInstanceComposer` checks Rules 1-12, the finalize pipeline and the compiled invariants, and not Rules 13 and 14.
* The seam is the verification step of the composer: the rule checks join it when the packages that own the role sets and the skill catalogue provide them.

== Alternatives Considered

1. *A stub resolver that admits every role and skill.*
_Rejected_: it would report a check that never ran as passed.
2. *Hold this package until the role sets exist.*
_Rejected_: composition, versions and the position rule do not depend on role resolution.

== Consequences

* A composed version can name a role or skill that does not resolve; nothing in this package detects it.
* The packages `profile-loader` and `role-sets-and-model-caller` add the checks to the composition.
