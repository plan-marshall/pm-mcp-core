= ADR 018: A plan's and an epic's token facts are carried per population, with no limit
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted.

== Date

2026-10-27.

== Context

The representation of a running plan and of an epic must carry the tokens used with the split and the coverage, and no
limit, budget line, projection or comparison with one (Model Work § Usage Reporting & Spend Classes). The engine had no
such value type; the figures are derived from the metrics rows, and sums are formed only between figures of one
population (Store Schemas § Metrics Store, Populations).

== Decision

`PlanTokens` carries the plan's `UsageAggregate` per population; `EpicTokens` sums its plans' figures and the epic's own
rows (decompose, landing, review) per population. Both derive on read and expose no limit, budget, projection or anchor.
The warm workers' `worker_idle` usage belongs to no plan and is not part of either representation.

== Alternatives Considered

Carrying one number across populations was rejected: the two populations count different units. Carrying a budget line
was rejected: nothing limits a plan's use, and an operator's own limit is a later change on the recorded rows.

== Consequences

A view renders each population's total with its split and coverage and shows a floor where a row is unmeasured. Adding
a limit later is a change on these values, never a new comparison inside them.
