= ADR 020: The Declaration Bounds Live in `workflow.verify`
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-13

== Context

The units `adjudicate.decisions` and `replan` declare values a project may change only within compiled bounds, and the adjudication unit carries the `decide` lines of the decision kinds.
The invariants `INV-ADJ-1` and `INV-REPLAN-1` in `workflow.verify` require the declarations to lie within their bounds, and the project-unit loader in `workflow.unit` checks the bounds as a stage of its own.
`workflow.unit` already depends on `workflow.verify` for the verifier and the invariants.

== Decision

`DeclarationBounds` lives in `workflow.verify`. It holds the schemas, names with type, bound and shipped value, and checks a unit's `declare` and `decide` lines, reporting every finding as `declaration_invalid` with the bound and the location of the offending line, or of the unit header for a missing declaration.
To name the line, the syntax tree records the location of every `declare` line (`ValueDeclaration`) and of every decision declaration (`ask` and the quorum).
The invariants map the findings to `INV-ADJ-1` and `INV-REPLAN-1`; the loader lists the findings once, as `DECLARATION` findings.

== Alternatives Considered

1. **`DeclarationBounds` in `workflow.unit`**:
*Rejected*: the invariants in `workflow.verify` would then depend on `workflow.unit`, which depends on `workflow.verify`: a package cycle.

2. **Locating a finding at the unit header**:
*Rejected*: the operator decided that a finding names the exact `declare` or `decide` line, so the tree records their locations.

== Consequences

* `workflow.unit` depends on `workflow.verify`, never the reverse.
* A declaration finding points at the line the operator has to change.
