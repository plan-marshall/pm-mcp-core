= ADR 030: The Lane Entry Ids Live in One Holder
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-21

== Context

The two states whose exits split the lanes — `refine.baseline-reconcile` for the `deep` lane and `init.light-gate` for
the `light` lane (link:../../../../doc/specification/state-catalogue/02-plan-phases-1-3.adoc#state-init.light-gate[State
Catalogue § Plan Phases 1-3]) — were named in three classes, each with its own constant: `InstancePruner` in
`workflow.plan` declared both as the targets of the lane split, `InitInvariants` declared the light entry as its
`LIGHT_GATE`, and `RefineInvariants` declared the deep entry as its `BASELINE_RECONCILE`. A rename of an entry in the
specification and the templates therefore had to reach three declarations, and a missed one left the pruner and the
phase invariants disagreeing with the templates: the pruner would keep a route the invariant no longer recognised, and
the invariant would require a state the pruner treated as not part of the lane.

The two ids are also the only state ids of the plan unit that a package outside `workflow.verify` reads by name: the
pruner has no other business with the invariants.

== Decision

* Add `LaneEntryIds` in `workflow.verify`, declaring `DEEP_ENTRY` (`refine.baseline-reconcile`) and `LIGHT_ENTRY`
  (`init.light-gate`) once.
* `InstancePruner`, `InitInvariants` and `RefineInvariants` read the two ids from it; their own constants are removed.
* `LaneEntryIds` declares no behaviour and holds no table beyond the two ids: it is a vocabulary holder, not a
  registry, and it deliberately does not name the lane split (`InstancePruner.LANE_SPLIT`), whose reader is the pruner
  alone.

== Alternatives Considered

1. *Read the ids from the default plan unit at run time*, resolving the targets of the `init.ready` exits. _Not taken_:
   the resolution depends on the unit under check, so an invariant fed a mutated or partial unit would lose the id it is
   meant to guard, and the check could no longer report a missing entry.
2. *Keep the constants where they were and cross-reference them from the pruner.* _Not taken_: the duplication is the
   defect; three owners of one id is what let a rename drift.
3. *Put the holder in `workflow.plan` beside the pruner.* _Not taken_: the invariants would then depend on
   `workflow.plan`, inverting the direction the package layout keeps — `workflow.plan` already reads
   `workflow.verify`.

== Consequences

* A rename of a lane entry is one edit in `LaneEntryIds`, and the pruner, `INV-INIT-2` and `INV-REFINE-1` move with it.
* `LaneEntryIdsTest` asserts that the default plan unit
  (`InvariantFixtures.passingUnits(UnitOrigin.DEFAULT).get("plan")`) declares a state under each holder constant, so a
  rename of either id that misses the holder fails the build instead of silently detaching the checks.
* *For the assembly in `plan-marshall-mcp`*: `LaneEntryIds` is a new public type of `workflow.verify`; no existing
  signature changes.
