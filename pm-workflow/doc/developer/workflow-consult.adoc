= Consultation Developer Guide (`workflow.consult`)
:nofooter:

`de.planmarshall.workflow.consult` is the consultation of one role by another: the `consult:<role>` link a task's
entity offers, the consultation task of the consulted role, the answer through the asking task's next wait, and the
`consultation_ids` of a submit. The persisted side — the queue task record, its `consultations[]` and
`consultation_of`, the lease and the attempts — is `de.planmarshall.core.queue` and is documented in
link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-queue.adoc[`core.queue`]; the serving of a task is
link:workflow-task.adoc[`workflow.task`].

Specification: link:../../../doc/specification/model-work/05-consultation.adoc[Model Work § Consultation];
link:../../../doc/specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#consultation-bound[Timeouts & Budgets §
Consultation Bound]; link:../../../doc/specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc[Store Schemas § Queue Task
Record].

== The types and how they work together

`ConsultationService`::
The service of one queue. It derives the links from the asking task's own record, opens a consultation, records the
answer or the loss, repairs a consultation that closed outside it, and validates the `consultation_ids` of a submit.
The consultation entry and the asking task change in one `QueueStore.updateAll` transaction on the queue lock; the
consultation task of the consulted role is created in its own write after that entry (ADR 003). The service is
constructed over the project's `QueueStore` and holds no state of its own.

`ConsultationRequest`::
The ask: the asking task and generation, the consulted role and its class, the roles the state declares, the question,
and the instant. The consulted role's harness, model and effort are resolved by the caller — the role's own worker is
started from its effective configuration, and the task protocol offers the consultation to a generation of that role.
The question travels with the ask; its durable home in the asking task's entity facts is the entity representation's.

`ConsultationLink`::
The `consult:<role>` link: the relation `consult`, the form `submit`, the identifier `consult:<role>`, and the
parameter `question: text[1..4000]`. A consultation entity offers exactly one link instead, `submit:answer`, and no
`consult`, `ask` or infeasible link; `TaskProtocol.submit` and `infeasible` refuse a consultation task
`link_not_available`.

`ConsultationOutcome`::
What a call did: `Opened` (the consultation id and the asking task in `consulting`), `Answered` (the asking task and
the consultations it carries) or `Refused` with a `ConsultationRefusal`. The refusal names are the server's:
`job_scope_mismatch`, `link_not_available`, `stale_entity`, `invalid_link_args`, `class_mismatch`, and this package's
own `consultation_open`, `consultation_bound_spent` and `consultation_depth`.

`ConsultationFact` and `SubmitValidation`::
The fact of one consultation as the asking task's `facts.consultations` carries it (`consultation_id`, role, status,
answer), and the verdict of the `consultation_ids` check: `Accepted`, or `Refused` with a reason that names the
missing or the unknown reference.

== The rules of the link

* *One at a time*: a task has at most one open consultation. While one is open, `links` offers none and `open`
  refuses `consultation_open`.
* *Bound*: a task follows at most the compiled `ConsultationService.MAX_CONSULT_LINKS` (`3`) consultation links over
  all its attempts, whether the consultation was answered or lost. After that, `links` offers none and `open` refuses
  `consultation_bound_spent`.
* *Depth one*: a consultation task (kind `consultation`) offers only `submit:answer`; `links` returns none for it,
  `open` refuses `consultation_depth`, and the task protocol refuses a generic `submit` or `infeasible` on it
  (link:../adr/workflow-consult/002-the-consultation-link-rules-are-derived-from-the-record.adoc[ADR 002]).
* *Declared*: a role is consulted only when the state declares it; an undeclared role is refused
  `link_not_available`.

== The answer through the next wait

The consultation task is a task of the consulted role like any other (`workflow.task`): it is offered before the work
tasks of its role, acknowledged, executed and answered. Its answer closes it `submitted`; a lost consultation closes it
`lost` with its reason. The asking task's consultation entry is answered in the same transaction:

* while the asking generation still holds the task's lease, the entry is delivered to `wait` and the asking task
  returns to `executing`, so its next `pm_wait` re-offers it with the answer in its facts;
* once the generation was fenced, the entry is delivered to `facts` and the successor's offer carries it; the successor
  is offered no `consult` link for that role.

A delivery recorded to `wait` whose generation no longer holds the task — a fence that landed after the answer and
before the next wait — is treated as a delivery to the successor when the task is represented: the answer is in the
record's facts, and `links` suppresses that role. `reconcile` rewrites the durable `delivered_to` to `facts`. Only an
answered consultation suppresses the role; an `unavailable` one leaves the successor free to consult again.

A consultation that closed `lost` is delivered the same way with the status `unavailable` and no answer, and the asker
continues on its own judgment (link:../adr/workflow-consult/001-delivery-is-decided-from-the-askers-lease.adoc[ADR
001]).

== A consultation closed outside the service

The service is the normal closer: the server routes `submit:answer` to `answer` and the model caller routes a role it
cannot serve to `lost`. A consultation the task protocol closes for another reason — for example its re-offers spent,
so it closes `lost` `attempts_spent` — leaves the asking task's entry `open`. `reconcile(at)` repairs every such entry
to `unavailable`, with the consultation's closing instant; it also settles an entry whose consultation task was never
created once the entry is older than the creation grace, and it rewrites a `wait` delivery whose generation no longer
holds the task to `facts`. It is idempotent, and the server calls it on its queue reconciliation tick.

== The submit refers to the consultation

`validateSubmit` compares the `consultation_ids` of a submit with the consultations delivered to the task: every
delivered id must be listed, and no other. A mismatch is refused `invalid_link_args` with a reason that names the
missing or unknown reference; the worker may submit again. The server calls it in its submit path before it accepts the
submission.

== How to use and extend it

Build the service over the project's queue: `new ConsultationService(queue)`. A caller resolves the consulted role (the
state's declaration, the role's class, and its own effective harness and model), then calls `open`; the consulted
role's supervisor serves the consultation task with its own wait. The server calls `answer` when a consultation entity
follows `submit:answer`, `lost` when the model caller cannot serve the role, `facts` for the asking entity's
representation, `links` for the consultation links of an executing task, `validateSubmit` before it accepts a submit,
and `reconcile` on its queue reconciliation tick.

A new rule of the link is a new check in `ConsultationService.links` and in the matching `openRefusal`, derived from
the task record; a new delivery place is a new `DeliveredTo` value of the record schema and a branch of `settleAsker`.
No rule is kept in a second store, and the service reads no clock: every call takes its instant.

== Invariants and failure modes

* A consultation task names its asking task in `consultation_of` and its own id as its entity; a work task names no
  asking task.
* A consultation entity offers no consultation; a task with an open consultation offers none.
* The consultation count is the length of `consultations[]` and only grows; an answered, unavailable or open
  consultation counts the same toward the bound.
* An answer or a loss is recorded once. The second answer is `stale_entity`, not a second result.
* The asking task records the consultation before the consultation task exists. If the task cannot be created, the
  failure propagates and `reconcile` settles the open entry `unavailable` after the creation grace; a crash between
  the two writes leaves the same state, repaired the same way.
* A delivery is effective once: to the asking generation's wait, else to the successor's facts
  (link:../adr/workflow-consult/001-delivery-is-decided-from-the-askers-lease.adoc[ADR 001]).
