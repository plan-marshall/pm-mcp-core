= `workflow.question`
:nofooter:

== What the package is for

The server's side of the question records' continuation and of the question bound (Model Work § Continuing after a
Question, § Question Bound; Timeouts & Budgets § Question hold, § Question resume window, § Question bound). A task that
asks may continue in the asking generation (hold), in a generation resumed from the stored harness session (resume), or
not at all (fallback). Which one it is, and how long it lasts, is decided here and never by the model.

== Main types

* `QuestionContinuation` — `decide(AskForm, envelopeTask, Questions, at)` answers a `Decision` of the form `HOLD`,
  `RESUME` or `FALLBACK` from the three sources the specification names: the state's asking axis, whether the task is an
  envelope task, and the role's effective `questions` bounds, which `EffectiveRole` has already cut to what the harness
  declares verified. `afterHold` decides the move from an expired hold to a suspension or to `hold_expired`. The caller
  assembles the queue record's `continuation` member from the decision and the facts of the suspension (session id,
  digest, worktree head), because those facts are the supervisor's at the moment it suspends the task.
* `QuestionBound` — the `ask` link's bound: three questions per entry of a state, re-entries through
  `<state>.asked` continuing the entry, and three per plan task over all its envelope tasks. `count(stateEntry,
  planTask)` counts on both axes or on neither, so a spent axis withholds the link without counting a question.

== How to use it

The package is the contract the engine and the receipt layers call: the engine asks `QuestionContinuation.decide` when a
task follows its `ask` link and records the outcome through `TaskProtocol.hold`, `TaskProtocol.suspend` or
`TaskProtocol.fallback`; the layer that composes a task representation asks `QuestionBound.mayOffer` before it offers the
link and calls `QuestionBound.count` when the question was filed. Neither caller exists in this repository yet: the
engine's `<state>.asked` wiring and the envelope's `ask:TASK-NNN` path are the remaining work of the package, and this
guide states the contract they must keep. The entry identity is the caller's: pass the same value on a re-entry through
`<state>.asked`, and a new value for a new entry of the state.

== Invariants and failure modes

* `decide` throws on a state that declares no `ask` link (there is no question to continue) and on a `null` instant; it
  never reads a representation, a model request or a plan.
* A bound of zero — an absent `questions` member, or a harness that verified no form — is `not_allowed`, never a
  compiled default.
* `ask fresh` falls back for a task of an ordinary state, and continues for an envelope task, which may always continue.
* A form the harness did not verify cannot be chosen, because `EffectiveRole` cuts the bound to zero; a test that stubs
  the bounds without that cut would hide the rule.
* The decision is pure; the limits are instants derived from the ask's instant, never from a clock read inside the
  package.
