== Purpose
_Requirements: link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-IMPL-7[PM-IMPL-7], link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-2[PM-EXEC-2], link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-4[PM-EXEC-4]_

Package `de.planmarshall.core.service` of `pm-core` holds the service interfaces the foundation declares and
`pm-runtime` implements. The engine and the stores of `pm-core` reach the runtime through them alone: no class of
`pm-core` implements one of them, and a component that needs a service depends on its interface, not on the class of
the runtime that implements it.

[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
|Interface |What it is |Implemented by

|`LockManager` |The one lock manager every lock acquisition of the runtime goes through: it opens a `LockTransaction`.
|`FileLockManager` of `runtime.lock`
|`LockTransaction` |The locks one transaction holds: `acquire(key)` asserts the total lock order and takes the lock in one
call, `holds(key)` asks, `close()` releases them, the last acquired first. |`FileLockManager` of `runtime.lock`
|`AuditSink` |Where a component hands over an audit record (`AuditEvent`); delivery of a store mutation's record is at
least once, deduplicated by its `transaction_id`. |`AuditLogSink` of `core.store` over the audit log
|`LiveWork` |The live work of an enrolled project (its non-terminal plans and epics and its running jobs), which refuses
its `unenrol` with `scope_active`. |the runtime, from its lease and scope records
|`JobRunner` |The job runner: start a job or a probe, cancel a job, read its record (<<_job_runner>>).
|`SubprocessJobRunner` of `runtime.job`
|===

The first four are described where their decisions lie and are not repeated here:

* The total lock order, its levels and the key of every lock are those of `LockKey` and `LockLevel` of `core.store`
  (link:../adr/core-store/011-one-total-lock-order-asserted-at-acquisition.adoc[ADR core-store/011]); an acquisition
  against it is refused with `InternalFaultException`, the failure class `internal_fault`. The lock manager of the runtime
  is link:../../../pm-runtime/doc/adr/runtime-lock/002-one-lock-manager-per-runtime.adoc[ADR runtime-lock/002].
* The audit log behind `AuditSink` is link:core-store.adoc#_audit_log[Scope Stores § The Audit Log].
* `LiveWork` and the refusal of an `unenrol` are
  link:../../../pm-runtime/doc/adr/runtime-enrolment/004-unenrol-refused-while-live-work.adoc[ADR runtime-enrolment/004].

[#_job_runner]
== The Job Runner
_Requirements: link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-4[PM-EXEC-4], link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-1[PM-EXEC-1], link:{docs}/Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-3[PM-EXEC-3]_

`JobRunner<R, C>` is the service through which a job of any kind is started, cancelled and read. It stands beside the
engine port `JobStarter` of `core.spi`, which starts only the job of a `job` state of the workflow; the runtime
implements both (link:../adr/core-service/001-job-runner-service-interface-beside-the-engine-port.adoc[ADR
core-service/001]). Its two type parameters are what `pm-core` cannot name: `R` is the request of a job as the runtime
verifies it (what the job runs and where, `JobRequest` of `runtime.job`), `C` the candidate record of a probe a scan or
a detection wrote (`ProbeCandidate` of `runtime.job`).

[horizontal]
`start(StartRequest<R>)`:: Starts a job. `StartRequest` names the kind (`JobId.Kind`, never `probe`), the owning
`JobRecord.Scope`, the budget key, the budget in seconds (at least 1) and its derivation, the request, and an optional
in-memory `stdin` payload that is never part of the argument vector. The job is verified, recorded and launched. While
the runtime no longer admits jobs because it drains, a verified job is recorded `queued` instead, never launched, and
settled without a process; a start does not wait for a free build or model slot.
`startProbe(ProbeRequest<C>)`:: Starts a probe of a program that is not enrolled yet, verified against its candidate
record instead of an enrolment, with the fixed budget of its probe. `ProbeRequest` carries the candidate and whether the
operator consented to probes of its kind; a probe without that consent is never started.
`cancel(jobId, CancelCause)`:: Cancels a job that is queued or running: a running job's process group is terminated, a
queued job is settled without a signal. The answer is whether this call cancelled the job; `false` for a job that has
settled or that the runner does not know.
`recordOf(jobId)`:: The record of the job as it is now; empty if the runner knows no such job.

=== The Contract

* *The runner opens and closes the verification transaction itself.* A caller never passes a transaction in: the
  runner verifies a job in a transaction it opens and closes before the launch, so no caller holds the enrolment lock
  while a job is launched or awaited, and a job that runs for its whole budget never holds a lock of its caller. This is
  concern (a) left by PLAN-18, stated in the contract of the interface.
* *A refusal is a value.* A start the runner declines answers `StartResult.Refused(reason)`, never an exception; the
  reason is a lower-case snake-case word (`[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`), for example `executable_digest_mismatch`, and anything
  else is refused at construction. Where a record was written, the refusal is recorded as the job's fate `refused`. A
  start that goes ahead answers `StartResult.Started(jobId)`, also for a job recorded `queued` during the drain, which
  is never launched.
* *A cancel names its cause, and the cause decides the fate.* `CancelCause` is a closed set of five causes, each with
  its row of the outcome derivation:

[cols="2,1,1,3"]
|===
|Cause |Row |Fate |When

|`ENGINE_CANCEL` |2 |`cancelled` |the engine cancelled the job: a cancelled job state, the short-circuit of a composite
wait, a scope end
|`PLAN_PAUSED_NOW` |2 |`cancelled` |the plan that owns the job was paused with the pause point `now`
|`SUPERVISOR_END` |2 |`cancelled` |the supervisor ended a worker: lost, fenced or recycled
|`BUDGET_EXPIRED` |3 |`timed_out` |the job's budget expired
|`DRAIN` |4 |`killed` |the runtime drains and the job did not end within the drain timeout
|===

The five causes cover exactly the rows 2 to 4 of the outcome derivation; the other rows are decided by what the runtime
observed of the job itself.

=== Invariants

* *No implementation in the foundation*: no class of `pm-core` implements `JobRunner`; an ArchUnit guard of
  `JobRunnerTest` fails otherwise.
* *Closed causes*: a cause outside the five does not exist, so no cancel can settle a job by a row outside 2 to 4.
* *Checked requests*: a `StartRequest` of kind `probe`, with a blank budget key or a budget under one second, and a
  `ProbeRequest` without its candidate cannot be built.

Tested by `JobRunnerTest` (the components of a start request and their refusals, the refusal reason form, the mapping
of each cause to its row and fate, the causes covering exactly rows 2 to 4, the implementation guard and its matched
control). The runner of the runtime is described in
link:../../../pm-runtime/doc/developer/runtime-job.adoc[Job Runtime (`runtime.job`)].
