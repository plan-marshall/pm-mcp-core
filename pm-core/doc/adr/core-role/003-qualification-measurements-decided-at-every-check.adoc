= ADR 003: A qualification record stores measurements and is decided at every check
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-26

== Context

A qualification record must not carry a pass flag. The operator sets the agreement level and the allowed warm-fresh gap
in `local-config.json`, and a changed level has to apply to a record that already exists without a second grading. The
store schema (`store-schemas/16#qualification-record`) therefore holds measurements only: per form and per topic the
agreement and the item count, per form the tokens and the time, the warm-fresh gap, and every item's answer, score,
tokens and time.

Two properties are not members of the schema but follow from the specification: a topic qualifies only when its
agreement also exceeds its *constant baseline* by the compiled margin `10`, and the screener's *catch rate* over its
suspicious items must reach the agreement level. The baseline can be recomputed from the recorded items and their
references; the catch rate can be recomputed from the recorded answers, because the record keeps every run of an item
with its answer.

== Decision

`QualificationCheck` decides, on each call, which form the record qualifies:

* the digest must equal the role's current qualification digest, the corpus revision the shipped one, and the record
  must have no ungraded item;
* the record must hold a result for every topic the role's corpus defines (`RoleTopics`: `own`, `generic`, both, or —
  a judgment role — `generic` alone);
* every topic's agreement must reach the project's current `agreement_level` and exceed the topic's recorded
  `constant_baseline` by the compiled `10`; a baseline whose sum with the margin exceeds `100` names the topic too
  uniform and invalidates it;
* the warm form qualifies only when its topics meet the same bounds and the recorded warm-fresh gap is at most the
  project's current `allowed_gap`; otherwise a warm artifact is run fresh (`form_downgraded`);
* the screener's catch rate — the share of the recorded runs of its `suspicious`-referenced items answered
  `suspicious` — must reach the agreement level. It is computed from the record's item answers and the corpus items at
  every check, so a changed level applies to it too and no schema member is needed.

The levels are read from `QualificationSettingsStore` at every lookup (`RecordedQualification`), never cached.

== Alternatives Considered

* *Store a pass flag and the pass-time levels.* Refused: the schema is closed, and the specification requires that a
  changed level apply to existing records at once.
* *Add a `catch_rate` member to the record.* Refused: the record schema is owned by the store specification and holds
  no such member. The recorded answers already determine the rate, and deriving it keeps the measurements authoritative.
* *Use the pooled agreement across topics.* Refused: the specification says a topic reaches the level on its own items
  only, and the generic items never cover the role's own items.

== Consequences

* The lookup must be given the effective corpus and the role's topic set; both are cheap reads of the shipped corpus.
* A record with every item ungraded cannot satisfy the schema's one-topic-with-one-item minimum. A grading whose whole
  form is ungraded is refused `no_graded_items` instead of writing a record the schema cannot hold (see
  `PM-WATCH-DISP-58`, still open for the item score of a task that ends without an answer).
* A changed margin or level needs no re-grading, only the next check.
