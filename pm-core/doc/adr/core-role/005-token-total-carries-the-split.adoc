= ADR 005: The token total of a grading record carries its split
:nofooter:

== Status

Superseded in part by link:006-grading-records-persist-the-total-only.adoc[ADR 006] (the qualification and judge-check
records persist the total only); its decision for the decision calibration record stands. The body below states only
the part that stands; what this ADR decided for the qualification and judge-check records is ADR 006's.

== Date

2026-10-27.

== Context

The decision calibration record carries `tokens_in` and `tokens_out` beside its total (Store Schemas § Decision
Calibration Record). The operator must see tokens with the split beside the total, and never a cost or a priced figure
(Client Profiles § Tokens, Not Money).

== Decision

`TokenTotal` carries `tokens_in` and `tokens_out` beside `total_tokens`. The split is a measured pair that sums to the
total, or `unmeasured` at both when it is unknown; an unmeasured total carries no split. `plus` sums the split only when
both addends have one. The split is kept in the value for the decision calibration record, whose schema requires the two
members; the qualification and judge-check records persist the total alone and their codecs refuse a total that carries
a split (ADR 006).

== Alternatives Considered

Deriving the split from the total was rejected: a total is never split into parts it did not measure, and the split is
measured beside it.

== Consequences

The decision calibration record's codec, owed with the package that builds it, writes and reads the two members from
`TokenTotal`. The format version of the grading records stays 1 (R13).
