= ADR 044: The Metrics and Change-Ledger Stores Are Written Through the Scope Transaction
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-26

== Context

The digest sidecar of a scope covers every store "each written by a transaction of the scope", and
`store-schemas/13` names `metrics.json` and the change-ledger JSONL files among them (Store Schemas § Store Digest
Verification Against Out-of-Band Edits). The stores that already met that rule (`WaiverLog`, `QuestionLog`,
`WorkflowStatusStore`) take the `JournalScope` of their scope and add their write as a target of the `ScopeTransaction`
the caller opened under the scope lock.

`MetricsStore` and `ChangeLedger` did not: each was built over a store file and opened its own lock transaction, so
their writes were atomic and locked but outside the journal and the digest sidecar. `metrics.json` and
`change-ledger.jsonl` were therefore not covered by the out-of-band-edit check the other stores of the same scope are.

The choice was whether to keep the self-contained writer and add a second integrity mechanism for these two files, or to
change their shape to the one the other stores already use.

== Decision

`MetricsStore` and `ChangeLedger` take the `JournalScope` of their scope and add their write to the caller's
`ScopeTransaction`:

* `MetricsStore(JournalScope)` writes `metrics.json` of a plan or an epic and `workspace/metrics.json` of the workspace
  through `ScopeTransaction.replace`, after reading the document under the caller's lock.
* `ChangeLedger(JournalScope)` writes `change-ledger.jsonl` of a plan or an epic and `workspace/change-ledger.jsonl` of
  the workspace through `ScopeTransaction.append`; the append-only repair of a torn tail and the digest entry are the
  transaction's, so the store no longer takes an `AuditSink` of its own.
* Each write refuses a transaction of another scope, as the other scope-transaction stores do.
* The public behaviour of both stores is unchanged: one row per attempt, a second row refused, a foreign row kept
  verbatim, an appended line never rewritten.

== Alternatives Considered

1. *Keep the self-contained write and verify these two stores by a second digest mechanism.* _Not taken_: it would be a
   second integrity vocabulary beside the scope sidecar, and the specification says the sidecar covers these stores.
2. *Let the caller keep the store's own lock and commit the store separately after the transaction.* _Not taken_: the
   store write and the other writes of the same decision would no longer be one journaled transaction, and a stop
   between them would leave a half-applied decision the recovery cannot see.

== Consequences

* A row of `metrics.json` or a line of `change-ledger.jsonl` is now journaled with the other stores of its scope and is
  covered by that scope's digest sidecar; an out-of-band edit is reported by `ScopeIntegrity` like any other store's.
* The constructor and the append signature change to the scope-transaction shape. There is no production caller yet, so
  no caller changes.
* The cross-scope reconciler and the executor's change-ledger writer must pass the scope's transaction when they are
  wired; that wiring is outside this plan.
