= ADR 026: The Audit Formats and the Chain in `core.store`, the Keyed Anchor in the Runtime
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-10-14

== Context

The audit trail has three files: the log `logs/audit.jsonl`, its head `logs/audit.head` and the index of rotated
files `logs/audit-index.jsonl`. The head and the checkpoint record of a rotation carry an HMAC under the enrolment
integrity key, which lives in the keyring or the key file of `pm-runtime`; `pm-core` holds no key and may not depend
on the runtime. The writer of the log, which every component reaches through the `AuditSink` of `core.service`, must
call the head and the rotation in every append, under the audit lock, and the first record after a rotation must link
to the checkpoint line that closed the rotated log.

== Decision

* The wire formats of the three files are in `core.store`: `AuditRecord`, `AuditHeadRecord` and `AuditIndexEntry`
  with the strict codec `AuditCodec`, each in `format_version` 1 and checked against the schemas of the specification
  by `AuditSchemaParityTest`. The chain, `AuditLog`, and the production sink, `AuditLogSink`, are there too.
* What needs the key, the head, the rotation and the index, is behind the seam `AuditAnchor`, implemented by
  `AuditHead` of `runtime.audit`. The writer calls its three methods under the audit lock: `beforeAppend` (rotation
  if due), `emptyLogPredecessor()` (the `checkpoint_hash` of the last index entry) and `afterAppend` (the head).
* `runtime.audit` writes into the log only through the public `AuditLog.appendCheckpoint`, which chains the checkpoint
  like every line, returns the hash of its line, and never calls the anchor, so a rotation cannot recurse.
* One predecessor rule decides what every line chains to: the SHA-256 of the last complete line of the log; when the
  log holds no complete line, `emptyLogPredecessor()` when present; genesis (64 `0`) only when no rotation ever
  happened. The first record after a rotation therefore links to the checkpoint line.

== Alternatives Considered

Alternatives not recorded.

== Consequences

* An implementation of `AuditAnchor` takes no lock of its own, since the audit lock is the leaf of the lock order, and
  never throws for a head or an index it cannot write: the record stays appended and the next verification reports
  the gap.
* `AuditAnchor.NONE` lets the chain run alone, for tests and for a runtime that wires no anchor.
* A change of a format is a change in `core.store` and in the specification together, guarded by the parity test.
