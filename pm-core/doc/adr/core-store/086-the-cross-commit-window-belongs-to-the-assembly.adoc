= ADR 086: The Recovery of the Cross-Commit Window Belongs to the Assembly
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-11-22

== Context

The creation of a scope from an entry commits two transactions: the new scope's, which writes the entry's sources,
transcript, question and decision stores and the composed `request.md`, and the workspace's, which deletes the entry's
stores. A stop between the two leaves the entry's record in place while the new scope already holds the copies: a
repeated move is refused by the create targets of the new scope's stores, and the plan scope exists while the entry
still offers itself, so the plan is stranded.

The startup recovery cannot derive this window. It completes the writes it can derive from an authoritative store (a
capture ref, the epic queue row, a lesson claim), but the new scope carries no entry id, so nothing in the new scope
says which entry it was created from. The specification's recovery sentence — a pending entry directory whose plan
exists is deleted — needs that link, and the store-level move cannot supply it without a new durable carrier in the new
scope, which is a contract between `pm-core` and the assembly that runs `start.create-scope`.

== Decision

The recovery of the window is owed to the assembly `plan-marshall-mcp`, whose `start.create-scope` unit creates the
scope: it makes the new scope carry the entry id it was created from, and then derives the deletion of the entry. Until
it does, the operator ends such an entry by abandoning it, and the store-level move keeps its current behaviour — it
refuses the repeated move and writes nothing.

The obligation is recorded in the implementation watch with that owner (PM-WATCH-STORE-42), and the specification's
`start.create-scope` recovery sentence names the assembly as the owner. The operator decided this on 2026-11-22
(question q3 of Deliverable 4).

== Alternatives Considered

1. *Recover here, in the store-level move.*
   _Rejected_: it needs a durable carrier of the entry id in the new scope (a record member, a sidecar or a store),
   which changes a contract every scope reader shares and that the assembly writes; the corrections of this plan do not
   reach that far.
2. *Delete the new scope when the workspace's transaction is missing.*
   _Rejected_: the new scope may be live and readable, and deleting it would destroy work; the entry's record is the
   authority that says the creation did not complete, not the scope's absence.
3. *Leave the window undocumented.*
   _Rejected_: an undocumented window with no owner is what let the residue accumulate; the analysis of `PLAN-V2-22`
   named it as a major item with the owner open.

== Consequences

* PM-WATCH-STORE-42 keeps the hazard with the assembly as its named owner, and the specification's recovery sentence is
  a requirement whose implementation is owed, not a description of today's code.
* A creation cut in that window is ended by the operator's abandon of the entry; a repeated creation is refused, never
  carried twice.
* When the assembly carries the entry id, this record is superseded by the recovery it makes possible.
