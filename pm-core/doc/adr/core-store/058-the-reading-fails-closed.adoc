= ADR 058: The Reading Fails Closed
:nofooter:

== Status

Accepted

== Date

2026-11-01

== Context

The specification says every gate or guard that depends on a `stale` or `failed` fact evaluates `indeterminate`
(`COULD_NOT_EVALUATE`, the `guard_indeterminate` condition), never pass, and that the next read that needs a fact past
its validity period refreshes it. A read may also find no entry at all. The store is read by consumers of three
different kinds: the committer identity and setup checks, the gate that depends on a fact, and the representations of
the assembly.

== Decision

A read answers a `DerivedFactReading`, a sealed interface of four variants: `Value(fact)` for a `current` entry inside
its validity period, `Indeterminate(reason)` for a `stale` or `failed` entry, `Expired()` for a `current` network entry
past its validity period, and `Absent()` for a key with no entry. `couldNotEvaluate()` answers a reason for every
variant but `Value`, and a consumer that maps a reading onto the three-valued guard result maps every non-`Value` to
`CouldNotEvaluate`.

`DerivedFactGuards.evaluate` in `pm-workflow` is that mapping: a current fact hands its value to the guard, and a
stale, failed, expired or absent fact is `GuardResult.CouldNotEvaluate` with the reading's reason. It lives in
`pm-workflow` because `pm-workflow` depends on `pm-core` and never the other way round.

== Alternatives Considered

* Refreshing inside the read: the read would need the derivation and the lock, which the caller of a snapshot read does
  not have; the specification places the refresh in the next call that needs the fact, not in every read.
* Answering an `Optional<DerivedFact>` and letting every consumer decide: two consumers would write their own mapping
  and one of them would fold a missing fact into a pass. The three-valued reading is the one place that decision is
  made.

== Consequences

* A missing or expired fact is never a pass, and a consumer cannot obtain a value for one without a refresh.
* An expired entry reads as `Expired`, not `Indeterminate`: only a failed refresh makes it `stale`.
* `DerivedFactStoreTest` states the four readings and `DerivedFactGuardsTest` the mapping.
