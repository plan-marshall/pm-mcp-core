== Runtime

[#PM-WATCH-RUN-6]
=== PM-WATCH-RUN-6: Drain releases slots and task leases, never provider leases

Anchor:: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_drain_stop_upgrade[Runtime Lifecycle § Drain, Stop & Upgrade]
Hazard:: A drain that releases merge-queue or review-bot leases lets another plan take a merge slot or a rate window
while the drained plan's merge or review round is still in flight on the provider; a drain that leaves a worker's
generation unfenced lets its late submit land after the restart, and one that counts its tasks as lost spends their
re-offer budget on an operator action.
Guard:: Drain with a running build, a busy writer worker, an idle warm worker, a held merge-queue lease, and a held
review-bot lease: the waits of idle workers are answered `end`; after the drain timeout the build and the remaining
worker are settled `killed`, the worker's generation is fenced and its job token revoked, its task lease released,
and the build and model slots released; both provider leases are still recorded after exit, and the next start
adopts or releases them by the recovery evidence rules exactly as after a crash; after the next start the released
task is offered again exactly once without counting a re-offer, and a late call of the fenced generation is refused
`generation_fenced`. Present in `pm-mcp-core`: the drain never touches a lease store; `RuntimeDrainTest` seeds a
merge-queue and a review-bot lease store, each holding a lease, and finds both byte-identical after the drain, and
`RuntimeDrainKilledRuntimeIT` after a runtime killed in the drain wait and the next start. The parts on workers,
generations, task leases and slots stay for the packages of the job runner and the supervisor, which join the drain
as participants.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-RUN-8]
=== PM-WATCH-RUN-8: A plan in an operator wait is listed as waiting, never as stalled or timed out

Anchor:: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#stalled-plans[Runtime Lifecycle § Stalled Plans],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-12[PM-WF-12]
Hazard:: A plan waiting for the operator (an operator wait never ended by time, like the integrity pause and a
pause) is invisible, or is treated as a bounded wait and expires.
Guard:: Aged fixture: a plan resting in an operator wait (an open decision) beyond the stalled threshold is listed by
`pm-operator status`, the web app, and the event stream as waiting for an answer with the decision named, never as
`stalled`, produces no `plan_stalled` record, and the decision never expires; a paused plan beyond the threshold is
listed as paused, never `stalled`; matched control: the same plan without
an open question and without progress beyond the threshold is reported `stalled` with cause `no_progress`.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-RUN-9]
=== PM-WATCH-RUN-9: The web listener never outlives, blocks, or relocates with the runtime

Anchor:: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_startup_sequence[Runtime Lifecycle § Startup Sequence] (Web listener),
link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_drain_stop_upgrade[Runtime Lifecycle § Drain, Stop & Upgrade],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7]
Hazard:: A web listener bound in the same start path as the socket makes an occupied `web.port` or
`web.lan_port` a start failure, so workers and operator verbs lose the runtime over a browser setting; a start that falls back to a free
port serves paired devices at an address nobody enabled; a drain that clears `web.enabled` silently switches web
access off, and one that leaves the TCP socket bound keeps a listener without a runtime behind it.
Guard:: Start test with `web.enabled` and `web.lan` true and `web.port` held by a fixture process: the Unix-socket
listener accepts an authenticated round trip, the LAN listener opens on its own port, no TCP socket is listening on
any other port, `pm-operator status` reports the loopback listener closed with the port, and `machine-config.json`
is unchanged; drain and stop tests with both listeners enabled: after the exit no process listens on `web.port` or
`web.lan_port`, `web` in `machine-config.json` is byte-identical, and the
next start opens both listeners again; matched control: with the port free the same start opens it.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-RUN-13]
=== PM-WATCH-RUN-13: A job created during drain is recorded, never admitted, and settled with the queue

Anchor:: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_drain_stop_upgrade[Runtime Lifecycle § Drain, Stop & Upgrade]
Hazard:: A transition that runs while the runtime drains creates a job that is spawned after admission stopped, so it
outlives the drain timeout unsupervised, or is dropped without a record, so the state waits on a job that never
settles; a `pm_build` during drain blocks until the exit instead of answering.
Guard:: Scenario test on a test clock: a transition during drain creates a build job and a `git` job; both are recorded
`queued`, never spawned, and settled `status: ended`, `fate: killed` at exit without a signal, and the next start's
recovery routes their states like any `killed` job; a `pm_build` during drain returns the queued job with its `job_id`
continuation at once. Matched control: a job admitted before the drain began runs to its end within the drain timeout.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-RUN-15]
=== PM-WATCH-RUN-15: An upgrade is never undone by an old relay or the service unit

Anchor:: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_drain_stop_upgrade[Runtime Lifecycle § Drain, Stop & Upgrade]
Hazard:: After the upgrade switched the `PATH` links, a binary of the old installation directory that starts a
runtime (a relay resolving `PM_MCP_HOME` from its running executable, or a service unit naming the old path) brings
the old `pm-mcpd` back, so the upgrade is reverted without notice and the old installation directory is in use
again.
Guard:: Upgrade test in a runtime-upgradable layout with a running worker relay of the old installation and the
service unit installed: the relay never starts a runtime (with the old runtime gone it answers
`runtime_unavailable` until its job ends), the unit names the new installation directory after the switch, and the
successor is the new `pm-mcpd`; every worker launched after the upgrade starts the job relay of the new installation
by absolute path; matched control: a successor that does not answer within the runtime start timeout switches the
links and the unit back and starts the previous version, which records the upgrade failed.
Source:: design review.

== Server-Driven Scopes

[#PM-WATCH-RUN-2]
=== PM-WATCH-RUN-2: Stalls and idle gaps on an active plan are derived

Anchor:: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#stalled-plans[Runtime Lifecycle § Stalled Plans],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-7[PM-ARCH-7], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-SEC-6[PM-SEC-6]
Hazard:: A stalled plan left no record; an agent that announced continuation and then ended its turn halted the plan
unnoticed.
Guard:: `pm-operator status`, the web app, and the epic view derive `stalled` (cause `no_progress`) for non-terminal
scopes without pending condition or awaited event (no running job, no task queued or leased within its budget, no
open external wait, operator wait, pause, or hold) whose last transition or task progress is older than the
compiled stalled threshold, and the runtime records one `plan_stalled` audit record per episode with the idle span;
a role the supervisor cannot serve stalls nothing, because its tasks close `role_unavailable`; metrics derive idle
gaps from the audit log; nothing of it reaches a model-facing representation; test with an aged fixture, a blocked
control, and a control with a leased task, neither of which is reported.
Fixture:: a 17-min gap 13:45:20 → 14:02:28; 12h54m idle vs 2h8m worked; a deferred stall nothing recorded
(D-087-g).
Source:: plan-marshall defect post-run-quality PLAN-PRQ-04 D0; truthful-signals (quality-aspect) PLAN-217 D3.
