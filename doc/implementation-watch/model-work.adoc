== Overview

Watch items for the link:../specification/model-work.adoc[Model Work Specification]: the task protocol and its
leases, worker supervision and skill delivery, envelope yield, worker cost records and the plan-marshall cost
baseline, the launch contract, the screener, and the epic ledger, landings and server-started plans. Many items carry
measured plan-marshall data that the PM-MIG-5 cost comparison and future cost levers need. The sections mirror the
specification. Item format and lifecycle: link:../ImplementationWatch.adoc[Implementation Watch].

== Roles, Composition & Qualification

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-49]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-49: A role whose class does not fit its use never runs

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-composition[Model Work § Composition Check],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-1[PM-WORK-1]
Hazard:: A role is defined but of the wrong class for its entity kind: a `read_only` role on the task envelope cannot
write, a `writer` role as the screener holds tools that untrusted text can steer, and a consulted `zero_tool` role
answers without the repository; the defect shows only when the state is reached, or after an artifact edit changed
the class.
Guard:: Composition test: a `job llm` state whose role's class differs from the class its entity kind registers, and a
`consult role` clause naming a `zero_tool` role, refuse the composition with `class_mismatch` naming the role, its
class, the state, and the required class; after an edit that changes a composed role's class, task creation closes
the task without an attempt with `class_mismatch`, recorded `cause_class: refused`, and the state takes its
conservative fallback. Matched control: every kind of the shipped units with its shipped role composes.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-51]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-51: A judge score counts only from a validated result of an isolated judge

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#grading[Model Work § Grading],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-6[PM-WORK-6]
Hazard:: A rubric score parsed from free text, or from a judge that could read the repository or reach the server,
qualifies a configuration on a grade nobody can trust; a missing score counted as zero or as met skews the
agreement either way.
Guard:: Fixtures: a judge reply without `score`, with `score` above `1`, or with a member outside `judge-score` is
not accepted; the task closes `lost`, the item is recorded `ungraded`, the record's `ungraded` count rises, and the
record qualifies no form; the next grading run re-grades only that item while the qualification digest and the
corpus revision are unchanged. The judge's job runs in the empty `cwd/` with no MCP connection and one turn.
Matched control: a reply that validates against `judge-score` closes `submitted` and its `score` is the item's score.
Source:: design review.

== Task Protocol, Supervision & Skill Delivery

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-41]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-41: A leased task is never offered to a second loop of the same generation

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#pm-wait[Model Work § `pm_wait`: The Worker's Only Wait],
link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#leases-fencing[§ Leases, Generations & Fencing],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-2[PM-WORK-2]
Hazard:: Two wait loops ran under one identity (after `/new` on OpenCode the previous loop went on pulling behind
the same relay), and the server handed a task leased to the caller to its second concurrent wait as well: both loops
decided it, and the second submit was refused.
Guard:: Scenario test: two concurrent `pm_wait` calls of one generation: the earlier is answered `wait_again` at once
and never with a task, the later receives the offer, and the task is leased exactly once; a generation that holds a
lease is offered no second task; a fenced generation's calls are refused `generation_fenced` and its lease is
released; matched control: two generations of one role each receive their
own task.
Source:: harness-as-worker evaluation (doc/specification/evaluation.adoc).

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-42]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-42: A task is lost only through a recorded attempt end

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#task-outcomes[Model Work § Closed Task Outcomes],
link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#job-completion[§ Task Completion & the `job_incomplete` Condition],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-2[PM-WORK-2]
Hazard:: Tasks still open when a measurement run ended were counted as lost, so the loss figure described where the
run was stopped instead of what the workers did.
Guard:: Table test over the queue records: `lost` is recorded only when the server closes the task (re-offers spent,
a closing role check, a withdrawal of its state or scope) and always with its reason; an attempt whose worker was
ended or fenced while holding the task is offered again first, never closed `lost` by the fence; a task that is queued, offered, or executing when the runtime drains or a report is taken
counts as open, never as lost; after a restart such a task is offered again without counting a re-offer; reports and
`pm-operator status` count outcomes only from closed queue records.
Source:: harness-as-worker evaluation (doc/specification/evaluation.adoc).

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-47]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-47: A task is offered by the role's current configuration digest

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#offer[Model Work § Offer],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-2[PM-WORK-2]
Hazard:: An offer matched by the digest stored at task creation strands every task queued before an artifact edit:
no generation started after the edit matches it, and the generation that does match is recycled; an offer matched by
role alone hands new work to a generation running the outdated configuration.
Guard:: Scenario test: a task queued before an edit of its role's artifact is offered to the first generation started
with the new configuration digest, and that digest is recorded on the attempt; a running generation with the outdated
digest gets no new offer and is recycled; matched control: an edit that leaves the digest unchanged keeps offering to
the running generation. Only a worker launched by the runtime as a job of its role is ever offered a task.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-62]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-62: A warm worker learns which files changed since its last task

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#changed-since-last-task[Model Work § Changed since the Last Task],
link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-2[PM-WORK-2]
Hazard:: A warm reviewer or writer that read a file in its previous task acts on that content after another worker
changed it, and nothing in its context says the file moved; a list computed from commits alone misses the uncommitted
changes, and one computed from the current status alone misses what was committed between the two tasks.
Guard:: Scenario test: a warm generation closes a task, another worker commits a change to one file and leaves a

carries no list.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-44]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-44: A new generation or a changed skill re-delivers the skills; a compaction is left to the shim

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc#skill-delivery[Model Work § Skill Delivery Record & Re-Delivery],

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-56]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-56: A repeated submit after the close is stale, not a scope violation

Anchor:: link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#execute-submit[Model Work § Execute & Submit]
Hazard:: The lease is released in the transaction that closes the task, so a lease check that only asks "is this
entity leased to the caller" answers a retried submit (after a lost response) with `job_scope_mismatch`, which the
worker reads as a contract breach, or lets it through as a second result.
Guard:: Test: a worker submits, the response is dropped, and it submits again: the second call is refused
`stale_entity` carrying the entity's representation, and the task stays closed exactly once; the same holds for any
other closing link of that task by that generation; matched control: a call on an entity never leased to the
generation is refused `job_scope_mismatch`.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-60]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-60: One stalled worker blocks every process of its harness

Anchor:: link:../specification/job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#worker-data-home[Job Runtime § Per-Worker Data Home],
link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#start-collisions[Model Work § Starts of One Harness],
link:../specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#headless-data-home[Client Profiles § Normative Client Profile JSON Schema] (Per-worker data home)
Hazard:: A harness keeps its local state in one per-user store. A worker stopped in the middle of a step holds
that store's write lock: every other worker of the harness exits at start, the start-failure guard stops the role
before the stalled worker's silence deadline, its task is never submitted, and the operator's own interactive
sessions of the harness are blocked until the stalled process is killed.
Guard:: The fault matrix of `opencode` runs with the per-worker data home: in `stop-long`, the other workers keep
starting and working while one is stalled, the stalled worker is detected by silence at its deadline and never
earlier, and its task is submitted exactly once by its successor; no two workers and no worker and an operator
session open the same data directory (fixture: an interactive session of the harness stays usable while a worker
is stopped); the data directory with the login copy is gone after the job's fate, also after a `SIGKILL` and after
a runtime crash (project recovery); the login file is never read into a store, a log, or the redaction registry's
persisted output; a harness that declares a data home is `worker.eligible` only with this run passed in all six
faults, and the run is repeated against the job runtime's own launch.
Fixture:: OpenCode 1.18.34, fault matrix, the stall longer than the silence limit: after the stop of `w2` generation 4, `w1`
generations 5 to 7 each exited at start with `Failed query: insert into "project" … on conflict …` on
`~/.local/share/opencode/opencode.db`; the guard ended the cell after 39 s, before the 50 s silence deadline. With
a data directory per worker the same cell passed ten of ten trials, with no start failure of another worker.
Source:: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#results[Evaluation Reference], Gate 16.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-61]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-61: A dead runtime leaves the login copies of its workers behind

Anchor:: link:../specification/job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#worker-data-home[Job Runtime § Per-Worker Data Home],
link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_per_project_recovery[Runtime Lifecycle § Per-Project Recovery]
Hazard:: The copy of a harness's login file in a worker's data directory is removed when the worker's job settles,
by the runtime that launched it. A runtime that is killed while workers run removes nothing: the copies of its
live workers stay on disk, a credential of the operator in a place the operator does not know, until something
removes them. A recovery that removes only the directories of jobs it finds `ended` in the store, and runs before
it has settled the records left `running`, skips exactly these.
Guard:: Recovery test: kill the runtime (`SIGKILL`) while two workers of a harness with a data home run, start it
again, and assert that after the project's recovery no login copy and no data directory of either worker exists,
that both job records are `ended` with fate `killed`, and that the operator's own login file is unchanged; the
same with a crash between the recorded fate and the removal; matched control: the data directory of a worker
that is still running under the new runtime is untouched.
Fixture:: OpenCode 1.18.34, fault matrix: the driver that launched the workers was killed from outside in the
`relay-kill` cell; the login copies of its two live workers (mode `0600`) stayed in their worker directories until
they were deleted by hand. In the complete run, 73 worker generations that were killed, stopped, fenced, or ended
left none.
Source:: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#results[Evaluation Reference], Gate 16.
