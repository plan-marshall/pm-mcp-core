== What

* *Implements* link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#continuation[Model Work § Continuing after a Question] with link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#answer-acknowledgement[its answer acknowledgement], link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#answer-in-next-task[§ The Answer in the Next Task], link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-bound[§ Question Bound], and the rows link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#question-hold[Question hold], link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#question-resume-window[Question resume window], and link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#question-bound[Question bound] of the Timeouts & Budgets (`PM-WORK-2`, its bullet on resumability). Nothing of it exists: the parser reads the `ask` declaration of a task state (`StateBehavior.ModelJob.ask`) and no `ask fresh`, and `workflow.task`, `workflow.question`, `runtime.worker`, and `runtime.launch` hold only their `package-info.java`.
* *The form, chosen by the server*: after an `ask` (or `ask:TASK-NNN` of an envelope task) the server holds while the role's `questions.hold_seconds` lasts, cut to the harness's `headless.resumability.max_hold_seconds`, then suspends where the task may resume, and falls back otherwise; the form comes from the state (`ask fresh` never continues, an envelope task may always continue), the role artifact (`questions`, absent: no continuation), and the harness (a form its profile does not declare verified counts as absent), never from the model. `ask fresh` is parsed, printed, and refused like `ask` on a `role each` state.
* *Hold*: the task stays leased to the asking generation in `awaiting_answer`; its representation offers only `pm_wait`, held in windows with progress frames where the host needs them; when the record closes the pending or next `pm_wait` answers with the task's representation, `facts.answers` carrying the answer and `ack` its only link; the task's links are offered again only after the acknowledgement. The worker keeps its model slot, is not idle (a `wait_again` to a holding generation is no idle wake-up), and the hold time does not count toward its worker budget.
* *Resume*: the worker of a role that may resume is launched without the harness's no-persistence flag and its session id read from the structured result; after its ask it ends with the session stored, the task `suspended` (lease released, no worker, no slot), its job-private directory and per-worker data home kept; when the record closes the server starts a new generation from `headless.resumability.resume.argv` on the stored session, leases the task to it, and offers it with `facts.answers`, the offer's acknowledgement being the answer acknowledgement. A suspension lasts at most `questions.resume_within_seconds`, cut to the harness's `max_age_seconds`.
* *The record of a continuation*: the member `continuation` of the queue task record (`question_id`, `form`, `hold_until`, `session_id`, `suspended_at`, `resume_until`, `suspended_digest`, `suspended_head`, `fallback_reason`), the statuses `awaiting_answer` and `suspended`, the attempt end `suspended`; the usage of a hold's wake-ups and of a resumed generation accounted to the task, marked `continuation`.
* *Fallback*: the task closes `asked`, the stored session is dropped, and the work continues in a new task of the role that carries the closed questions in `facts.answers`, with `fallback_reason` recorded: not allowed, the hold or the resume window passed (`resume_expired`), the session missing or too old, the role's configuration digest or the enrolled harness version changed, the resumed generation not acknowledging, or the plan's worktree no longer at its head of the suspension. An answer that arrives during a hold or a suspension continues the task; the scope shows the task as in its question state, never as stalled.
* *Losses of a continuing worker* (link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#detection[Model Work § Detection]): a missing answer acknowledgement within the host's `worker.ack_deadline_seconds` ends the worker as lost (by silence alone where the host's acknowledgement is informational), and the task is resumed where the session was stored, else offered again with `facts.answers`; a worker that reaches its token budget while holding ends the hold, its task suspended or fallen back, and is recycled; a fenced holding generation releases the task, offered again within its re-offer budget with the answer once the record closes; a runtime restart keeps a `suspended` task suspended and ends a hold like every held task.
* *Question bound*: the `ask` link is offered to the tasks of one entry of a state at most `3` times, re-entries through `<state>.asked` continuing the entry, and `ask:TASK-NNN` at most `3` times per plan task over all its envelope tasks, whichever form each question took; after that the link is not offered.
* *The stage `resumability`* of the profile verification (link:../specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#profile-verification[Client Profiles § Profile Verification]) with the probe task of link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/question-loop-verification.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `question-loop-verification`]: it measures hold and resume of the harness and records the `headless.resumability` values; a form it did not measure stays `false` or `null`. The parsing and validation of `headless.resumability` are link:profile-loader.adoc[`profile-loader`]'s, the role artifact's `questions` member link:role-sets-and-model-caller.adoc[`role-sets-and-model-caller`]'s; running the stage in the onboarding is link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`]'s.
* *Measured first*: the values and the specification this package implements are those `question-loop-verification` settled; a result of it that proves the specification wrong is corrected there before this package's code follows.

== Done When

On fixture harnesses and a fixture role, a held task continues in the same generation after its answer and offers its links only after `ack`; a suspended task resumes in a new generation from the stored session; each fallback reason closes the task `asked` with its `fallback_reason` and the next task carries the answer in `facts.answers`; an `ask fresh` state and a role without `questions` never continue; a missing answer acknowledgement ends the worker as lost and the task resumes or is offered again exactly once; a token budget reached while holding suspends or falls back and recycles the worker; a restart keeps a suspended task suspended; the third `ask` of a state entry and the third `ask:TASK-NNN` of a plan task are the last offered; the hold time is not charged to the worker budget and the continuation's usage is marked `continuation`; the stage `resumability` records the measured forms of a fixture harness and leaves an unmeasured form `false`.
