== What

Task 4 of link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/question-corpus-public-datasets.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `question-corpus-public-datasets`] (`question/ask-or-proceed`) generates several items from one source row and needs them to share a `group` and their `contamination` notes to name each other. Neither member can be generated today:

* The overlay row and the imported item have neither member, and both formats are closed (`additionalProperties: false`); the `ImportedItem` record states that the generation never writes a group or a contamination note.
* The item schema of the corpus in `plan-marshall-mcp` allows both as optional members, so the corpus's own items can carry them and the generated ones cannot.
* The members are functional, not descriptive: the warm form of the role grading never gives one worker items that an item's `contamination` note names (link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#grading[Model Work § Grading]), and a control is the original of a modified problem and gives its answer away.

The task:

* The members `group` and `contamination` on the overlay row and on the imported item, written into the generated item.
* *Formats changed together*: record, codec and schema resource of the overlay and of the imported item; the validator's rules follow; an ADR of `core.role.corpus` records the change against link:../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-role-corpus/001-source-manifest-overlay-and-imported-item-formats.adoc[ADR 001] and link:../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-role-corpus/006-source-fetch-kind-repeated-rows-and-answer-schema.adoc[ADR 006] of that package. `ProductCorpusValidationTest` stays green on the product corpus.

Left open by this package: how the members are given in the overlay (literal, or a template over the row's fields). Required is only that items of one source row, generated from several overlay rows, can name each other.

== Done When

The codec and schema tests cover `group` and `contamination` on the overlay row and the imported item; `CorpusImportTest` generates, from several overlay rows of one source row, items that share one `group` and whose `contamination` notes name each other, and an overlay row without the members generates an item without them; `CorpusValidator` applies its rules to the members; the ADR is written; and `ProductCorpusValidationTest` stays green.
