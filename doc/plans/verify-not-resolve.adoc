== What

* The verify-not-resolve pipeline over the enrolment store: nothing a job runs is resolved at launch, everything is verified against what the operator enrolled.
* Wrapper verification over the pinned files recorded at enrolment; a changed build configuration is refused until re-enrolment.
* Adversarial confinement tests with their positive controls.
