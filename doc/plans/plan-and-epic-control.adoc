== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the changes Pausing and Starting a Plan, Cancelling and Abandoning Archive, The Epic Is Told, An Epic's Actions, Holds and Their Acts, and Releasing Ends Work as the Operator Ends It into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once UC-CTL-1, UC-CTL-2, UC-CTL-4, UC-CTL-5, and UC-PRJ-3 are agreed (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]).
* *A plan's actions by state* (UC-CTL-1): running: pause, cancel, chat, settings; pausing: start (the pause withdrawn), cancel, chat, settings; paused: start, abandon, chat, settings; waiting for the operator: those of running, beside the decision it waits for; held: those of running and the acts of its hold; ended: its view and a read-only chat. Pause with its point (now: tasks withdrawn and jobs stopped, both run again on start; after the current task, the default; after the current phase); a plan of an epic created and started in one act, never waiting to be started.
* *The ends of a plan*: cancel (a running, pausing, waiting, or held plan) and abandon (a paused one) through `plan.aborted` with the outcome `cancelled` or `abandoned` and the operator's reason, then the archive; the cancel of a plan held by an integrity pause is that pause's _abort_, with the outcome `aborted` and the operator's reason. The closed list of plan outcomes stays the terminal-state catalogue's (below), which this package extends by `cancelled`, `abandoned`, and `released`, each archived with its reason; the open pull request closed with the reason or left open; a claimed lesson released on every end without a landing (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/05-starting-work.adoc#change-lesson-claim[A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing], built by link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`]).
* *A plan's settings* (UC-CTL-5): `plan-settings.open` (each value with where it comes from: the plan, the project, the machine), `plan-settings.aspect`, `plan-settings.value` (the current value preselected, or remove the plan's value), `plan-settings.validate` (an enrolled harness, an allowed model, an available provider, a landing variant that fits the project and the plan; refused with the reason, back to `plan-settings.value`), `plan-settings.apply` (written to the plan's `overrides.json` of link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`], audited, a new instance version of the composition event `override` under the position rule); a change whose new version would not contain the plan's current state and call stack is refused as too late, back to `plan-settings.aspect`; a change made against an older revision of the plan's settings is refused at `plan-settings.apply` with `conflict` and the current value, back to `plan-settings.value` (the rule of link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/01-onboarding.adoc#concurrent-changes[Concurrent Changes of a Setting]); what runs keeps its value, the new one applies where the plan next reads it.
* *Its epic told*: an inbox record per operator act on a plan of a live epic (`paused`, `started`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `revised`, `settings_changed` with the aspect), the queue row following the plan's state (its status gains `pausing`, `paused`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`), `coordinate` deciding on it.
* *A restart*: a paused plan stays paused, a pausing one holds at its point.
* *The epic as a resource* (UC-PLN-5): `GET /api/v1/epics/{epic_id}` with its rows, sources, landings, registers' counts, the draft while one is open, the archive check, its history; the operator's acts as its endpoints.
* *Holds and their acts* (UC-CTL-4): each hold a record with its kind from the closed list `stalled`, `condition`, `integrity_pause`, `held`, `landing_failed`, its cause, attempts, and the acts that resolve it, each an operator endpoint of both clients, and beside them cancel (for an integrity pause its _abort_: a plan `aborted`, an epic `cancelled`); after an act `hold.apply` goes to `hold.show` with what still holds and its cause, or to `hold.released`, the plan, the epic, or the landing going on; a condition of an expired credential resolved by the machine's settings (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/01-onboarding.adoc#UC-ONB-10[UC-ONB-10]); `paused` is neither a hold nor a kind of `needs_operator`, a paused plan shows by its state; `facts.operator_command` goes.
* *Chat*: offered among the actions of a plan and of an epic in every state that has not ended, and read-only on an ended plan or epic beside its view, over its archive entry; the conversation itself, also its follow-up plan from ended work, is the sub-workflow of link:chat.adoc[`chat`].
* *An epic's actions by state* (UC-CTL-2): running: next plan, decompose again, findings review, watches review, archive, cancel, chat; waiting for the operator: those of running, beside what it waits for; held: those of running and the acts of its hold; landing: those of running, archive offered disabled until the ledger has landed; ended: its view and a read-only chat.
* *Next plan*: start the next launchable row, creating its plan; the reasons per row when none is; with the scope full the operator wait `epic.raise-scope` (raise `parallelization_scope` by one, a ledger write of the header, then `epic.next-work`; or leave it).
* *Archive*: the archive check before `epic.archive`, where only an `open` (undecided) finding holds it; kept findings and watches are listed as carried and archived with the ledger; `terminal_reason` `completed`.
* *Cancel*: with the choice for its plans; `terminal_reason` `cancelled`, the value `abandoned` of today renamed (pre-1.0, no shim).
* *The release of a project* (UC-PRJ-3, moved here from link:project-onboarding-unit.adoc[`project-onboarding-unit`], since it ends plans and epics with the outcomes of this package): release and unenrol as operator acts of both clients, confirmed with what they end; unenrol refused while work runs, listing it; `project.release-wait` lets a running `git.land-removal` of the project finish within its bound and refuses the release, ending nothing, when it does not; `project.release` deletes the pending entries with their sources, ends a running onboarding of the project, ends every plan whatever its state through `plan.aborted` with the outcome `released` (never `cancelled` or `abandoned`) and every epic with the `terminal_reason` `released`, archives each, then closes the plan lifecycle; nothing is left to tell the epics.

== Done When

The specification of the changes is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; a test per action and state of a plan and of an epic shows what it does and what it refuses, also waiting for the operator, held, and an epic landing with archive disabled; each pause point holds where it says, also at an operator wait and at a loop-back; a paused plan serves nothing until started and resumes where it stopped; an ended plan is archived with each of its outcomes and reason, the cancel of an integrity pause with `aborted`; a settings change applies from the plan's next read, is refused with its reason for an invalid value, as too late where the new version would not contain the plan's position, and with `conflict` and the current value where another change came first, and composes an instance version of event `override`; every act on a plan of an epic, a settings change included, reaches the epic's inbox; next plan with `epic.raise-scope`, the archive check (an open finding holds it, kept findings and watches carried), and cancel behave as specified, also with no launchable row, and an epic ends `completed`, `cancelled`, or `released`, never `abandoned`; every hold of the closed list offers its acts and cancel as endpoints, an act ending in `hold.show` while something still holds and in `hold.released` otherwise, and no representation carries `facts.operator_command` any more; a release waits for a running `git.land-removal` and is refused when it does not finish, deletes the pending entries, ends a running onboarding, and ends every plan `released` and every epic `released`, each archived.
