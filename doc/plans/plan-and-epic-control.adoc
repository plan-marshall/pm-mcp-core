== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *A plan's activity*, pause with its points and start, also across a restart (UC-CTL-1): link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#plan-pause[Runtime Lifecycle § Pausing and Starting a Plan].
. *The ends of a plan*, cancel and abandon with their archive and report, and the outcomes of the terminal catalogue: link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#plan-end[State Catalogue § Cancelling and Abandoning Archive]; the release of a claimed lesson: link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-claim[Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing].
. *A plan's settings* (UC-CTL-5), read with their origin, written, validated and composed: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-overrides[Workflow DSL § Plan Overrides] and link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#concurrent-changes[CLI & Security § Concurrent Changes of a Setting].
. *The epic told* of every act on its plans, and the queue row following the plan: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-informed[Model Work § The Epic Is Told].
. *Holds and their acts* (UC-CTL-4), each act an operator endpoint, and no operator relay: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[State Catalogue § Holds and Their Acts]; link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#operator-command-relay[Hypermedia Format § No Operator Relay]. The hold record carries `awaited` where the scope awaits something (`kind`, the id of the job, task, wait or lease, `since`, the bound or the expected duration, and the last observed change), the scope's position (`state`, `call_path`, `instance_version`) and the correlation ids that apply (`transaction_id`, `job_id`, `queue_task_id`, `generation_id`). The hold kind `overdue`, of a scope whose stall-watched wait passed its bound, has the causes `no_change` and `long_running` and the acts `observe_now` and `cancel_run` (confirmed first) beside the scope's cancel, and leaves the route of the wait unchanged until an act is taken: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#overdue-waits[Runtime Lifecycle § Overdue Waits]; its audit record `plan_overdue`: link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Security & Transition Audit Log], _Stall and overdue records_. This package builds the acts; the engine side of the record and of the hold `overdue` is task 1 and task 3 of link:stall-clocks-and-overdue-waits.adoc[`stall-clocks-and-overdue-waits`]. Open: which store records a CI wait's expected duration and last observed change, and the `Retry-After` deferral, since no store member defines them.
. *Operator acts over the local API*, never workflow states: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#operator-flows[State Catalogue § Operator Flows That Are Not Workflow Units].

Left out: the lesson claim itself (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`]); the epic cancel the integrity hold offers as _abort_ (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]); the plan overrides' axes (link:../Traceability.adoc#PM-WF-25[Traceability PM-WF-25]); the views in the clients.
