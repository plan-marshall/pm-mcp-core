== What

* *Implements* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/04-sessions-and-workers.adoc#plan-pause[Runtime Model § Pausing and Starting a Plan], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#plan-end[State Catalogue § Cancelling and Abandoning Archive], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[§ Holds and Their Acts], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-overrides[Workflow DSL § Plan Overrides] (a plan's settings), link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-informed[Model Work § The Epic Is Told], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-actions[§ An Epic's Actions], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#epic-resource[Runtime Model § The Epic as a Resource], and link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-credentials-and-operator.adoc#project-release[CLI & Security § Releasing Ends Work as the Operator Ends It] (`PM-WF-23`, `PM-MIG-5`). The step names the use cases give these flows (`plan.*`, `plan-settings.*`, `hold.*`, `epic.actions`, `epic.raise-scope`, `epic.confirm-cancel`, `epic.cancel`) are operator acts over the local API, never workflow states (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#operator-flows[State Catalogue § Operator Flows That Are Not Workflow Units]).
* *A plan's activity* (UC-CTL-1): beside its terminal states a plan is `running`, `pausing`, or `paused`, part of its persisted state and never a kind of `needs_operator`. Pause (`POST /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/pause`) with its point: `after_task` (the default: what runs finishes, no new task or job starts), `after_phase` (the phase sub-workflow on the call stack runs to its return), or `now` (running tasks withdrawn uncounted, their workers ended and fenced, running jobs cancelled, a retry's backoff clock stopped); a plan inside the rebase-to-merge section holds when it leaves it. A paused plan keeps its operator waits, holds, and leases but no slot, is offered no task, is never `stalled`, and keeps its place in its epic's `parallelization_scope`. Start (`POST /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/start`) resumes from the current state, and on a pausing plan withdraws the pause; an act the activity does not offer is refused `act_not_available`, on an ended plan `plan_ended`. A plan of an epic is created and started in one act, never waiting to be started. Every pause and start is audited with its channel and announced (`activity.changed`).
* *The ends of a plan*: cancel (a running, pausing, waiting, or held plan, outcome `cancelled`) and abandon (a paused one, outcome `abandoned`) through `plan.aborted` with the operator's required reason, confirmed with what they end, `pm-operator abort plan` their shell form; the open pull request closed with a comment naming the reason or left open, its branch kept; the archive under `archive/<plan_id>/` like a completed plan; the report `plan.ended`. Only the cancel of a plan held by an integrity pause is that pause's resolution _abort_, outcome `aborted`. The closed list of plan outcomes of the terminal-state catalogue gains `cancelled`, `abandoned`, and `released`. A claimed lesson is released on every end without a landing (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-claim[Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing], built by link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`]).
* *A plan's settings* (UC-CTL-5): the plan's overrides of link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`] read with each value's origin (the plan, the project, the machine) and written by an operator act into `overrides.json`, validated like the machine's values (an enrolled harness, an allowed model or `model_not_allowed`, an available provider, a landing variant validated as for the project), audited, composing a new instance version of the composition event `override` under the position rule; a change whose new version would not contain the plan's position is refused `position_not_contained` and not written; a change made against an older revision is refused `conflict` with the current value (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#concurrent-changes[CLI & Security § Concurrent Changes of a Setting]); what runs keeps its value, the new one applies where the plan next reads it; an override to an unqualified role configuration is shown unqualified and takes the qualification fallback.
* *Its epic told*: an inbox record in the act's transaction per operator act on a plan of a live epic (`paused`, `started`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `revised`, `settings_changed`), with the reason and channel; the queue row following the plan (its status gains `pausing`, `paused`, and `cancelled`; `abandoned` also for a paused plan the operator abandoned); `coordinate` deciding on each record; a pausing or paused plan's row counting toward `parallelization_scope`.
* *A restart*: a paused plan stays paused, a pausing one holds at its point.
* *The epic as a resource* (UC-PLN-5): `GET /api/v1/epics/{epic_id}` with its rows, sources, landings, registers' counts, the draft while one is open, the archive check, its history; the operator's acts as its endpoints.
* *Holds and their acts* (UC-CTL-4): a hold is a record derived from the scope's records and kept on read, never a workflow state or an operator wait, with its `kind` from the closed list `stalled` (acts `retry_now`, `recycle_worker`), `condition` (the acts its reason names), `integrity_pause` (the act `accept`, which shows the tampered file with its recorded and current size and digest and accepts it as it is; no diff and no restore), `held` (`retry` and the item's own acts), `landing_failed` (`retry`, and `give_up` for the removal of moved documents), its `cause`, `since`, what was tried, and its `acts` as operator endpoints of both clients, each act audited with its channel and device and answered with the hold as it stands then; beside them the scope's cancel, for an integrity pause its resolution _abort_ (a plan `aborted`, an epic `cancelled`); a hold that clears by itself ends without an act and writes the report `plan.hold_released`; `paused` is no hold; no hold carries an operator command as relay text (`facts.operator_command` goes).
* *Chat*: not built here. The action Chat among the actions of a plan and of an epic in every state, read-only on an ended plan or epic, its endpoint, and the conversation itself, also its follow-up plan from ended work, are link:chat.adoc[`chat`]'s, which adds the action to the lists of actions this package builds; this package leaves its place in them and does not wait for it.
* *An epic's actions* (UC-CTL-2): next plan, decompose again, the reviews, archive, cancel, chat, and the toggle _Automatic: on/off_ with the overlap limit, each an operator endpoint of the epic's resource, validated, answered with the epic's new state, audited, and re-entering `epic.next-work` as an `operator_action`; what the epic starts and does by itself is link:epic-auto.adoc[`epic-auto`]'s, the review link:epic-findings-review.adoc[`epic-findings-review`]'s, decompose again link:epic-decomposition.adoc[`epic-decomposition`]'s.
* *Next plan*: `epic.next-work` starts the next launchable row now under the same admission tests; with no launchable row it answers why per row (`dependency`, `lock`, `scope`, `overlap`, `stale`, `held`); with `parallelization_scope` full it offers to raise it by one, a ledger write of the header.
* *Archive*: `epic.review-completion` first where it has not run since the epic was through, then the archive check `epic.archive-check` (no plan running, pausing, or paused, every row shipped or ended, no `open` finding, the ledger landed, the completion review done), archiving through `epic.archive` only when it passes and otherwise answering what is open; kept findings and watches are listed as carried and archived with the epic; `terminal_reason` `completed`.
* *Cancel*: with the operator's reason (`pm-operator abort epic` its shell form) and the choice for its plans (cancel them, each `cancelled`, or let them run on detached); the epic ends through `epic.aborted` with `terminal_reason` `cancelled`, the value `abandoned` of the code renamed (pre-1.0, no shim).
* *The release of a project* (UC-PRJ-3, an act of both clients, `POST /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/release`, `pm-operator project release` its terminal form, confirmed with what it ends): a running short landing of the project (`git.land-removal`, `git.land-unit`) finishes first within `release.landing_wait_seconds`, no new start or short landing admitted meanwhile (`lifecycle_closed`), and when it does not, the release is refused `release_landing_pending` and ends nothing; then `released_at` is written into the enrolment with an `operator` audit record; every plan that has not ended, whatever its state, ends through `plan.aborted` with the outcome `released` (never `cancelled` or `abandoned`) and is archived, its open decisions and waiver requests closed `cancelled` and its lesson claims released; every epic ends with the `terminal_reason` `released` by an engine-owned transition into `epic.aborted` that bypasses `epic.archive`, its open tasks withdrawn and its machine scope archived in the project's machine store only, its ledger left in `.marshall/epics/<epic_id>/` untouched, with no ledger commit and no epic landing; the outcome `released` changes no queue row; the pending entries are deleted with their sources, each entry ended as a scope first so that whatever stands on its call stack ends with it (a running task withdrawn with the reason `withdrawn`), and a running onboarding or setup run is ended; the report `project.released`; the release touches no repository. While `released_at` is set, a start is refused `lifecycle_closed`; enrolling again clears it. Unenrol stays refused while work runs, `scope_active` naming it.

== Done When

A test per act and activity of a plan and per act of an epic shows what it does and what it refuses (`act_not_available`, `plan_ended`), also waiting for the operator, held, and an epic whose ledger has not landed failing the archive check; each pause point holds where it says, also at an operator wait, at a loop-back, and leaving the rebase-to-merge section; a paused plan holds no slot, is offered no task, and resumes where it stopped; an ended plan is archived with each of its outcomes and reason, the cancel of an integrity pause with `aborted`; a settings change applies from the plan's next read, is refused with its reason for an invalid value, with `position_not_contained` where the new version would not contain the plan's position, and with `conflict` and the current value where another change came first, and composes an instance version of event `override`; every act on a plan of an epic, a settings change included, appends its inbox record in the act's transaction and moves the queue row; next plan with the raise of `parallelization_scope`, the archive check (an open finding holds it, kept findings and watches carried), and cancel behave as specified, also with no launchable row, and an epic ends `completed`, `cancelled`, or `released`, never `abandoned`; every hold of the closed list offers its acts and cancel as endpoints, the integrity pause's `accept` showing sizes and digests and offering no diff or restore, an act answering with the hold released or still held with its cause, and no representation carrying `facts.operator_command`; a release waits for a running short landing and is refused `release_landing_pending` when it does not finish, deletes the pending entries, a task running in one withdrawn, ends a running onboarding, ends every plan `released` and every epic `released`, archives the epics machine-side only with their ledgers untouched, changes no queue row, and makes no commit in any repository.
