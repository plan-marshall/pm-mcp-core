== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the changes Pausing and Starting a Plan, Cancelling and Abandoning Archive, The Epic Is Told, and An Epic's Actions into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once UC-CTL-1 and UC-CTL-2 are agreed (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]).
* *A plan*: running, pausing, or paused; pause with its point (now: tasks withdrawn and jobs stopped, both run again on start; after the current task, the default; after the current phase), start (a paused plan, or withdrawing the pause of a pausing one); a plan of an epic created and started in one act, never waiting to be started; cancel and abandon through `plan.aborted` with their outcome and reason, then the archive; the open pull request closed with the reason or left open.
* *Its epic told*: an inbox record per operator act on a plan of a live epic, the queue row following the plan's state, `coordinate` deciding on it.
* *A restart*: a paused plan stays paused, a pausing one holds at its point.
* *The epic as a resource* (UC-PLN-5): `GET /api/v1/epics/{epic_id}` with its rows, sources, landings, registers' counts, the draft while one is open, the archive check, its history; the operator's acts as its endpoints.
* *Holds and their acts* (UC-CTL-4): each hold a record with its kind, cause, attempts, and the acts that resolve it, each an operator endpoint of both clients; `facts.operator_command` goes.
* *Chat*: offered among the actions of a plan and of an epic in every state that has not ended, and read-only on an ended plan or epic beside its view, over its archive entry; the conversation itself, also its follow-up plan from ended work, is the sub-workflow of link:chat.adoc[`chat`].
* *An epic*: next plan (start the next launchable row, creating its plan, the reasons when none is, the offer to raise the scope); the archive check before `epic.archive`; cancel with the choice for its plans.

== Done When

The specification of the changes is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; a test per action and state shows what it does and what it refuses; each pause point holds where it says, also at an operator wait and at a loop-back; a paused plan serves nothing until started and resumes where it stopped; an ended plan is archived with its outcome and reason; every act on a plan of an epic reaches the epic's inbox; next plan, the archive check, and cancel behave as specified, also with the scope full and with no launchable row; every hold offers its acts as endpoints, and no representation carries `facts.operator_command` any more.
