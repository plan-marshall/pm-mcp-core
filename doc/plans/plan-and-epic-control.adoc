Roadmap item:: _Implement the operator's acts on a plan in either client_ (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/roadmap.adoc[Roadmap]), and the epic told of them
Where:: pm-workflow (the activity with its pause points, the end through `plan.aborted`, the composition trigger of the overrides); pm-core (the activity in the workflow state, the archive, the inbox records, the queue row statuses, the hold records); pm-runtime (the operator endpoints and their verbs)
Status, milestone and dependencies:: in the link:README.adoc#packages[index], the only place that states them

== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *A plan's activity*, pause with its points and start, also across a restart (UC-CTL-1): link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#plan-pause[Runtime Lifecycle § Pausing and Starting a Plan].
. *The ends of a plan*, cancel and abandon with their archive and report, and the outcomes of the terminal catalogue: link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#plan-end[State Catalogue § Cancelling and Abandoning Archive]; the release of a claimed lesson: link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-claim[Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing].
. *A plan's settings* (UC-CTL-5), read with their origin, written, validated and composed: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-overrides[Workflow DSL § Plan Overrides] and link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#concurrent-changes[CLI & Security § Concurrent Changes of a Setting].
. *The epic told* of every act on its plans, and the queue row following the plan: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-informed[Model Work § The Epic Is Told].
. *Holds and their acts* (UC-CTL-4), each act an operator endpoint, and no operator relay: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[State Catalogue § Holds and Their Acts]; link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#operator-command-relay[Hypermedia Format § No Operator Relay].
. *Operator acts over the local API*, never workflow states: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#operator-flows[State Catalogue § Operator Flows That Are Not Workflow Units].

Left out: the lesson claim itself (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`]); the epic cancel the integrity hold offers as _abort_ (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]); the plan overrides' axes (link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`]); the views in the clients.
