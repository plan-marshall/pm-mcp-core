== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the change Tokens, Not Money into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once it is agreed: the usage pointers of the profile schema (Client Profiles § Usage Pointers, § Profile Verification), the usage columns and the aggregates (Store Schemas § Metrics Store, Phase Workflows § Metric Aggregates), the usage parsing (Model Work § Worker Result & Usage Parsing), the removal of the spend limits (below), and the evaluation's re-measurement of a harness.
* *Record everything a harness reports, report usage only*: every figure the harness reports is recorded raw, so reports and limits can be added later on the same rows: input, output, cache read, cache creation, reasoning, and the harness-reported cost, with the harness, its version (the new row field `harness_version`), the model, the effort, the role, and the phase. For now the product only reports, and only tokens.
* *No money shown*: no price table of the product. The harness-reported cost keeps its pointer `cost_usd`, its capability `cost_field`, its column `cost_usd` in the metrics rows, and its field in the qualification and decision calibration records: it is stored as reported, or `unmeasured`, and never shown, reported, or used in a decision; the qualification and calibration records gain tokens with the split beside it, and those are what is shown. `priced_units` stays the internal figure of the migration trigger (`PM-MIG-5`) as the specification has it and is never a figure shown to the operator: `print-phase-breakdown` and every other operator view render tokens, never `priced_units`.
* *The usage pointers*: `headless.usage` gains the pointer `reasoning`, the harness's reasoning or thinking figure, filling the new column `reasoning_tokens`. A profile maps it only where the harness reports reasoning apart from its output figure; where the output figure already contains it, `reasoning` is declared not applicable, so nothing is counted twice.
* *Not reported or not applicable*: each figure of `headless.usage` is a JSON Pointer, `null` (the harness does not report it: the column is `unmeasured` with the reason `not_reported_by_harness`, as today), or `not_applicable` (the harness has no such figure by design, the cache creation of Antigravity for one: the column is recorded `not_applicable` and counts as `0`). A harness that lacks a figure by design is therefore still measured; a `not_applicable` column is never written as a measured `0`.
* *The unified figures*: one figure for every harness, unweighted. *input* is input, cache read, and cache creation; *output* is output and reasoning; `tokens` is input plus output. A row's figure is measured when each of its five components is measured or `not_applicable`, otherwise `unmeasured`; a partial sum is never recorded. The rows keep their raw components (Raw Components Only); the persisted `total_tokens` becomes this sum, and input and output are derived in views by one derivation of pm-core that every view calls.
* *Coverage*: every aggregate (per plan, phase, role, model and harness, cause class, spend class, epic) carries the total with its split and its coverage: the count of tasks measured and unmeasured with the reasons. A total over a population with unmeasured rows is a floor (`total_is_floor: true`), shown as _at least_, never as exact.
* *A running plan and an epic*: the representation of a running plan carries its tokens so far with the split and the coverage; an epic's carries the sum of its plans and of its own tasks (decompose, landing, review), with the split. Neither carries a limit, a budget line, a projection, or a comparison with one: every figure the operator sees is usage only.
* *No limits for now*: the token limits of a plan go from the specification and from the code, without a deprecation, so nothing compares a plan's tokens with a line: the anchor table of Model Work § Spend Accounting with its fallback ceilings; the planning-time projection and its fact `split_advice`; the re-planning trigger `spend_exceeded`; the pre-merge finalize spend check at `merge.gate-entry`, the remediation unit `remediation.spend`, and its spend waiver; the post-run comparison of the spend with an anchor in `record-metrics` and the quality report. The specification sites are the spend accounting, the planning lanes, the finalize step bands and merge, the questions and waivers, the dynamic re-planning, the state catalogue's remediation and re-planning units, the workflow and re-planning stores, the workflow language's remediation units, the identifiers, the requirement `PM-WF-14`, and their watch items and roadmap rows. In the code, which link:store-substrate.adoc[`store-substrate`] and link:workflow-engine.adoc[`workflow-engine`] built and which are not reopened, the policy class `spend` leaves `WorkflowStatus.POLICY_CLASSES` (and so the loop-back budgets of `LoopBackBudget`), and the package `de.planmarshall.workflow.spend` keeps the completion and the usage only, its Javadoc and its row in the Module Structure Specification saying so. The spend classes stay (they report waste), and so do the wall-clock job budgets and the token budget by which the supervisor recycles a warm worker: they bound a job or a context, not a plan's use. An operator's own limit per plan or epic is a later change on the recorded rows and not part of this package.
* *The verification records what a harness reports*: a stage `usage` of the profile verification, after `faults`, checks that each pointer resolves on the real output of the `launch` stage's worker and records, per figure, `reported`, `not_applicable`, or `missing` (the cost among them), a declared pointer that does not resolve failing the stage; the record names the harness and its version. From it the harness's usage coverage follows: `complete`, `partial` with the figures missing, or `not_reported`; link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`] runs the stage and shows the coverage in the harness list.
* *Parsing fixtures*: a recorded output of each shipped harness and version (one fresh run, and the turns of a warm worker) is a test fixture with its expected row: the usage is parsed through the profile into each column, the unified figures and the coverage are derived from it; a changed output format of a harness fails its fixture. The fixtures are written for the test from recorded runs, never copied from the content of the product.
* *The evaluation*: the usage measurement of the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#results[Evaluation Reference] (V7, Gate 13) is repeated for every new harness and harness version before its figures are trusted; until then its profile's usage stays provisional.

== Done When

The specification of the change is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; every figure a harness reports lands raw in its row, the cost included, with harness, harness version, model, effort, role, and phase, and no representation or view shows a cost or a price; a fixture profile with a `not_applicable` figure yields measured rows and one with a `null` figure unmeasured rows with their reason; a harness that reports reasoning apart and one that includes it in its output give the same unified figure for the same usage; the unified figures and the coverage of every aggregate come from the one derivation, a total over unmeasured rows marked a floor; each shipped harness and version has its recorded output parsed into its expected row; the verification records per figure `reported`, `not_applicable`, or `missing`, and fails for a pointer that does not resolve; the representations of a running plan and of an epic carry their tokens with split and coverage and no limit; no anchor table, `split_advice`, `spend_exceeded`, pre-merge spend check, `remediation.spend`, or spend waiver is left in the specification or the code, a workflow state naming the policy class `spend` is refused, and a plan over what the anchors allowed runs and lands unchanged.
