== What

* *Implements* link:../specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles § Tokens, Not Money], the usage pointers and the stage `usage` of link:../specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#profile-verification[§ Profile Verification], link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#job-result-schema[Model Work § Worker Result & Usage Parsing], link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#spend-accounting-budget-anchors[§ Usage Reporting & Spend Classes], and the usage columns and aggregates of link:../specification/store-schemas/05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Store Schemas § Metrics Store] (`PM-WF-14`, `PM-WORK-3`, `PM-CLIENT-5`).
* *Record everything a harness reports, report usage only*: every figure the harness reports is recorded raw, so reports and limits can be added later on the same rows: input, output, cache read, cache creation, reasoning, and the harness-reported cost, with the harness, its version (the new row field `harness_version`), the model, the effort, the role, and the phase. For now the product only reports, and only tokens.
* *No money shown*: no price table of the product. The harness-reported cost keeps its pointer `cost_usd`, its capability `cost_field`, its column `cost_usd` in the metrics rows, and its field in the qualification and decision calibration records: it is stored as reported, or `unmeasured`, and never shown, reported, or used in a decision; the qualification and calibration records gain tokens with the split beside it, and those are what is shown. `priced_units` stays the internal figure of the migration trigger (`PM-MIG-5`) as the specification has it and is never a figure shown to the operator: `print-phase-breakdown` and every other operator view render tokens, never `priced_units`.
* *The usage pointers*: `headless.usage` gains the pointer `reasoning`, the harness's reasoning or thinking figure, filling the new column `reasoning_tokens`. A profile maps it only where the harness reports reasoning apart from its output figure; where the output figure already contains it, `reasoning` is declared not applicable, so nothing is counted twice.
* *Not reported or not applicable*: each figure of `headless.usage` is a JSON Pointer, `null` (the harness does not report it: the column is `unmeasured` with the reason `not_reported_by_harness`, as today), or `not_applicable` (the harness has no such figure by design, the cache creation of Antigravity for one: the column is recorded `not_applicable` and counts as `0`). A harness that lacks a figure by design is therefore still measured; a `not_applicable` column is never written as a measured `0`.
* *The unified figures*: one figure for every harness, unweighted. *input* is input, cache read, and cache creation; *output* is output and reasoning; `tokens` is input plus output. A row's figure is measured when each of its five components is measured or `not_applicable`, otherwise `unmeasured`; a partial sum is never recorded. `input_tokens` and `output_tokens` can never be `not_applicable`: profile validation refuses a profile that declares either of them so, so a measured row always rests on a reported input and output. The rows keep their raw components (Raw Components Only); the persisted `total_tokens` becomes this sum, and input and output are derived in views by one derivation of pm-core that every view calls.
* *Coverage*: every aggregate (per plan, phase, role, model and harness, cause class, spend class, epic) carries the total with its split and its coverage: the count of tasks measured and unmeasured with the reasons. A total over a population with unmeasured rows is a floor (`total_is_floor: true`), shown as _at least_, never as exact.
* *A running plan and an epic*: the representation of a running plan carries its tokens so far with the split and the coverage; an epic's carries the sum of its plans and of its own tasks (decompose, landing, review), with the split. Neither carries a limit, a budget line, a projection, or a comparison with one: every figure the operator sees is usage only.
* *No limits*: nothing compares a plan's tokens with a line; the specification has no anchor table with fallback ceilings, no planning-time projection or `split_advice`, no re-planning trigger `spend_exceeded`, no pre-merge spend check, no remediation unit `remediation.spend` and no spend waiver, and no post-run comparison with an anchor. The removal of the code built for them (the policy class `spend`, `RemediationInvariants.SPEND_UNIT`, the spend limits of the package `de.planmarshall.workflow.spend`) is link:spend-removal.adoc[`spend-removal`]'s, for roadmap Milestone 3; this package adds no limit back. The spend classes stay (they report waste), and so do the wall-clock job budgets and the token budget by which the supervisor recycles a warm worker: they bound a job or a context, not a plan's use. An operator's own limit per plan or epic is a later change on the recorded rows and not part of this package.
* *The verification records what a harness reports*: a stage `usage` of the profile verification, after `faults`, checks that each pointer resolves on the real output of the `launch` stage's worker and records, per figure, `reported`, `not_applicable`, or `missing` (the cost among them), a declared pointer that does not resolve failing the stage; the record names the harness and its version. From it the harness's usage coverage follows: `complete`, `partial` with the figures missing, or `not_reported`; link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`] runs the stage and shows the coverage in the harness list.
* *Parsing fixtures*: a recorded output of each shipped harness and version (one fresh run, and the turns of a warm worker) is a test fixture with its expected row: the usage is parsed through the profile into each column, the unified figures and the coverage are derived from it; a changed output format of a harness fails its fixture. The fixtures are written for the test from recorded runs, never copied from the content of the product.
* *The evaluation*: the usage measurement of the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#results[Evaluation Reference] (V7, Gate 13) is repeated for every new harness and harness version before its figures are trusted; until then its profile's usage stays provisional.

== Done When

Every figure a harness reports lands raw in its row, the cost included, with harness, harness version, model, effort, role, and phase, and no representation or view shows a cost or a price; a fixture profile with a `not_applicable` figure yields measured rows and one with a `null` figure unmeasured rows with their reason; a harness that reports reasoning apart and one that includes it in its output give the same unified figure for the same usage; the unified figures and the coverage of every aggregate come from the one derivation, a total over unmeasured rows marked a floor; each shipped harness and version has its recorded output parsed into its expected row; the verification records per figure `reported`, `not_applicable`, or `missing`, and fails for a pointer that does not resolve; the representations of a running plan and of an epic carry their tokens with split and coverage and no limit, budget line, projection, or comparison with one.
