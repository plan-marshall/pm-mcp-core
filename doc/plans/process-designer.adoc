== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The loader of project-owned units (`project-units-and-invariants`) and the frontmatter parser and interface rules (link:../../pm-workflow/doc/developer/workflow-verify.adoc#interface-rules[Workflow Verification Developer Guide § Interface Rules and Unit Types]) are built and extended here.

. *The designable set and the levels*, with the unit resolver in the loader and the composition and the level recorded in the instance header: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#project-owned-units[Workflow DSL § Designable Workflow Units, Levels & Compiled Invariants], items 1, 2, 4 and 5.
. *No first copy*: the setup and the loader without copied default units, adoption through `config_drift`: § Designable Workflow Units; link:../specification/domain-extensions/06-configuration.adoc#config-snapshot[Domain Extensions § Configuration Snapshot, Composition Rules & Adoption].
. *The installed units, the catalogue in two scopes, validation with its dry-run*: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#process-designer[Workflow DSL § Designing a Process].
. *Placing and removing* at machine and project level, with path confinement and the refusals: § Designing a Process.
. *Project level as a short landing* `git.land-unit`: link:../specification/state-catalogue/18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-short-landings[State Catalogue § Short Landings].
. *The recipes' places* at machine and project level: link:../specification/domain-extensions/05-recipes-and-extension-points.adoc#recipe-workflow[Domain Extensions § A Recipe Is a Workflow].
. *The designer endpoints on the web listeners*: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Runtime Model § Local API]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/03-web-server.adoc#web-proxy[CLI & Security § API Access & Device Secret].
