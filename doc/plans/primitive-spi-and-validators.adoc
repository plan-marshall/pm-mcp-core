== What

* The typed `Primitive<P, R>` interface: canonical name, parameter record, closed outcome enumeration, declared store and job side effects, awaited events.
* The in-process content validators a primitive runs over what a model or an operator submits.
* The declarations `OutcomeClass` and `AwaitedEvent` exist; this package gives them their contract and the registry of primitives that the assembly fills.

A test primitive of each outcome class runs through the SPI; a primitive with a duplicate name, an open outcome set, or an undeclared side effect is refused at registration.
