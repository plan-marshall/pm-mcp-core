== What

* One supervisor per project and role: starting fresh and warm workers within the role's count and the model slots, acknowledgement, silence and exit detection, idle and token-budget recycling, ending and fencing, the start-failure guard.
* *Changed configuration*: a warm worker is recycled after its task when the role's configuration digest over the effective values differs from its generation's (an edited artifact, a changed role assignment, or a plan override); the start-failure guard clears on such a change.
* *Pause at once*: a worker whose plan the operator pauses at once is ended by force at once, its task queued again without counting a re-offer.
* *Idle*: a `wait_again` answer to a generation holding a lease (consulting, or holding for an answer) is no idle wake-up.
* The missing answer acknowledgement as a loss of a continuing worker, and the token budget reached during a hold (the hold ends, the task suspends or falls back, the worker is recycled), are link:question-continuation.adoc[`question-continuation`]'s; the off-track signals and their actions on live workers are link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]'s. Reports of the supervisor go to both clients and the audit log.

== Done When

With a fixture harness and a virtual clock: each loss is detected at its deadline and never before; a start failure repeated to the guard's count ends the role's starts, and a changed role configuration clears the guard; a pause at once ends the plan's worker by force and re-queues its task without counting a re-offer; a changed role assignment recycles a warm worker after its task; a consulting worker's `wait_again` answers do not recycle it as idle.
