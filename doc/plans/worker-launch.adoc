== What

* The job kind `llm`: the launch composed from role artifact and client profile, the job-private directory, the MCP configuration delivered as the profile says.
* The job token: minted at spawn, only its hash in the job record, revoked with the job's fate; the registry over the job records.
* The harness enrolment store and the confinement exceptions of `llm` jobs.
