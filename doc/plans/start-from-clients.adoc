== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. Where the done packages `entity-addressing`, `plan-instance` or `project-units-and-invariants` built a session-driven bootstrap, these tasks restructure it.

. *The operator's clients only*: the session-driven units, the entry session and the answer channel `session` removed from the code (`OutcomeClass`, the static verifier's rules and fixtures): link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#clients-only[Workflow DSL § All Interaction in the Operator's Clients].
. *The bootstraps* of a plan and an epic as units of operator waits: link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#start-from-clients[State Catalogue § Starting from the Clients]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-catalogue-bootstrap[`bootstrap-plan`]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-catalogue-bootstrap-epic[`bootstrap-epic`].
. *The setup gate* at `start.open`, `start-epic.open` and the re-check of `start.create-scope`: § Starting from the Clients.
. *Sources as a list* and the source units `source-documents` and `source-issue`: link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-catalogue-source[§ Source Units]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#issues[§ Issues by Number or URL].
. *The other sources*: pull request, lesson with its premise check, and local changes: § Source Units; link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-record[Store Schemas § Lesson Record].
. *The start's end*: the local changes under `landing.files` and the re-check at `start.create`: § Starting from the Clients.
. *Room for the choice Chat* at `start.sources` and `start-epic.sources`, the entry's `chat/` kept: § Starting from the Clients.
. *Asked when needed*: posture and base branch in `init.options`, and `epic.parallelization_scope` as the project's operator value: link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#options-when-needed[§ Asked When Needed]; link:../specification/store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#local-config[Store Schemas § Operator Local Configuration].

Left out: the rate limits, documents copy or move, the source directory, the screening, failing closed and the claimed lesson (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`]); the landing variants (link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`]); the chat (link:chat.adoc[`chat`]); _Next plan_ (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]).
