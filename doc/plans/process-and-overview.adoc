== What

* *Implements* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-resource[Runtime Model § The Plan's Process as a Structure], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-events[§ Events of the Process], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#needs-operator[§ What Needs the Operator, at a Glance], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[§ The Overview Resource and Its Events], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[§ Events per Scope], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[§ Ended Work], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[State Catalogue § Running Work], and link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#titles[Store Schemas § Titles and Names] (`PM-ARCH-11`).
* *The process*: `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/process` answers the composed process of the instance in force as a tree (the plan, its phases, every workflow's states in their order, every call expanded to the leaves), never DSL text; each node with its `node_id`, its kind, and its state derived from the plan's history of link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`] (`done`, `running`, `planned`, `waiting_for_operator`, `failed`, `not_taken`, `not_in_plan` with its prune reason), how often it ran, its entry and exit times, a leaf's job or tasks with a link to their activity (link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]); the path to the current position; the context (project, epic, activity, `needs_operator`, tokens so far with split and coverage); the `instance_version`.
* *The events of the process*: on the plan's stream `state.entered`, `state.left`, `subworkflow.called`, `subworkflow.returned`, `step.opened`, `step.closed`, `instance.changed`, `activity.changed`, and `tokens.changed` (at most once per metrics event interval), each with its `node_id`.
* *What needs the operator*: a `needs_operator` summary per plan, epic, and project, derived on read and never stored, with its count and per item its kind and link, in the closed order `decision`, `waiver_request`, `operator_wait`, the holds (`integrity_pause`, `stalled`, `condition`, `held`, `landing_failed`), `findings`.
* *Running work and the overview*: active plans and epics and a project's running work as the state catalogue defines them; `GET /api/v1/projects` (the projects with running work, each with its `display_name`, `needs_operator`, and counts) and `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}` (its active plans with title, phase, activity, `created_at`, `phase_entered_at`, task progress, `needs_operator`, `off_track`, and epic; its active epics; its open entries; its running short landings); titles and names as the stores keep them.
* *Events per scope*: the streams `GET /api/v1/events`, `/plans/{plan_id}/events`, `/epics/{epic_id}/events`, `/projects/{project_id}/workspace/events`, `/machine/events`, and `/entries/{entry_id}/events`, one envelope (`event`, `id`, `data` with `at`, `scope`, and the payload of the closed event catalogue, never a model-facing representation or a secret), the resume by `Last-Event-ID` within the event buffer and otherwise one `reset`, no subscription kept; the runtime-wide stream announcing every scope's start and end, every change of running work, and every change of a `needs_operator` summary.
* *Ended work*: `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/ended`, newest first, paged by a cursor, each plan with its outcome (`merged`, `no_change`, `handed_back`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released`, `aborted`), reason, and end time, each epic with its `terminal_reason`; each resolving read-only from its archive to its process and history as it ended, its completion report, decisions, re-planning records, and events; a deleted one left out and resolving to its tombstone.
* Where the stream and the resources of the runtime exist only in a first form (the runtime-wide stream of Milestone 2), this package brings them to the closed catalogue and envelope; the transport of the streams on the web listener is the product's.

== Done When

The process of a fixture plan answers every node with its derived state, also `failed`, `not_taken`, and `not_in_plan` with its reason, the path to its position, and its context, after a restart as before; each change of a node is announced on the plan's stream with its `node_id`; the `needs_operator` summary lists each kind in its order and is never stored; the overview lists exactly the projects with running work and their active plans, epics, entries, and short landings; every stream carries the one envelope, resumes from `Last-Event-ID` within the buffer and sends `reset` otherwise; ended work lists each outcome newest first by cursor, resolves an ended plan read-only from its archive, and a deleted one to its tombstone.
