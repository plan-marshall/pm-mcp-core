== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The process* of a plan as a tree with its node states, path and context (UC-PLN-1): link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-resource[Runtime Model § The Plan's Process as a Structure].
. *The events of the process* on the plan's stream: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-events[§ Events of the Process].
. *What needs the operator*, derived per plan, epic and project, and its change event: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#needs-operator[§ What Needs the Operator, at a Glance].
. *Running work and the overview* (UC-PLN-4): link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[§ The Overview Resource and Its Events]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[State Catalogue § Running Work]; link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#titles[Store Schemas § Titles and Names]; link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#phase-start[§ The Start of the Current Phase].
. *Events per scope*, brought from the first form of the runtime-wide stream to the closed catalogue and envelope: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[§ Events per Scope]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc[Runtime Model § Event Catalogue].
. *Ended work* (UC-CTL-1), read-only from the archive, a deleted one as its tombstone: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[§ Ended Work]; link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout § Archives].
. *The tokens of a running plan and of an epic*, from `PlanTokens` and `EpicTokens`: link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#spend-accounting-budget-anchors[Model Work § Usage Reporting & Spend Classes]; link:../specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles § Tokens, Not Money].
. *Schema parity* of the status store, `WorkflowStatusSchemaParityTest.membersEqualSchema` enabled again: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Store Schemas § Workflow State Store].

Left out: the plan's history (link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the activity and the holds (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]); the activity of a task (link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]); the envelope and the event catalogue as the clients' contract (`pm-api` in `pm-mcp-clients`); the transport of the streams on the web listener.
