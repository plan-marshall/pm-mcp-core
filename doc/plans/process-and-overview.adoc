== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The process* of a plan as a tree with its node states, path and context (UC-PLN-1): link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-resource[Runtime Model § The Plan's Process as a Structure].
. *The events of the process* on the plan's stream: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-events[§ Events of the Process].
. *What needs the operator*, derived per plan, epic and project, and its change event, the holds among its kinds with `overdue` beside `stalled`: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#needs-operator[§ What Needs the Operator, at a Glance].
. *Running work and the overview* (UC-PLN-4), the plan resource carrying its counters, each with its cap (the retry counters, the counters of the remediation policies, the watch restarts, the re-offers of a running task, the outline loop-backs and the re-planning budget), and the elapsed time of a running job against the job's budget (`budget_seconds`) and of an open CI wait against the wait's expected duration: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[§ Local API], `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}`; link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#ci-wait[Timeouts § CI Wait]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[§ The Overview Resource and Its Events]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[State Catalogue § Running Work]; link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#titles[Store Schemas § Titles and Names]; link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#phase-start[§ The Start of the Current Phase]. Open: which store records a CI wait's expected duration and last observed change, and the `Retry-After` deferral, since no store member defines them.
. *Events per scope*, brought from the first form of the runtime-wide stream to the closed catalogue and envelope: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[§ Events per Scope]; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc[Runtime Model § Event Catalogue].
. *Ended work* (UC-CTL-1), read-only from the archive, a deleted one as its tombstone: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[§ Ended Work]; link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout § Archives].
. *The tokens of a running plan and of an epic*, from `PlanTokens` and `EpicTokens`: link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#spend-accounting-budget-anchors[Model Work § Usage Reporting & Spend Classes]; link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Harness Profiles § Tokens, Not Money].
. *Schema parity* of the status store, `WorkflowStatusSchemaParityTest.membersEqualSchema` enabled again: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Store Schemas § Workflow State Store].
. *The plan's timeline* (`GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/timeline`), one list in the order it happened, a page at a time, merging the entries of the plan's history, the firings of its steps, its job records with outcome, elapsed time and budget, the attempts of its queue tasks, its wait slices with their reason, and its conditions and holds; each entry with its instant, its kind, its `node_id` where it has one and its correlation ids; derived on read from the plan's stores, with no store of its own and never reading the audit log: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#timeline-resource[§ The Plan's Timeline]. The engine supplies the data from its stores; the assembly builds the resource.

Left out: the plan's history (link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the activity and the holds (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]); the activity of a task (link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]); the envelope and the event catalogue as the clients' contract (`pm-api` in `pm-mcp-clients`); the transport of the streams on the web listener.
