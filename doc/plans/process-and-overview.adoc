== What

* *Implements* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-resource[Runtime Model § The Plan's Process as a Structure], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-events[§ Events of the Process], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#needs-operator[§ What Needs the Operator, at a Glance], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[§ The Overview Resource and Its Events], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[§ Events per Scope], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[§ Ended Work], link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[State Catalogue § Running Work], and link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#titles[Store Schemas § Titles and Names] (`PM-ARCH-11`).
* *The process*: `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/process` answers the composed process of the instance in force as a tree (the plan, its phases, every workflow's states in their order, every call expanded to the leaves), never DSL text; each node with its `node_id`, its kind, and its state derived from the plan's history of link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`] (`done`, `running`, `planned`, `waiting_for_operator`, `failed`, `not_taken`, `not_in_plan` with its prune reason), how often it ran, its entry and exit times, a leaf's job or tasks with a link to their activity (link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]); the path to the current position; the context (project, epic, activity, `needs_operator`, tokens so far with split and coverage); the `instance_version`.
* *The events of the process*: on the plan's stream `state.entered`, `state.left`, `subworkflow.called`, `subworkflow.returned`, `step.opened`, `step.closed`, `instance.changed`, `activity.changed`, and `tokens.changed` (at most once per metrics event interval), each with its `node_id`.
* *What needs the operator*: a `needs_operator` summary per plan, epic, and project, derived on read and never stored, with its count and per item its kind and link, in the closed order `decision`, `waiver_request`, `operator_wait`, the holds (`integrity_pause`, `stalled`, `condition`, `held`, `landing_failed`), `findings`, `setup` (a project's setup repair or a step its onboarding owes); an epic's summary including its plans', a project's its plans', epics', and workspace scope's; a change of a summary announced on the runtime-wide stream as `needs_operator.changed`.
* *Running work and the overview*: active plans and epics and a project's running work as the state catalogue defines them; `GET /api/v1/projects` (the projects with running work, each with its `display_name`, `needs_operator`, and counts) and `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}` (its active plans with title, phase, activity, `created_at`, `phase_entered_at`, task progress, `needs_operator`, `off_track`, and epic; its active epics; its open entries; its running short landings); titles and names as the stores keep them.
* *Events per scope*: the streams `GET /api/v1/events`, `/plans/{plan_id}/events`, `/epics/{epic_id}/events`, `/projects/{project_id}/workspace/events`, `/machine/events`, and `/entries/{entry_id}/events`, one envelope (`event`, `id`, `data` with `at`, `scope`, and the payload of the closed event catalogue, never a model-facing representation or a secret), the resume by `Last-Event-ID` within the event buffer and otherwise one `reset`, no subscription kept; the runtime-wide stream announcing every scope's start and end, every change of running work, and every change of a `needs_operator` summary; an entry's stream announcing the scope it starts and ending, a project onboarding's entry announcing at `project.enrol` the workspace stream to follow; a stream on a web listener checked against its device secret on connect and ended by a revoked device, a closed listener, or the runtime's stop with `stream.closed` and its reason.
* *Ended work*: `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/ended`, newest first, paged by a cursor, each plan with its outcome (`merged`, `no_change`, `handed_back`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released`, `aborted`), reason, and end time, each epic with its `terminal_reason`; each resolving read-only from its archive to its process and history as it ended, its completion report, decisions, re-planning records, and events; a deleted one left out and resolving to its tombstone.
* Where the stream and the resources of the runtime exist only in a first form (the runtime-wide stream of Milestone 2), this package brings them to the closed catalogue and envelope; the envelope and the event catalogue as the clients' contract are `pm-api`'s in `pm-mcp-clients` (roadmap Milestone 2), this package their runtime side; the transport of the streams on the web listener is the product's.
* *The tokens of a running plan and of an epic*: the plan's process and the epic's representation carry their tokens so far, with the split and the coverage: the plan's from `PlanTokens`, the epic's from `EpicTokens` (link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#spend-accounting-budget-anchors[Model Work § Usage Reporting & Spend Classes]; link:../specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles § Tokens, Not Money]); a change is announced on the plan's stream as `tokens.changed`, at most once per metrics event interval. `PlanTokens` and `EpicTokens` exist in `pm-workflow` built with no caller; this package consumes them, and neither representation carries a limit, a budget line, a projection, or a comparison with one.
* *Schema parity*: the members of the status store the specification added (`title`, `created_at`, `phase_entered_at`, `pause`, `terminal_reason`, `setup_aspect`, `onboard_run`, `onboard_steps`) are in its codec, the ones of the packages before this one included, and `WorkflowStatusSchemaParityTest.membersEqualSchema` is re-enabled.

== Done When

The process of a fixture plan answers every node with its derived state, also `failed`, `not_taken`, and `not_in_plan` with its reason, the path to its position, and its context, after a restart as before; each change of a node is announced on the plan's stream with its `node_id`; the `needs_operator` summary lists each kind in its order, `setup` included, is never stored, and each change of it is announced with `needs_operator.changed`; the overview lists exactly the projects with running work and their active plans, epics, entries, and short landings; every stream carries the one envelope, resumes from `Last-Event-ID` within the buffer and sends `reset` otherwise, and a web stream ends with `stream.closed` and its reason when its device is revoked, its listener closed, or the runtime stopped; ended work lists each outcome newest first by cursor, resolves an ended plan read-only from its archive, and a deleted one to its tombstone; `WorkflowStatusSchemaParityTest.membersEqualSchema` runs without `@Disabled` and passes; the process of a running plan and the epic's representation carry their tokens with the split and the coverage, a change is announced as `tokens.changed`, and neither carries a limit, a budget line, a projection, or a comparison with one.
