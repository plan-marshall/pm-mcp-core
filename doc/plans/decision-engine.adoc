== What

* *Implements* the engine side of UC-PLN-2 (`PM-WF-12`, `PM-WF-18`, `PM-WF-20`): link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-structure[Model Work § The Decision Structure], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-delivery[§ Delivery to the Operator], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#own-words[§ Answers in the Operator's Own Words], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#answers-and-amendments[§ Answers & Amendments]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#cli-answers[Store Schemas § Answers from the Terminal Client], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[§ Question Record]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/16-decision.adoc[State Catalogue § Unit `decision`]. The client side is `decision-contract` of `pm-mcp-clients`.
* The decision as a structure with its id (`<scope_id>/<question_id>`) and revisions; what it blocks and how reversible it is; the support per option derived by the runtime from the quorum's votes.
* The interpreting role `interpret` (`read_only`, its corpus topic `own_words`), the read-back and the clarification.
* *None fits and later*: _none fits_ (with a note; offered only where the decision names a role that can ask anew or a quorum is declared that can vote again) goes to `decision.withdraw`: a worker's question closes with the answer `none_fits`, which the continuing task carries in `facts.answers` with its `ask` link offered again within the question bound, and a quorum's decision is voted again on a new revision; _later_ answers nothing, the decision stays open and announced (`decision.announce`), the client leaves it, and its subject (the plan, the epic, or the setup) waits.
* *A waiver request* is announced like a decision and answered by `waive` or `decline` (the outcome `declined`, the remediation unit going on; the wait is link:operator-wait-and-machine-scope.adoc[`operator-wait-and-machine-scope`]'s), both operator overrides: no preselection, no interpretation, no chat.
* *A project's setup asks*: a decision of a project's workspace scope (a question of its setup) is delivered, announced with its project, and answered like a plan's, through the same states and record; it holds the setup only, never a plan; Chat over it is offered as for a plan's, disabled with its reason where the setup's workflow raised the question and no planning role exists (the routing is link:chat.adoc[`chat`]'s).
* The open decisions across scopes, the workspace scope of a project included, with since when each waits; the channel `cli`, the second channel of content answers beside `web` (and `quorum` for a decision a quorum took); *the answer endpoint* (pm-runtime), one scope-qualified endpoint for both clients, whose validation and recording of the answer are one transaction of the decision's scope, so the first valid answer of either channel wins: an answer to an older revision refused with `conflict` and the current revision, a second answer or an answer to a closed record with `already_answered`, an answer the part does not allow (an unknown option, a value failing the part's `value_schema`, own words, a note, a delegation, or _none fits_ where not allowed) with `invalid_answer`, and an answer to a part whose dependency is unanswered with `dependency_unanswered`.
* *Chat before answering*: `decision.answer` and the decision record leave room for the choice Chat that link:chat.adoc[`chat`] adds on top of this package (the record saying that an answer followed a chat, the transcript kept with the decision, a decision closed as withdrawn with its reason); the chat itself, its routing, and its rules are that package's.

== Done When

Every scenario of `decision-contract` replays against the runtime; an answer in own words reaches the decision only after its read-back; _none fits_ closes a worker's question with `none_fits` and offers its `ask` link again, and has a quorum vote again on a new revision, _later_ leaves the decision open and changes nothing; the first valid answer of either channel wins, also when two answers arrive at once, and a test per refusal shows `conflict` with the current revision, `already_answered`, `invalid_answer`, and `dependency_unanswered`, none of them changing the record; a decision of a project's setup is announced, answered, and recorded like a plan's, holds no plan, and offers its chat disabled where the setup's workflow raised it.
