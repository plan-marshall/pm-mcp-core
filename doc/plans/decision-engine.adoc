== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The decision structure* with its id, revisions, parts and support per option, _none fits_ and _later_: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-structure[Model Work § The Decision Structure].
. *The delivery to the operator* across scopes (a plan, a project's setup, a start's entry), the waiver request answered by `waive` or `decline`, and the channels: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-delivery[Model Work § Delivery to the Operator]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Workflow DSL § Waiting for the Operator].
. *The unit `decision`* with its read-back, confirmation and withdrawal states: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-unit[Model Work § The Decision Unit]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/16-decision.adoc#state-catalogue-decision[State Catalogue § Unit `decision`].
. *Own words* and the interpreting role `interpret`: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#own-words[Model Work § Answers in the Operator's Own Words].
. *Answers and amendments*: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#answers-and-amendments[Model Work § Answers & Amendments].
. *The question record and the answer endpoint* with its refusals: link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Store Schemas § Question Record], link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#cli-answers[§ Answers from the Terminal Client].
. *Room for Chat before answering* in `decision.answer` and the decision-log record: § Delivery to the Operator, _Chat before answering_.

Left out: the chat, its routing and its rules (link:chat.adoc[`chat`]); the entry's decision logs and their move (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`]); the quorum (link:decision-quorum-and-calibration.adoc[`decision-quorum-and-calibration`]); the client side (`pm-mcp-clients` / `decision-contract`).
