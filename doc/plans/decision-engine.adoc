== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the engine side of The Decision Structure, Own Words, and Answers from the Terminal Client into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once UC-PLN-2 is agreed; `decision-contract` of `pm-mcp-clients` writes the client side beside it.
* The decision as a structure with its id (`<scope_id>/<question_id>`) and revisions; what it blocks and how reversible it is; the support per option derived by the runtime from the quorum's votes.
* The interpreting role `interpret` (`read_only`, its corpus topic `own_words`), the read-back and the clarification.
* *None fits and later*: _none fits_ goes to `decision.withdraw`, which closes the decision withdrawn with the operator's note, and the asking role thinks again and asks anew through `decision.open`, a new decision; _later_ answers nothing, the decision stays open and announced (`decision.announce`), the client leaves it, and its subject (the plan, the epic, or the setup) waits.
* *A waiver request* is announced like a decision and answered by `waive` or `decline` (the outcome `declined`, the remediation unit going on; the wait is link:operator-wait-and-machine-scope.adoc[`operator-wait-and-machine-scope`]'s), both operator overrides: no preselection, no interpretation, no chat.
* *A project's setup asks*: a decision of a project's workspace scope (a question of its setup) is delivered, announced with its project, and answered like a plan's, through the same states and record; it holds the setup only, never a plan; Chat over it is offered as for a plan's, disabled with its reason where the setup's workflow raised the question and no planning role exists (the routing is link:chat.adoc[`chat`]'s).
* The open decisions across scopes, the workspace scope of a project included, with since when each waits; the channel `cli`, the second channel of content answers beside `web`; the refusal of an answer to an older revision.
* *Chat before answering*: `decision.answer` and the decision record leave room for the choice Chat that link:chat.adoc[`chat`] adds on top of this package (the record saying that an answer followed a chat, the transcript kept with the decision, a decision closed as withdrawn with its reason); the chat itself, its routing, and its rules are that package's.

== Done When

The specification is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; every scenario of `decision-contract` replays against the runtime; an answer in own words reaches the decision only after its read-back; _none fits_ closes the decision withdrawn and a new one is asked, _later_ leaves it open; the first valid answer of either channel wins; a decision of a project's setup is announced, answered, and recorded like a plan's, holds no plan, and offers its chat disabled where the setup's workflow raised it.
