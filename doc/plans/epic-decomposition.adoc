== What

* *Implements* link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#decomposition-review[Model Work § The Decomposition Is Reviewed], link:../specification/state-catalogue/17-epic-decomposition-and-review.adoc#state-catalogue-decomposition[State Catalogue § Decomposition: `decomposition`], and link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-decomposition-draft[Store Schemas § Decomposition Draft] (`PM-WF-15`, `PM-WF-20`).
* The draft of the role `decompose` (`decomposition.draft`) held apart from the ledger, kept across leaving the review and a restart and served by the epic's resource while open; a task that falls back holds the decomposition (`decomposition.held`); the server's checks over it (disjoint surfaces of plans that may run together, no dependency cycle, a workstream per plan); a failing check shown on the draft with its row and cause, never discarding it; the operator wait `decomposition.review` with keep, edit, drop, add, chat (`decomposition.chat`, the sub-workflow link:chat.adoc[`chat`] with the draft as its scope), and draft again with an instruction; _agree_ offered only while every check passes; `decomposition.commit` writing rows, sections, and workstreams (with the epic's sources on the first run) in one ledger write under the epic lock, committed through `git.commit_and_push` and landed by `epic.landing`.
* *Edit and add*: `decomposition.instruct` (the operator's words for the plan), `decomposition.rewrite` (the role drafts or rewrites the plan), `decomposition.read-back` (old against new: accept to `decomposition.check`, instruct again to `decomposition.instruct`, discard back to `decomposition.review`); every model change through that read-back or the chat's.
* *How many plans run at once* is no part of the decomposition: it is the project's `epic.parallelization_scope` (machine default 1), never asked at the start; the operator raises it through _Next plan_ (`epic.raise-scope`, link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]) or the project's settings.
* A later run (decompose again, stale rows) over staged rows only, each change against the row it replaces; no plan of a new epic starts before its first decomposition is committed. A recut (`epic.recut`, for a `stale` row or a landing analysis) that only re-orders staged rows or amends a staged row's declared surface or prerequisites is applied without the review once the checks pass (outcome `applied`, committed by `epic.commit-ledger`, reported `epic.recut_applied`); one that adds, drops, or re-scopes a plan goes to `epic.decompose` and waits in `decomposition.review` (outcome `review`), whatever the epic's automatic mode (link:epic-auto.adoc[`epic-auto`]).
* Where `epic.decompose` and the commit of a decomposition have already been implemented without the review, this package restructures them.
* *Implements* link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#findings-watches[Model Work § An Epic's Findings and Watches], link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-review[§ The Review], link:../specification/state-catalogue/17-epic-decomposition-and-review.adoc#state-catalogue-review[State Catalogue § Review: `review`], and the registers of link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-registers[Store Schemas § Findings and Watches Registers] (`PM-WF-15`).
* The registers of findings (a problem no plan owns yet, to be fixed; never called a defect) and watches (an observation to check again, with its trigger) in the epic's ledger; each item with its epic-scoped id, statement, rules, origin, and state `open` (not decided yet), `kept`, `dropped` (with the reason), or `promoted` with its row; the epic's tasks adding items with the repeatable submit `submit register_item`; a watch's trigger (`landing` or `base_advanced`) re-checked, the watch becoming a finding or closed; new findings as a `needs_operator` kind. Only an `open` finding holds the epic's archive; kept findings and watches are carried into it (the check is link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]'s).
* The sub-workflow `review`, called by `epic.review`: `review.open` with the planning role's proposal per item; at `review.item` keep (`review.keep`: kept as a finding, or moved to the watches where the kind changes), drop (`review.drop`, with the reason), promote, edit, chat (the sub-workflow link:chat.adoc[`chat`] with one item or the whole register as its scope); the remaining proposals accepted at once after one confirmation; `review.commit` writes the dispositions and lands them in one ledger write.
* *Promote*: `review.draft-plan` by the planning role, then `review.confirm-plan` with stage (a staged row under the surface checks), back, and edit: `review.instruct-plan` (the operator's words for the drafted plan), `review.redraft-plan` (the role drafts the plan again), `review.read-back-plan` (old against new: accept back to `review.confirm-plan` with the new draft, instruct again, or discard back to `review.confirm-plan` with the draft before).
* *Edit*: `review.instruct`, the role's rewrite `review.rewrite`, `review.read-back` (accept: applied, back to `review.item`; instruct again; discard: back to `review.item`, the item unchanged).
* *The operator's words* in edit and chat are not interpreted: the role works from them, and only what it proposes (a rewrite, a redrafted plan, a change in a chat) passes a read-back; the role reads the items as data, a finding from outside the machine still marked untrusted.

== Done When

A decomposition reaches the ledger only through the operator's agreement with every check passing, the first one and every later run that adds, drops, or re-scopes a plan; only a recut after a landing that re-orders staged rows or amends a staged row's surface or prerequisites is applied without it, once the checks pass (link:epic-auto.adoc[`epic-auto`]); each operator act of the review and each branch of `decomposition.read-back` is tested; no state of the start or the decomposition asks `parallelization_scope`; a later run changes only staged rows and reports each change. A landing and an inbox finding each add an item; a review takes every disposition, also keeping a finding as a watch and the reverse, and lands them in one ledger write; no model's change reaches a register or a drafted plan without the operator's read-back, and each branch of `review.read-back` and `review.read-back-plan` is tested; a promoted item is a staged row that passed the surface check.
