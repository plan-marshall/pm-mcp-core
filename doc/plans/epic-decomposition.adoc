== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The draft* of the role `decompose`, held apart from the ledger, kept across a restart, served while open: link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-decomposition-draft[Store Schemas § Decomposition Draft].
. *The sub-workflow `decomposition`*: the server's checks, the review with its acts, edit and add through the read-back, the chat on the draft, _agree_ and the commit in one ledger write: link:../specification/state-catalogue/17-epic-decomposition-and-review.adoc#state-catalogue-decomposition[State Catalogue § Decomposition: `decomposition`]; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#decomposition-review[Model Work § The Decomposition Is Reviewed]; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-surface-disjointness[§ Staging-Time Surface Disjointness].
. *A later run and a recut*, over staged rows only, the recut applied without the review or routed to it: § The Decomposition Is Reviewed; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-workflow[§ Epic Workflow].
. *`epic.decompose` and its commit* restructured around the review where they exist without it: § The Decomposition Is Reviewed.
. *The epic intent*, submitted, persisted with the draft and the decomposition, seeding each plan's required claims, read by `epic.through`: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-intent[Model Work § Epic Intent]; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Store Schemas § Epic Header]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#intent-record[Phase Workflows § The Intent Record].

Left out: the chat sub-workflow (link:chat.adoc[`chat`]); the raise of `parallelization_scope` (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]); the automatic mode (link:epic-auto.adoc[`epic-auto`]).
