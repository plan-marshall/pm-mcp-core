== What

* The runner behind the `JobRunner` interface, through `pm-exec`: in-memory `stdin`, termination escalation, the write-ahead job record and log, the orphan rules of recovery, the fixed write order at job end.
* `pm-exec` already makes the job the leader of its process group and reports its PID and start instant.

== Done When

A job that ignores `SIGTERM` is killed after the grace period with its whole group; a runtime killed at each point of the job's life recovers to the specified fate; the outcome table of the specification is covered row by row.
