== What

* The runner through `pm-exec`: in-memory `stdin`, termination escalation, the write-ahead job record and log, the orphan rules of recovery, the fixed write order at job end. It implements the engine port `JobStarter` of pm-core; the module structure names the service interface `JobRunner`, which does not exist: the trace matrix settles whether the runner implements `JobStarter` or `JobRunner` is created in `core.spi`.
* Termination for every cause of the outcome table, a pause of the plan that holds now among the engine's cancels (`cancelled`); the job log readable while the job runs, the source the worker's output is read from (its mapping is link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]'s).
* *Probe jobs* (kind `probe`, `job_id` prefix `v`): a program found on the machine or in a checkout and not enrolled yet, run only after the consent of its kind; step 0 of the pipeline is the verification against the probe's candidate record (`executable_digest_mismatch`, `path_traversal_rejected`, `argument_rejected`), extending link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/verify-not-resolve.adoc[`verify-not-resolve`]; the record and log in the store of its scope (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/machine/jobs/`, `<PM_MCP_BASE>/onboardings/<entry_id>/jobs/`) with `scope.type` `machine` or `entry`; the fixed budgets `probe:version`, `probe:models`, `probe:login`, and `probe:script`, without learning; rows 1 to 4 of the outcome derivation and the probe's success marker, otherwise the exit code and output read by the step that asked; never retried by itself. Its confinement and environment are link:job-confinement-and-environment.adoc[`job-confinement-and-environment`]'s.
* `pm-exec` already makes the job the leader of its process group and reports its PID and start instant.

== Done When

A job that ignores `SIGTERM` is killed after the grace period with its whole group; a runtime killed at each point of the job's life recovers to the specified fate; the outcome table of the specification is covered row by row, the `probe` success marker included; a job of a plan paused now ends `cancelled`; a probe whose executable digest differs from its candidate record is refused `executable_digest_mismatch`, a probe over its fixed budget ends `timed_out` and is not started again, and its record and log lie in the store of its scope.
