== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The operator's message as its own turn*, recorded when `message` is taken, its id the answer of the request: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat-reply-stream[State Catalogue § The Reply as It Is Written]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#chat-routes[Runtime Model § The Chat's Routes], _A message_.
. *The reply written into its turn* from the worker's activity, each piece and each derived step announced, the turn replaced by the submitted reply at its end: § The Reply as It Is Written; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc#chat-events[Event Catalogue § Chat].
. *Stop* while `chat.reply` runs, the one link that ends a job, the state taking `cancelled` and the text so far kept `stopped`: § The Reply as It Is Written; link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#state-chat.reply[`chat.reply`]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#workflow-definition-units[Workflow DSL § Workflow Definition Units].
. *Writing on while a proposal is open* (`message` and `end` in `chat.read-back`): link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#state-chat.read-back[`chat.read-back`].
. *A failed reply* (`infeasible`, `lost`) keeping its text so far with its reason, and a new proposal replacing the open one: § The Reply as It Is Written.
. *The transcript's turns* with `turn_id`, `kind`, status, `format`, `untrusted`, `model`, `steps`, and `refs`, read in pages: Runtime Model § The Chat's Routes, _Reading_; link:../specification/identifiers/02-registry-stores-to-web.adoc#id-chat-id[Identifiers § `chat_id`], link:../specification/identifiers/02-registry-stores-to-web.adoc#id-turn-id[§ `turn_id`]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#chat-page[Timeouts § Chat Transcript Page].

Left out: the routes, the events on the streams, and the `202` answer of the daemon (`plan-marshall-mcp`); the client contract and the mock runtime (`pm-mcp-clients`).
