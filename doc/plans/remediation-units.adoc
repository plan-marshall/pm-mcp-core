== What

* *The six policy units.* `remediation.ci-failure`, `remediation.analysis`, `remediation.review-findings`, `remediation.build-failure`, `remediation.merge-conflict` and `remediation.qgate-findings`, each an ordinary `scope subworkflow` of one failure class with its evidence, its `decide` actions and its domain outcomes `resolved | waived | exhausted | fix_requested` plus the control outcome `stepped_out` (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#remediation-units[Workflow DSL § Remediation Policy Units]).
* *The evidence of each class.* The gathering job that persists one failure envelope per failure (`failure_id`, `class`, `kind`, `source`, `file`, `line`, `message`, `fingerprint`, `excerpt_uri`) with the evidence a decision rests on, the externally authored text batched and screened and withheld where a verdict is missing, and the base result of the same check in the same job so that a `baseline_red` failure never reaches `decide` (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#unit-template[Workflow DSL § Unit Template]).
* *The closed action set.* `retry` (with its `enum[flaky|infra|timeout]` reason), `fix` (in-branch and re-verified), the `loop_back` form of `fix` (the fix as Phase 5 remediation tasks), and `request_waiver` (an operator wait answered `waive` or `decline`): the server takes the remedy itself where the bound actions and the budgets left admit exactly one, otherwise a task of the unit's triage role chooses it, and while that role is unqualified the server takes the conservative fallback (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#remediation-units[Workflow DSL § Remediation Policy Units]).
* *The one template and its runtime selection.* Every unit declares the full template `gather`, `budget-check`, `decide`, `choose`, `conservative` and the remedy states of its class, with the class's own states named `remediation-<class>.<state>` and no state a link state; who decides is never hard-wired — it follows at runtime from the remedies the call binds, the budgets left and whether a waiver can be requested, so the same unit copy binds a second remedy without a change (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#unit-template[Workflow DSL § Unit Template]; invariant `INV-REM-7`).
* *The calls and their budgets.* The call states and their default retry/fix budgets: `remediation.qgate-findings` from `refine|outline|plan.remediate-qgate` (0/3), `remediation.build-failure` from `execute|land|finalize.remediate-build` (1/3), `remediation.ci-failure` from `finalize.remediate-ci` and `finalize.remediate-barrier-ci` (2/3), `remediation.analysis` from `finalize.remediate-analysis` (1/3), `remediation.review-findings` from the review and barrier call states (1/3) and from the self-review and security-audit steps (`actions=[loop_back]`, `retry_budget=0`), `remediation.merge-conflict` from `finalize.remediate-sync-conflict` and `finalize.remediate-merge-conflict` (0/2); the effective ceiling per policy with its source (the template's call argument, a composition rule, or a `raise` waiver id) capped by the compiled hard limits of `retry_budget` and `fix_budget` (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#_call_sites_default_budgets[Workflow DSL § Call Sites & Default Budgets]).
* *The one-at-a-time order and the ceiling.* In the finalize wait region one unit runs at a time in the fixed order CI, analysis, review findings; a unit's await state watches only its own class at the new `HEAD`; after `resolved` or `waived` verdict currency re-fires the region for every class stale at the new `HEAD`; an exhausted unit enters the re-planning with the trigger `ceiling` or the hand-back as its call states name; a `land` plan's composition binds `actions=[retry]`, so exhaustion hands the plan back with the operator's change and the failure documented (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#_call_sites_default_budgets[Workflow DSL § Call Sites & Default Budgets]).
* *The compiled invariants.* The invariants every load and every plan instance version holds the units to: the full template, the closed action set, the budgets and their caps, the conservative fallback, the sole owner of a refusal and the matched controls the spec names (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#remediation-invariants[Workflow DSL § Compiled Invariants of Remediation Units]).
* *Kept, or owned elsewhere.* The Q-Gate itself, its findings contract and its phase call states are link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]'s; the wait region, the barrier and the merge path that call the units are link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]'s; the waiver wait and its decline are the operator wait of link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Workflow DSL § Waiting for the Operator]'s; the triage roles and their qualification are link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#qualification[Model Work § Role Qualification]'s. The default unit files `remediation-*.pmwf` and the state-catalogue drift test over them are link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/default-units-and-drift-tests.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `default-units-and-drift-tests`]'s: this package delivers the units' semantics — the template's states, the gather and screen jobs, the decide and choose rules, the compiled invariants and the call-site budgets — with fixture copies of the units in its tests. Left out: those packages' own subjects; the unit loader and the designable-unit machinery (`process-designer`).

== Done When

Each of the six units loads with the full template and its class's states, and a copy that omits a template state, binds an action outside the closed set, or exceeds a compiled budget cap is refused by the static verifier and at composition with the rule or invariant named.

The evidence of each class is the failure envelope set the specification names, with its kinds and its no-verdict kind where a constituent failed (ci `infra`, build `unparsed`, analysis `undecidable`); the externally authored items are screened, a missing or suspicious verdict withholds the item and leaves the finding pending, the unit then ends through its budget and fallback, and a problem of the gathering itself is remediable.

`decide` takes the one admitted remedy without a task; with two admitted remedies it creates the decision task; a role that is unqualified takes the conservative fallback; a `fix` that lands, a `loop_back` that allocates Phase 5 tasks, a `retry` within the policy's window and a `request_waiver` that ends `waive` or `decline` are each shown with their call-state routing; `merge-conflict` with only `fix` bound takes it without a task and `qgate-findings` with `fix` and `request_waiver` bound creates the task.

Each call state's default retry/fix budget is bound, the effective ceiling records its source and is capped by the compiled limits, and a `raise` waiver id moves it; a spent budget enters the re-planning with `ceiling` or the hand-back as the call state names; in the wait region two failing classes run one after the other in the order CI, analysis, review findings and the region re-fires for every class stale at the new `HEAD`; a `land` plan's calls bind `retry` only and exhaustion hands back with the change and the failure documented.
