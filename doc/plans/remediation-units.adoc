== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The six policy units*, their outcomes and the closed action set: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#remediation-units[Workflow DSL § Remediation Policy Units]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/08-remediation-units.adoc#state-catalogue-remediation[State Catalogue § Remediation Units].
. *The unit template* with the evidence of each class, its screening and the base result: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#unit-template[Workflow DSL § Unit Template]; link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#evidence-directory[Store Schemas § Evidence Directory].
. *Decide and choose* at runtime, with the conservative fallback: link:../specification/state-catalogue/08-remediation-units.adoc#remediation-decide[State Catalogue § Remediation Units, decide].
. *The call sites, their default budgets and the effective ceiling*, the one-at-a-time order in the wait region and the exhausted unit's exit: § Remediation Policy Units, _Call Sites & Default Budgets_; link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#remediation-budgets[Timeouts & Budgets § Remediation Budgets].
. *The compiled invariants*: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc#remediation-invariants[Workflow DSL § Compiled Invariants of Remediation Units].

Left out: the Q-Gate, its findings contract and its phase call states (link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the wait region, the barrier and the merge path (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]); the default unit files and their drift test (`plan-marshall-mcp` / `default-units-and-drift-tests`; this package tests with fixture copies); the unit loader (link:process-designer.adoc[`process-designer`]).
