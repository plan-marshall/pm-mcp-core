== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The plan chat of link:chat.adoc[`chat`] proposes no revision until this package.

. *The proposal* within the re-planning window: link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-chat[Phase Workflows § Revision from a Chat], _Proposal_.
. *Guards and apply at the next task boundary* through `replan.apply-revision`: § Revision from a Chat, _Guards_ and _Next Task Boundary_; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-apply[§ Apply, Re-Entry & Instance Version]; the plan's part of _Apply by subject_ of link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat[State Catalogue § Chatting with the Planning Role].
. *The record*, the report `plan.revised` and the epic told: § Revision from a Chat, _Record_.
. *Inside a decision*: the decision closed `withdrawn`: § Revision from a Chat, _Record_; link:../specification/state-catalogue/16-decision.adoc[State Catalogue § Decision].
. *A revision that changes intent* submits a new intent record revision: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#intent-record[Phase Workflows § The Intent Record], _Revisions_; link:../specification/store-schemas/06-task-and-references.adoc#intent-record-store[Store Schemas § Intent Record].

Left out: the re-planning path itself (link:re-planning.adoc[`re-planning`]); the chat sub-workflow (link:chat.adoc[`chat`]); the chat in the clients.
