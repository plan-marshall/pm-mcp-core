== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The epic as a resource* (UC-PLN-5) with the operator's acts as its endpoints: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#epic-resource[Runtime Model § The Epic as a Resource].
. *An epic's actions* (UC-CTL-2) as operator endpoints re-entering `epic.next-work`, with a place for the action Chat: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-actions[Model Work § An Epic's Actions]; operator acts, never workflow states: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#operator-flows[State Catalogue § Operator Flows That Are Not Workflow Units].
. *Next plan*, with the answer per row and the raise of `parallelization_scope`: § An Epic's Actions.
. *Archive*, with the completion review first and the archive check: § An Epic's Actions; link:../specification/state-catalogue/06-setup-epic-other.adoc#state-catalogue-epic[State Catalogue § Epic Workflow].
. *Cancel*, also as the integrity pause's resolution _abort_, and the epic's `terminal_reason` values replacing the code value `abandoned`: § An Epic's Actions; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Store Schemas § Epic Header].
. *The release of a project* (UC-PRJ-3), its wait for a running short landing, its ends of plans, epics, entries and runs, and the closed lifecycle until enrolled again: link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#project-release[Credentials & Enrolment § Releasing Ends Work as the Operator Ends It]; link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#enrolment-lifecycle-release[§ Enrolment, Lifecycle Gate & Release].

Left out: the activity, the ends of a plan and the holds (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]); the action Chat and its conversation (link:chat.adoc[`chat`]); what the epic does by itself (link:epic-auto.adoc[`epic-auto`]); the review (link:epic-review.adoc[`epic-review`]); decompose again (link:epic-decomposition.adoc[`epic-decomposition`]).
