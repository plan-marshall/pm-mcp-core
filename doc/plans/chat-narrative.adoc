== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The narrative lines* `{key, args, refs, at}`, the closed catalogue `chat.line.*` derived from the subject's events, never a model's words or untrusted text: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat-narrative[State Catalogue § The Subject's Narrative].
. *The notice turn* carrying `lines[]`, the lines the runtime writes together between two replies, a line held while a reply is written: § The Subject's Narrative.
. *The offer fact `since_last_turn`*, the lines and the outcomes of the chat's own proposals since the generation's previous turn: link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#since-last-turn[Model Work § Since the Last Turn].
. *The lines read from a point on* for the history resources of a plan and an epic: § The Subject's Narrative.
. *The queued revision* `queued_revision: {chat_id, turn_id, subject: {kind, id}, applies}` in the plan's status, with `chat_open` derived on read, from `chat.apply` (`queued`) to its application or its lapse: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat-proposal-states[State Catalogue § A Proposal's States]; link:../specification/store-schemas/17-re-planning.adoc#replan-record[Store Schemas § Re-Planning Record]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-chat[Phase Workflows § Revision from a Chat].
. *`lapsed`* for a proposal open or accepted when the plan leaves the revision window, *`refused`* for an accepted revision that fails its guards again at its boundary (`lapsed` winning where both hold), and *`conflict`* for an accept naming a plan's proposal made against an older instance version or accepting a revision while another of that plan is queued, each told by a notice: § A Proposal's States; link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#state-chat.read-back[`chat.read-back`]; link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#state-chat.apply[`chat.apply`].

Schema changes: `status.schema.json` (`queued_revision`); the chat's turn record (a runtime notice turn's `lines[]`, the proposal state `lapsed`).

Left out: the routes of `pm://plan/{plan_id}/history{?since}` and `pm://epic/{epic_id}/history{?since}`, and the events that carry a notice turn, a proposal's state, and a queued revision (`plan.revision_queued`) to the clients (`plan-marshall-mcp`, specified in link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/mcp-tools/03-resource-uris.adoc#chat-resources[MCP Tools § Resource URIs] and link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc#chat-events[Event Catalogue § Chat]); the client contract, the mock runtime, the folded lines, the status cards, and the marks of a queued revision in the views (`pm-mcp-clients`); the chat worker and its core (link:chat-context.adoc[`chat-context`]).

== Done When

* Watch: no item is anchored to the sections the tasks name.
* Tests: each line key derived from its event and none from a left-out or context-only event; no line carrying model or untrusted text; a line arising during a reply written after it ends; lines arising together written as one notice turn; `since_last_turn` holding exactly the lines and the chat's own proposal outcomes since the generation's previous turn, absent on a generation's first turn; the history read from a point on equal to the lines written; `queued_revision` set by `chat.apply` and by the accept of a revision in a decision's chat, cleared when the revision is applied, refused at its boundary, and when it lapses, kept across a restart; a revision both refused and out of the window at its boundary recorded `lapsed`; a proposal open or queued when the plan leaves the window recorded `lapsed`; an accept naming a proposal made against an older instance version refused `conflict` with its notice; another subject's proposal its subject's changes overtook refused by its own path's checks with its finding, never `conflict`.
