== What

* Grading a role's configuration against the model verification corpus, its record bound to the qualification digest, its invalidation, and the conservative fallback of an unqualified or unavailable role.
* The digest over the effective `harness`, `model`, and `effort` (from link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-assignment[Model Work § Role Assignment]) with `tools`, `class`, the skill digests, and the harness major.minor, so a changed assignment or plan override makes the record stale per project; every record the operator makes written to the project set. A plan override without a current record is shown unqualified and its tasks take the conservative fallback. The `judge` and the `steward` are exempt.
* The record: `total_tokens` per item and per form, the open `harness` pattern, and the required `missed_sources`. A grading starts only behind the operator's consent with the expected tokens shown, a declined one recorded as owed; the offer and the consent are link:project-onboarding-unit.adoc[`project-onboarding-unit`]'s and `pm-operator roles qualify`'s.
* The corpus is completed from its sources before grading, through link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/corpus-sources.adoc[`corpus-sources`]: on a failed import no role is qualified; a grading on a partial corpus records the missed sources, and a role counts as qualified only on what was graded.
* The corpus itself and its validation by the build are link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/qualification-corpus-in-the-build.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `qualification-corpus-in-the-build`].
* *Agreement per topic*: the record keeps per form and per topic (`own` and `generic`, defined by the corpus items' `role` field) the agreement and the item count in `topics` (`topicResult`), never a pooled agreement; `warm_fresh_gap` is the largest over the topics of the fresh minus the warm agreement, and the own items of the review roles include planted defects at their correct severity, among them a defect that every criterion and every test accepts but that a constraint `CN-<n>` of the intent record rules out. A record is valid, in the fresh form, only when every topic of the record reaches the project's `qualification.agreement_level` (read at every check, so a changed level applies to existing records) and the record holds a result for each of those topics; a role whose `generic` topic reaches it and whose `own` topic does not is not valid, and neither is the reverse; a role without items of its own is valid on the `generic` topic alone, and a `zero_tool` role without items of its own has no topics and cannot qualify (link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#qualification-topics[Model Work § Corpus Topics of a Role]; link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#qualification-records[§ Qualification Records]; link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#qualification-record[Store Schemas § Role Qualification Record]; link:../specification/store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#local-config[§ Operator Local Configuration]; link:../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-48[Implementation Watch § Model Work]).

== Done When

A changed member of the digest invalidates the record, a changed role assignment or plan override included; an unqualified role takes its conservative fallback; a grading run over a fixture corpus with a scripted harness writes the specified record with `total_tokens`; a grading over a failed import writes no valid record, and one over a partial import names its missed sources. A record whose `generic` topic reaches the level and whose `own` topic does not is not valid, and neither is the reverse; a record that lacks a topic of the corpus is not valid; a record whose every topic reaches the level is valid; a role without items of its own is valid on the `generic` topic alone, and a `zero_tool` role without items of its own cannot qualify; `warm_fresh_gap` is the largest over the topics of the fresh minus the warm agreement; each topic's item count is recorded beside its agreement, and a changed agreement level applies to an existing record at its next check without re-grading; the review roles' own items include a defect that every criterion and every test accepts but a constraint `CN-<n>` rules out, and an answer that grades such a plan clean disagrees with the reference.
