== What

* *Implements* link:../specification/store-schemas/09-derived-facts-and-build-timings.adoc#derived-facts[Store Schemas § Derived Facts Store], the two value schemas of link:../specification/store-schemas/09-derived-facts-and-build-timings.adoc#derived-fact-kinds-discovery-calibration[Store Schemas § Derived fact kinds: `module_discovery`, `cost_calibration`], and the validity period of link:../specification/timeouts/03-retention-host-config.adoc#derived-fact-validity[Timeouts & Budgets § Derived Facts] (watch link:../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-43[PM-WATCH-STORE-43]).
* *The data side*: the entry records and the strict codec of `derived-facts.json`, and the registry through which a consumer registers a fact kind with its value schema, its class and its inputs; no concrete kind is registered here, because each belongs to the package that derives it.
* *The store*: the input-digest recompute rule for local derivations and the validity period for network derivations; the `stale` and `failed` semantics with the failure record; the gate-evaluation helper that turns a stale, failed or missing fact into `COULD_NOT_EVALUATE`; and the exclusion of the operator-only fact `agent_identity` from every model-facing view.
* *Left out*: the derivations of the concrete kinds (each belongs to the package that consumes the kind) and the Build Timings Store (built, `adaptive-timeout-learning`); the resources and representations that serve the facts (`plan-marshall-mcp`).

== Done When

A `derived-facts.json` round-trips and is pinned to the schema; a local fact is recomputed exactly when its `input_digest` changes and a network fact after `86400` seconds or on a refresh; a failed derivation keeps the last value as `stale` (or `failed` when none was ever derived) with its failure and makes every dependent gate `COULD_NOT_EVALUATE`; `agent_identity` is in no model-facing view; two refreshes at once lose no update.
