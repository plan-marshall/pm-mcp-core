== What

* The machine model slots beside the build slot scheduler; a paused plan holds no model slot.
* The metrics row per task and job with its raw columns, `reasoning_tokens`, `harness_version`, and the open `harness` included; the usage pointers, `not_applicable`, the unified figures, and the coverage are link:token-usage.adoc[`token-usage`]'s.
* The per-role learned worker budget; the pause of the worker budget from the ask to the answer acknowledgement during a hold is link:question-continuation.adoc[`question-continuation`]'s, and the fixed probe budgets are link:job-runner.adoc[`job-runner`]'s.
* The minimal writers and readers of this milestone: worker metrics, change ledger, learned budgets, qualification settings.

== Done When

Usage of a recorded harness output lands in the metrics row with each column; a missing figure is recorded unmeasured with its reason; slots never exceed their count under concurrent claims, and a plan paused at once releases the slots of its workers.
