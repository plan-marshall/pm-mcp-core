== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The two trees*: the crawl at `base_ref` from git objects and at `HEAD`, `edges_partial`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#change-map[Phase Workflows § Change Map], item 1.
. *The delta*, with `replaced[]` and the `partial` map: § Change Map, item 2, and the removed-coordinates half of link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#dependency-delta[§ Dependency Delta Check].
. *The rendering* and the resource `pm://plan/{plan_id}/change-map`: § Change Map, item 3.
. *The sections `## Own Calls` and `## Change Map`* of the pull-request body, the landing record and the completion report, and the shortcut facts of the `simplify` review: link:../specification/phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#own-calls[Phase Workflows § Own Calls of a Writer], _Reading_.
. *The self-review facts*: § Change Map, item 4.
. *The result* `change_map` in the finalize facts: § Change Map, item 5.

Left out: the module discovery and the build-file dependency parser (link:domain-extensions-and-architecture.adoc[`domain-extensions-and-architecture`]); the Dependency Delta Check and the finalize pipeline (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]); the recording of own calls (link:own-calls.adoc[`own-calls`]); the guidance and the rendering in the clients.
