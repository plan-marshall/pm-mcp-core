== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The declared surface*: paths, directories and globs, an untracked path admitted inside a tracked directory and refused otherwise: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-surface-disjointness[Model Work § Staging-Time Surface Disjointness], item 1; the refusal `surface_undeclared` of link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/mcp-tools/04-errors-and-testing.adoc[MCP Tools § Errors & Protocol Testing].
. *The staging check and its overlap*: the tracked files at the epic's `HEAD` that both surfaces match, plus the untracked entries they share: § Staging-Time Surface Disjointness, items 2 and 4.
. *An ended epic refuses its acts*: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-actions[Model Work § An Epic's Actions].

Left out: the toggle's refusal on an ended epic and the run of `epic.next-work` after a restart (`epic-auto`); admission at start against the running plans (§ Staging-Time Surface Disjointness, item 3, `epic-auto`).
