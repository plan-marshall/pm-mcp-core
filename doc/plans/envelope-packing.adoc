== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The cost estimator* `TaskCostEstimator` (`derive-cost-size`), its size rubric and its six sizes: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#cost-sizing[Model Work § Cost Sizing, Envelope Budget & Minimum Task Work], items 1 and 2.
. *The size-to-token table* `SizeTokenTable` with its compiled defaults and their basis `uncalibrated`: § Cost Sizing, item 3.
. *The packer* `EnvelopePacker`: Next-Fit under the budget, the sharing by module and role, the below-minimum merge and the sealing into `envelope-N`: § Cost Sizing, items 5 and 6; link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#_deterministic_envelope_packing_algorithm_envelopepacker[Model Work § Deterministic Envelope Packing Algorithm].
. *The envelope budget* `EnvelopeBudget`: its precedence and the cap of its two fallbacks at the long-context threshold: § Cost Sizing, item 4.
. *The profile member* `headless.long_context_thresholds` that supplies the threshold: link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#long-context-thresholds[Harness Profiles § Long-Context Price Tier]; the second plan of this package, PLAN-V2-63 (`long-context-thresholds`).

Left out: the wiring into `plan.describe-tasks` and the Phase 4 states (no work package); the sequential task yield within an envelope (link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#_sequential_task_yield_within_an_envelope[Model Work § Sequential Task Yield Within an Envelope]) and the Phase 5 states `execute.envelope-ready` and `execute.evaluate-envelope-return` (no work package); the recalibration of the size-to-token table, the derived fact `cost_calibration` (no work package); the reading of `envelope_budget_tokens` from `local-config.json` (no work package); the role view that shows a budget above the threshold (PLAN-V2-63); the fact `below_minimum_task` as the plan representation renders it (no work package).
