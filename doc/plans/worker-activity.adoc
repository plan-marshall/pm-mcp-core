== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The activity kinds and the mapping `headless.events`*, read while the worker runs: link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#worker-activity[Harness Profiles § What a Worker Does]; link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#headless-usage[§ Usage Pointers]; link:../specification/model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc#worker-output[Model Work § Output Read While the Worker Runs].
. *The activity journal*: link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#activity-journal[Store Schemas § Activity Journal].
. *The off-track signals and their actions*, `agent_started` recorded like the others: link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[Model Work § A Worker Off Track]; link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#queue-task-record[Store Schemas § Queue Task Record] (`signals[]`).
. *The off-track signals `failing_again` and `edit_churn`*, from the `pm_build` results and the `file_changed` entries of the current plan task, with their compiled thresholds and the role artifact's `off_track` members: link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[Model Work § A Worker Off Track]; link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#off-track-thresholds[Timeouts § Off-track signal thresholds]; link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-artifact[Model Work § Role Artifact Schema]. The schemas of `signals[]` and of the activity entry's `signal` name the implemented signals, which their parity test holds to the code; the task adds both names to the two enums with `SignalName`.
. *The activity served to the clients* from a cursor and as a live stream: § What a Worker Does.
. *The stage `events`* of the profile verification with a recorded output fixture per shipped harness version: link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#profile-verification[Harness Profiles § Profile Verification].

Left out: the report `task.off_track` (link:operator-reports.adoc[`operator-reports`]); a running plan's `off_track` in the project's resource (link:process-and-overview.adoc[`process-and-overview`]). Dropped: the navigation figures and the skill residency (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/plan-marshall-documentation/blob/main/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-40[L-40]).

== Done When

* Watch: link:../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-68[PM-WATCH-DISP-68]; the trace matrix names any other it finds.
* Tests: a recorded output per shipped harness version mapped to its entries; an unmatched line counted; no tool-result content or whole argument list recorded; the journal masked, bounded, archived; cursor and live stream; each signal at and below its threshold; a lowered and a refused raised threshold; each action recorded; one action per attempt and plan task; one report per `yield` or `restart`; no plan ended by a signal; the stage `events` per kind and on an unresolvable mapping.
