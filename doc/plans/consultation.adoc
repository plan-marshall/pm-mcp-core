== What

* A worker's consultation of another role, served by the server with its own wait; the consulted role runs on its own effective harness and model (link:role-sets-and-model-caller.adoc[`role-sets-and-model-caller`]).
* The rules of the link: one open consultation per task, no `consult` link while it is open; at most the compiled `3` consultation links per task over all its attempts; depth one, the consultation entity offering only `submit:answer`.
* The answer through the next wait: a consultation closed `lost` delivered with `status: unavailable`; for a fenced asker the answer recorded in the asking task's facts and carried by the successor's offer, which offers no `consult` for it; the `consultation_ids` of the asker's submit validated against the consultations delivered to the task.

== Done When

A consultation is answered once, a second answer refused; a consultation whose asker was fenced delivers its answer to the successor's offer once, and the successor is offered no `consult` for it; a fourth `consult` link of a task is not offered; a `lost` consultation is delivered as `unavailable`; a submit that omits a delivered `consultation_id` or names one not delivered is refused.
