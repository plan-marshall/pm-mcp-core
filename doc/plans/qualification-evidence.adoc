== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The mark `thin`* beside each topic's graded item count, never a condition of validity: link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#qualification-records[Model Work § Qualification Records].
. *The member `thin`* of every topic result, written and read by the codec: link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#qualification-record[Store Schemas § Role Qualification Record].
