== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The router* (`LaneRouter`): the request text, the path references, the scope estimate, the concrete request, the change-type reading, the signals `S1` to `S5`, the minimum lane, and the record `lane_route` in `audit.jsonl`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#initial-lane-router[Phase Workflows § Initial Planning-Lane Router].
. *The bound of the clarification batch*: a `refine_prompt` of more than four parts refused with `invalid_link_args`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#_single_batch_clarification_relaunch_ceiling[§ Single-Batch Clarification & Relaunch Ceiling], item 1.
. *The builder check's overflow*: the operator questions left when the refine relaunch is spent, kept as `facts.builder_open_questions`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#builder-check[§ Builder Check], _Routing_; link:../specification/state-catalogue/02-plan-phases-1-3.adoc[State Catalogue § Plan Phases 1 to 3], `refine.builder-check`.
. *The outline review's revisions of a light plan*, charged by `outline.route-review` to the counter `outline_review_revisions`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-gate[§ Outline Gate], _Outline Review_; link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#outline-review-revisions[Timeouts § Outline Review Revisions]; link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Store Schemas § Workflow State Store], `outline_review_revisions`.

Left out: the phases `refine` and `outline` themselves and their role skills (the milestone's other packages, not cut yet).
