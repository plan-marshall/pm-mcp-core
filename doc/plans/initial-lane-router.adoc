== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The router* (`LaneRouter`): the request text, the path references, the concrete request, the signals `S1`, `S4` and `S5`, the minimum lane, and the record `lane_route` in `audit.jsonl`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#initial-lane-router[Phase Workflows § Initial Planning-Lane Router].
. *The bound of the clarification batch*: a `refine_prompt` of more than four parts refused with `invalid_link_args`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#_single_batch_clarification_relaunch_ceiling[§ Single-Batch Clarification & Relaunch Ceiling], item 1.
. *The builder check's overflow*: the operator questions left when the refine relaunch is spent, kept as `facts.builder_open_questions`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#builder-check[§ Builder Check], _Routing_; link:../specification/state-catalogue/02-plan-phases-1-3.adoc[State Catalogue § Plan Phases 1 to 3], `refine.builder-check`.
. *The outline review's revisions of a light plan*, charged by `outline.route-review` to the counter `outline_review_revisions`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-gate[§ Outline Gate], _Outline Review_; link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#outline-review-revisions[Timeouts § Outline Review Revisions]; link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Store Schemas § Workflow State Store], `outline_review_revisions`.
. *The light gate* `outline.light-gate` over the affected files of the accepted light outline: the neighbourhood cap (`cap = 25`, the outline's files plus their one-hop neighbours), the declared-file band (`band = 8`), the neighbours read language-neutrally from the module graph (no import scanner), `neighbours_indeterminate` when the graph cannot place a neighbour, and the ratchet to `deep` on a failed gate: link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#light-gate[Phase Workflows § Light Gate]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/02-plan-phases-1-3.adoc#state-outline.light-gate[State Catalogue § `outline.light-gate`]; the module graph is link:domain-extensions-and-architecture.adoc[`domain-extensions-and-architecture`]'s.
. *The tie rule of `init.classify`*: an ambiguous domain detection asks no operator question — the domain set is the union of the tied top candidates, over-provisioned and narrowed exactly once in Phase 3 after the deliverables are declared: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#_deterministic_init_classifiers_workflow_settings[Phase Workflows § Deterministic Init Classifiers]; link:../specification/model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#decision-points[Model Work § Decision Points].

Left out: the phases `refine` and `outline` themselves and their role skills (the milestone's other packages, not cut yet).
