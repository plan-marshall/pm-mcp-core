== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The registers* of findings and watches in the ledger, their item states, `submit register_item`, the watch re-check, new findings as `needs_operator`: link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-registers[Store Schemas § Findings and Watches Registers]; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#findings-watches[Model Work § An Epic's Findings and Watches].
. *The sub-workflow `review`* with its per-item acts and the commit in one ledger write: link:../specification/state-catalogue/17-epic-decomposition-and-review.adoc#state-catalogue-review[State Catalogue § Review: `review`]; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-review[Model Work § The Review].
. *Promote*, the drafted plan staged under the surface checks, with its read-back: § Review: `review`; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-surface-disjointness[Model Work § Staging-Time Surface Disjointness].
. *Edit* through the rewrite and its read-back: § Review: `review`.
. *The operator's words* worked from, never interpreted, the items read as data: § The Review.

Left out: the archive check that an `open` finding holds (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]); the chat sub-workflow (link:chat.adoc[`chat`]).
