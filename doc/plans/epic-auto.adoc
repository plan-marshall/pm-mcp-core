== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The settings* `auto_start` and `overlap_max_files`, project values copied into the header at the epic's creation: link:../specification/store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#local-config[Store Schemas § Operator Local Configuration] (the `epic` object); link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[§ Epic Header].
. *The toggle and the overlap limit* as operator endpoints, header writes told in the epic's events: link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-auto[Model Work § An Epic Runs by Itself], _The epic's mode_ and _`overlap_max_files`_; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#epic-resource[Runtime Model § The Epic as a Resource].
. *On and off*: § An Epic Runs by Itself, _On_ and _Off_.
. *Overlap at admission*, the wait reason `overlap`: § An Epic Runs by Itself, _Overlap at admission_; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-queue-row[Store Schemas § Queue Row].
. *Overlap found while running*, `overlap_found` on both rows: § An Epic Runs by Itself, _Overlap found while running_; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-triggers[Phase Workflows § Re-Planning Triggers].
. *Reacting on an ended plan*: § An Epic Runs by Itself, _An ended plan_.
. *Through*: § An Epic Runs by Itself, _Through_.

Left out: next plan, the archive and the raise of `parallelization_scope` (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]); `epic.recut` and the review (link:epic-decomposition.adoc[`epic-decomposition`]); the acts on a plan (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]); the reports (link:operator-reports.adoc[`operator-reports`]); the clients.
