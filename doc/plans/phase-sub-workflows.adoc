== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The default units as phase sub-workflows*: `plan.pmwf` and the six phase units with their interfaces, a lane as a choice of phases, the call depth and the composition of the phases: link:../specification/store-schemas/03-findings-and-review-records.adoc#phase-sub-workflows[Store Schemas § Phases as Sub-Workflows and the Phase of a Record]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-plan-unit[State Catalogue § Plan Unit]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#plan-unit-phases[Phase Workflows § Plan Unit Phases].
. *The land lane's `land.*` sub-workflow within `execute`*: link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#land-lane[Phase Workflows § Land Lane].
. *The plan's history* and the node states derived from it: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#plan-history[Store Schemas § The Plan's History], link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#phase-start[§ The Start of the Current Phase].
. *The intent record* in the phases (submissions, rendering of § Clarified Request, the seeding for an epic-started plan, `history[]`): link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#intent-record[Phase Workflows § The Intent Record]; link:../specification/store-schemas/06-task-and-references.adoc#intent-record-store[Store Schemas § Intent Record]; link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#source-directory[Filesystem Layout § The Source Directory]; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Store Schemas § Epic Header].
. *The outline record* and its validation: link:../specification/store-schemas/06-task-and-references.adoc#outline-record-store[Store Schemas § Outline Record]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-validation-gate[Phase Workflows § Phase 3 Validation]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-satisfies[§ `satisfies` Coverage].
. *The builder check*: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#builder-check[Phase Workflows § Builder Check].
. *The Q-Gate and execute parts the phases own*, the opt-in mutation step among them: link:../specification/phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc[Phase Workflows § Q-Gate & Execute], link:../specification/phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#mutation-step[§ Mutation Step].
. *The knowledge search of the planning roles*: link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#intent-record[Phase Workflows § The Intent Record], _Searching what is known_.
. *The simplify step*: states, roster wiring and model tasks of `finalize.simplify` and `finalize.simplify-apply`: link:../specification/phase-workflows/04-finalize-step-bands.adoc#finalize-step-bands[Phase Workflows § Finalize Step Ordering Bands].
. *The self-review results per constraint, non-goal and decision* in the phase: § Finalize Step Ordering Bands, `pre-submission-self-review`.

Left out: the instance versions (link:plan-instance-versions.adoc[`plan-instance-versions`]); the finalize pipeline (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]); the drill-down in the clients.
