== What

* *Implements* link:../specification/store-schemas/03-findings-and-review-records.adoc#phase-sub-workflows[Store Schemas § Phases as Sub-Workflows and the Phase of a Record], link:../specification/state-catalogue/18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-plan-unit[State Catalogue § Plan Unit `phases`], and link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#plan-history[Store Schemas § The Plan's History], with link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#phase-start[§ The Start of the Current Phase] (`PM-WF-16`, `PM-IMPL-5`).
* *Phases as sub-workflows*: the plan unit (`plan.pmwf`, unit id `plan`, its call states `phases.*`) becomes a workflow of the six phases `init`, `refine`, `outline`, `plan`, `execute`, and `finalize`, each the sub-workflow `phase-1-init.pmwf` … `phase-6-finalize.pmwf` the link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc[Phase Workflows] specify, with the entries and exits of its frontmatter (`start` for every phase, `rework` of `outline`, `land` and `reentry` of `execute`, `adopt`, `hand_back`, and `post_run` of `finalize`), called by the plan unit at the entry its call state names; the frontmatter, `entry <name> -> <state>`, `call <unit> entry <name>`, and the interface rules are implemented (link:../../pm-workflow/doc/developer/workflow-dsl.adoc#unit-frontmatter[Workflow DSL Developer Guide § The Unit Frontmatter], link:../../pm-workflow/doc/developer/workflow-verify.adoc#interface-rules[Workflow Verification Developer Guide § Interface Rules and Unit Types]); a loop-back a call of a phase again, a re-entry from finalize into execute an outcome of finalize. The `phase` clause of a state (`StateNode.phase`, `phase inherit`) and every mapping of a state to its phase go, and with the merge of the phase files `PlanUnitMerger` and `partVersions`; the call stack holds at most five frames, the phase level adding one frame to every call path: the code's limit of four goes up to five in every place that states it, the status store (`WorkflowStatus.MAX_CALL_DEPTH`), the engine's `CallStack.MAX_DEPTH` and its push, and the static verifier's depth rule (`WorkflowValidator`, `VerificationRule`), so a fifth frame is admitted and a sixth refused, statically and at run time alike; the plan instance composes the phases, one instance file per composed unit, and prunes a phase as a call, with its reason.
* *Lanes as a choice of phases*: a lane is never a phase. The land lane calls no refine, outline, or plan phase, and its `land.*` states (today `land.pmwf`, run in phase `execute`) become a sub-workflow called within the phase `execute`; the adopt lane calls `init` and then `finalize`.
* *The phase of a sub-workflow*: a plan's phase is the phase sub-workflow on its call stack, and every sub-workflow called within a phase (a remediation unit, `land.*`, a chat) has that phase; no unit and no state declares a phase of its own, the entity units included.
* *The plan's history*: an append-only history per plan of states entered and left, sub-workflows called and returned, with their outcomes, the instance version in force, and the time; archived with the plan; the runtime derives each node's state from it: done, running, planned, waiting for the operator, failed (with its outcome), not taken, and, for a pruned phase, not in this plan with its reason.
* Where `workflow-engine`, `plan-instance`, or `project-units-and-invariants` have already implemented the flat plan unit, this package restructures them.
* *The plan's intent record*: the record `intent.json` beside `request.md` in the plan store, its schema `intent-record` (the named parameter schema the model submits), with `criteria` (`AC-<n>`, an `unchanged` one verified by a test where the plan guards behaviour), `constraints` (`CN-<n>`, each ruling something out with its `source`), non-goals (`NG-<n>`), decisions (`DC-<n>`), assumptions and source claims; `submit:refine`, the light lane's `submit:outline` and a re-planning that changes intent carry the record, a risk is written only as an `unchanged` criterion with its verification, a tested assumption or a non-goal, never as prose, and `request.md` § Clarified Request is rendered from the record; for a plan its epic started, the server seeds the required claims from the epic row's `done_when` and the epic's non-goals (`epic-intent`); every earlier revision moves to `history[]` in the accepting transaction and is never changed. The record's new link and identifier forms and the watch items `PM-WATCH-PHASE-106` and `-107` are implemented with it (link:../specification/store-schemas/06-task-and-references.adoc#intent-record-store[Store Schemas § Intent Record]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#intent-record[Phase Workflows § The Intent Record]; link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#source-directory[Filesystem Layout § The Source Directory]; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Store Schemas § Epic Header]; link:../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-106[PM-WATCH-PHASE-106]; link:../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-107[PM-WATCH-PHASE-107]).
* *The outline record and the specification parts of the planning phases*: the outline task submits the outline record `outline:schema[outline-record]`, the server keeps `outline.json` and renders `outline.md` from it, and validation is no state of its own; the builder check asks the operator about two criteria that cannot both hold, and about a decision or criterion that permits what a constraint rules out; review results and escalation are carried by the phases; the capability specification (`.marshall/capabilities/<capability>.json`) and its measurement are read by the planning roles; the scope-held Q-Gate row, the opt-in mutation verification step (`default:mutation`) and the knowledge search are the parts of the same specification that the phases own (link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-validation-gate[Phase Workflows § Phase 3 Validation]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#builder-check[§ Builder Check]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc[§ Q-Gate & Execute]; link:../specification/store-schemas/07-project-configuration.adoc#config-project-json[Store Schemas § Project Configuration]; link:../specification/store-schemas/06-task-and-references.adoc#capability-spec[§ Capability Spec]; link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc[Model Work]; link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc[§ Completion, Usage & Spend]; link:../specification/model-work/11-decision-records.adoc[§ Decision Records]).
* *The `review-simplify` restraint ladder*: the finalize step `finalize.simplify` runs a task of the read-only role `review-simplify` (vocabulary `skill://pm/finalize/simplify`, no write tools, never the plan's writer slot) that submits `submit:simplify`, a list of proposals (`file`, `change`, `rationale`) recorded as proposal records and never prose, and edits nothing; its facts carry the clone candidates the server computes before the task in-process and without a model (token-based clone detection of the added lines against the rest of the tree, identifiers and literals normalized, a run of at least `50` tokens, at most `20`, the longest first), which are evidence for the restraint ladder's "is it already in the codebase?" and never a finding by themselves; `finalize.simplify-apply` applies the accepted subset in the plan worktree, records every other proposal `declined`, and routes the applied change through `finalize.settle-commit`; the restraint ladder and the never-simplify list (behaviour-pinning tests, trust-boundary validation, security checks, data-loss error handling, operator-kept code) are vocabulary content (link:../specification/phase-workflows/04-finalize-step-bands.adoc#finalize-step-bands[Phase Workflows § Finalize Step Ordering Bands]).
* *Constraints and non-goals in the planning phases*: the pre-submission self-review returns one result per constraint `CN-<n>` (`respected` with its evidence, `violated` as a finding, or `not_verified`) and per non-goal `NG-<n>` (`respected`, `crossed` as a finding, or `not_verified`) beside the criteria, and a submission that omits one is refused `invalid_link_args`; the builder check always asks the operator about a decision or criterion that permits what a constraint rules out, restated as condition and response and naming both items, and a default the operator accepted as recommended does not settle it; `OutlineValidator` refuses a deliverable that satisfies an `unchanged` criterion verified by `test` downstream of the deliverable that satisfies the `new` or `fix` criterion it guards — directly or through others — naming both deliverables and the criterion, while a deliverable that satisfies both kinds is not refused. The watch items `PM-WATCH-PHASE-110` and `-111` guard the two new checks and give each its tests (link:../specification/phase-workflows/04-finalize-step-bands.adoc#finalize-step-bands[Phase Workflows § Finalize Step Ordering Bands]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#builder-check[§ Builder Check]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-satisfies[§ `satisfies` Coverage]; link:../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-110[PM-WATCH-PHASE-110]; link:../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-111[PM-WATCH-PHASE-111]).

== Done When

Every phase is a sub-workflow the plan unit calls, and the static verifier and the fuzzer pass over the default units; the plan unit enters each phase at the entry its call state names; a test per lane shows the phases it calls and prunes, the land lane its `land.*` sub-workflow within `execute`; a sub-workflow called within a phase reports that phase; a call path of five frames is accepted by the static verifier and pushed by the engine, and one of six refused by both; a loop-back calls a phase again and the history records both runs; a plan's history reproduces the node states of its process, also failed and not taken, after a runtime restart. The intent record round-trips through `submit:refine`, the light `submit:outline` and a re-planning, `request.md` § Clarified Request is rendered from it, an epic-started plan's required claims are seeded from the row's `done_when` and the epic's non-goals, and a revision moves the current record to `history[]`; the outline record round-trips and `outline.md` is rendered from it; the `review-simplify` step submits proposals with the clone candidates as evidence — a run of 49 tokens is no candidate and 50 is, at most 20 candidates are named longest first, and a candidate alone is never a finding — applies only the accepted subset, records every other proposal `declined`, routes the applied change through `finalize.settle-commit`, and its task carries no write tool and never takes the plan's writer slot; the intent record's link and identifier forms round-trip and the controls of `PM-WATCH-PHASE-106` and `-107` hold; and the builder check, the review results and escalation, the capability specification and its measurement, the scope-held Q-Gate row, the opt-in mutation step `default:mutation` and the knowledge search are each exercised by the phase that owns them; and the constraint and non-goal checks hold: a self-review that omits a `CN-<n>` or `NG-<n>` is refused, the builder check asks the operator about a default that permits what a constraint rules out, and the outline refuses an `unchanged` guard downstream of the change it guards, with the matched controls of `PM-WATCH-PHASE-110` and `-111`.
