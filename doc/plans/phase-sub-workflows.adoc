== What

* *Implements* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/03-findings-and-review-records.adoc#phase-sub-workflows[Store Schemas § Phases as Sub-Workflows and the Phase of a Record], link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-plan-unit[State Catalogue § Plan Unit `phases`], and link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#plan-history[Store Schemas § The Plan's History], with link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#phase-start[§ The Start of the Current Phase] (`PM-WF-16`, `PM-IMPL-5`).
* *Phases as sub-workflows*: the plan unit (`plan.pmwf`, unit id `plan`, its call states `phases.*`) becomes a workflow of the six phases `init`, `refine`, `outline`, `plan`, `execute`, and `finalize`, each the sub-workflow `phase-1-init.pmwf` … `phase-6-finalize.pmwf` the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc[Phase Workflows] specify, with the entries and exits of its frontmatter, called by the plan unit; a loop-back a call of a phase again, a re-entry from finalize into execute an outcome of finalize. The `phase` clause of a state (`StateNode.phase`, `phase inherit`) and every mapping of a state to its phase go, and with the merge of the phase files `PlanUnitMerger` and `partVersions`; the call stack holds at most five frames, the phase level adding one frame to every call path: the code's limit of four goes up to five in every place that states it, the status store (`WorkflowStatus.MAX_CALL_DEPTH`), the engine's `CallStack.MAX_DEPTH` and its push, and the static verifier's depth rule (`WorkflowValidator`, `VerificationRule`), so a fifth frame is admitted and a sixth refused, statically and at run time alike; the plan instance composes the phases, one instance file per composed unit, and prunes a phase as a call, with its reason.
* *Lanes as a choice of phases*: a lane is never a phase. The land lane calls no refine, outline, or plan phase, and its `land.*` states (today `land.pmwf`, run in phase `execute`) become a sub-workflow called within the phase `execute`; the adopt lane calls `init` and then `finalize`.
* *The phase of a sub-workflow*: a plan's phase is the phase sub-workflow on its call stack, and every sub-workflow called within a phase (a remediation unit, `land.*`, a chat) has that phase; no unit declares a phase of its own.
* *The plan's history*: an append-only history per plan of states entered and left, sub-workflows called and returned, with their outcomes, the instance version in force, and the time; archived with the plan; the runtime derives each node's state from it: done, running, planned, waiting for the operator, failed (with its outcome), not taken, and, for a pruned phase, not in this plan with its reason.
* Where `workflow-engine`, `plan-instance`, or `project-units-and-invariants` have already implemented the flat plan unit, this package restructures them.

== Done When

Every phase is a sub-workflow the plan unit calls, and the static verifier and the fuzzer pass over the default units; a test per lane shows the phases it calls and prunes, the land lane its `land.*` sub-workflow within `execute`; a sub-workflow called within a phase reports that phase; a call path of five frames is accepted by the static verifier and pushed by the engine, and one of six refused by both; a loop-back calls a phase again and the history records both runs; a plan's history reproduces the node states of its process, also failed and not taken, after a runtime restart.
