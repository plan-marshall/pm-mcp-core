== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the changes Phases as Sub-Workflows and The Plan's History into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once UC-PLN-1 is agreed (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]).
* *Phases as sub-workflows*: the plan unit becomes a workflow of the six phases `init`, `refine`, `outline`, `plan`, `execute`, and `finalize`, each the sub-workflow the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc[Phase Workflows] specify, called by the plan unit; a loop-back a call of a phase again, a re-entry from finalize into execute an outcome of finalize. The `phase` clause and the phase value table go; the call depth grows by one; the plan instance composes the phases and prunes a phase as a call, with its reason.
* *Lanes as a choice of phases*: a lane is never a phase. The land lane calls no refine, outline, or plan phase, and its `land.*` states (today `land.pmwf`, run in phase `execute`) become a sub-workflow called within the phase `execute`; the adopt lane calls `init` and then `finalize`.
* *The phase of a sub-workflow*: a plan's phase is the phase sub-workflow on its call stack, and every sub-workflow called within a phase (a remediation unit, `land.*`, a chat) has that phase; no unit declares a phase of its own.
* *The plan's history*: an append-only history per plan of states entered and left, sub-workflows called and returned, with their outcomes, the instance version in force, and the time; archived with the plan; the runtime derives each node's state from it: done, running, planned, waiting for the operator, failed (with its outcome), not taken, and, for a pruned phase, not in this plan with its reason.
* Where `workflow-engine`, `plan-instance`, or `project-units-and-invariants` have already implemented the flat plan unit, this package restructures them.

== Done When

The specification of both changes is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; every phase is a sub-workflow the plan unit calls, and the static verifier and the fuzzer pass over the default units; a test per lane shows the phases it calls and prunes, the land lane its `land.*` sub-workflow within `execute`; a sub-workflow called within a phase reports that phase; a loop-back calls a phase again and the history records both runs; a plan's history reproduces the node states of its process, also failed and not taken, after a runtime restart.
