== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The pull-request observation* `ci.pr-observe`: `pr_state`, `base_branch`, `queue_state`, `behind`, `merge_queue_required`, `merge_commit_sha`, `head_sha` and `observed_at`, with the GitHub and GitLab mapping of `behind`, `null` where a forge reports none; the Forgejo mapping is `forgejo-provider`'s (see Left out): link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#merge-outcome-mapping[Store Schemas § Merge Outcome Mapping]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-finalize.merge-gate[State Catalogue § `finalize.merge-gate`], which reads `pr_state` and `base_branch`; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/05-primitives.adoc[Workflow DSL § Primitives], the re-observing primitives.
. *The merge `ci.merge` and the enqueue `ci.enqueue`*, both bound to the expected head SHA, and the queue membership observed on its own: link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#_cross_plan_merge_mutex_fifo_admission_queue[Phase Workflows § Cross-Plan Merge Mutex & FIFO Admission Queue]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-finalize.merge-dispatch[State Catalogue § `finalize.merge-dispatch`].
. *The provider refusal mapped into its closed class*: `conflict`, `behind`, `queue_required`, `checks_pending`, `checks_failed`, `review_required`, `blocked`, `draft`, `method_not_allowed`, `closed`, `merged` and `unknown`: link:../specification/state-catalogue/07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-finalize.merge-refused[State Catalogue § `finalize.merge-refused`].

Left out: the routing of every class of `merge.route-refusal` to its state and the choice of `ci.enqueue` or `ci.merge` at `merge.dispatch`, with their tests, and the sync decision over the realized footprint, which link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`] owns in its task 3 and which read the operations and the class of this package; the Forgejo mapping to `behind` (the CI-contract task of link:forgejo-provider.adoc[`forgejo-provider`]), which that provider owns.
