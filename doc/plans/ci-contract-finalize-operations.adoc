== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The pull-request observation* `ci.pr-observe`: `pr_state`, `queue_state`, `behind`, `merge_queue_required`, `merge_commit_sha`, `head_sha` and `observed_at`, with the GitHub, GitLab and Forgejo mapping of `behind`, `null` where a forge reports none: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#merge-outcome-mapping[Store Schemas § Merge Outcome Mapping]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/05-primitives.adoc[Workflow DSL § Primitives], the re-observing primitives.
. *The merge `ci.merge` and the enqueue `ci.enqueue`*, both bound to the expected head SHA, the enqueue selected when `ci.pr-observe` reports `merge_queue_required: true` or the plan's last dispatch was refused `queue_required`, and the queue membership observed on its own: link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#_cross_plan_merge_mutex_fifo_admission_queue[Phase Workflows § Cross-Plan Merge Mutex & FIFO Admission Queue]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc[State Catalogue § Merge Path].
. *The provider refusal and its closed class* `merge.route-refusal`: `conflict`, `behind`, `queue_required`, `checks_pending`, `checks_failed`, `review_required`, `blocked`, `draft`, `method_not_allowed` and `unknown`, with `behind` and `conflict` re-entering the gate: link:../specification/state-catalogue/07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc[State Catalogue § Merge Path], `merge.route-refusal`.

Left out: `finalize-pipeline-and-merge` task 3, which keeps the `behind` route of `merge.route-refusal` and the sync decision over the realized footprint and depends on this package; the Forgejo provider itself (link:forgejo-provider.adoc[`forgejo-provider`]), which supplies the Forgejo mapping to `behind`.
