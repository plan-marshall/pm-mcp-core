== What

* The detection of a stalled plan or epic by the definition of _Stalled Plans_, with its cause `no_progress` and the idle span, as a hold `stalled` that clears by itself with the scope's next transition or task progress; one `plan_stalled` audit record per episode; counted in `needs_operator` and announced on the event streams, and never in a model-facing representation. The hold's acts and how the clients show it are link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]'s. It reads scope stores that Milestone 3 delivers: plan this task after the store substrate of Milestone 3, or deliver the detection over a fixture store and wire the real stores then; decide in the trace matrix.

== Done When

A scope past its threshold is held `stalled`, one below it, or in an operator wait or another hold, is not; the threshold comes from the timeouts specification; a second detection in the same episode appends no second `plan_stalled` record; the hold clearing by itself announces `plan.hold_released`; neither the hold, its cause, nor `plan_stalled` appears in any model-facing representation.
