Status, milestone and dependencies:: in the link:README.adoc#packages[index], the only place that states them

== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The record* `replan_pending`, `replans[]`, `follow_ups[]`: link:../specification/store-schemas/17-re-planning.adoc#replan-record[Store Schemas § Re-Planning Record].
. *The triggers and their detection* at the fixed checkpoints, with the declared thresholds: the trigger kinds `infeasible`, `merge_conflict` and `ceiling` only — the re-planning trigger `base_advanced`, `downstream_limit`, the declaration `base_overlap_min_files` and the checkpoint `finalize.review-cap-check` are removed (the epic watch trigger `base_advanced` stays where it is) — and `execute.replan-check` fetches nothing and only applies a queued chat revision: link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-triggers[Phase Workflows § Triggers & Detection]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-constants[§ Declared Thresholds]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-chat[§ Revision from a Chat].
. *The unit `replan.pmwf`*, its task and its outcomes: link:../specification/state-catalogue/12-re-planning.adoc#state-catalogue-replan[State Catalogue § Re-Planning]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-task[Phase Workflows § The Re-Planning Task].
. *The actions and their guards*, apply and re-entry, among the guards the refusal of a `wait_for_prerequisite` whose prerequisite waits on the submitting plan, directly or through its own prerequisites (a third plan, or a `pull_request:<number>` that is the recorded pull request of a running plan, counting as that plan), answered `rejected` with the reason `prerequisite_cycle` and the plans of the cycle in `detail`, nothing applied and the task continuing: link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-actions[Phase Workflows § Actions & Guards], item 4. The reason is already in the closed list of rejection reasons of the documentation repository (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/mcp-tools/04-errors-and-testing.adoc#rejection-reasons[MCP Tools § Rejection Reasons], its table's `prerequisite_cycle` row), which this package does not edit; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-apply[§ Apply, Re-Entry & Instance Version]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-follow-ups[§ Follow-Up Plans & Split Pull Requests].
. *The apply path of a revision from a chat*: link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-chat[Phase Workflows § Revision from a Chat].
. *The budget and the conservative fallback*: link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#replan-budget[Timeouts & Budgets § Re-Planning Budget]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-fallback[Phase Workflows § Conservative Fallback].

The default copy of `replan.pmwf` and its drift test are `plan-marshall-mcp` / `default-units-and-drift-tests`; this package tests against a fixture copy.

Left out: the chat and its read-back (link:chat.adoc[`chat`], link:chat-plan-revision.adoc[`chat-plan-revision`]); the phase sub-workflows whose checkpoints raise the triggers (link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the finalize re-entry and merge path (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]).
