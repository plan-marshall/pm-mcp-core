Status and dependencies:: in the link:README.adoc#packages[index], the only place that states them

== What

* *The record.* `mcp/status.json` → `replan_pending` (the trigger a checkpoint raised and the unit has not yet judged, `null` otherwise), `replans[]` (append-only, one record per judged trigger whose evidence keys decide whether a checkpoint raises a trigger again and whose task count is charged against `replan_budget`, and one record per revision the operator accepted in a chat, which has no trigger and is never charged) and `follow_ups[]` (append-only), each written only by the server in the transaction of the trigger point, of the `replan` unit, or of `chat.apply`, with the record's schema: `RP-<n>`, `trigger`, `decided_by` (`task`, `fallback`, `operator`), `action`, `reentry`, `queue_task_id`, `fallback_reason`, `recorded_at` (link:../specification/store-schemas/17-re-planning.adoc#replan-record[Store Schemas § Re-Planning Record]).
* *The triggers and their detection.* The server detects every trigger itself at the fixed checkpoints from recorded or freshly observed facts, writes it as `replan_pending` in the entering transaction and never lets a model report one: `infeasible` (`execute.evaluate-envelope-return`, `execute.exit-barrier`), `base_advanced` (`execute.replan-check`, `finalize.sync-baseline`, `finalize.merge-sync`, with `base_overlap_min_files`), `merge_conflict` (the trial merge with `conflict_min_files`, no change to the worktree), `downstream_limit` (`execute.replan-check` projected, `finalize.review-cap-check` measured, against a team-declared `structural_caps` of a required review bot), and `capability_conflict` (`finalize.architecture-refresh`) (link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-triggers[Phase Workflows § Triggers & Detection]).
* *The unit.* `replan.pmwf` (`scope subworkflow`, project-owned, run in the phase of its caller) called from `execute.replan` and `finalize.replan`: `replan.gather-trigger` reads `replan_pending` and composes the facts with their evidence, `replan.review` is the task of the role `replan`, and the outcomes carry the re-entry: `done` continues where the plan stood, `reentered` re-enters `execute.envelope-ready`, `reauthor` enters the lane's outline entry (deep `outline.deep`, light `outline.light-regate`), `hand_back` and `stepped_out` enter `finalize.hand-back`; the conservative fallback where the budget is spent, no outline exists or the review task ended `lost`; every terminal clears `replan_pending` and completes the record (link:../specification/state-catalogue/12-re-planning.adoc#state-catalogue-replan[State Catalogue § Re-Planning]).
* *The actions and their guards.* `reintegrate_in_plan`, `split_change`, `defer_to_follow_up_plan`, `wait_for_prerequisite` (the `await-prerequisite` state), `rebase_and_reverify_only` (`integrate-base`), `hand_back` and `continue`; a re-integration passes the guards of a first plan (outline validation, derivation dry-run, task graph, Q-Gate) and takes effect at the next task boundary; `reentry` names `execute` or `outline`; a wait for a prerequisite releases the scope and resumes the unit without a new record; a follow-up plan is recorded in `follow_ups[]`; and a revision applied from a chat is `decided_by: operator` with its `chat` object, never charged against the budget (link:../specification/phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-actions[Phase Workflows § Re-Planning, Actions & Guards]).
* *The budgets.* `replan_budget` bounds the task-count per plan and the `replan` unit's re-entry, the conservative fallback is taken where a bound is spent, and the record's `fallback_reason` names it (`budget_spent`, `no_outline`, `role_unqualified`, `role_undefined`, `class_mismatch`, `role_unavailable`, `attempts_spent`, `infeasible`) (link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#replan-budget[Timeouts & Budgets § Re-Planning Budget]).
* *Kept, or owned elsewhere.* The `chat` and `chat-plan-revision` packages call this path and stay its callers: `chat-plan-revision` is `blocked` on this package and adds nothing of the apply path. Left out: the chat itself and its read-back (link:chat.adoc[`chat`], link:chat-plan-revision.adoc[`chat-plan-revision`]); the phase sub-workflows whose checkpoints raise the triggers (link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the finalize step bands and the re-entry and merge path that consume the triggers (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]).

== Done When

Every trigger is raised by the server at its checkpoint from the recorded or observed evidence the specification names: `infeasible` where no runnable task remains and one deliverable has an `infeasible` task, `base_advanced` where at least `base_overlap_min_files` footprint paths changed since the last judged base commit, `merge_conflict` where a trial merge conflicts in at least `conflict_min_files` files and no worktree was touched, `downstream_limit` projected before the merge and measured at the review cap gate, and `capability_conflict` where a capability requirement's fingerprint changed; a raised trigger is written to `replan_pending` in the entering transaction; no trigger is raised twice for evidence the record already carries.

A `done` continues where the plan stood, a `reentered` re-enters `execute.envelope-ready`, a `reauthor` enters the deep or the light lane's outline entry as its `reentry` names, and `hand_back` and `stepped_out` enter `finalize.hand-back`; every terminal clears `replan_pending` and completes the record.

A re-integration passes the guards of a first plan and takes effect only at the next task boundary (a paused plan's when started again); a `wait_for_prerequisite` releases the scope and resumes without a second record; a `defer_to_follow_up_plan` writes the follow-up into `follow_ups[]`; the budget's task count is charged per judged trigger and a spent budget takes the conservative fallback and names its reason; a revision applied from a chat records `decided_by: operator` with its chat object, is never charged, and composes an instance version of event `replan`.

The record validates against its schema for each `decided_by` (a chat revision carries no trigger and a chat object; a fallback taken without a task carries `queue_task_id: null`), and a record written by another binary version is refused `format_newer` rather than read.
