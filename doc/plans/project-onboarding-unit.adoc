== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The gate* before a project's onboarding and the refusal of an act whose onboarding step is not done: link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#project-onboarding[Credentials & Enrolment § Onboarding a Project as One Process].
. *The unit* from `project.checkout` to `project.enrol` in a scope of its own, its detection, its entry endpoint and its abandonment: link:../specification/state-catalogue/15-project-onboarding.adoc[State Catalogue § Project Onboarding]; § Onboarding a Project as One Process.
. *The checkout's scripts and the recording build* under `run_checkout_scripts`: the consent and its scripts, then one build of the base branch through the build egress proxy in record mode, whose recorded hosts are proposed as `egress_hosts` for confirmation — no host list is parsed from build configuration — and whose read of every compiled credential location of the project's build domains that exists on the machine proposes `build_credential_files`, confirmed or removed one by one, with no configuration parsed and a missing credential showing as a failing recording build; the credential list is `CredentialLocations` of link:job-confinement-and-environment.adoc[`job-confinement-and-environment`] (PLAN-V2-146, staged), not a second one: link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#project-onboarding[Credentials & Enrolment § Onboarding a Project as One Process]; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#consent[Store Schemas § Consent Before Acting].
. *The setup in the specification's order*, first run only, with the merge-queue probe that reads the base branch's queue live (a queue present: `not_needed`; absent and available: asked; unreadable: `not_needed` with its reason; nothing stored) and the credential `verify` that probes the visibility of check runs without storing it:link:../specification/state-catalogue/06-setup-epic-other.adoc#state-catalogue-setup[State Catalogue § Project Setup]; link:../specification/domain-extensions/04-setup-state-machine.adoc[Domain Extensions § Setup State Machine].
. *The consents of model use* in tokens: § Project Setup; link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Harness Profiles § Tokens, Not Money].
. *The project's values over the machine's*, with the revision rule: link:../specification/store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#project-levels[Store Schemas § Project Values over Machine Values]; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#concurrent-changes[CLI & Security § Concurrent Changes of a Setting].
. *The projects of the machine*: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#project-explorer[Runtime Model § The Projects of the Machine]; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#explorer-store[Store Schemas § The Operator's Projects].

Left out: the later runs `reconcile` and `aspect:<name>` (the reconcile path of the `project-setup` workflow, no work package yet); the release of a project (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]); the consent store and the landing variants (link:../Traceability.adoc#PM-WF-25[Traceability PM-WF-25]); the cleanup's reader (link:cleanup.adoc[`cleanup`]).
