== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The prune reasons `no_code_delta` and `no_production_delta`* in `PruneReason`, in the order of the enum: link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#plan-instance[Store Schemas § Plan Instance], `prune_reason`; where each is set: link:../specification/phase-workflows/04-finalize-step-bands.adoc#finalize-step-bands[Phase Workflows § Finalize Step Ordering Bands], the step catalogue (`prunable` (`no_code_delta`)), and link:../specification/phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#mutation-step[§ Mutation Step].
. *The hand-over `default:land-files`* at order `70` in the default roster, pruned with `plan_default` against `default:branch-cleanup` where the plan's landing is not `landing.files`: § Finalize Step Ordering Bands, the step catalogue, row `70`; link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#landing-variants[§ Landing Variants]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL § Plan Behaviour Axes].
. *Rule 1 of the pipeline validator over the composed instance*: two steps at one order are refused per plan instance, after the axis pruning, so that the two steps at `70` of the default roster pass: link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#_build_time_startup_static_invariant_verification_finalizepipelinevalidator[§ Build-Time & Startup Static Invariant Verification]; link:../../pm-workflow/doc/developer/workflow-finalize.adoc#_the_rules[Finalize Pipeline Developer Guide § The Rules].

Left out: the state `finalize.land-files` and the landing variants' states (the milestone's other packages, not cut yet).
