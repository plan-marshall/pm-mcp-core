== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. Built today: `AwaitedEvent` with its nine members, `Primitive.awaitedEvent()`, and `PrimitiveRegistry`, which refuses a primitive that declares no awaited event (`MISSING_AWAITED_EVENT`); the four primitives of `pm-provider-git` all await `NONE`. Not built: any declaration of a bound.

. *The bound of every awaiting primitive*: each primitive whose `awaitedEvent()` is not `NONE` takes its bound from the closed list (`job budget`, `counter <name>`, `deadline <key>`, `holder lease`, `operator`, `resident`, `stall-watched`), and what happens at the bound is the row's; primitives that wait alike share one row: link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#awaiting-bounds[Timeouts § Bounds of Awaiting Primitives]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#liveness-guarantee[Workflow DSL § Liveness Guarantee]. Module `pm-core`, paths `pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/spi/Primitive.java`, `PrimitiveRegistry.java`, `AwaitedEvent.java`; extends `PrimitiveContractTest` and `PrimitiveRegistryTest`. Open: whether the bound is a method of `Primitive` or a table keyed by primitive group, since the rows are grouped and not one per primitive; the plan that builds it decides by an ADR.
. *The registry test*: every registered primitive whose awaited event is not `NONE` has a row, its bound source is one of the closed list, no row is unbounded, and a `stall-watched` row names its hold; matched control: a fixture primitive that awaits `EXTERNAL_STATE` without a row fails, and one that awaits `NONE` needs none: § Bounds of Awaiting Primitives, the paragraph after the list. Module `pm-core`, path `pm-core/src/test/java/de/planmarshall/core/spi/PrimitiveRegistryTest.java`; the test over the registered primitives of the product runs where they are registered, in the assembly.

Left out: the verifier rules 11a and 11b of the cycles, whose shape is built (link:../../pm-workflow/doc/developer/workflow-verify.adoc[`workflow-verify` developer guide]); the open verifier gaps of the guarantee (link:../implementation-watch/workflow-dsl.adoc#PM-WATCH-DSL-1[PM-WATCH-DSL-1], link:../implementation-watch/workflow-dsl.adoc#PM-WATCH-DSL-4[PM-WATCH-DSL-4], link:../implementation-watch/workflow-dsl.adoc#PM-WATCH-DSL-5[PM-WATCH-DSL-5]), which no package of this index owns; the hold `overdue` a `stall-watched` row names (link:stall-clocks-and-overdue-waits.adoc[`stall-clocks-and-overdue-waits`]).
