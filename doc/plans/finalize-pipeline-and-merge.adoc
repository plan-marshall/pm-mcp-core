. *The merge path* as its chain of states, with the cross-plan merge mutex. The routing of `merge.route-refusal` (every class of the closed refusal class to its state) and the choice of `merge.dispatch` (`ci.enqueue` when `ci.pr-observe` reports `merge_queue_required: true` or the plan's last dispatch was refused `queue_required`, otherwise `ci.merge`) are this package's, as is the sync decision over the realized footprint; each reads the operations and the refusal class of link:ci-contract-finalize-operations.adoc[`ci-contract-finalize-operations`]. The task also builds: the gate entry `merge.gate-entry`, which observes every plan's recorded pull request through `ci.pr-observe`, follows a retargeted base as `finalize.adopt-pr` does, and routes `closed`, `merged` with the plan's `HEAD`, `pr_merged_unlanded` and an observation that cannot be made; . *The Pre-Merge Review Barrier*, its waivable gates, the waiver lookup and the ADR number at landing, with the read of the provider's result for every failing check at the current head of the base branch (through `ci.checks`) before the barrier ends `baseline_red_required`, and the outcomes `baseline_cleared` (the lease released, the gate re-entered, the next sync rebasing onto that base commit, counted with `indeterminate` against `merge_barrier_indeterminate`), `ci_failed` (the failing checks green at a base head `HEAD` contains) and `baseline_red_required` (to `finalize.hand-back` with the gap `cause: baseline_red`): link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#pre-merge-barrier[Phase Workflows § Pre-Merge Review Barrier & Waivers]; link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#merge-path[§ Merge Path], item 5; link:../specification/state-catalogue/07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-finalize.merge-barrier[State Catalogue § `finalize.merge-barrier`] and link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#state-finalize.hand-back[§ `finalize.hand-back`]; link:../specification/job-runtime/03-parsers-and-gates.adoc#_gate_honesty_verification[Job Runtime § Gate Honesty Verification], Baseline Separation; link:../specification/state-catalogue/08-remediation-units.adoc#state-catalogue-remediation-ci[State Catalogue § `remediation.ci-failure`].
