== What

* *Verdict currency and re-entry.* The fail-closed `head_at_completion` anchor obligation (a `headDependent` step records its 40-hex SHA from the worktree's `HEAD` in the same transition that records `done`; a record without it is `unverified` and re-fires, and no link accepts the SHA as a parameter), the `VerdictCurrencyEvaluator` with its closed `CurrencyVerdict` values (`CURRENT_SAME_HEAD`, `PRESERVED_INPUTS_UNAFFECTED`, `STALE_MISSING_ANCHOR`, `STALE_REBASE_ADVANCED`, `STALE_UNSCOPED_HEAD_CHANGE`, `STALE_INPUTS_MODIFIED`), `input_scope` (`remote_state`, `open_ended`, `plan_footprint`, `declared`) validated by `FinalizePipelineValidator`, the footprint-scoped input resolution, and the SKIP/RE-FIRE decision on every re-entry (link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#_deterministic_verdict_currency_algorithm_verdictcurrencyevaluator[Phase Workflows § Deterministic Verdict Currency Algorithm]).
* *The step records of a firing.* Settled completions only (`UNSETTLED` emits none), one completion per firing with `firings[]` (`fired_at`, `cause`, `loop_back_iteration`, `job_id`, `queue_task_ids`, `outcome`, `productive`), `unverified` before a re-fire and never a surviving prior `done`, the positional Phase-Exit Completeness over every step of lower order evaluated by one shared `PhaseExitCompleteness` evaluator, no operator skip, and the stale-finding re-anchor before a `fix` (link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#_step_records_re_fire_loop_back_rules[Phase Workflows § Step Records, Re-Fire & Loop-Back Rules]).
* *The merge path.* `default:branch-cleanup` (`order = 70`) as the chain of states `finalize.merge-gate` (finding transmission re-attempt and Phase-Exit Completeness), `finalize.merge-lease` (FIFO admission to the cross-plan merge mutex, the lease held only while no wait is open), `finalize.merge-sync` (the ADR number at landing, the sync only when required, the lease-protected push, the re-planning triggers `merge_conflict` and `base_advanced`, `remediation.merge-conflict`), `finalize.merge-evidence-wait` (the composite wait on the pushed `HEAD` with the lease released), `finalize.merge-barrier` (the Dependency Delta Check and the Pre-Merge Review Barrier, `pass_hand_back` where the asking variant leaves the merge to the operator), `finalize.merge-dispatch` (bound to the evaluated head; `ci.merge` or `ci.enqueue`), `finalize.merge-queue-wait`, `finalize.merge-queue-exit` and `finalize.hand-back` in its four forms, with every close bound to the compiled retry, watch-restart and condition budgets and every spend entering `finalize.replan` (trigger `ceiling`) or `finalize.hand-back` (link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#merge-path[Phase Workflows § Merge Path]).
* *The Pre-Merge Review Barrier.* The two fail-closed predicates against the current `HEAD`: zero unhandled findings with a terminal disposition that is not `pending` and a transmitted provider response where the finding has a provider address, and the required-bot participation record with the registered author and the review-currency test (`exact_sha` or `after_last_push`, a roster aggregate of `reviewed`, `vacuous` or `unreviewed_by_waiver`); the waivable merge gates of the closed list with the waiver lookup and the logged consumption, never a waiver for a security finding; the ADR number confirmed against the base before the merge (link:../specification/phase-workflows/06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#pre-merge-barrier[Phase Workflows § Pre-Merge Review Barrier & Waivers]).
* *The pre-merge Dependency Delta Check.* From the realized footprint (only when `measured`): the changed build files selected by the build-file dependency parser of the build domains (Maven, Gradle, `package.json`, `pyproject.toml`), parsed at `base_ref` and at `HEAD` over every dependency scope, the added coordinates (a version change is not an addition, the project's own modules are excluded), one blocking `undeclared_dependency` finding of source `dependency_delta` per coordinate the approved dependency set does not name and none for one it names (the matched negative control), no duplicate finding on a repeated run, the triage restricted to `FIX` or an operator waiver `findings:dependency_delta`, `indeterminate` — never clean — where the footprint is not measured, a build file has no parser or a parse fails on either side, the `dependency_delta` result (`clean`, `findings`, `indeterminate` with its counts and causes) in the finalize facts and the merge record, and the removed coordinates left to the self-review (link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#dependency-delta[Phase Workflows § Dependency Delta Check]).
* *The composite wait.* `finalize.concurrent-wait` over CI, the analysis providers and the review bots: one joint observation with its closed per-bot status enumerations, the deterministic evaluation and immediate short-circuit matrix, the constituent outcomes (`all_green`, `watch_interrupted{constituent, fate}`, `cancelled`), and the ordered remediation (CI, analysis, review findings) with the wait region re-fired for every class whose evidence is stale at the new `HEAD` (link:../specification/hypermedia-format/03-composite-wait-and-encoder.adoc#_deterministic_evaluation_immediate_short_circuit_matrix[Hypermedia Format § Deterministic Evaluation & Immediate Short-Circuit Matrix]).
* *Kept, or owned elsewhere.* `FinalizeStepDescriptor`, `FinalizePipelineValidator` and `PipelineRule` are present (the descriptor and the pipeline checks over it) and are extended here, never rewritten; the TOON encoder of the same part is implemented (`core.toon.ToonEncoder`, `ToonEncoderTest`) and stays as it is. Left out: the step bands, the step catalogue and the finalize callers (link:../specification/phase-workflows/04-finalize-step-bands.adoc[Phase Workflows § Finalize Step Ordering Bands], package link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the automatic review and the post-run metrics (link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#automatic-review[Phase Workflows § Automatic Review], packages link:token-usage.adoc[`token-usage`] and link:operator-reports.adoc[`operator-reports`]); the change map's own delta rendering and pull-request section (link:change-map.adoc[`change-map`], blocked); the re-planning path and the detection of its triggers (package link:re-planning.adoc[`re-planning`]).

== Done When

A `headDependent` step that records `done` without a valid `head_at_completion` is refused with `missing_head_at_completion` and the prior record re-fires `unverified`; a `reviewed_commit_sha`, `commit_sha`, `branch_pushed_at.head_sha` or `pr_number` supplied by a link is refused; every `CurrencyVerdict` value has its case (same head, unaffected inputs, missing anchor, rebase advanced, unscoped head change, modified declared inputs), with one `input_scope` fixture per scope and a descriptor missing `input_scope` refused by `FinalizePipelineValidator`.

A step's firing completes exactly once, `UNSETTLED` emits no completion, a `RE_FIRE` sets `unverified` before the step runs, the firings per cause and the productive/unproductive split are derived, and Phase-Exit Completeness refuses an entry into a higher order while a lower-order `done` is stale and never selects `branch-cleanup` at `order = 70` while one is unmet; no verb skips or force-completes a step.

Each merge-path state answers its outcome set with the lease held only while no wait is open: a lease job ended other than in success re-enters the lease job within the condition budget and a spent budget hands back; a rebase runs only where `branch_up_to_date_required` holds or the base changed in the footprint; a lease-rejected push and a rebase conflict call `remediation.merge-conflict` and a clean server rebase resolves without a fix; a moved head re-enters the gate and a merge counts only from `ci.pr-observe`; an `ejected` pull request re-enters the gate, a `behind` or `conflict` refusal marks the fact stale, and a provider protection refusal is not waivable and hands back; each of the four hand-back forms and the `returned` end are shown.

Predicate 1 and Predicate 2 each have a passing and a failing fixture, a `FIX` whose fix did not land still fails, a finding with a provider address and no transmitted response fails, a participation record of another author never satisfies Predicate 2, each currency mode is shown, an absent key fails and an empty list is `vacuous`, a waiver lets the merge proceed only through a logged consumption and never for a security finding, and an unevaluable findings store ends `indeterminate` and hands back, never a pass.

The Dependency Delta Check parses a changed `pom.xml`, Gradle file, `package.json` and `pyproject.toml` at `base_ref` and at `HEAD`; a coordinate the approved outline names files no finding (the matched negative control) and one it does not files exactly one blocking `undeclared_dependency`; a repeated run files none twice; a version change is no addition and a project module is excluded; a file with no parser and a parse failure on either side yield `indeterminate` with a blocking finding; a footprint that is not `measured` yields `indeterminate`; `clean` states `build_files_checked`, including `0`.

The composite wait evaluates its closed per-bot statuses into its short-circuit matrix, a constituent ended `killed`, `timed_out`, `indeterminate`, `refused` or `failed` ends it `watch_interrupted{constituent, fate}`, the restart runs within the watch-restart budget and a spent budget hands back, the ordered remediation runs one class at a time and re-fires the wait region for every class stale at the new head.
