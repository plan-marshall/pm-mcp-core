== What

* *Implements* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/domain-extensions/05-recipes-and-extension-points.adoc#recipe-workflow[Domain Extensions § A Recipe Is a Workflow] with its descriptor schema, and link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-catalogue-recipe[State Catalogue § Recipe Choice `recipe`] (`PM-EXT-4`).
* A recipe is a unit of type `recipe` without entries and exits beside its declarative descriptor: a workflow of phase sub-workflows, its own where it differs and the plan's shared ones (finalize, the landing) elsewhere, its parameters, and whether it takes sources; starting it creates a plan whose process is that workflow; the recipe states of Phases 1 and 3 go. Recipes come from the domains' bundles (built in at build time, a duplicate key failing the build), from the machine (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/recipes/<recipe_id>.json` with its `.pmwf`, offered in every project), and from the project (`.marshall/recipes/`), a project's recipe taking the place of one of the same key; an empty harvest ends the recipe's plan with the composition event and prune reason `empty_harvest`.
* *Through `start.ready`*: a recipe's start passes `start.ready` of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`] like a plan's (the recipe and its parameters, the title and id proposed from the recipe and the time, both changeable); `recipe.create` re-checks what creation needs and returns to `start.ready` with the cause where the recipe, the id, or a gate changed meanwhile, creating nothing. A parameter without an option is shown with its reason and the recipe cannot run; a request-scoped recipe gathers its sources as a plan does, without a chat; leaving keeps the entry with the recipe and its parameters.

== Done When

A recipe runs as its own workflow from its parameters to its landing, an empty harvest ends it without a pull request, and no recipe state is left in the plan's phases; a recipe's start passes `start.ready`, and a change found at `recipe.create` returns there with its cause.
