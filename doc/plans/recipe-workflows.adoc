== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the change A Recipe Is a Workflow into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once UC-STA-5 is agreed.
* A recipe declares a workflow of phase sub-workflows, its own where it differs and the plan's shared ones elsewhere, its parameters, and whether it takes sources; starting it creates a plan whose process is that workflow; the recipe states of Phases 1 and 3 go.
* *Through `start.ready`*: a recipe's start passes `start.ready` of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`] like a plan's (the recipe and its parameters, the title and id proposed from the recipe and the time, both changeable); `recipe.create` re-checks what creation needs and returns to `start.ready` with the cause where the recipe, the id, or a gate changed meanwhile, creating nothing. A parameter without an option is shown with its reason and the recipe cannot run; a request-scoped recipe gathers its sources as a plan does, without a chat; leaving keeps the entry with the recipe and its parameters.
