== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The lesson record and its codec are implemented (link:../Traceability.adoc[Traceability]); the tasks build the store around them.

. *Placement and lock*: one journaled transaction per write under the lesson store lock, with the id allocation: link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-record[Store Schemas § Lesson Record], _Placement_ and _Lock_.
. *The lesson-store digest* and its three integrity states: § Lesson Record, _Lesson-Store Digest_; link:../specification/store-schemas/13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#store-digest-verification[§ Store Digest Verification Against Out-of-Band Edits].
. *The one resolver*, with `present_unreadable` for a file without parseable front matter: § Lesson Record, _One Resolver, Four States_ and _File Format_.
. *Revisions* of a body: § Lesson Record, _Revisions_.
. *Retirement* with its closed verdicts: § Lesson Record, _Retirement_.
. *Claim and release*, and the retirement `applied` on landing: § Lesson Record, _Claim_; link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-claim[§ A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing].

Left out: the `submit` transition that writes a lesson, the audience predicate and the upstream release, the `arch-constraint` recurrence, the lesson maintenance, the retention sweeps, the operator acts on a lesson, the `stalled_lessons` population (no work package yet); the calls of `start.create-scope`, the plan end and the recovery (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`], link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]).
