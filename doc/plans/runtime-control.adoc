== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The drain on a signal, the recovery after it, the version mismatch and the unit file are implemented (the done packages `runtime-lifecycle` and `service-unit`); these tasks add the operator's acts on top.

. *The lifecycle state and the version state* in the runtime status and the release notes: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#runtime-lifecycle-operations[Runtime Lifecycle § Drain, Stop & Upgrade], _Lifecycle state_; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#machine-state-records[Store Schemas § Machine State Records].
. *Stop*, `now` and `drain`: § Drain, Stop & Upgrade, _Drain_ and _Stop_.
. *Withdrawing a drain*: § Drain, Stop & Upgrade, _Stop_.
. *Restart*, detached and under the service unit: § Drain, Stop & Upgrade, _Restart_.
. *Update check* under the consent `check_updates`: § Drain, Stop & Upgrade, _Update check_; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#consent[Store Schemas § Consent Before Acting]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#runtime-lifecycle[Timeouts § Runtime Lifecycle].
. *Upgrade*, its withdrawal, its way back and its settlement at start: § Drain, Stop & Upgrade, _Upgrade_; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#installation-layout[CLI & Security § Installation Layout].
. *The acts* as privileged, audited operator acts announced on the runtime-wide stream: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-channel-isolation[CLI & Security § Operator Channel Isolation]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc[Event Catalogue].

Left out: the registry credential and the form of a release, which block the check and the fetch (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/open-questions.adoc#registry-credential[Open Questions, registry credential]); the start of a stopped runtime and the rendering in both clients (`pm-mcp-clients`).
