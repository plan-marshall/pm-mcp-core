== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The periodic sweep exists as a schedule of steps; this package adds its steps of the target.

. *The two classes*, work history only through the cleanup, technical residue by the sweep with `pm-operator sweep`: link:../specification/timeouts/03-retention-host-config.adoc#cleanup[Timeouts § One Cleanup]; link:../specification/timeouts/03-retention-host-config.adoc#retention[§ Retention & Rotation]; link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#periodic-sweep[Runtime Lifecycle § Periodic Sweep]; link:../specification/store-schemas/13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Store Schemas § Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps].
. *The settings*, machine and project values, due and never due: § One Cleanup; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#machine-config[Store Schemas § Machine Configuration]; link:../specification/store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#local-config[§ Local Configuration].
. *The check* at start and per day, one job and one report `system.cleanup_due`: § One Cleanup; § Periodic Sweep.
. *The journal* with its events, job states and kept list: link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#cleanup-journal[Store Schemas § Cleanup Journal].
. *The decisions* delete, remind and ignore: § One Cleanup.
. *Deleting* per kind with its audit record, the tombstones, the usage aggregate first, resumed after a stop: § One Cleanup; link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout § Plan & Epic Archives]; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Audit Log].
. *Unenrolment* recording `unenrolled_at`: link:../specification/store-schemas/11-enrolment-store.adoc#project-store-identity[Store Schemas § Project Store Identity].
. *The endpoints, verbs and events*: § One Cleanup; operator acts, no workflow unit: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#operator-flows[State Catalogue § Operator Flows That Are Not Workflow Units].
. *Recurring waivers* counted over the audit log: § One Cleanup.

Left out: the pending entry records by age (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`]); the report journal and its kind (link:operator-reports.adoc[`operator-reports`]); the system view's figures (link:runtime-metrics.adoc[`runtime-metrics`]); the machine's and the project's settings (link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`], link:project-onboarding-unit.adoc[`project-onboarding-unit`]); the audit trail and the entity addressing (`audit-trail`, `entity-addressing`, done).
