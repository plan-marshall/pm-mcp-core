== What

* *The record and its shapes.* `grants.jsonl` holds one `AccessGrantRecord` per line: `format_version`, exactly one target of `project_id` (with `read` or `read-write`), `org` (read only) or `repo` (read only, with the forge's `host` where it is not the project's provider), the operator's `reason`, and the operator object with its channel and `os_principal` (and the `device_id` of a paired device); `additionalProperties: false` and the `oneOf` that makes the three target shapes (`project_id`, `org`, `repo`) exclusive (link:../specification/store-schemas/15-screening-and-grants.adoc#grant-record[Store Schemas § Access Grant Record]).
* *The three stores and the move.* A plan's grant to `plans/<plan_id>/grants.jsonl`; a source's grant given once for that start to the pending entry's `pending/<entry_id>/grants.jsonl`, which moves into the plan's store at `start.create-scope`; a source's grant given for the project to `workspace/grants.jsonl`, which every later start of the project reads (link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc[Filesystem Layout § Per-Project Machine Store]).
* *The operator act and its audit.* A grant exists only through the operator's act in one of the two clients — `pm-operator grant-access <plan_id> <project_id> read|read-write --reason <text>`, or `pm-operator grant-access <plan_id> --org <owner>|--repo <owner>/<name> read --reason <text>`, never a link and never a model request; the runtime executing the act is the record's only writer, every line is mirrored into the audit log as `access_granted`, and the digest sidecar of the scope covers the file (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-grant-access[CLI & Security § Cross-Project Access]).
* *The check.* An access to another enrolled project, to an archived plan of one, to the read-only provider reads of an organisation, group or repository outside the project, or to an issue or pull request of another repository named as a source of a start runs only under a grant for the needed access: without one it is refused `grant_missing`, raised in a plan's step as the `precondition_unmet` condition and held for the operator with the grant as its act — never a model request and never a silent read (link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[State Catalogue § Holds and Their Acts]; link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/01-provider-credentials.adoc#cross-repository-reads[Credentials & Enrolment § Cross-Repository Reads]).
* *What the granted read reports.* The completeness of a provider read (`complete`, or `incomplete` with its bounds `page_cap`, `provider_result_cap`, `not_visible`, never read as "absent"), the rate limit as the outcome `rate_limited` with its `resets_at` and no retry of its own, and the grant of the operation that answered (link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/01-provider-credentials.adoc#cross-repository-reads[Credentials & Enrolment § Cross-Repository Reads]).
* *Kept, or owned elsewhere.* The screen verdict cache in the same part is the screening's, not this package's: it is built by link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`] (the source's batch and its manifest verdict). Left out: the screening cache and the screening batch (`start-source-intake`); the provider reads themselves (the provider packages and `pm-provider-ci`); the operator CLI and its verbs (`plan-marshall-mcp`); the multi-repo epic landing that uses the grant from Milestone 12 on (`epic-resource-and-release`).

== Done When

Each of the three target shapes validates and every mixed or partial shape is refused with the member named; a record with an unknown key is refused; a `read-write` grant naming an `org` or a `repo` is refused while a `project_id` grant admits it; an act naming the plan's own project, a project that is not enrolled, or a plan that has already ended is refused with the reason named, and nothing is written.

A grant written by the operator's act lands in the store the act names (the plan's, the pending entry's, or the project's workspace) and nowhere else; the entry's grant moves into the plan's store in the `start.create-scope` transaction and is absent from the pending entry afterwards; the project's `grants.jsonl` is read by every later start of the project; every line is mirrored as an `access_granted` audit record and the file is covered by the scope's digest sidecar.

An access outside the project without a grant is refused `grant_missing` and raised as `precondition_unmet` held for the operator with the grant as its act, for a cross-project read, for an archived plan of another project and for another repository's issue named as a source; the same access with the grant proceeds; a grant of the one access does not admit another; a write outside the project without a `read-write` grant is refused.

The read-only provider reads of an organisation, group or repository outside the project (`--org`, `--repo`) are refused `grant_missing` without their grant and proceed with it, and each reports its completeness and is never read as absent when incomplete; a rate-limited read ends `rate_limited` with `resets_at` and is not retried by itself.
