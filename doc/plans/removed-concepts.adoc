== What

The specification has no scope session log, no session generation (a generation is a worker's, issued with its job token), no maximum age of a pending entry, and no decision that ends by time. This package removes the code of these concepts that done packages built (link:session-log-and-generations.adoc[`session-log-and-generations`], link:runtime-lifecycle.adoc[`runtime-lifecycle`], link:scenario-replay-harness.adoc[`scenario-replay-harness`]), without a deprecation (pre-1.0).

* *The scope session log*: `ScopeSessionLog`, `ScopeSessionRow`, and `ScopeSessionRowCodec` with their tests (`ScopeSessionLogTest`, `ScopeSessionRowCodecTest`, `ScopeSessionRowSchemaParityTest`), and the member `session` of the pending entry record with its codec field and schema parity (`PendingEntryRecord`, `PendingEntryCodec`); the pending entry record itself stays for link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`], which gives it its directory form.
* *The session generation record*: `SessionGenerationStore`, `SessionGeneration`, `SessionGenerationCodec`, `GenerationCheck`, and `GenerationCause` with their tests (`SessionGenerationStoreTest`, `SessionGenerationCodecTest`, `SessionGenerationSchemaParityTest`); `GenerationId` stays only if the trace matrix finds the worker generation of link:task-queue-and-protocol.adoc[`task-queue-and-protocol`] reusing it, and goes otherwise.
* *The periodic sweep's step of fenced generations*: `FencedSessionGenerationsSweep` with its test, and its registration in `PeriodicSweep`; the sweep step of the pending entry records by age is link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`]'s, and the steps of the target are link:cleanup.adoc[`cleanup`]'s.
* *The operator command of a job refusal*: `JobRefusal.operatorCommand` and the check that ties it to the reason; a `build_config_changed` refusal carries the changed paths and names no operator act (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#operator-command-relay[Hypermedia Format § No Operator Relay]).
* *The scenario directive `expire_question`*: refused by name like every other unknown directive, its runner code and tests removed (no decision ends by time).
* *The documents*: the developer guide of session generations (`pm-core/doc/developer/core-queue.adoc`) and the session log's parts of `core-store.adoc` and `runtime-start.adoc` removed or rewritten, every remaining mention of `pm-mcp enter` and of the entry session in Javadoc and developer guides of pm-core and pm-runtime corrected to the entry a client opens, and the ADRs of `core-queue` and ADR `core-store/009` marked superseded with the reason; in `plan-marshall-documentation` the roadmap items' _Remaining_ parts updated.

== Done When

No class, test, or document of pm-core, pm-runtime, or pm-workflow names the scope session log, a session generation, or the directive `expire_question`; a pending entry record without a session member is written and read; `PeriodicSweep` registers no step of fenced generations; a scenario with `expire_question` is refused by name; a `build_config_changed` refusal carries the changed paths and no operator command; `verify`, `verify -Pcoverage`, and `verify -Pintegration-tests` stay green.
