== What

The specification has no scope session log, no session generation (a generation is a worker's, issued with its job token), no maximum age of a pending entry, and no decision that ends by time. This package removes the code of these concepts that done packages built (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/session-log-and-generations.adoc[`session-log-and-generations`], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/runtime-lifecycle.adoc[`runtime-lifecycle`], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/scenario-replay-harness.adoc[`scenario-replay-harness`]), without a deprecation (pre-1.0).

* *The scope session log*: `ScopeSessionLog`, `ScopeSessionRow`, and `ScopeSessionRowCodec` with their tests (`ScopeSessionLogTest`, `ScopeSessionRowCodecTest`; its schema parity test is already gone with the section it read), and the member `session` of the pending entry record with its codec field and schema parity (`PendingEntryRecord`, `PendingEntryCodec`); the pending entry record itself stays for link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`], which gives it its directory form.
* *The session generation record*: `SessionGenerationStore`, `SessionGeneration`, `SessionGenerationCodec`, `GenerationCheck`, and `GenerationCause` with their tests (`SessionGenerationStoreTest`, `SessionGenerationCodecTest`; its schema parity test is already gone with the section it read); `GenerationId` stays only if the trace matrix finds the worker generation of link:task-queue-and-protocol.adoc[`task-queue-and-protocol`] reusing it, and goes otherwise.
* *The periodic sweep's step of fenced generations*: `FencedSessionGenerationsSweep` with its test, and its registration in `PeriodicSweep`; the sweep step of the pending entry records by age is link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`]'s, and the steps of the target are link:cleanup.adoc[`cleanup`]'s.
* *The audit category `identity`*: no audit record carries an identity a client declared (link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Audit Log]), so `AuditRecord.Category.IDENTITY` and every record written with it go; the scope `entry` the schema added is `start-from-clients`'s, which re-enables `AuditSchemaParityTest.enums`.
* *The operator command of a job refusal*: `JobRefusal.operatorCommand` and the check that ties it to the reason, for every reason that carries one today (`build_config_changed`, `enrolment_tampered`); a refusal carries its reason and details (for `build_config_changed` the changed paths) and names no operator act (link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#operator-command-relay[Hypermedia Format § No Operator Relay]).
* *The scenario directive `expire_question`*: refused by name like every other unknown directive, its runner code and tests removed (no decision ends by time).
* *The documents*: the developer guide of session generations (`pm-core/doc/developer/core-queue.adoc`) and the session log's parts of `core-store.adoc` and `runtime-start.adoc` removed or rewritten, every remaining mention of `pm-mcp enter` and of the entry session in Javadoc and developer guides of pm-core and pm-runtime corrected to the entry a client opens, and the ADRs of `core-queue` and ADR `core-store/009` marked superseded with the reason; in `plan-marshall-documentation` the roadmap items' _Remaining_ parts updated.

== Trace

Requirements stay in `plan-marshall-documentation`; the specification sections and watch documents are those of this repository, or of `plan-marshall-documentation` where a link leads there. They are the starting set; the trace matrix of `traced-implementation` is built from them before code (link:README.adoc#how[How to work a package]).

* Requirements: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/requirements/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-10[PM-ARCH-10] (no pending records and no fenced generations among the residue); link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-20[PM-WF-20] (nothing decided by time); link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/requirements/12-testing.adoc#PM-TEST-4[PM-TEST-4]
* Specification: link:../specification/store-schemas/01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Store Schemas § Pending Entry]; link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#leases-fencing[Model Work § Leases, Generations & Fencing]; link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#periodic-sweep[Runtime Lifecycle § Periodic Sweep]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/07-loop-back-diagrams-verification.adoc#_deterministic_scenario_replay_harness_pm_scenario_pm_test_4[Workflow DSL § Deterministic Scenario Replay Harness]
* Roadmap: the items of the pending entry of a start (Milestone 3), the scenario replay harness (Milestone 3), and drain, startup order, recovery, and sweep (Milestone 2) in link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/roadmap.adoc[Roadmap]

== Done When

No class, test, or document of pm-core, pm-runtime, or pm-workflow names the scope session log, a session generation, or the directive `expire_question`; a pending entry record without a session member is written and read; `PeriodicSweep` registers no step of fenced generations; a scenario with `expire_question` is refused by name; `JobRefusal` has no operator command and no check that ties one to a reason, and no refusal of any reason (`build_config_changed`, `enrolment_tampered` included) carries one, its tests updated; `verify`, `verify -Pcoverage`, and `verify -Pintegration-tests` stay green; no audit category `identity` is left.
