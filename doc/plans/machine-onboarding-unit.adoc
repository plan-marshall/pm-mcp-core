== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The client side is `onboarding-contract` of `pm-mcp-clients`.

. *The onboarding's sub-workflows in the machine scope*, first run, automatic or guided, and a later run of one aspect: link:../specification/state-catalogue/14-machine-onboarding.adoc[State Catalogue § Machine Onboarding]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/19-onboarding-aspects-models-providers.adoc[§ Onboarding Aspects]; link:../specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#machine-scope[Workflow DSL § Machine Scope].
. *The consent store* and the consent state before every action: link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#consent[Store Schemas § Consent Before Acting].
. *The harness scan, the login and model probes, and the verification of an unknown harness* as confined probe jobs, the harness order and `harness.none`: link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#harness-scan[Harness Profiles § Harness Scan], link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#login-probe[§ Login Probe], link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#unknown-harness[§ Unknown Harness], link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#harness-icons[§ Harness Icons].
. *The recorded harness outputs as parsing fixtures, the repeated usage evaluation, and the handoff of a verification stage's usage result to the enrolment*: link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Harness Profiles § Tokens, Not Money]; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#results[Evaluation Reference § Results]; link:../specification/store-schemas/11-enrolment-store.adoc#harness-enrolment-store[Store Schemas § Harness Enrolment Store].
. *The model catalogue, the default and the operator's role assignment, and the steward role*: link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#model-catalogue[Store Schemas § Model Catalogue]; link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-assignment[Model Work § Role Assignment], link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#default-assignment[§ Default Role Assignment], link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#steward-role[§ Steward Role].
. *The credential-store aspect* over `CredentialStoreChoice` and `CredentialStoreMove`: link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/01-provider-credentials.adoc#credential-store-choice[Credentials & Enrolment § Credential Store as an Operator Choice].
. *The machine's provider defaults and their verification*: link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#provider-defaults[Store Schemas § Machine Provider Defaults]; link:../specification/credentials-and-enrolment/01-provider-credentials.adoc#provider-verification[Credentials & Enrolment § Provider Verification].
. *The automatic onboarding* and _change_ on its result: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#auto[CLI & Security § Automatic Onboarding].
. *The machine's settings* (UC-ONB-10): the `settings.*` states of § Machine Onboarding; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#machine-config[Store Schemas § Machine Configuration Store].
. *Concurrent changes of a setting*, for the machine's settings, a project's operator values and a plan's own values: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/01-cli-and-installer.adoc#concurrent-changes[CLI & Security § Concurrent Changes of a Setting].
. *The plan behaviour axes not yet built*: the variants as sub-workflows, their binding at the plan's start, the landing variants in the step list of `phase-6-finalize`, and `overrides.json`: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL § Plan Behaviour Axes], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-overrides[§ Plan Overrides]. The composition event `override`, the prune reasons and the removal of `auto-merge-off` are implemented (link:../Traceability.adoc[Traceability]).
. *The validation of the landing* at `defaults.validate` and where a project is known: § Plan Behaviour Axes; § Onboarding Aspects.

Left out: the documents mode's reader (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`]); the cleanup's reader (link:cleanup.adoc[`cleanup`]); a project's values (link:project-onboarding-unit.adoc[`project-onboarding-unit`]); a plan's settings (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]).
