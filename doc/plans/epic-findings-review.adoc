== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the changes An Epic's Findings and Watches and The Review into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once UC-CTL-3 is agreed.
* The registers of findings and watches in the epic's ledger; `epic.analyze-landing` and `coordinate` adding items; a watch's trigger re-checked; new findings as a `needs_operator` kind.
* The sub-workflow `epic.review`: the planning role's proposal per item; keep, drop, promote (the drafted plan staged under the surface checks), edit (instruct, rewrite, read-back), talk; the remaining proposals accepted at once; the dispositions written and landed in one ledger write.

== Done When

The specification of both changes is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; a landing and an inbox finding each add an item; a review takes every disposition and lands them in one ledger write; no model's change reaches a register without the operator's read-back; a promoted item is a staged row that passed the surface check.
