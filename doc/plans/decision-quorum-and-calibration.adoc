Roadmap item:: _Implement the decision quorum with the decision declarations, the adjudication unit `adjudicate.decisions`, the project-owned `adjudicate-decisions.pmwf`, and the shipped quorum roles_; _Implement the quorum calibration `pm-operator roles qualify --decisions` with its decision calibration records and the shipped calibration of the default declarations_; _Demote a calibrated quorum declaration by the operator's corrections of its automatic decisions_ (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/roadmap.adoc[Roadmap])
Where:: pm-workflow packages `workflow.decision` (the declarations, the quorum rule, the adjudication unit and its calibration) and `workflow.verify` (the compiled bounds of the declarations); pm-core package `core.role` (the decision calibration record and the corpus topics of a listed role)
Status, milestone and dependencies:: in the link:README.adoc#packages[index], the only place that states them

== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The declarations*, their unit declarations, compiled bounds, availability and validation (with the model identity): link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-declarations[Model Work § Decision Declarations], without the form `shadow`.
. *The adjudication unit* `adjudicate.decisions`: its check, vote tasks, end and vote-wait bound: link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#adjudication[Model Work § The Adjudication]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/13-question-adjudication.adoc#state-catalogue-adjudicate[State Catalogue § Unit `adjudicate.decisions`].
. *Blind input and the vote link*: link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#blind-input[Model Work § Blind Input].
. *The decision rule*: link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-rule[Model Work § Decision Rule].
. *The record, the delivery with the votes, and the audit*: link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#adjudication-record[Model Work § Record, Delivery & Audit].
. *The shipped quorum roles* with their skills, configurations and qualification records: § Decision Declarations, _Shipped unit_.
. *The quorum calibration* with its run, measurements, validity, record and shipped calibration: link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#quorum-calibration[Model Work § Quorum Calibration].
. *The decision calibration record and its codec*, the token split included: link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#decision-calibration-record[Store Schemas § Decision Calibration Record].
. *Live corrections*: link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#live-corrections[Model Work § Live Corrections].

Left out: the question records, their delivery and the answers (link:decision-engine.adoc[`decision-engine`]); the default unit files and their drift test (`plan-marshall-mcp` / `default-units-and-drift-tests`; this package tests with a fixture copy of the unit); the unit loader (link:process-designer.adoc[`process-designer`]); the rendering in the clients. Dropped: the judgment vote (`judgment-votes`, link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/plan-marshall-documentation/blob/main/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-39[L-39]), the shadow votes (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/plan-marshall-documentation/blob/main/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-38[L-38]), the unconfirmed decisions left out of the calibration (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/plan-marshall-documentation/blob/main/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-37[L-37]).
