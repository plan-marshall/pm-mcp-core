== What

* *The declarations.* One `decide <decision_kind> -> ask` or `decide <decision_kind> -> quorum(<role>, …, <score>)` line per decision kind of the closed set (`interpretation`, `approach`, `granularity`, `placement`, `test_strategy`, `scope`, `product_behaviour`, `assurance`, `external_fact`), in the header of the project-owned `adjudicate-decisions.pmwf` or of the default unit; a kind without a declaration is `ask`; the asking role votes only where it is listed; the declared `vote_wait_seconds`, `max_false_auto_percent` and `kind_<server question>` values; and the compiled bounds `DeclarationBounds` the static verifier holds them to (link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-declarations[Model Work § Decision Declarations]).
* *The adjudication unit.* `adjudicate.decisions` (the project's unit in a plan, the default unit elsewhere) runs for the scope's new question set before delivery: the check and its first-holding escalation reason (`kind_ask`, `quorum_invalid`, `calibration_failed`, `role_unavailable`, `role_unqualified`, `below_score`), one vote task per listed role that is not the asking role over its own `vote` entity, the decision rule, and the record on every question of the set — its states, links and exits as the state catalogue lists them (link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#adjudication[Model Work § The Adjudication]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/13-question-adjudication.adoc[State Catalogue § Unit `adjudicate.decisions`]).
* *Blind input and the vote.* The entity's facts carry the questions with their options sorted by `branch_key` and the evidence the decision rests on, never the recommendation or another role's answer; one closing `submit:vote` link with one item per question, its answer type `one`, `several` or `value`, and its refusals; no `ask` and no `consult`; a task ended `infeasible` escalates `role_unavailable`; a vote task still open at the vote-wait expiry closes `lost` with `deadline_expired` and counts as no answer (link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#blind-input[Model Work § Blind Input]).
* *The decision rule and the record.* A question is decided on a choice when every listed role voted for the same choice, each at or above the declared score; the set is decided only when every question is; otherwise it escalates with `role_unavailable`, `disagreement` or `below_score`, never counting a missing vote as agreement. The decision record of `question.adjudicate` carries the outcome, the declaration applied with its source (`unit` and its digest, or `compiled`), the measurements against the score and the roles, and every vote with its role, harness, model, choice, confidence, rationale and task; an escalated set is delivered to both clients with the votes and each option's support, and every automatic decision is listed in the pull-request body and the landing record (link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-rule[Model Work § Decision Rule]; link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#adjudication-record[§ Record, Delivery & Audit]).
* *The shipped quorum roles and their artifacts.* The roles `quorum-a` and `quorum-b` with their role skills, their effective configurations and their qualification records, shipped on the host the roadmap names (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/roadmap.adoc[Roadmap], Milestone 8).
* *The quorum calibration.* `pm-operator roles qualify --decisions [--kind <kind>]` over the corpus items tagged `decision-kind:<kind>`: per item every listed role answers in the form of a vote task and in the form of its artifact, the declared rule applies, and the record stores the items graded, the automatic share, the false automatic share and the constant baseline with every item's votes, tokens and time — measurements, never a pass flag. A quorum declaration decides only while its record is valid, at least one item was graded, the false automatic share is at most `max_false_auto_percent` read at every check, and its agreement on the automatically decided items exceeds the constant baseline by the qualification margin (link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#constant-baseline[Model Work § Constant Baseline]); otherwise every question of the kind escalates `calibration_failed`. The record is `qualification/decisions/<declaration-digest>.json`, keyed by the digest over kind, roles with their qualification digests and forms, and the score, bound to the corpus revision; the release ships the calibration of every quorum declaration of the default unit and the setup offers to calibrate the rest under the operator's consent, with `qualification.skip_decisions` (link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#quorum-calibration[Model Work § Quorum Calibration]).
* *The calibration record's tokens.* The decision calibration record gains the token split beside its per-item duration: each item's `total_tokens`, with `tokens_in` and `tokens_out` measured as a pair summing to the total, each a measured value or `unmeasured` (link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#decision-calibration-record[Store Schemas § Decision Calibration Record]). The schema is in the store specification; its codec is owed here, in `core.role`, where the record is owned.
* *Kept, or owned elsewhere.* The question records, their delivery and the answers are `decision-engine`'s and stay so; the judgment endpoint's two requests and the lower-probability vote belong to link:judgment-endpoint.adoc[`judgment-endpoint`] and link:judgment-votes.adoc[`judgment-votes`]. The default unit files of the assembly, `adjudicate-decisions.pmwf` among them, and the state-catalogue drift test over them are link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/default-units-and-drift-tests.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `default-units-and-drift-tests`]'s: this package delivers the semantics the declaration lines and the unit's states name — the check, the vote tasks, the rule, the record — with a fixture copy of the unit in its tests. Left out: the judgment endpoint and its protocol (`judgment-endpoint`); the vote members and their rendering (`judgment-votes`, `decision-engine`); the rendering of the vote in both clients (`pm-mcp-clients`); the unit loader and the designable-unit machinery (`process-designer`).

== Done When

A declaration per kind parses and validates: an unknown kind, a `quorum` of a role that does not exist, a score outside its compiled bounds and a missing `kind_<server question>` are each refused with the line; a kind without a declaration behaves `ask`; the asking role votes only where it is listed and its recommendation reaches no listed role where it is not.

Each escalation reason is shown by a fixture: `kind_ask`, `quorum_invalid`, `calibration_failed`, `role_unavailable`, `role_unqualified` and `below_score`; a vote task's facts carry no recommendation and no other role's answer, the options sorted by `branch_key`; an item with an unknown `branch_key`, a missing item or an answer for an unknown question is refused `invalid_link_args`; a vote that is still open at the vote wait closes `lost` with `deadline_expired` and the set escalates.

Two roles that agree at the score decide the set, and one below the score, one disagreement and one missing vote each escalate with their reason, never counted as agreement; a set with one `ask` kind stays together and is delivered whole; a decided record carries the agreed option, the declaration with its source, every vote and its measurements, and an escalated one carries `choice: null` and its reason; an automatically decided question appears in the pull-request body and the landing record with the roles that voted for it.

A calibration run grades every item of its kind and writes `qualification/decisions/<digest>.json` with the automatic share, the false automatic share and every item's votes, tokens and time; a declaration whose false automatic share is above `max_false_auto_percent`, or whose automatic agreement does not exceed the constant baseline by the margin, escalates `calibration_failed` at once, a changed level applies to the existing record without a re-run, a changed declaration or role qualification digest has no record, a changed corpus revision invalidates it, and a kind without items cannot be calibrated; the identical declaration of the project's unit and of the default unit shares one record; the record writes each item's `total_tokens` with `tokens_in` and `tokens_out` summing to it, keeping `unmeasured` apart from a measured `0`.
