== What

* *Implements* link:../specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#unit-frontmatter[Workflow DSL § Unit Frontmatter], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#unit-types[§ Unit Types], and link:../specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#interface-rules[§ Interface Rules], with the exit contract of link:../specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#sub-workflow-exits[§ Result Guards and the Sub-Workflow Exit Contract] (`PM-IMPL-5`, its statement on the frontmatter). None of it exists: no frontmatter is read, a unit has its `start` and no entries, a `call` names no entry, a call frame records none (`WorkflowStatus.CallFrame`, `ast.CallFrame`; the schema's `entry` is marked not implemented), and the exits of a sub-workflow are derived from its terminals.
* *Its own parser* (link:../implementation-watch/workflow-dsl.adoc#PM-WATCH-DSL-36[PM-WATCH-DSL-36]): the frontmatter is the closed, line-oriented subset of YAML between the two `---` lines that open every `.pmwf` file, read by the parser of `workflow.dsl` and never by a general YAML loader: one `key: value` per line at column 1, the closed keys only (`name`, `description`, `type`, `modification_date`, `version`, `author` exactly once, `signature` at most once), plain or double-quoted values with the escapes `\"` and `\\` only; a tag, an anchor, an alias, a merge key, a flow collection, a block scalar, a comment, a blank or indented line, a control character, and an unknown key are refused with file, line, and column; `entry` and `exit` are repeated per name and kept in order, never collapsed; an `entry_description` stands directly below its `entry` line and an `exit_description` below its `exit` line, at most one each, and a description anywhere else is refused.
* *The fields*: `name` 3 to 100 characters, `description` 1 to 1000, each entry and exit description 1 to 500; `type` from the closed set; an entry an identifier and an exit an outcome id, each unique, an exit never `stepped_out`; `modification_date` a valid calendar date not after the day of validation; `version` in the release version grammar; `author: plan-marshall` refused for a unit outside the binary (a unit of the machine or project level); `signature` carried and not verified. The frontmatter is readable without the body, for the lists that show a unit, and the printer writes it in the order of the field table, so the round trip of a unit holds with its frontmatter.
* *The six types* (`workflow`, `recipe`, `subworkflow`, `system`, `task`, `instance`) checked against the unit's `scope` and the presence of its entries and exits as their table states (interface rule 6); a type whose scope the AST does not have yet (`system` with `scope machine`) is checked once link:operator-wait-and-machine-scope.adoc[`operator-wait-and-machine-scope`] adds the scope.
* *Entries and the call through an entry*: the header line `entry <name> -> <state_id>` binds each declared entry exactly once to a state of the unit, and a unit with entries declares no `start` (rule 1); `call <unit> entry <name> (…)` enters the callee through an entry it declares, the entry omitted only for a callee with exactly one (rule 5); the engine pushes a frame that records the entry and enters the bound state, and the status store writes and reads the frame's `entry`.
* *Exits and stop points*: a unit without exits declares at least one terminal, the resident workspace and machine units excepted; a unit with exits declares every terminal's outcome as an exit (or `stepped_out` on a terminal of that class) and every exit is reached; a landing variant declares one entry per step it holds, no exit, and no terminal; a called unit declares entries and exits, and only a unit with entries is the target of a `call` (rules 2 and 3); reachability from the starting point or from every entry, and every exit reached from an entry (rule 4, with Rule 3); the call routes every declared exit and `stepped_out` (Rule 6). Each violation is a value naming the rule, the file, the line, and the column.
* *The interface a project-owned unit promises*: `INV-GEN-1` compares the type, the entries, the exits, and the entry bindings of a machine's or a project's unit with those of the default unit it replaces, beside its scope, start state, and terminals.
* *Fixtures and existing units*: every test unit of `pm-workflow` gains its frontmatter; the units shipped by `plan-marshall-mcp` get theirs where they are written (link:../../../plan-marshall-mcp/doc/plans/default-units-and-drift-tests.adoc[`plan-marshall-mcp` / `default-units-and-drift-tests`]). The phase clause of a state and the phases as sub-workflows are link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]'s, the units of type `recipe` link:recipe-workflows.adoc[`recipe-workflows`]'s, the designer's validation of a placed unit link:process-designer.adoc[`process-designer`]'s; each builds on this package.

== Done When

A unit with a valid frontmatter parses, prints, and parses again to the same tree; each refused form of the frontmatter (a tag, an anchor, an alias, a merge key, a flow collection, an indented or blank line, an unknown or repeated single key, a misplaced description) has its refusing fixture with file, line, and column, and a unit with three entries keeps three in order; each interface rule and each type row has a refusing fixture and a passing control; a call through a named entry pushes a frame with that entry, which the status store round-trips, and the engine enters the bound state; a project's unit whose interface or type differs from the default unit's fails `INV-GEN-1`, and one outside the binary with the author `plan-marshall` is refused; `verify`, `verify -Ppre-commit`, `verify -Pcoverage`, and `verify -Pintegration-tests` are green.
