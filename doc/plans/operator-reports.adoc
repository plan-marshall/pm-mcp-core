== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The report record* and its identifier: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#reports[Runtime Model § Reports to the Operator]; link:../specification/identifiers/02-registry-stores-to-web.adoc#id-report-id[Identifiers § `report_id`].
. *The closed catalogue* of kinds with their subject texts and `detail`: § Reports to the Operator.
. *The sources*: each kind written once after its server transition committed, an owed report written at the next start: § Reports to the Operator; the transitions are those of the packages named under _Left out_.
. *The store*, the journal with its events, its fold, its ids and the cleanup's rewrite: link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#report-journal[Store Schemas § Report Journal].
. *Read*, per machine with channel and device: § Reports to the Operator; § Report Journal.
. *The endpoints and verbs*, the list, marking read, the event stream, the representation: § Reports to the Operator; link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#representation-kinds[Hypermedia Format § Representation Kinds].
. *The completion report of a plan*, a view of an ended plan from its archive: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#completion-report[Runtime Model § Completion Report].
. *The Tokens section* of the completion report: link:../specification/harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Harness Profiles § Tokens, Not Money]; link:../specification/model-work/09-completion-usage-and-spend.adoc#post-completion-invariants[Model Work § Post-Completion Integrity Invariants].
