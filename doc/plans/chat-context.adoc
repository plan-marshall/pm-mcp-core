== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The chat worker*: one warm generation of the routed role per open chat (fresh per turn for ended work, and where only the fresh form of the routed role qualifies, its task recording `form_downgraded`), bound to the chat, in the subject's working directory, released when idle and at once under slot pressure, recycled and re-primed as a warm worker, a turn above the offer bound re-priming it, read-only by its confinement or, where the platform confines no job, by an enforced tool set: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat-context[State Catalogue § What a Chat Knows]; link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#chat-worker[Model Work § The Chat Worker]; link:../specification/model-work/02-task-protocol.adoc#offer[Model Work § Offer]; link:../specification/job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#_linux_filesystem_confinement_landlock[Job Runtime § Linux Filesystem Confinement].
. *The class `read_only`* declared by the kind `chat-reply`, a routed `writer` role narrowed to the `read_only` tool set instead of refused: link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-class-fit[Model Work § Composition Check]; link:../specification/model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc#job-tool-sets[Model Work § Tool Sets].
. *The pushed core per subject*, rendered from the structured store at each re-prime, with the transcript window, within the chat core budget and the chat transcript window, a long text (a description, an epic's intent) given as its start of at most 1 500 characters with its URI, a list over the budget becoming its count with its URI: § What a Chat Knows; link:../specification/model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc#job-prompt[Model Work § Launch Prompt & Contract Envelope]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#chat-page[Timeouts § Chat Transcript Page and Chat Context].
. *The chat skill* `skill://pm/chat/SKILL.md` in the required skill set of every chat task: link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#chat-skill[Model Work § The Chat Skill]; link:../specification/model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc#skill-delivery[Model Work § Skill Delivery Record & Re-Delivery].
. *The context summary* `context: {knows: [{key, args}], as_of}` on the chat, message keys `chat.knows.*`, rendered by `chat.open` from the store without a worker, a snapshot at its `as_of` of what the role would be given at its first turn, refreshed by every turn: § What a Chat Knows; link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#state-chat.open[`chat.open`].
. *A paused plan's chat*: its `chat-reply` task offered, its worker holding its own slot, a pause `now` leaving its reply: § What a Chat Knows; link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#plan-pause[Runtime Lifecycle § Pausing and Starting].
. *The act named, never performed*: the reference of the kind `act` in the reply, checked against the acts the subject offers: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat-names-acts[State Catalogue § The Role Names an Act].
. *The origin* `origin: {kind: chat, chat_id, turn_id}` of every change a chat leads to: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat-origin[State Catalogue § Origin]; link:../specification/store-schemas/17-re-planning.adoc#replan-record[Store Schemas § Re-Planning Record]; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-drafts[Store Schemas § Epic Drafts]; link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-queue-row[§ Queue Row]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-start.store-description[`start.store-description`]; link:../specification/model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#decision-record-content[Model Work § Decision Log, Content].

Schema changes: `replan-record.schema.json` (`chat` gains `chat_id` and `turn_id`); `decision-record.schema.json` (`after_chat` from a boolean to `null` or `{chat_id, turn_id}`); `decomposition-draft.schema.json`, `review-draft.schema.json`, `register-item.schema.json` (`disposition_origin`, carried through `review.commit`), `queue-row.schema.json`, and the source manifest entry of an entry (`origin`).

The chat's bounds: the chat core budget, the chat transcript window, and the chat idle release keep their values (`6000` tokens, `12000` tokens, `600` s, link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#chat-page[Timeouts § Chat Transcript Page and Chat Context]) as engine constants; they are set again from what is measured once the chat runs on real plans in Milestone 9: the cores rendered, the older turns read by URI, and the gaps between the operator's messages.

Left out: the resources `pm://chat/{chat_id}/turns{?before}`, `pm://entry/{entry_id}/sources[/{source_id}]`, `pm://archive/{plan_id}/record`, and `pm://epic/{epic_id}/record`, their routes and the chat's `context` on the wire (`plan-marshall-mcp`, specified in link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/mcp-tools/03-resource-uris.adoc#chat-resources[MCP Tools § Resource URIs]); the content of the chat skill (`plan-marshall-mcp`); the client contract, the mock runtime, and the clients' line of what the role knows (`pm-mcp-clients`); the narrative and `since_last_turn` (link:chat-narrative.adoc[`chat-narrative`]).
