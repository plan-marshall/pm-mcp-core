== What

The checks link:static-verifier-rule-gaps.adoc[`static-verifier-rule-gaps`] (PLAN-54) left short of the specification (Workflow DSL § Static AST Verification Rules, § Units, Jobs and Decisions, § Remediation Units), and the removal of a check that could never refuse anything:

* *Rule 9, a firing per step.* A firing of a step ends at the next opening of the same step, not at the opening of any step. The duplicate "complete `a`, enter the opening of `b`, complete `a`" is refused; a path that passes the opening of another step before it completes is no longer refused. The cycle measure `STEP_LIST` is checked as specified (every pass of the dispatch hub settles one step), and `ARCHIVE_STEPS` sits on the commit and land path (ADR 021, ADR 022).
* *Rule 6, result guards.* A result guard sits only on the `done` of a call, on no other transition, and only on a call whose callee declares a `result` schema (ADR 023).
* *Rule 6, the remediation budget re-check.* Inside a remediation unit, `stepped_out` may target the state `remediation-<class>.budget-check` that runs `remediation.check-budgets`, besides the targets the scope rule admits; any other target is refused (ADR 024, which closes the seam of ADR 010).
* *Rule 2, `lost` in sub-workflows.* An undeclared `lost` is admitted in a plan unit and in a sub-workflow whose every caller chain through the catalog ends in plan units; a sub-workflow with any other caller, or none, routes `lost` (ADR 025).
* *Rule 3, configuration reachability removed.* The all-keys-true activation of the `depends_on_config` keys, the configuration-reachability check `CONFIG_REACHABILITY` and the advisories of `VerificationResult` are removed. The specification defines no rule by which a `depends_on_config` value activates a guard, and under the Boolean all-keys-true activation the check could refuse nothing (ADR 004, ADR 006 and ADR 008, rewritten). The missing activation rule is filed as the specification issue "define how a `depends_on_config` value activates a guard" in `plan-marshall-documentation`.

Left as a seam, each named by an ADR of `pm-workflow/doc/adr/workflow-verify/`:

* Rule 3: configuration reachability as a whole, the enumeration of the `depends_on_config` values, the rule by which a value activates a guard, and the refusal of a state or transition no combination reaches (ADR 004, ADR 006).
* Rule 4: the leaf-task constraint over the callees of a worker unit, and the recognition of a consultation unit by its identifier, which is a heuristic (ADR 001).
* Rule 11: the 11a measure of an engine-chained cycle that runs inside a callee (ADR 001).
* Rule 6: which of the fallback states `plan.return` and `finalize.hand-back` belongs to the phase of a plan unit's calling state (ADR 001).
* Rule 12: an `escalate` link told by its identifier `request_waiver` rather than by a primitive that files a waiver request (ADR 001).
* Rule 9: whether a `lost` or `infeasible` route that completes a step `skipped` is the declared conservative route of an optional step (ADR 002).
* Rule 1: `each` over a read-only, list-returning primitive and `role each` over a primitive returning role names (ADR 010).
* Rule 6: the guarded field of a result guard is a boolean or optional field of the callee's result schema; `SchemaRegistry` registers names only (ADR 010).

Not in this package, so no scope is duplicated:

* The retirement of the implemented watch items of the Workflow DSL and the roadmap item of the static verifier are made in `plan-marshall-documentation`, in a pull request of their own that this plan opens.
* Everything link:static-verifier-rule-gaps.adoc[`static-verifier-rule-gaps`] § What lists as not in that package stays where it names it.

== Done When

* Every check of § What is implemented to the specification or removed as stated there, and every seam of § What is named by its ADR.
* One refusing fixture with an exact assertion and one matched control per new check; no assertion weakened.
* `VerificationRule` has no `CONFIG_REACHABILITY` and `VerificationResult` carries violations only.
* No ADR contradicts the specification without a specification pull request or issue.
* `verify`, `verify -Ppre-commit`, `verify -Pcoverage` and `verify -Pintegration-tests` are green on the pushed head.
