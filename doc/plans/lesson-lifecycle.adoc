== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. The lesson store with its id allocation, resolver, revision, retirement, claim, release and end of a claim is implemented (link:../Traceability.adoc[Traceability]); the tasks build the rest of the lesson's lifecycle around it.

. *The `submit:lesson` transition and its Q-Gate*, the only writer that creates a lesson: link:../specification/phase-workflows.adoc[Phase Workflows Specification], Lesson Creation & Body; link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-record[Store Schemas § Lesson Record], _File Format_, _Version Provenance_, _Provenance_, _Derived from Untrusted Text_ and _Remedy_.
. *The audience classification and the upstream release* with its rendering, duplicate detection and `promotions[]` entry: § Lesson Record, _Audience Classification_, _Upstream Release_ and _Promotion Provenance_.
. *The `arch-constraint` recurrence* and its quiet-window retirement: § Lesson Record, _arch-constraint Recurrence_.
. *The lesson maintenance submits* of `lessons.pmwf`: § Lesson Record, _Lesson Maintenance_.
. *The retention sweep* of a retired lesson's body: § Lesson Record, _Retirement_.
. *The operator acts on a lesson*: `accept-edit` (§ Lesson Record, _Lesson-Store Digest_), the retirement of a `present_unreadable` lesson (§ Lesson Record, _Retirement_) and the release of a stale claim (§ Lesson Record, _Claim_).
. *The `stalled_lessons` population*: § Lesson Record, _Claim_.

Left out: the calls of `start.create-scope`, the plan end and the recovery (link:start-source-intake.adoc[`start-source-intake`], link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]), which call the `LessonStore` this repository ships.
