== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The protected-path decision at the commit check*: the server asks the operator one question over the diff of exactly the touched protected paths, `accept` or `refuse`, and a `refuse` leads to a `fix` task; the family of protected paths is unchanged: link:../specification/phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#protected-paths[Phase Workflows § Protected Paths].

Left out: the main-checkout clause made conditional on confinement (link:../specification/job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#_linux_filesystem_confinement_landlock[Job Runtime § Linux Filesystem Confinement]) and the lift removal of a declared path (§ Protected Paths), which build nothing of their own here: their code is the main-checkout guard of the commit primitive in `pm-provider-git` (`FootprintBoundary` and `CommitSite`, documented in `provider-git-primitive.adoc` and `pm-provider-git` ADR `provider-git-primitive/006`) and the protected-path matcher `ProtectedPaths` in `pm-core`.
