== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The registry and its seam* over Micrometer: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/03-runtime-metrics.adoc#runtime-metrics[Runtime Lifecycle § The Runtime's Metrics], the opening paragraph.
. *The closed catalogue*, each metric recorded by the code its table row names: § The Runtime's Metrics, the table; the disk-use step of link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#periodic-sweep[§ Periodic Sweep]. Among its rows the metric `work.waits`, a count kept by the wait registrations whose label `kind` is the closed enum of the thirteen wait reasons of link:../specification/job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#_bounded_long_poll_progress_reset_waiting_primitives_pm_wf_8_pm_impl_4[Job Runtime § Wait Attribution] (`build`, `git`, `ci`, `model_job`, `review_bot_window`, `operator_wait`, `paused`, `merge_queue`, `merge_mutex`, `server_retry`, `provider_rate_limit`, `prerequisite`, `integrity_hold`). A span the machine slept is attributed as a pause in the wait slices, as the specification states (link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#clock-gap[Runtime Lifecycle § Clock Gap]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#clock-gap-threshold[Timeouts § Clock Gap Threshold]; link:../implementation-watch/runtime-lifecycle.adoc#PM-WATCH-LIFE-4[PM-WATCH-LIFE-4]). Open: whether `paused` is widened to cover a sleep span or the span gets a wait reason of its own, since Wait Attribution defines `paused` as the operator's pause alone (link:../specification/job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#_bounded_long_poll_progress_reset_waiting_primitives_pm_wf_8_pm_impl_4[Job Runtime § Wait Attribution]; `pm-core/src/main/resources/de/planmarshall/core/store/metrics.schema.json:123`). Open: the metrics library of the registry, whose dependency the section names and which needs the operator's word before it is added.
. *The gauges and hourly windows set at start*: § The Runtime's Metrics, _Where they come from_.
. *The endpoints and the stream*: § The Runtime's Metrics, _Local API_; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#metrics-event-interval[Timeouts § Metrics Event Interval].
. *No export of its own*: the metrics are read only through the local API, with no listener and no export route of the runtime for them: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/03-runtime-metrics.adoc#exposition[§ The Runtime's Metrics, _Exposition_]; the OpenMetrics text format is not part of this package (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-86[Later Improvements, L-86]).

Left out: the usage aggregate (link:cleanup.adoc[`cleanup`]); the addressing of the entities a figure links to (the done package `entity-addressing`); `pm-operator system` and the system view (`pm-mcp-clients`).
