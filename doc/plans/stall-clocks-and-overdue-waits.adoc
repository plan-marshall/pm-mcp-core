== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. Built today: the hold `stalled`, derived on read with one `plan_stalled` record per episode (`StalledScopeDetector`), its threshold in `stalled-scope.properties`, and the periodic sweep (`PeriodicSweep`). The tasks run in the order 1, then 2, then 3 and 4. `audit-record.schema.json` needs no change for any of them: `event` is a free string and `details` a free object.

. *The hold record's engine side*: the hold `stalled` carries `awaited` where the scope awaits something (`kind`, the id of the job, task, wait or lease, `since`, the bound or the expected duration, the last observed change), the scope's position (`state`, `call_path`, `instance_version`; `StalledHold` carries `state` today, `StalledScopeDetector.java:145`, and `AuditEvent.planStalled` writes it, so only `call_path` and `instance_version` are new) and the correlation ids that apply (`transaction_id`, `job_id`, `queue_task_id`, `generation_id`), on `StalledHold` and in the `plan_stalled` record: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[State Catalogue § Holds and Their Acts], _Record_; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Security & Transition Audit Log], _Stall and overdue records_. Module `pm-runtime` and `pm-core`, paths `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/StalledScopeDetector.java`, `pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/service/AuditEvent.java` (`planStalled`); extends `StalledScopeDetectorTest`, `StalledScopeDetectorRecordsTest` and `AuditEventTest`. Open: which store records a CI wait's expected duration and last observed change, and the `Retry-After` deferral, since no store member defines them.
. *The clocks on awake time*: the runtime reads two monotonic clocks, one that counts while the machine sleeps and one that does not (on Linux `CLOCK_BOOTTIME` against `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, on macOS `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` against `CLOCK_UPTIME_RAW`), keeps every sleep span whose divergence over one observation interval passes the clock gap threshold of 30 seconds, and excludes the spans from the clocks of `stalled`, `overdue` and the heartbeats, so no scope becomes stalled or overdue through a sleep and a hold already open stays open; the wall clock is never the signal: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#clock-gap[Runtime Lifecycle § Clock Gap]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#clock-gap-threshold[Timeouts § Clock Gap Threshold]. `StalledScopeDetector` reads the idle span from wall-clock instants today. Module `pm-runtime`, path `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/StalledScopeDetector.java`; extends `StalledScopeDetectorTest`. The specification answers where the span is attributed: each sleep span is attributed as a pause in the wait slices of the metrics (link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#clock-gap[Runtime Lifecycle § Clock Gap]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#clock-gap-threshold[Timeouts § Clock Gap Threshold]; the Clock-gap tests; link:../implementation-watch/runtime-lifecycle.adoc#PM-WATCH-LIFE-4[PM-WATCH-LIFE-4]). Open: whether `paused` is widened to cover a sleep span or the span gets a wait reason of its own, since Wait Attribution defines `paused` as the operator's pause alone (link:../specification/job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#_bounded_long_poll_progress_reset_waiting_primitives_pm_wf_8_pm_impl_4[Job Runtime § Wait Attribution]; `pm-core/src/main/resources/de/planmarshall/core/store/metrics.schema.json:123`).
. *The hold `overdue`*: a scope resting in a stall-watched wait is `overdue` after no observed change for the overdue factor (3) times the expected duration recorded with the wait, cause `no_change`, or after the wait lasted the long-wait factor (10) times it, cause `long_running`, time a provider deferred counting; derived on read, never stored, the route unchanged; one `plan_overdue` record per episode with the scope, the wait and what it awaits; never shown for a paused or pausing plan, an operator wait or an integrity hold, and never in a model-facing representation: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#overdue-waits[Runtime Lifecycle § Overdue Waits]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#overdue-factor[Timeouts § Overdue Factor]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#long-wait-factor[§ Long-Wait Factor]; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Security & Transition Audit Log]. `stalled-scope.properties` holds the stalled threshold alone today. Module `pm-runtime` and `pm-core`, paths `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/`, `pm-runtime/src/main/resources/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/stalled-scope.properties`, `pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/service/AuditEvent.java`; extends `StalledScopeDetectorTest`, `StalledScopeDetectorRecordsTest` and `AuditEventTest`. Open: which store records a CI wait's expected duration and last observed change, and the `Retry-After` deferral, since no store member defines them.
. *The runtime's self-watch*: the drive loop and the periodic sweep each stamp an in-memory heartbeat on every pass and are judged by their own stamps alone; a watchdog thread compares each with the heartbeat threshold (60 seconds) and writes one thread dump of the runtime to the runtime log per episode of a late heartbeat, stops and restarts nothing, and does not count time the machine slept: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#runtime-self-watch[Runtime Lifecycle § Runtime Self-Watch]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#heartbeat-threshold[Timeouts § Heartbeat Threshold]. No heartbeat stamp is built yet: a search of `pm-runtime/src/main` finds none beside the workers' own calls (`WorkerCalls.callHeartbeat`, `SupervisorCalls`), so `PeriodicSweep` is the one place a stamp can be taken; the drive loop is the assembly's, so its stamp is a seam this task declares. Module `pm-runtime`, paths `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/PeriodicSweep.java` and `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/`; extends `PeriodicSweepTest`. SPECIFICATION DRIFT TO REPORT: the heartbeat threshold is 60 seconds (link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#heartbeat-threshold[Timeouts § Heartbeat Threshold]) and the sweep runs every 3600 seconds (`PeriodicSweep.java:50`; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#periodic-sweep-interval[§ Periodic Sweep Interval]), so a heartbeat stamped once per pass and judged by its own stamps is late 59 minutes of every 60. Which cadence reconciles the two is an open question of the specification's owner (link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#runtime-self-watch[Runtime Lifecycle § Runtime Self-Watch]); the requirement stands as the specification states it, and this package names no owner.

Left out: the acts of the holds and their rendering in both clients (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]); the status report "not responding" (link:runtime-control.adoc[`runtime-control`]); the stall-watched waits of the finalize pipeline themselves (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]); the metric `work.waits` (link:runtime-metrics.adoc[`runtime-metrics`]); the bound of each awaiting primitive (link:awaiting-bounds.adoc[`awaiting-bounds`]). Open: which store records a CI wait's expected duration and last observed change, and the `Retry-After` deferral, since no store member defines them.
