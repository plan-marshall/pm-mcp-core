== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. Built today: the hold `stalled`, derived on read with one `plan_stalled` record per episode (`StalledScopeDetector`), its threshold in `stalled-scope.properties`, and the periodic sweep (`PeriodicSweep`). The tasks run in the order 1, then 2, then 3 and 4. `audit-record.schema.json` needs no change for any of them: `event` is a free string and `details` a free object.

. *The hold record's engine side*: the hold `stalled` carries `awaited` where the scope awaits something (`kind`, the id of the job, task, wait or lease, `since`, the bound or the expected duration, the last observed change), the scope's position (`state`, `call_path`, `instance_version`) and the correlation ids that apply (`transaction_id`, `job_id`, `queue_task_id`, `generation_id`), on `StalledHold` and in the `plan_stalled` record: link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[State Catalogue § Holds and Their Acts], _Record_; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Security & Transition Audit Log], _Stall and overdue records_. Module `pm-runtime` and `pm-core`, paths `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/StalledScopeDetector.java`, `pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/service/AuditEvent.java` (`planStalled`); extends `StalledScopeDetectorTest`, `StalledScopeDetectorRecordsTest` and `AuditEventTest`.
. *The clocks on awake time*: the runtime reads two monotonic clocks, one that counts while the machine sleeps and one that does not (on Linux `CLOCK_BOOTTIME` against `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, on macOS `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` against `CLOCK_UPTIME_RAW`), keeps every sleep span whose divergence over one observation interval passes the clock gap threshold of 30 seconds, and excludes the spans from the clocks of `stalled`, `overdue` and the heartbeats, so no scope becomes stalled or overdue through a sleep and a hold already open stays open; the wall clock is never the signal: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#clock-gap[Runtime Lifecycle § Clock Gap]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#clock-gap-threshold[Timeouts § Clock Gap Threshold]. `StalledScopeDetector` reads the idle span from wall-clock instants today. Module `pm-runtime`, path `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/StalledScopeDetector.java`; extends `StalledScopeDetectorTest`. Open: under which wait reason a sleep span is attributed in the wait slices, since `paused` names the operator's pause alone.
. *The hold `overdue`*: a scope resting in a stall-watched wait is `overdue` after no observed change for the overdue factor (3) times the expected duration recorded with the wait, cause `no_change`, or after the wait lasted the long-wait factor (10) times it, cause `long_running`, time a provider deferred counting; derived on read, never stored, the route unchanged; one `plan_overdue` record per episode with the scope, the wait and what it awaits; never shown for a paused or pausing plan, an operator wait or an integrity hold, and never in a model-facing representation: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#overdue-waits[Runtime Lifecycle § Overdue Waits]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#overdue-factor[Timeouts § Overdue Factor]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#long-wait-factor[§ Long-Wait Factor]; link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas § Security & Transition Audit Log]. `stalled-scope.properties` holds the stalled threshold alone today. Module `pm-runtime` and `pm-core`, paths `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/`, `pm-runtime/src/main/resources/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/stalled-scope.properties`, `pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/service/AuditEvent.java`; extends `StalledScopeDetectorTest`, `StalledScopeDetectorRecordsTest` and `AuditEventTest`.
. *The runtime's self-watch*: the drive loop and the periodic sweep each stamp an in-memory heartbeat on every pass and are judged by their own stamps alone; a watchdog thread compares each with the heartbeat threshold (60 seconds) and writes one thread dump of the runtime to the runtime log per episode of a late heartbeat, stops and restarts nothing, and does not count time the machine slept: link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#runtime-self-watch[Runtime Lifecycle § Runtime Self-Watch]; link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#heartbeat-threshold[Timeouts § Heartbeat Threshold]. `PeriodicSweep` is the one stamp point built; the drive loop is the assembly's, so its stamp is a seam this task declares. Module `pm-runtime`, paths `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/PeriodicSweep.java` and `pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/start/`; extends `PeriodicSweepTest`.

Left out: the acts of the holds and their rendering in both clients (link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]); the status report "not responding" (link:runtime-control.adoc[`runtime-control`]); the stall-watched waits of the finalize pipeline themselves (link:finalize-pipeline-and-merge.adoc[`finalize-pipeline-and-merge`]); the metric `work.waits` (link:runtime-metrics.adoc[`runtime-metrics`]); the bound of each awaiting primitive (link:awaiting-bounds.adoc[`awaiting-bounds`]). Open: which store records a CI wait's expected duration and last observed change, and the `Retry-After` deferral, since no store member defines them.
