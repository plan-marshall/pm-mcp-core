== What

This package implements link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#source-directory[Filesystem Layout § The Source Directory]; link:../specification/store-schemas/01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Store Schemas § Pending Entry]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#documents-source[State Catalogue § Documents as a Source]; link:../specification/model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[Model Work § What Is on This Machine Is Trusted] and link:../specification/model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#screen-stored[§ Screened Once Stored]; link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-claim[Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing]; the entry's address in link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[Runtime Model § Events per Scope] (`PM-WF-10`, `PM-SEC-1`). It builds on the setup gate, the source units, and the other sources of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].

* *Rate limits*: a fetch of an issue or a pull request that the forge refuses for its rate limit, with or without a credential, fails the source with the cause `rate_limited` and `resets_at` from the forge's answer; it is not retried by itself, and the operator tries again or adds a credential for the forge. It builds on the source units `source-issue` and of the pull request of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].
* *Documents, copy or move*: the machine key `sources.documents_mode` (`copy`, `move`, or `ask`, the default); with `ask` the operator wait `source-documents.mode` with _copy_ preselected, where _always_ stores `copy` or `move` as the machine's value; otherwise straight to `source-documents.store`. A move removes a file git tracks from git through the short landing `git.land-removal`, landed by the project's landing (`landing.pull-request` or `landing.main`); with `landing.files` the removal stays uncommitted in the working tree. It builds on the source unit `source-documents` of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].
* *An entry never expires*: a pending entry has no time limit (link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#pending-entry-max-age[Timeouts § Pending Entry]); it stays among the project's open entries, offered to continue in both clients, until it is started or abandoned, or deleted with the release of its project. The pending entry record's maximum age in the code (24 hours, `PENDING_ENTRY_MAX_AGE` of `PeriodicSweep`) and the sweep step `PendingEntryRecordsSweep` that removes older records go; the sweep removes only the directory of an entry already started or abandoned whose removal a stop cut off, by that evidence (link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#periodic-sweep[Runtime Lifecycle § Periodic Sweep]).
* *The entry as a directory*: `pending/<entry_id>/` with `entry.json` (the record of its schema), `sources/` with its manifest `sources.json`, `questions.jsonl` and `decisions.jsonl` (the entry's decisions and their records), and `chat/` (the transcript), created in one workspace transaction when the entry is opened; it replaces the file form `pending/<entry_id>.json` of `PendingEntryStore`, `EntryId`, `PendingEntryReader`, and its codec. A digest mismatch of an entry pauses only that entry (`integrity_breach`, kind `store_tampered`, a hold of kind `integrity_pause`), ended by `accept-edit workspace pending/<entry_id>/entry.json` or by abandoning the entry, no other entry and no workspace workflow affected.
* *The source directory and the move at creation*: `start.create-scope` moves the source directory into `plans/<plan_id>/sources/` or the epic's ledger, the transcript into `plans/<plan_id>/chat/` or `epics/<epic_id>/chat/`, never into a ledger, and the entry's decision stores into the new scope's question and decision stores, deleting the entry's directory last; the manifest; `origin` naming the sources; the epic header listing them; `request.md` composed from them; `epic.decompose` reading them. It builds on the steps `start.open`, `start-epic.open`, and `start.create-scope` and on the source units of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`] (`source-documents`, `source-issue`, `source-lesson.*`, `source-local-changes.*`).
* *Screened once stored*: fetch, level-1 validator, store, screener over the stored copy, the verdict in the manifest; nothing but the screener reads an external source before a clean verdict or the operator's acceptance; the operator's text and the documents on the machine are never screened. It builds on the source units of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].
* *Fail closed*: a screener that fails, or a role `screener` with no qualified configuration, leaves the source unscreened with the cause in the operator wait `source-<kind>.unscreened` (`source-issue.unscreened`, `source-pr.unscreened`): try again, remove it, or accept it explicitly as untrusted, recorded with its reason like an accepted flagged source; a failure is never shown as `suspicious`. It builds on the source units of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].
* *A claimed lesson ends with the landing*: the lesson of a source is claimed at the plan's creation and retired when the plan lands its changes (the merge of `landing.pull-request`, the push of `landing.main`; verdict `applied`, `by` the plan), released when it ends without landing them (handed back with `landing.files`, cancelled, abandoned, released, aborted, deleted), the lesson staying `active`. It builds on the lesson source `source-lesson.*` of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].
* *An epic's sources in its ledger* (`.marshall/epics/<epic_id>/sources/`, committed and landed), so that adoption and every decomposition read them.
* *Entries addressed like plans and epics*: created under their project (`POST /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/entries`, the kind `plan` or `epic`), addressed at the top level by id (`/api/v1/entries/{entry_id}`, its events at `/api/v1/entries/{entry_id}/events`); `POST /api/v1/entries` goes.
* *Loopback only*: the listing of files, a move, and documents outside the checkout only from the terminal client and the loopback listener.
* *Schema parity*: `PendingEntrySchemaParityTest` reads the schema of the entry's directory form and is re-enabled, and `AuditSchemaParityTest.enums` is already enabled and green with the scope `entry` of the audit record (the category `identity` and the entry row of the pending entry record are gone).

== Done When

An entry is opened as its directory, and its creation moves the sources, the transcript, and the decision stores into the new scope and deletes the directory last; a tampered entry pauses only that entry until its edit is accepted or it is abandoned; each stored source has its manifest entry; no model but the screener reads an external source before its verdict, shown by a test that fails the screening and one whose screener fails or has no qualified configuration, leaving the source unscreened until tried again, removed, or accepted as untrusted; a rate-limited fetch of an issue and of a pull request fails with `rate_limited` and `resets_at` and is not retried; a pending entry older than a day is still offered and no sweep removes it, while the directory of an entry already started or abandoned whose removal a stop cut off is removed by the sweep; documents are asked copy or move only with `ask`; a claimed lesson is retired on a merge and on a push to the base branch and released on every other end; `PendingEntrySchemaParityTest` and `AuditSchemaParityTest.enums` run without `@Disabled` and pass.
