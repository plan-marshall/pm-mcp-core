== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds. Each task builds on the setup gate, the source units and the other sources of link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`].

. *The entry as a directory*, replacing the file form of `PendingEntryStore`, `EntryId`, `PendingEntryReader` and its codec, with its integrity pause: link:../specification/store-schemas/01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Store Schemas § Pending Entry].
. *An entry never expires*: the code's maximum age and the sweep step that removes older records go; the sweep keeps only the removal of a cut-off directory: link:../specification/timeouts/02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#pending-entry-max-age[Timeouts § Pending Entry]; link:../specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#periodic-sweep[Runtime Lifecycle § Periodic Sweep].
. *The source directory and the move at creation*, the manifest, `origin`, the epic header, `request.md` and `epic.decompose` reading them: link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#source-directory[Filesystem Layout § The Source Directory].
. *An epic's sources in its ledger*: § The Source Directory.
. *Rate limits of a fetched source*: link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#issues[State Catalogue § Issues by Number or URL].
. *Documents, copy or move*, and the short landing of a removal: link:../specification/state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#documents-source[State Catalogue § Documents as a Source].
. *Screened once stored*, and nothing screened that is on this machine: link:../specification/model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#screen-stored[Model Work § Screened Once Stored]; link:../specification/model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[§ What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]; link:../specification/model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#two-level-guard[§ Two-Level Guard].
. *Fail closed*, the wait `source-<kind>.unscreened`: § Screened Once Stored.
. *A claimed lesson ends with the landing*: link:../specification/store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#lesson-claim[Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing].
. *Entries addressed like plans and epics*, `POST /api/v1/entries` removed: link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[Runtime Model § Events per Scope].
. *Loopback only* for the listing of files, a move, and documents outside the checkout: § Documents as a Source.
. *Schema parity*: `PendingEntrySchemaParityTest` re-enabled against the directory form.

Left out: the source units, the setup gate and the other sources (link:start-from-clients.adoc[`start-from-clients`]); the lesson store (link:lesson-store.adoc[`lesson-store`]).
