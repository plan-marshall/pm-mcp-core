== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *Scenarios of the supervision fault matrix*: worker faults, recycling, the start-failure guard, consultation, fencing, a runtime crash with a leased task: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/07-loop-back-diagrams-verification.adoc#_deterministic_scenario_replay_harness_pm_scenario_pm_test_4[Workflow DSL § Deterministic Scenario Replay Harness]; link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc[Model Work § Supervision & Recycling].
. *Scenarios of a worker off track*, one per signal and per action, from a scripted output stream: link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[Model Work § A Worker Off Track].
. *Scenarios of a pause at once with a leased task and of a missing answer acknowledgement*: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#continuation[Model Work § Continuing after a Question].
. *Operator-act directives* the replay harness lacks, as the trace matrix names them: § Deterministic Scenario Replay Harness.
. *The runs and baselines of the behavioural conformance harness*, its scenario records and its rule selection: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/07-loop-back-diagrams-verification.adoc#behavioural-conformance-harness[Workflow DSL § Behavioural Conformance Harness].

Left out: the activity and the signals themselves (link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]); the continuation (link:question-continuation.adoc[`question-continuation`]); the same faults against real processes (`plan-marshall-mcp` / `worker-integration-tests`). Dropped: the comparison footprint, a method of the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#comparison-footprint[Evaluation Reference] that defines nothing, with its navigation figures (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/plan-marshall-documentation/blob/main/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-40[L-40]).
