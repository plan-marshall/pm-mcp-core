== Discussion

This section is the package's first task, worked with the operator before any implementation. Its outcome is a decision on the design of the lane-route pruning, recorded as an ADR of `pm-workflow/doc/adr/workflow-plan/` that supersedes or extends ADR 011, and the tasks of § What that it confirms adjusted to it.

=== Current design

* The `outline` event composes a version like every other event: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/plan-instance.adoc[`plan-instance`] evaluates the composition rules over the plan facts, prunes and binds, and verifies the version (Rules 1-12, the finalize pipeline, the compiled invariants). No rule reads the lane to prune a route, so a `deep` instance keeps the `light` route and a `light` instance the `deep` one; the lane is recorded in the header (`lane`).
* The default plan unit routes both lanes through shared states that read the plan's lane:
** `init.light-gate` routes `pass` into the light lane (`outline.detect-change-type` and on to `outline.light`) and its budget, `neighbours_indeterminate` and `baseline_conflict` outcomes to `refine.baseline-reconcile`, the one-way ratchet to `deep`.
** `outline.route-rework` is a router over `planning_lane`: `deep` -> `outline.deep`, `light` -> `outline.light-regate`. It is entered from the deep route (`outline.qgate` `blocking` through `outline.remediate-qgate` `fix_requested`), from the join's `findings_pending` path (`outline.gate` through `outline.remediate-qgate`), and from the four replan entries `plan.revise-tasks`, `outline.route-review`, `execute.replan` and `finalize.replan`.
* Pruning removes a state and every transition into it; it never re-routes the outcome that led there.
* Why pruning cannot remove the light route: in a `deep` plan the router's `light` outcome is never taken, but the graph cannot know that. Plain reachability from the deep entry reaches `outline.light-regate` and `outline.light` through `outline.route-rework`, so it prunes nothing; a rule that prunes them anyway leaves the router's `light` outcome and `init.light-gate`'s `pass` unrouted.

=== Limitations

* *Rule 2.* An outcome must be routed. Pruning `outline.light` and `outline.light-regate` in a `deep` version leaves `init.light-gate` `pass` and `outline.route-rework` `light` unrouted; the composer refuses that version with `invariant_failed`. This was built and refused in link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/plan-instance.adoc[`plan-instance`] (ADR 011, option C).
* *`INV-INIT-2`.* Its clause "the `light -> deep` ratchet transitions of the default unit are present" demands the transitions of `init.light-gate` and `outline.light-regate`, so any version without the light gates fails it, whatever its lane.
* *No engine handling.* The engine has no notion of a lane-specific route or of a version that drops states the plan may still escalate through; a symmetric prune of the deep route of a `light` plan would remove the escalation target, and a new composition event for the escalation does not exist.
* *No vocabulary.* The DSL marks no state with a lane, and the closed `prune_reason` enumeration has no reason for the other lane.

=== Next steps

* *The operator's model.* Escalation is an ordinary outcome of the light lane: a `light` plan's route contains the escalation into `deep`, a `deep` plan's route contains nothing of `light`.
* *Per-lane routes in the default plan unit.* Replace the shared lane-reading routers by per-lane rework and replan routes in the State Catalogue and in the plan templates of `plan-marshall-mcp`: the deep route re-enters `outline.deep` directly, the light route `outline.light-regate`; `init.light-gate` stays the entry of the light lane.
* *`INV-INIT-2` reworded per lane.* The ratchet clause requires the `light -> deep` transitions only in a version whose lane is `light` (or before the lane is known), not in a `deep` version.
* *Reachability pruning.* With the routes separated, the `outline` event prunes every state not reachable from the chosen lane's entry over all transitions, escalation included, with a new prune reason `other_lane` (header schema and identifier registry); a `light` version keeps the deep route, a `deep` version drops the light one.
* *Specification correction.* The sentence of the `outline` event ("lane known, the other lane pruned") is corrected to state exactly what is pruned, or kept as it is once the above is implemented.

== What

The composer of link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/603d4f7/doc/plans/plan-instance.adoc[`plan-instance`] implements an earlier form of the header and of the shipped rule file. This package aligns the code with link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#plan-instance[Store Schemas § Plan Instance], link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#composition-rules[§ Composition Rules], and link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL § Plan Behaviour Axes] as far as the composition reads them, without a deprecation (pre-1.0). The engine's side of the versions is link:plan-instance-versions.adoc[`plan-instance-versions`]'s.

* *The closed reasons*: `PruneReason` loses `recipe_override` and `auto_merge_off` and gains `plan_default` and `merge_by_operator`, so it equals the `prune_reason` enum of the header schema (`posture`, `provider_unconfigured`, `footprint_disjoint`, `change_type_exempt`, `local_only`, `land_lane`, `adopt`, `merge_by_operator`, `plan_default`, `returned`, `handed_back`, `empty_harvest`); `CompositionCause.FallbackCause` loses `question_expired` (no decision ends by time) and `auto_merge_off`, and gains `merge_by_operator` and `files_not_applicable`, so it equals `fallback_cause` of the composition record; `HeaderSchemaCheck` and the header's element `call` (a pruned call of a phase sub-workflow or an axis variant) follow.
* *The shipped rule file*: the rules `auto-merge-off` (fact `merge.auto_merge`) and `simplify-recipe` (fact `recipe_id`, reason `recipe_override`) leave `composition-rules.json`, and with them the configuration fact `merge.auto_merge` of `CompositionRules` and `PlanFacts.RECIPE_ID_FACT`; the simplify recipe is its own workflow (link:recipe-workflows.adoc[`recipe-workflows`]). A new rule prunes the merge execution (`finalize.merge-dispatch`, `finalize.merge-queue-wait`, `finalize.merge-queue-exit`) with the reason `merge_by_operator` where the bound asking variant declares `self_merge: false`, its source fact the bound variant; the file's `revision` is raised.
* *The plan axes in the composition*: a rule's `when` may read the variant bound on a behaviour axis and its declarations; the declaration schemas of the method variants (`order`) and the asking variants (`wait_after`, `outline_review`, `self_merge`) join the compiled schemas of `DeclarationBounds`; the composer binds the variant of each axis from the plan's own value (`overrides.json`), else the project's, else the machine's, where a level sets it, and prunes the variants not chosen with the reason `plan_default` as pruned calls; `overrides.json` is read as a plan fact and counted in `plan_facts_digest`; a value of the plan set or changed later composes a version with the event `override`, refused with `position_not_contained` where it would not contain the plan's position. Writing the machine's and the project's values and the plan's overrides is link:machine-onboarding-unit.adoc[`machine-onboarding-unit`]'s and link:plan-and-epic-control.adoc[`plan-and-epic-control`]'s; the landing variant composed into the file of `phase-6-finalize`, and one composed file per phase sub-workflow and bound variant, are link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]'s.
* *The adjudication unit in the header*: `adjudicate.decisions`, which no call site calls and the engine runs for the decisions of the version, is listed in `inputs.sub_units` with its digest and its level, and its verified text is stored like every bound sub-unit.
* *`diff_summary`*: each composition record of a composed version carries the pruned elements added and removed, the ceilings changed, and the gates waived or unwaived relative to the previous version, `null` for version 1; the specification names only the content, so the package states the members of the object in the schema of `plan-marshall-documentation` before it writes them.
* *The documents*: the developer guide of `workflow.plan` and ADR `workflow-plan/008` without the shipped rules on `merge.auto_merge` and `recipe_id`; in `plan-marshall-documentation` the parts of `PM-IMPL-5` and of the roadmap item of the plan instance that this package implements.

To be confirmed by the § Discussion:

* The per-lane rework and replan routes in the State Catalogue and in the plan templates of `plan-marshall-mcp`.
* `INV-INIT-2` reworded per lane, in the specification and in `CompiledInvariants`.
* The prune reason `other_lane` in `PruneReason`, `HeaderSchemaCheck` and the header schema.
* Reachability pruning at the `outline` event in `workflow.plan`, with a test per lane and a matched control: a `deep` version without the light route verifies; a `light` version keeps the deep route.
* The corrected `outline` event sentence; the developer guide of `workflow.plan` without the limitation.

== Done When

The § Discussion is decided with the operator first and recorded as an ADR. `PruneReason` and `FallbackCause` equal their enums of the specification, and a header or a composition record with `recipe_override`, `auto_merge_off`, or `question_expired` is refused; the shipped rule file has no rule on `merge.auto_merge` or `recipe_id`, and a fixture plan whose asking variant declares `self_merge: false` loses the merge execution with `merge_by_operator` while one with `self_merge: true` keeps it; a fixture with an axis set at plan, project, and machine level binds the plan's variant and prunes the others as calls with `plan_default`, and an override after the start composes an `override` version or is refused with `position_not_contained`; the header lists `adjudicate.decisions` with its digest; each composed version after the first records its `diff_summary`; a `deep` instance composed at the `outline` event contains no state of the light route and verifies, and a `light` instance keeps the escalation into `deep` and verifies, each with its test; the specification states what the `outline` event prunes, and `INV-INIT-2` holds per lane; `verify`, `verify -Ppre-commit`, `verify -Pcoverage`, and `verify -Pintegration-tests` are green on the pushed head.
