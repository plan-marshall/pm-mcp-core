== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The unit `chat`*, called by a state or hosted by its subject scope, with its states: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat[State Catalogue § Chatting with the Planning Role: `chat`].
. *Routing* to the subject's planning role, Chat disabled with its reason while the role does not qualify: § Chatting with the Planning Role, _Routing_.
. *Data, never instructions*, and every change through a read-back: § Chatting with the Planning Role; link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#own-words[Model Work § Answers in the Operator's Own Words].
. *Apply paths* of an epic, the decomposition draft and a register item: § Chatting with the Planning Role, _Apply paths_; link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-surface-disjointness[Model Work § Staging-Time Surface Disjointness].
. *Chat as an action* on a plan and an epic (UC-CTL-1, UC-CTL-2), an operator endpoint of both clients: § Chatting with the Planning Role.
. *Chat inside a decision* (UC-PLN-2): § Chatting with the Planning Role, _Apply paths_, _A decision_.
. *Chat while starting* (UC-STA-1, UC-STA-2), the description stored as a new source, the transcript moved or ended with the entry: § Chatting with the Planning Role, _A start_ and _Transcript_.
. *Chat about ended work* and its follow-up plan: § Chatting with the Planning Role, _Ended work_; link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[Runtime Model § Ended Work].
. *The transcript* kept with its subject: § Chatting with the Planning Role, _Transcript_.

Left out: a plan's revision from a chat (link:chat-plan-revision.adoc[`chat-plan-revision`]); the call stack (link:phase-sub-workflows.adoc[`phase-sub-workflows`]); the decomposition and review paths (link:epic-decomposition.adoc[`epic-decomposition`], link:epic-review.adoc[`epic-review`]); the release that deletes an entry (link:epic-resource-and-release.adoc[`epic-resource-and-release`]).
