== What

* *Specification first*: the package writes the change Chatting with the Planning Role into `plan-marshall-documentation` as part of its own work, once it is agreed (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#slicing[Specification Changes for the Clients, Packages]): a new unit section of the state catalogue, the unit among the compiled units of the workflow language, the operator-started revision in re-planning, and Chat in epic coordination.
* *The unit `chat`*: compiled like the source and epic units, its states `chat.<state>`, of phase `inherit` when called from a plan; called with its subject (a plan or an epic) and a scope (the whole plan, the whole epic, the decomposition draft, one register item, or the whole register).
* *Routing*: a plan's chat goes to its planning role `replan`, whose facts carry the plan's outline, its tasks with their status, the done deliverables and the footprint; an epic's chat, also in its decomposition and its review, goes to the epic's planning role `decompose`. Chat is offered disabled, with the reason, while no configuration of the routed role qualifies.
* *The states*: `chat.open` assembles the facts of the scope as data, untrusted items still marked untrusted; `chat.turn` waits for the operator's message (an operator wait) and then runs one task of the routed role that answers and may propose changes, again until the operator ends the chat; `chat.read-back` shows each proposed change as a read-back to accept or reject, nothing changing without accept; `chat.apply` applies an accepted change through the caller's own path and its checks; `chat.end` ends the chat.
* *Apply by subject*: a plan through a revision under the guards of a first plan (outline validation, derivation dry-run, task graph, Q-Gate), composing an instance version of event `replan`; an epic through staged rows under the epic's checks (disjoint surfaces, dependencies without a cycle). The decomposition draft and a register item are applied by the path their callers pass in (`epic-decomposition`, `epic-findings-review`).
* *A running plan*: a question never touches it; an accepted revision takes effect at the next task boundary, what runs finishing first. A chat's revision is the operator's own act: it spends nothing of the plan's re-planning budget, and the server never starts a chat itself. A change to a plan an epic started reaches the epic's inbox like every act.
* *Chat as an action*: on a plan in every state that has not ended (running, pausing, paused, waiting for you, held) and on an epic in every state that has not ended, each an operator endpoint of both clients.
* *The transcript*: kept with its subject for the audit, never sent elsewhere; the operator's words interpreted with a read-back, the role reading the content of the plan or the epic as data.

== Done When

The specification of the change is merged in `plan-marshall-documentation`; a test per subject and scope shows the routing to its role, a turn, a read-back accepted and one rejected, and the end; nothing changes without an accept; an accepted revision of a running plan takes effect at the next task boundary, composes an instance version of event `replan`, passes the guards of a first plan, and leaves the re-planning budget untouched; an accepted change to an epic is a staged row that passed its checks; a change to a plan of an epic reaches the epic's inbox; Chat is disabled with its reason while the routed role is not qualified; the transcript is kept with its subject.
