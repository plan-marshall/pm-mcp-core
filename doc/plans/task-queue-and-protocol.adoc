== What

* The task queue store: queue task records and task leases per worker generation; a generation is a worker's, issued with its job token by link:worker-launch.adoc[`worker-launch`] (roadmap Milestone 4, worker generations and fencing), never a session's: the record of the former `session-log-and-generations` package is no part of the target and was removed by `removed-concepts`.
* The task protocol: offers, acknowledgement, leases, fencing, task outcomes and re-offers, and skill delivery per generation.
* The queue task record: the queue of the machine scope at `<PM_MCP_BASE>/machine/queue/` under its own queue lock beside the per-project queue (`scope.type` `machine`; an entry's tasks in the workspace scope); the statuses `awaiting_answer` and `suspended` and the member `continuation` stored and read, the attempt end `suspended` among the ends; the behaviour of these states (the hold, the suspension and resume, the answer acknowledgement, the fallback to `asked`, the answer in the next task, the question bound) is link:question-continuation.adoc[`question-continuation`]'s.
* *Pause*: a task of a paused plan stays `queued` and is offered to no worker until the plan is started; a pause at once ends the attempt with `end: paused`, releases the lease, and queues the task again without counting against the re-offer budget.
* *Off track*: the attempt end `off_track` re-offers the task within its budget; the member `signals[]` is stored with each entry the runtime appends, its signals and actions being link:worker-activity.adoc[`worker-activity`]'s.
* The compiled `worker-task` unit is content of the assembly; this package proves the protocol against a fixture unit.
* *The `agent_started` signal*: the `signal` enum of the queue task record's `signals[]` and of the activity journal's `signal` entry gains `agent_started`, whose action is always `restart` (the schema's `if`/`then` refuses any other action); the runtime records the supervisor's detection with the attempt, the measurement and the instant, ends the attempt with `end: off_track`, re-offers the task within its budget, and stores the `security` audit record `agent_started` the supervisor appends, naming the executable (link:../specification/store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#queue-task-record[Store Schemas § Queue Task Record]; link:../specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[Model Work § A Worker Off Track]; link:supervisor.adoc[`supervisor`]).

== Done When

Exactly-once submit under every interleaving the specification names; a late call of a replaced generation is refused; a lease survives a restart; a task of a paused plan is offered to no worker, and a pause at once re-queues a leased task with `end: paused` without spending a re-offer, the task offered again once the plan is started; a machine-scope task is queued and leased in the machine's queue; a record with a `continuation`, a `suspended` attempt end, and `signals[]` entries round-trips. A queue task record and an activity `signal` entry with `agent_started` and the action `restart` round-trip, recorded with the attempt, the measurement and the instant; an `agent_started` entry with any other action is refused, and the attempt ends `off_track` with the task re-offered within its budget.
