= Open Points and Next Steps
:nofooter:
:toc: macro
:toclevels: 2

Part of the link:README.adoc[study on Git as the persistence of versioned documents]. Statements marked
_(unverified)_ come from general knowledge and were not checked against a current source.

toc::[]


. Prototype of the store: concurrent saves against one branch, measuring conflicts and retries.
. Verify the JGit API used in the sketch (`DirCache.read`, `DirCacheEditor`, in-core merge, `RefUpdate` results) and
  the `git notes` support (`NoteMap`) against the JGit version in use.
. Push and fetch with token credentials against GitHub, GitLab and Gitea; token lifetimes and refresh of the hosts
  _(unverified)_.
. Choose a timestamp authority (qualified under eIDAS or internal).
. Agree the GDPR rules (pseudonymisation, crypto-shredding, retention classes) with the data protection officer,
  and whether destroying the KEK counts as erasure.
. Choose the key source of the KEK (KMS, Vault, configuration value) and clarify whether Token Sheriff can hold
  versioned secrets; verify Google Tink for envelope encryption.
. Decide the threat model format (OTM or Threat Dragon JSON); verify the state of pytm, Threagile and OTM.
. Prototype the detection of outdated threat models from Git diffs of the application's repository.
. Draft the schema of incident response reports with the notification deadlines (GDPR Art. 33; NIS2 where it
  applies), after checking the deadlines and the applicability of NIS2; design the import of the timeline from the
  ticket system or the chat.
. Verify the state and licences of DFIR-IRIS, TheHive and Dispatch.
. Choose the template engine for the AsciiDoc output and the schema language of the forms (JSON Schema).
. Decide name, group, licence and parent POM of `git-foundation` (see
  link:extraction-from-pm-mcp-core.adoc[Extraction from `pm-mcp-core`]).
. Verify the verinice statements (versioning, approval, licence of veo, catalogue coverage) with a demo instance.
. Draft the library API and the SAMM repository schema.
. Prototype the graded signature: commit signature through a KMS with JGit's signer interface, the trailer schema,
  `allowed_signers` with validity periods, and the approval with a passkey (WebAuthn challenge = commit id) as a note.
. Clarify with Token Sheriff which claims (`sub`, `sid`, `acr`, `amr`, `auth_time`) reach the application and how
  the step-up authentication for approvals is triggered.
. Align the schema of the service catalog with the Backstage descriptor format and prototype the import; clarify
  whether the identity provider delivers groups (through Token Sheriff, possibly SCIM) to prefill the directory.
. Agree the legal basis and the information text of the directory with the data protection officer.
