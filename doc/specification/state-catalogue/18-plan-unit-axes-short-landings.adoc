[#state-catalogue-plan-unit]
== Plan Unit: `phases`
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-16[PM-WF-16: Reusable Compound Sub-Workflows & Config-Driven Git Commit/Push]_

The plan unit (`plan.pmwf`, unit id `plan`, `scope plan`, its call states prefixed `phases`) is a workflow of the six phases `init`, `refine`, `outline`, `plan`, `execute`, and `finalize`, each a sub-workflow it calls (`phase-1-init.pmwf` … `phase-6-finalize.pmwf`) (link:../store-schemas/03-findings-and-review-records.adoc#phase-sub-workflows[Store Schemas Specification, Phases as Sub-Workflows]; link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#workflow-definition-units[Workflow DSL Specification, Workflow Definition Units]). Each phase declares in its frontmatter an entry for each point at which it is entered and an exit for each outcome the plan unit continues on differently; the plan unit has neither: it starts at its starting point and ends in its stop points, the plan's terminals and `plan.aborted` (xref:04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#state-catalogue-terminal[Plan Terminal States]). A lane is the plan unit's choice of the phases it calls: the land lane calls `init` and `execute` at its entry `land`, then `finalize`; the adopt lane calls `init` and then `finalize` at its entry `adopt`; a phase a plan's lane skips is a pruned call, with its reason. The deep and the light lane each have their own call states from the split on, the exits `deep` and `light` of `init`: the light lane calls `outline`, `plan`, `execute`, and `finalize` through the call states suffixed `-light` and continues through `phases.ask-light` and `phases.route-light`, the deep lane through the unsuffixed ones, which the land and adopt lanes share. Each lane routes the rework and replan exits of its calls (`rework` of `outline`, `loop_back_outline` of `plan`, `execute`, and `finalize`, `reenter_execute` of `finalize`) to its own call states, so a rework re-enters the authoring entry of the lane: `phases.outline-rework` the entry `rework` (`outline.deep`), `phases.outline-rework-light` the entry `rework_light` (`outline.light-regate`). No state reads the lane to choose a route, and no transition leads from the deep lane's call states into the light lane's; the light lane keeps its escalation into `deep`, the exit `escalate_to_deep` of `outline`, which continues in the deep lane's `phases.ask`. A loop-back is the plan unit calling a phase again; a re-entry from `finalize` into `execute` is the phase `finalize` ending with that outcome. The entries and exits of the phases are those of the call states below: the entries `start` (every phase), `light`, `rework`, `rework_light` (`outline`), `land` (`execute`), `reentry` (`execute`), `adopt`, `hand_back`, and `post_run` (`finalize`); the exits `deep`, `light`, `land`, `adopt`, `returned` (`init`), `ready`, `returned` (`refine`), `passed`, `rework`, `escalate_to_deep`, `returned` (`outline`), `ready`, `loop_back_outline`, `returned` (`plan`), `exit`, `loop_back_outline`, `hand_back` (`execute`), and `merged`, `no_change`, `handed_back`, `returned`, `reenter_execute`, `loop_back_outline` (`finalize`). A plan's phase is the phase sub-workflow on its call stack. A recipe's plan runs the recipe's workflow in place of this unit, its phases its own where it differs and these where it does not (link:../domain-extensions/05-recipes-and-extension-points.adoc#recipe-workflow[Domain Extensions Specification, A Recipe Is a Workflow]).

[#state-catalogue-axes]
== Axis Variants
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-25[PM-WF-25: Plan Behaviour Axes & Plan Overrides]_

Each plan behaviour axis is a call site of the plan workflow and each of its variants a designable sub-workflow whose name's prefix names its axis; a variant is changed, never added (link:../workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL Specification, Plan Behaviour Axes]). The plan instance binds the variant of each axis at the plan's start, from the variant a level sets, else the plan's own value, else the project's, else the machine's, else the shipped `plan_defaults`, and prunes the others with the reason `plan_default`, so the process in force shows the choice; a later change composes a new instance version. The asking axis decides which phase transitions wait for the operator: `ask.every-phase` waits after the phases `init`, `refine`, `plan`, and `execute` (declared `wait_after`; not after `outline`, whose review it asks), asks the outline review (xref:02-plan-phases-1-3.adoc#state-outline.gate[`outline.gate`]), and leaves the merge to the operator (prune reason `merge_by_operator`, xref:07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-finalize.merge-barrier[`finalize.merge-barrier`]); `ask.key-points` waits at no boundary, asks the outline review, and leaves the merge to the operator; `ask.questions-only` waits at no boundary, asks no outline review, and merges itself. The method axis orders each deliverable's implementing and testing tasks (call site `plan.order-tasks`). The landing axis is a call site inside the phase `finalize` (xref:04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#landing-call-site[The Landing Axis]); the states of `landing.pull-request` are the finalize steps of orders `11`, `20`, `22..40`, and `70` catalogued in parts 4 and 7, and the states of the other two variants run at order `70` with the prefix of the phase that calls them.

[#state-catalogue-short-landings]
== Short Landings: `git.land-removal`, `git.land-unit`
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-16[PM-WF-16: Reusable Compound Sub-Workflows & Config-Driven Git Commit/Push]_

A short landing lands one change of a project by one commit, through the project's landing: `git.land-removal` the removal from git of documents moved into a source directory (xref:09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#documents-source[Documents as a Source]), `git.land-unit` a workflow unit or a recipe's workflow placed or removed at project level (link:../workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#process-designer[Workflow DSL Specification, Designing a Process]). Both are compiled sub-workflows, run as instances hosted by the project's workspace scope, so the work that started them goes on while they run; phase value `workspace`. Until it has landed, a short landing shows in the project's running work, and a failed one waits for the operator as the hold `landing_failed` (xref:01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[Holds and Their Acts]). The release of the project lets a running short landing finish first, within its compiled bound. A generic state `git-land-<kind>.x` stands for one concrete state per unit (anchor `state-git-land.x`).
