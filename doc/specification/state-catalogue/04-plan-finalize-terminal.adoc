[#state-catalogue-finalize]
== Phase 6: `finalize`

The start state of `phase-6-finalize.pmwf` is `finalize.settle-commit`. The finalize steps are the step list of the plan instance in force, executed in `order` (link:../phase-workflows/04-finalize-step-bands.adoc#_normative_step_catalogue_across_ordering_bands[Phase Workflows Specification, Normative Step Catalogue]); every default step has one declared state, a template state that the instance composition includes or prunes (with its prune reason, for example `local_only`, `land_lane`, `posture`, `returned`, `plan_default`). Every change of a `mutates_source` step routes through `finalize.settle-commit`, whose `done` continues and whose `stepped_out` enters `finalize.hand-back`. Every model state asks one task of its role for one judgment, naming its skill section; while the role is unqualified the state takes the fallback its row names. A project step (orders in the gaps of `3..10` and `12..69`, `71..899`, `900..989`) runs in a state of the project's own unit (`project.run-step`), published with the project's effective configuration and not catalogued here: a `mutates_source` step (only before order `70`) routes through `finalize.settle-commit`, a step that files findings to `remediation.review-findings`, and its failure fates enter `finalize.step-failed`. The branch-cleanup step (order `70`) runs as the merge path, and the post-push ADR step, the post-run band (`900..999`), the landing, and the archive follow in xref:07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-catalogue-finalize-merge[Merge Path & Post-Run Band]. A plan of source `pr` (lane `adopt`) enters Phase 6 through `finalize.adopt-pr`; its `sync-baseline` step only checks (action `noop` or `check`) and never rebases.

[[landing-call-site]]*The landing axis*: The landing is a call site inside the phase `finalize`, apart from the land lane (link:../workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL Specification, Plan Behaviour Axes]). The default variant `landing.pull-request` is the landing part of the step list: the push (order `11`), the pull request (`20`), the wait region (`22..40`), and the merge path of the branch-cleanup step (`70`), whose states keep their `finalize.*` identifiers below and in xref:07-plan-finalize-merge-post-run.adoc#state-catalogue-finalize-merge[Merge Path & Post-Run Band]. The variants `landing.main` and `landing.files` take over the same orders: the plan instance prunes the steps of `landing.pull-request` with reason `plan_default` and binds the variant's step state at order `70` (`finalize.land-main`, `finalize.land-files`, xref:18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-axes[Axis Variants]). Every check and review of the phase before the landing runs in every variant.

This part lists the start, the settle band (orders `3..10`), push and pull request (`11`, `20`), and the concurrent wait region (`22..40`). Remediation calls follow the fixed class order CI -> analysis -> review findings; after `resolved` or `waived`, verdict currency re-fires the wait region for every class that is stale at the new `HEAD`.

|[[state-finalize.open-pr]]`finalize.open-pr`
|`phase-6-finalize`
|`composite_job`
|`finalize.create-pr` (`submitted`).

[#plan-end]
=== Cancelling and Abandoning Archive
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-23[PM-WF-23: Operator Acts on Plans and Epics]_

[[plan-end-acts]]A plan ends early only by an act of the operator or the release of its project, each through `plan.aborted` with its own outcome and reason (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-1[UC-CTL-1]):

* *Cancel and abandon* are one end, named by the state the plan was in: a running or pausing plan, and one waiting for the operator or held, is *cancelled* (outcome `cancelled`), a paused one *abandoned* (outcome `abandoned`); the operator's reason is required. They are operator acts of both clients over the local API, confirmed with what they end (the running work, the open pull request, the reason); `pm-operator abort plan <plan_id> --reason` is their shell form, which takes the outcome by the plan's activity in the same way. Only the cancel of a plan held by an integrity pause is that pause's resolution _abort_, outcome `aborted`.
* *The pull request*: in the confirmation the operator chooses whether an open pull request of the plan is closed, with a comment naming the reason, or left open; its branch stays either way.
* *Released*: the release of the project ends every plan of the project that has not ended, whatever its state, with the outcome `released`, never `cancelled` or `abandoned` (link:../credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#project-release[Credentials & Enrolment Specification, Releasing Ends Work as the Operator Ends It]).
* *Deleted*: `delete-plan` ends the plan through `plan.aborted` with the outcome `cancelled` (reason `plan_deleted`) and then removes it; its id is never given again.
* *Archived like a completed plan*: the transaction entering `plan.aborted` moves the plan to `archive/<plan_id>/` with its outcome and reason, as `finalize.archive-plan` moves a plan that ran to its end (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout Specification, Plan & Epic Archives]); an ended plan is read from the archive and is never among the project's active plans (<<running-work>>).
* *The epic is told*: every operator act on a plan whose `origin.epic_id` names a live epic, its cancel and abandon included, appends a record to the epic's inbox with the reason and the operator's channel, and the epic decides on it in its own workflow, never ending a plan itself (link:../model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc#epic-informed[Model Work Specification, The Epic Is Told]); the release ends the epic with its plans, so nothing is told.
* *Reported*: the end is a report `plan.ended` with its outcome (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#reports[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Reports to the Operator]).

[#running-work]
=== Running Work
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11: The Operator's Two Clients & Their Contract]_

[[running-work-definition]]A plan or an epic is *active* while it is not in a terminal state: a plan in `completed`, `handed_back`, `returned`, or `plan.aborted`, an epic in `epic.archived` or `epic.aborted` is not active, wherever its directory lies. A paused plan is active. A project has *running work* while it has an active plan or epic, a running or failed short landing (`git.land-removal`, `git.land-unit`, xref:18-plan-unit-axes-short-landings.adoc#state-catalogue-short-landings[Short Landings]), a running project onboarding or setup run (its workspace scope not resting in `project.completed`), or anything of its workspace scope in its `needs_operator` summary; a released or unenrolled project has none. The runtime derives both on read and serves them to the clients' overview of running work (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, The Overview Resource and Its Events]).
