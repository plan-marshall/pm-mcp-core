== Questions, Waivers & Operator Influence
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-12[PM-WF-12: Question Records vs Operator Overrides]_

_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-20[PM-WF-20: Waiting for the Operator]_

_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-23[PM-WF-23: Operator Acts on Plans and Epics]_

_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-24[PM-WF-24: Chatting with the Planning Role]_

_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-69[PM-WATCH-PHASE-69], link:../../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-87[PM-WATCH-PHASE-87]_

`PM-MCP` keeps two kinds of input of the operator apart. A *content question* (a request clarification, an init classifier question, the outline review, a task question, a question a worker raises before its judgment, a team-configuration choice of the workspace setup) is a server-owned question record; the questions of one creation form one *decision* (link:../model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-structure[Model Work Specification, The Decision Structure]). An *override* (a waiver, its revocation, or its decline, the end of a plan or an epic, `delete-plan`, an integrity resolution, a grant) is never a content question: it is an operator act of the operator's two clients, the terminal client (`pm-operator`) and a paired device of the web app, and a value relayed by a model is never accepted for it. The operator acts only in these two clients (link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#clients-only[Workflow DSL Specification, All Interaction in the Operator's Clients]): no model relays the operator's words or choices, and MCP elicitation is never used. An override is never taken as a content answer, and a content answer is never an override.

=== Question Records

* *One Mechanism*: Every content question is a record in the scope's append-only `questions.jsonl` (plan, epic, or workspace store; schema: link:../store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Store Schemas Specification, Question Record]). Records survive the end of every worker; answered records are listed in the pull-request body, the landing record, and the retrospective. The delivery is specified in link:../model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc[Model Work Specification, Envelope Tasks, Questions & Decisions].
* *Creation*: A worker creates a question through the `ask` link (form `submit`, `decision:schema[decision-envelope]`, whose parts take the schema `question-envelope`) of its task entity where its state declares `ask` in its `job llm` clause (link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#model-job-states[Workflow DSL Specification, Task States (`job llm`), item 8]); the task ends with the outcome `asked`, the state waits in its question state `<state>.asked`, and on the answer the asking state is entered again and a new task of the same role follows with the answer in `facts`, unless the worker holds its task for the answer within its role's hold limit (link:../model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#continuation[Model Work Specification, Continuing after a Question]). An envelope task asks through `ask:TASK-NNN`, which records the question and ends the task (xref:03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#_task_envelope_execution_independent_server_verification[Task Envelope Execution], item 3); the refine and outline tasks ask through the optional `decision` of their `submit` (each part becomes a record). Server-raised questions (the init classifiers, the outline review, the team-configuration choices of the setup) are created by their primitive with server-owned options.
* *Recommendation & Adjudication*: Every question carries its recommended option, the asker's confidence, a rationale, and a decision kind, and every new decision passes the adjudication `adjudicate.decisions` before delivery, which answers it only where the quorum declarations of its kinds decide it (link:../model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc[Model Work Specification, Recommendation Score & Decisions: Ask or Quorum]); whether a quorum runs is said only by the decision's declaration.
* *Delivery*: A decision the quorum does not decide is an operator wait of its scope (link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Workflow DSL Specification, Waiting for the Operator]), delivered to both clients of the operator: the web app (channel `web`, with the paired device) and the terminal client (channel `cli`, link:../store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#cli-answers[Store Schemas Specification, Answers from the Terminal Client]), each of which announces it and answers it through the same scope-qualified answer endpoint; the first valid answer wins, and every client withdraws what it showed once the decision was answered in another one. A decision offers, beside its answers, the operator's own words where a part allows them, _none fits_, _later_, and a chat with the role that asked (link:../state-catalogue.adoc#chat[State Catalogue, Chatting with the Planning Role]); a waiver request offers no chat.
* *Answer Validation*: Every channel passes the same check: an unknown option is refused with `invalid_link_args`, a second answer with `already_answered`, an answer to an older revision of the decision and an answer to a part whose dependency is unanswered are refused (link:../model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-structure[Model Work Specification, The Decision Structure]).
* *Waiting State*: The state that waits for a decision is a question state and an operator wait: it has no deadline and no default taken by time, holds no worker and no model slot, and halts its own plan or epic only, which the status shows as waiting for the operator, never as stalled. Nothing is decided by the passing of time: a decision is taken by its quorum or by the operator. The operator's cancel or abandon of the plan, the integrity abort, the release of the project, and `delete-plan` close an open record `cancelled`; a revision accepted in a chat that removes the task or state that asked it closes it `withdrawn` with the reason.
* *Choices of a Start*: Before a plan exists nothing is a question record: choosing what to work on (`start.choose`), the list of sources (`start.sources`), a source's own choices (a lesson premise, a text the screener flagged), and the title and id at `start.ready` are operator waits of the start's entry (link:../state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#start-from-clients[State Catalogue, Starting from the Clients]). An entry never expires and is never removed by time. A plan its epic starts has no such choices: the server runs its start with the source `epic_row` (xref:06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#epic-plan-start[Epic Plan Start]).

=== Options Asked Where Needed

Who decides a question of which kind is not operator configuration: it is the decision declarations of the project-owned adjudication unit (`ask` or a quorum per decision kind) and the artifacts of the roles they list (link:../model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-declarations[Model Work Specification, Decision Declarations]). A plan's options beyond its sources are asked where the plan first needs them, each as an operator wait with its proposal, and not at all where the plan's own value, the project, or the machine sets them (link:../state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#options-when-needed[State Catalogue, Asked When Needed]): the posture where the plan's lane is chosen and the base branch before the plan's branch is created, both in the one operator wait `init.options` of `phase-1-init` (xref:01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc#_deterministic_init_classifiers_workflow_settings[Deterministic Init Classifiers & Workflow Settings], items 5 and 6), and the outline review when the outline is ready, which the plan's asking variant decides (xref:02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#outline-gate[Server Outline Gate]). Which phase transitions wait for the operator is the plan's asking variant (link:../workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL Specification, Plan Behaviour Axes]); no other setting stops a plan between phases.

=== Waivers & Operator Acts

A *waiver* declares a waivable gate met or raises a budget. It is written only by an operator act of either client (`pm-operator waive <plan_id> <gate> --reason "…"` or `--raise <budget>=N`, and the web app's waive), stored in the plan's append-only `waivers.jsonl` (`waiver_id`, `gate`, `mode: met | raise`, `value`, mandatory `reason`, operator identity and channel, `event: waived | revoked`, `at` (the creation time of a `waived` line, the revocation time of a `revoked` line); mirrored to `audit.jsonl`), and composed into a new plan instance version (composition event `waiver`). A waiver is plan-scoped, not bound to `HEAD`: it may be set at any time until the gate's fallback is taken (after that it is refused `plan_ended`), and it applies to every later evaluation of its gate. A gate admits a failure only through a waiver lookup at evaluation time, which logs a consumption (`HEAD`, covered items such as check names or finding ids); the consumptions are listed in a pull-request comment and in the landing record. `--raise` targets only a policy budget (`fix_budget:<policy>`, `retry_budget:<policy>`) or the outline loop-back budget (`fix_budget:outline-loop-back`) and sets an absolute ceiling above the effective ceiling in force and at most the compiled hard cap. `waive` refuses a gate outside the list (`not_waivable`), `unwaive` refuses a consumed waiver (`already_consumed`).

|===
|Gate identifier |Waives

|`review_evidence` or `review_evidence:<bot_kind>`
|Missing review evidence of the required bots (Predicate 2 of the pre-merge barrier); the merge records `review_state: unreviewed_by_waiver`.

|`ci_check:<check_name>`
|A named failing CI check.

|`analysis_gate:<provider>`
|The quality gate of a code analysis provider (for example `analysis_gate:sonar`).

|`findings:<class>`, `findings:<provider>`
|Open findings of a class, or of an analysis provider, after triage.

|`qgate_findings:<phase>` (`refine`, `outline`, `plan`)
|Blocking Q-Gate findings of the phase; they are dispositioned accepted-by-waiver.

|`--raise fix_budget:<policy>=N`, `--raise retry_budget:<policy>=N`, `--raise fix_budget:outline-loop-back=N`
|Raises a policy budget, or the outline loop-back budget (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#loop-back-outline-budget[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Outline Loop-Back Budget]).
|===

Never waivable: every gate on the non-waivable list of the waiver record (link:../store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#waiver-record[Store Schemas Specification, Waiver Record, Refusals]), among them integrity breaches, security findings, a build failure, a merge conflict, and a provider refusal of the merge action. No plan is held to a limit of the tokens it uses, so no gate measures them.

*Waiver Requests*: The `request_waiver` action a remediation unit's `decide` task chooses (offered for a failing waivable gate wherever the call binds it) files a record in `waiver_requests[]` of the plan status (`request_id`, `gate`, `rationale`, `evidence_uri`, `requested_at`, `outcome?`). The request is an operator wait like a decision, without a time limit: announced in both clients, shown in the plan's view as `facts.waiver_request` (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/mcp-tools/01-core-workflow-tools.adoc#question-waiver-facts[MCP Tools Specification, Question Records & Waiver Requests in Representations]), listed by `pm-operator waivers <plan_id> --pending`, and posted as an idempotent pull-request comment with the label `pm-mcp:waiver-requested` when a pull request exists. A waiver request is never a question record and offers no chat: it ends only by the operator's `waive` of its gate (outcome `waived`, the unit ends `waived`) or `decline` (outcome `declined`, recorded with the reason; the remediation unit takes its next transition with the remaining actions), or `cancelled` when the plan ends.

*Operator Acts*: `waive`, `unwaive`, `decline`, `waivers`, `grant-access`, pausing and starting a plan (now, after the current task, the default, or after the current phase; link:../runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#plan-pause[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Pausing and Starting a Plan]), cancelling a running, pausing, waiting, or held plan and abandoning a paused one (`pm-operator abort plan` is the shell form of both; link:../state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#plan-end[State Catalogue, Cancelling and Abandoning Archive]), the acts of a hold (link:../state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc#holds[State Catalogue, Holds and Their Acts]), a revision accepted in a chat (xref:08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-chat[Revision from a Chat]), and the plan's own settings (link:../workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-overrides[Workflow DSL Specification, Plan Overrides]) act on a running plan, each an operator endpoint of both clients that the server validates and audits with its channel and device; each that changes the process composes a new plan instance version. `grant-access <plan> <project> read|read-write --reason` appends an audited grant record to the plan store; an access to another project without a grant raises `precondition_unmet`, shown as a hold of kind `condition` whose act is the grant, with its bounded server `retry` and the fallback when its budget is spent. `delete-plan`, `accept-edit`, `credentials`, `config`, and `roles` are operator commands (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc[CLI & Security Specification]). *Deliberate change vs. `plan-marshall`*: outline approval is a server gate (the operator's review is a decision where the asking variant waits at the outline), scope deviations are recorded (xref:03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#unplanned-changes[Unplanned Changes]), infeasible deliverables and refuted hypotheses are decisions of role tasks, baseline-only red is a server rule, and merge authorization is the waiver lookup; the verbs `decide`, `authorize-merge`, and `force-gate`, the artifact-gate bypass `--allow-bare-transition`, the handshake and baseline drift override, the pre-rebase overlap override, and the escalation beside a timed-out `retry` do not exist.

*Intent Amendments*: Phase 2 answers are written into `request.md` (`## Clarifications`). Every later answer or decision that changes the plan's intent (an answered question in Phases 3–6, an answered task question, a narrowed scope after a refuted hypothesis, a removed deliverable, a revision accepted in a chat, or a refuted premise) is persisted as an entry of `mcp/status.json -> amendments[]` (`at`, `phase`, `source: user | quorum | model | refutation`, `question_id?`, `text`, `supersedes`). Every later representation and task that renders the request (outline, `create-pr`, landing envelope) includes the amendments, so a superseded statement of the request never reaches a later phase unmarked.

=== Question Envelope Schema

Workers never prompt the operator (a headless worker has no one to prompt) and never wait on a question beyond their role's hold limit. A refine or outline task completes its pass on best judgment and includes a batched question list in its `submit` payload (`refine_prompt` or `outline_prompt`) as the optional free parameter `decision:schema[decision-envelope]?`; an envelope task asks through `ask:TASK-NNN`, and any other task through the `ask` link its state declares. The decision is validated against the named schema `decision-envelope`, and each of its questions against `question-envelope` (link:../hypermedia-format/02-link-grammar.adoc#named-parameter-schemas[Hypermedia Format Specification, Named Parameter Schemas]; link:../model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-envelope-schema[Model Work Specification, Question Envelope Schema]). Each accepted question is persisted as a question record (link:../store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Store Schemas Specification, Question Record]).

Every option is authored as a two-sided contract whose label names the workflow branch selected when chosen, with the `recommended` option listed first.
