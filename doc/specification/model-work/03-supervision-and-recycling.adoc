[#supervisor]
== Supervisor
_See Requirements link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3: Supervision & Recycling], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14: Task Envelopes, Model Slots & Usage per Task]_

Neither the transport nor the model reports that a worker is lost or about to give up. The runtime runs one supervisor per project and role. It keeps a worker available for every queued task of the role (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#queue-task[Queue Task]), detects lost workers, ends and replaces them, watches live workers for the off-track signals (<<worker-off-track>>), and recycles warm workers before they give up or their context grows too large. A worker is a job of kind `llm` (link:../job-runtime.adoc[Job Runtime Specification]); its job record carries its role, form, `generation_id`, and worker state (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#worker-states[Worker States]; link:../store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#job-record[Store Schemas Specification, Job Record]). The launch of every worker is the launch contract of xref:04-worker-launch.adoc[Worker Launch].

* *Fresh roles*: For every queued task of the role, the supervisor starts one fresh worker for that task, up to the role's `count` of running fresh workers in the project. The worker ends after its task closes.
* *Warm roles*: While the role has queued tasks, the supervisor keeps up to the role's `count` warm workers of the project, each started for one working directory (a plan's worktree or the project checkout) and serving only the tasks that run there, because a worker's working directory is fixed at launch (xref:04-worker-launch.adoc#job-working-directory[Worker Launch, Working Directory]). Without queued work it starts none; idle workers end by the idle recycle (<<recycling>>).
* *Bounds*: Every start also needs a model slot and respects one writer per plan (<<warm-slots>>).

[#host-values]
=== Values per Host and per Role

The supervisor takes its timing from the client profile of the worker's harness (link:../client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc[Client Profiles Specification, Worker Block]) and its recycling rules from the role's artifact (xref:01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-artifact[Role Artifact Schema]). No value is compiled per role or per harness into the supervisor.

[cols="2,2,3"]
|===
|Value |Source |Use

|Bounded wait, progress frames
|`connection.bounded_wait_seconds`, `connection.progress_resets_timeout`
|How long a `pm_wait` is held, and whether progress frames are sent (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#pm-wait[`pm_wait`]).

|Acknowledgement deadline, informational
|`worker.ack_deadline_seconds`, `worker.ack_informational`
|<<detection>>

|Silence grace
|`worker.silence_grace_seconds`
|<<detection>>

|Idle wake-ups
|`worker.idle_wakeups`, lowered by the artifact's `recycling.idle_wakeups`
|<<recycling>>

|Token budget, tasks per generation
|The artifact's `recycling.token_budget` and `recycling.tasks_per_generation`
|<<recycling>>
|===

The measured basis of the host values is in the link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc#parameters[Evaluation Reference, Substantiated Values].

[#detection]
== Detection
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3: Supervision & Recycling]_

The supervisor ends a worker as `lost` on any of these signals, and on no other:

* *Exit*: The worker's process exits without the server's instruction after its first call. The supervisor observes the exit of the job's process itself and records the loss within the exit detection bound (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#exit-detection-bound[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Exit Detection Bound]). An exit before the first call is a start failure (<<start-failure-guard>>).
* *Missing acknowledgement*: An offer is not acknowledged within the host's acknowledgement deadline (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#ack[Acknowledge]), where the host's acknowledgement is not informational.
* *Missing answer acknowledgement*: A worker that continues after its question (xref:06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#continuation[Continuing after a Question]) does not acknowledge the representation that carries the answer within the host's acknowledgement deadline, where the host's acknowledgement is not informational. The acknowledgement tells the server that the worker received the answer and continues; without it a worker that took the answer and then stopped would hold its task until its silence limit.
* *Silence*: The worker has no open call (a held `pm_wait` or `pm_build` is an open call) and has made no other call for longer than the bounded wait plus the host's silence grace. For a worker that has not made its first call, the time counts from its spawn, and its end counts as a start failure (<<start-failure-guard>>).

A `zero_tool` worker (the screener, the judge) has no MCP connection and makes no call, so none of these signals applies to it: it receives no offer to acknowledge, it is never silent, and it is never counted by the start-failure guard (<<start-failure-guard>>). Its fate is decided by its exit and its worker budget alone (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#model-job-budget[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Worker Budget]): an exit with a structured final result that validates against the result schema of its role (`screen-verdict`, `judge-score`) closes its task `submitted`, every other exit and the expiry of its budget close it `lost` (xref:07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#screener[Screener]; xref:01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#grading[Grading]).

Liveness never rests on the transport: a held call the host abandoned does not keep a worker alive past its silence limit, and no signal ends a worker earlier than its deadline. A worker that stalled for less than its silence limit and then stays silent is ended by the silence signal like any other: the limit counts from its last call, not from the end of the stall. This is the only recovery from a harness process that stays alive without continuing (`opencode`, stopped while a model request is open, may never continue after it is resumed): it costs one silence limit before the task is offered again. A lost worker's lease is released at its fence (<<ending>>), and its task is offered again (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#re-offer[Re-Offer]).

[#worker-off-track]
== A Worker Off Track
_See Requirements link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-7[PM-WORK-7: Worker Activity & Off-Track Signals], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3: Supervision & Recycling], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-1[PM-WORK-1: Roles as Configuration]_

Detection decides only whether a worker is alive. A worker that is alive and busy but makes no progress (it repeats one call, works for many turns without following a link of its task, spends far more on a plan task than its size predicts, or changes files outside the plan task's footprint) is noticed by the *off-track signals*, which the runtime computes from the worker's activity journal (link:../client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#worker-activity[Client Profiles Specification, What a Worker Does]) and the task's records, never by a model. A signal is not a limit: it never ends a plan, and it never becomes a decision or waits for the operator.

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Signal |Holds when

|`repeating`
|The worker made the same tool call with the same target `N` times in a row (a `tool_call` entry of the activity: tool name and target). A repeat is not counted when, between the two calls, the target was written (a `file_changed` entry for it), the harness compacted the worker's context (a compaction the profile maps), or the earlier call failed or was rejected: then the repeat has a cause, and the counting starts again.

|`no_progress`
|The worker completed `N` turns (`turn` entries) without following a link of its task (a server entry of the activity: `ack`, a `submit`, `ask`, `consult:<role>`, `yield`, `infeasible`).

|`over_size`
|The tokens spent on the current plan task of an envelope task (the `total_tokens` of its turns since the plan task was yielded) exceed `N` times its `predicted_cost_tokens` (xref:06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#cost-sizing[Cost Sizing]). Only for envelope tasks.

|`outside_footprint`
|The unplanned changes already recorded for the current plan task of an envelope task (changes inside the worktree beyond its declared footprint, link:../phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#_task_envelope_execution_independent_server_verification[Phase Workflows Specification, Task Envelope Execution]) reach `N` files. Only for envelope tasks.

|`agent_started`
|A process of the worker's recorded process tree (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#_process_termination_cancellation_protocol[Job Runtime Specification, Process Termination & Cancellation Protocol], step 3a) runs an enrolled harness executable or the operator binary `pm-operator`, compared by path and digest with the harness enrolment and the installation. Its threshold is `1` and fixed, its action is always `restart` whatever the role artifact names, and it writes a `security` audit record `agent_started` naming the executable: a second agent started by a worker would run outside every task, tool set, and usage record.
|===

* *Thresholds*: every `N` is a compiled threshold (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#off-track-thresholds[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Off-track signal thresholds]); a role artifact may lower it in its `off_track` member and never raise it (xref:01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-artifact[Role Artifact Schema]). The compiled values are set from the activity of real plans once it is recorded.
* *When it is evaluated*: at every activity entry of the worker's current task, in the transaction that appends it; a signal holds at most once per attempt and plan task, so one cause never takes its action twice.
* *What the server does*: per signal the action the role artifact chooses from the closed list below, `show` where the artifact names none. Every action is recorded with the signal, its measured value, and its threshold in the queue task's `signals[]` (link:../store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#queue-task-record[Store Schemas Specification, Queue Task Record]) and as a `signal` entry of the activity journal (link:../store-schemas/16-task-queue.adoc#activity-journal[Store Schemas Specification, Activity Journal]).

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Action |Effect

|`show`
|The task and its plan say so: the plan's process and the task's activity carry the signal, and the overview shows the plan off track (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Overview Resource]); nothing else changes.

|`nudge`
|The worker's next tool result of `PM-MCP` carries a server note naming the signal and the expected next step of its task protocol, within the worker's own task protocol (link:../hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc[Hypermedia Format Specification]); the note never changes the task's links.

|`yield`
|The worker's next call is answered with the end of its attempt: for an envelope task the server takes the envelope's yield as `yield:envelope-M` does, with the `yield_reason` `off_track`, so the remaining plan tasks go to the next envelope task (link:../phase-workflows/03-q-gate-and-execute.adoc#_task_envelope_execution_independent_server_verification[Phase Workflows Specification, Task Envelope Execution]); for any other task it acts as `restart`.

|`restart`
|The worker is ended as in <<ending>>, the attempt ends with `end: off_track`, and its task is offered again as a new attempt within its re-offer budget (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#re-offer[Re-Offer]).
|===

* *Told, not asked*: a `yield` or a `restart` the server took is a report to the operator of the kind `task.off_track`, whose detail is the task's activity (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#reports[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Reports to the Operator]); no signal adds to what needs the operator.
* *Verified by scenarios*: each signal and each action is a scenario of the fixture harness with a scripted output stream (a looping worker, a busy worker without links, a worker far over its size, a worker changing an unplanned file), beside the fault matrix of link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-TEST-4[PM-TEST-4].

A model that watches the activity and judges whether a worker is on track is a later improvement (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-17[Later Improvements, L-17]).

[#recycling]
== Recycling Warm Workers
_See Requirements link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3: Supervision & Recycling], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14: Task Envelopes, Model Slots & Usage per Task]_

_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-43[PM-WATCH-DISP-43]_

A warm worker is recycled by the answer `end` to its next `pm_wait` (<<ending>>), never in the middle of a task. The supervisor recycles it when:

* *Idle*: it received its idle wake-ups in a row as `wait_again` answers without a task; a `wait_again` answer to a generation holding a lease (a consulting worker or one holding for an answer, for example) is not an idle wake-up. A successor is started only when a task of the role is queued again.
* *Token budget*: its context reaches the role's `token_budget`. The context of a worker is the input, cache-read, and cache-creation tokens of its latest turn, taken from the harness's per-turn usage through the profile's usage pointers (the same per-turn usage that is accounted per task, xref:04-worker-launch.adoc[Worker Launch]); a run-total record is never read as a context size. A worker holding its task for an answer that reaches the budget at a wake-up ends its hold: the task is suspended where it may resume, else it falls back (xref:06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#continuation[Continuing after a Question]), and the worker is recycled. The budget otherwise recycles a generation only after it closed at least one task: the context a worker starts with differs by harness and model and can exceed a small budget, which would otherwise recycle every generation before its first task.
* *Tasks per generation*: it closed the role's `tasks_per_generation` tasks.
* *Changed configuration*: the role's configuration digest (xref:01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-artifact[Role Artifact Schema]) differs from the one the generation was started with: an edited artifact or a changed role assignment (xref:01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#role-assignment[Role Assignment]).

A successor for queued work is started at once after the recycle. Recycling needs no handover: the successor starts with an empty context and receives every skill again (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#leases-fencing[Leases, Generations & Fencing]).

[#ending]
== Ending a Worker
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3: Supervision & Recycling]_

* *Instructed end*: The server answers the worker's `pm_wait` with `end`: after a fresh worker's task closed, on a recycle, and on a drain. A worker whose plan the operator pauses at once is ended by force at once; its task is queued again without counting a re-offer (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#task-outcomes[Closed Task Outcomes]). A worker that does not exit within the termination grace is ended as below.
* *Forced end*: A lost worker, and a worker that did not exit after `end`, is ended by `SIGTERM`, then `SIGKILL` after the termination grace, by the termination protocol of every job (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#_process_termination_cancellation_protocol[Job Runtime Specification, Process Termination & Cancellation Protocol]).
* *Fence*: Then its job token is revoked, its generation fenced, and its lease released at once (xref:02-task-protocol.adoc#fencing[Fencing]). A successor is started when the role still has queued work.
* *Fate and usage*: The job's fate is derived by the job runtime from what it observed (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#outcome-derivation[Job Runtime Specification, Outcome Derivation]). A worker ended by a signal reports no run usage; its run usage is recorded as `unmeasured`, while the per-turn usage of the turns it completed stays accounted to their tasks.

On a drain (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model.adoc[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification]) the runtime offers no new task, answers the waits of idle warm workers with `end`, lets running tasks continue up to the drain timeout, and then ends the remaining workers by force; their leases are released and their tasks offered again after the next start, without counting a re-offer.

[#start-failure-guard]
== Start-Failure Guard
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-3[PM-WORK-3: Supervision & Recycling]_

A worker of a `writer` or `read_only` role that exits before its first call (its job record has no `connected_at`) has failed to start; it is not lost. A `zero_tool` worker makes no call and is never counted here (<<detection>>). A harness whose account is at its usage limit, or that cannot start at all, makes every worker exit this way, and replacing it would start workers without end. The guard:

. *Count*: The supervisor counts consecutive start failures per role and harness; the first call of any worker of the role on that harness resets the count. A launch refused before spawn counts as a start failure as well (xref:04-worker-launch.adoc#harness-selection[Worker Launch, Launch Refusals]). A fresh worker's task is queued again without counting a re-offer.
. *Hold*: After the compiled number of consecutive start failures (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#start-failure-guard[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Start-Failure Guard]), the guard holds: the supervisor starts no worker of the role on that harness. The queued tasks of the role and every new one close `lost` with the reason `role_unavailable` and take their conservative fallback (xref:01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#conservative-fallback[Conservative Fallback]).
. *Report*: The harness's own error text (the last lines of the failed worker's output, bounded to 2 KiB and redacted like every job log) is reported to the operator in both clients and in the audit log, with the role and the harness.
. *Clear*: The guard clears when the role's configuration changes or the harness is enrolled again. After the compiled probe backoff (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#start-failure-guard[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Start-Failure Guard]), the next task of the role starts one worker as a probe and waits for it instead of closing: a probe that makes its first call clears the guard; a probe that fails closes the task `role_unavailable` and restarts the backoff.

[#start-collisions]
=== Starts of One Harness

A harness that keeps its local state in one per-user store shares it between all its processes: a stalled worker that holds the store's lock makes every other start of the harness fail, and the operator's own sessions of it with them. Such a harness declares a per-worker data home in its client profile, so that no two workers and no worker and the operator's session share that store (`opencode`; link:../job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#worker-data-home[Job Runtime Specification, Per-Worker Data Home]). _Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-60[PM-WATCH-DISP-60]_

Concurrent starts of one harness can still collide in what the harness shares otherwise (its caches, its login refresh). The supervisors therefore start the workers of one harness one after another on the machine: the next start waits until the previous worker made its first call or ended, at most the compiled spacing bound (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#harness-start-spacing[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Start Spacing of One Harness]). A collision that still makes a worker exit before its first call is a start failure and counts for the guard.

[#warm-slots]
== Model Slots & Warm Workers
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14: Task Envelopes, Model Slots & Usage per Task]_

Every worker holds a machine-wide model slot from its spawn to its exit, a warm worker for its whole generation, idle or not; the slot rules and one writer per plan are specified in xref:04-worker-launch.adoc#model-slots[Worker Launch, Model Slots & One Writer per Plan]. The supervisor adds two rules:

* *Idle recycle returns slots*: The idle recycle (<<recycling>>) is what returns the slot of an unused warm worker.
* *Idle yields*: When a task of another role waits for a slot and every slot is held, the supervisor recycles the longest-idle warm worker that holds no lease, so idle warm workers never starve queued work.
