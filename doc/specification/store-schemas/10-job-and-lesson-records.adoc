[#job-record]
== Job Record (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/jobs/<job_id>.json`)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/job-runtime.adoc#PM-WATCH-JOB-23[PM-WATCH-JOB-23], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/implementation-watch/job-runtime.adoc#PM-WATCH-JOB-41[PM-WATCH-JOB-41]_

A job that must wait for a build slot or a worker that must wait for a model slot (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#machine-lease-stores[Machine Lease Stores]) is recorded `queued` first and becomes `running` in the transaction that acquires its slot; the write-ahead record of an in-process `git` or `ci` operation of the native variant is written before the operation starts (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#in-process-operations[Job Runtime Specification, In-Process Operations]); and a job enters `active_jobs` only once its post-spawn record is written. A job record lies in the `jobs/` of its scope's store: `projects/<project_id>/jobs/` for a job of a project, `machine/jobs/` for a job of the machine scope, and `onboardings/<entry_id>/jobs/` for a job of an onboarding entry (link:../filesystem-layout/02-machine-runtime.adoc#machine-scope-home[Filesystem Layout Specification, Machine Scope & Onboarding Entries]).

*Write order at job end*: The fixed order in which a settling job writes its fate, a worker's metrics row of the attempt that ended with it, the change-ledger row, the timing observation, and the slot release, and what a crash in between loses, is owned by link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#outcome-derivation[Job Runtime Specification, Outcome Derivation] (Write order at job end). For a worker, the fate and its terminal worker block are one write of its job record; the fence of its generation is no write of the settlement, the supervision pass performs it after it reads the settled record, and the runner revokes its token once the settlement has recorded the fate; the release of the lease of a task it held follows as a write of the queue task record (xref:16-task-queue.adoc#queue-task-record[Queue Task Record]), in the fixed order of xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal] (Writes outside the scope). The usage of every other attempt of a task is a metrics row of the task's scope, written when that attempt ends (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store (`metrics.json`)]).

*Recovery and log*: The project's recovery settles every record a previous runtime instance left `queued` or `running`; the raw log `<job_id>.log` lies next to the record in the `jobs/` of its store. The log of an ended job is work history: only the cleanup deletes it, once the job ended longer ago than `cleanup.logs_after_days` (the project's, else the machine's; link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#cleanup[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, One Cleanup]). The record stays after its log: the record of a job of a plan or epic goes when the cleanup deletes the archived plan or epic, the record of an ended job of the workspace scope, of the machine scope, or of a worker is technical residue that the periodic sweep removes after `retention.task_records_days` (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]); the records of an onboarding entry move into the project's `jobs/` with its enrolment, or go with the entry's directory when the operator abandons it.

*Workers*: A job of kind `llm` is a worker (link:../model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc[Model Work Specification, Supervision & Recycling]): the headless harness CLI of the enrolled harness of its role's effective configuration (xref:11-enrolment-store.adoc#harness-enrolment-store[Harness Enrolment Store]), launched by the supervisor with the launch contract recorded in `worker` (its composed argument vector is digested in `argv_digest` like every job's), in the working directory fixed at its launch (a plan worktree or the project checkout), and a `zero_tool` worker (the screener, the judge) or a graded worker of a qualification item whose corpus context travels in-band in the empty subdirectory `cwd/` of its job-private directory `<PM_MCP_BASE>/run/jobs/<job_id>/` (link:../filesystem-layout/02-machine-runtime.adoc#job-private-directories[Filesystem Layout Specification, Job-Private Directories]). Its raw output is the job log; the server parses the per-turn usage and the run totals from it, and, while the worker runs, the activity events its profile maps (link:../harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#worker-activity[Harness Profiles Specification, What a Worker Does]) into the task's activity journal (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store], `tasks/activity/`). The worker block holds the protocol state of the worker (its state, the task it holds, the skills its generation has received, its recycling counters); the usage of each attempt of a task is a metrics row of the task's scope (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store (`metrics.json`)]). A worker's generation is its `job_id`. The job token is valid only while `status` is `running` and is revoked by the runner once the settlement has recorded the fate, and again by the supervision pass (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#outcome-derivation[Job Runtime Specification, Outcome Derivation]); a call over a revoked token is refused `job_ended`, and a call of a fenced generation `generation_fenced`. The job record states in `egress` the job's route out: for a worker `enforced` (the platform makes its egress proxy its only route out, as its launch report states) or `unenforced` (the launch report states nothing of it, or no platform mechanism does; link:../job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#worker-egress[Job Runtime Specification, Worker Egress Proxy]); for a `build` and a `project_script` job `enforced`, `open` (the project's `build_network: open`), or `unenforced` (link:../job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#build-egress[§ Build Egress]); and `null` for every other kind. A worker records in `git_metadata` whether the launcher states that the git directory of the project and the `.git` link file of every linked worktree are unwritable for it (`enforced`), or states nothing or no such thing (`unenforced`); every other kind keeps it `null`.

*Build egress*: The record of a `build` or `project_script` job names the hosts its egress proxy admitted and its `egress` value (link:../job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#build-egress[Job Runtime Specification, Build Egress & Build Read Set]).

[#lesson-record]
== Lesson Record (project lesson store)
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-31[PM-WF-31: Lessons]_
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/credentials-and-enrolment.adoc#PM-WATCH-CLI-4[PM-WATCH-CLI-4], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-14[PM-WATCH-STORE-14], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-15[PM-WATCH-STORE-15], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-16[PM-WATCH-STORE-16], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-19[PM-WATCH-STORE-19], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-20[PM-WATCH-STORE-20], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-34[PM-WATCH-STORE-34], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-58[PM-WATCH-STORE-58]_

Lessons live in the project's lesson store in the per-project machine store and outlive the plans that create them. Each lesson is one Markdown file: YAML front matter carrying the fields below, followed by its Markdown body. The narrative stays Markdown text (`PM-IMPL-2`); only the front matter is structured. The lesson `submit` transition (link:../phase-workflows.adoc[Phase Workflows Specification], Lesson Creation & Body) writes both atomically. The store is a Ported store (`PM-TEST-1`; its legacy mapping lives in test code only, `PM-TEST-2`). How the record, the codec, the resolver and the transitions are built is the link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-store.adoc#_lesson_record_and_file_format[`core.store` Developer Guide § Lesson Record and File Format] and link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-store.adoc#_the_lesson_store[§ The Lesson Store].

* *Placement*: The store is `<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/lessons/` (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store]): machine-local, outside the repository, resolved identically from the main checkout and every worktree, one file `<lesson_id>.md` per lesson and the lesson-store digest sidecar `.digests.json`. A lesson file is never moved and never deleted by a lifecycle transition: applying a lesson claims it, and retiring it records the retirement in its front matter. Legacy lessons never enter it: `PM-MCP` never reads the project's legacy `.plan/local/lessons-learned/` (`PM-MIG-2`), and every project starts with an empty lesson store. A lesson therefore stays on the machine that recorded it and reaches another only through a promotion destination (`promotions[]`).
* *Lock*: Lesson id allocation and every write, claim, release, or retirement run under the lesson store lock `locks/lessons.lock` as one journaled transaction of the caller, never of the store itself, so two concurrent writers never allocate the same `lesson_id` and never claim the same lesson (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#lock-free-snapshot-reads[Lock-Free Snapshot Reads, Transaction Locks & Leases (`PM-IMPL-7`)], xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal]; link:../../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-store/075-the-lesson-id-is-the-utc-hour-and-a-per-hour-sequence.adoc[ADR core-store/075]).
* *File Format*: A lesson file is a line `---`, the front matter, a line `---`, and the free Markdown body; the front matter is a YAML 1.2 flow mapping restricted to the JSON data model, checked on every read and write against the closed `LessonFrontMatter` schema below, a file without parseable front matter resolves to `present_unreadable`, and a write through the store refuses a blank body (link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-store.adoc#_lesson_record_and_file_format[`core.store` Developer Guide § Lesson Record and File Format]).
* *Revisions*: A change of the body replaces it wholesale and increments `revision`; the new body carries its own `sources[]`, and `revisions[]` appends `{n, at, sources, body_sha256}` for the new revision. Old bodies are not kept; `revisions[]` keeps their digests and sources, so the derivation of every revision stays stated. A body is never edited in part.
* *Lesson-Store Digest*: Every server write, claim, release, or retirement in the lesson store records the size and SHA-256 of the lesson file in `.digests.json` (the sidecar format of xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#store-digest-verification[Store Digest Verification Against Out-of-Band Edits]) in the same transaction as the lesson write. On read, a lesson whose file differs from its entry raises `integrity_breach` (kind `store_tampered`) for that lesson only; a lesson file that disappeared while its entry remains is reported as `deleted_out_of_band`, and a file without an entry is reported as `untracked`. None of these states is ever read as `absent`, and an out-of-band edit is accepted only by the operator's act `accept-edit` of the lesson in either client (`pm-operator accept-edit lesson <lesson_id>` in the terminal), which shows the path and the recorded and current size and SHA-256 before confirmation, re-baselines that lesson's entry, and records the action in `audit.jsonl`. The breach of a lesson ends only by that `accept-edit` (no abort applies to the lesson store); until then the lesson is not listed as a candidate, `source-lesson` fails for it with `lesson_unavailable` (state `store_tampered`), and no claim, release, or retirement touches it.

* *One Resolver, Four States*: Listing and lookup use one resolver over the lesson store. Every listed lesson resolves by the exact id it is listed under to one of `found`, `absent`, `present_unreadable` (front matter missing or invalid; listed with its path, never omitted), or `empty_shell` (valid front matter, a body without a non-whitespace character), and a lesson whose file differs from its sidecar entry is `store_tampered`, `deleted_out_of_band` or `untracked`. `present_unreadable` is never reported as `absent`, and `empty_shell` is never a content verdict. A lesson write without valid front matter is rejected.
* *Lifecycle*: `status` is `active`, `upstream_pending`, or `retired`; the lifecycle is binary and carries no moving confidence value. Whether a lesson is being applied is its `claim`, never the location of its file.
* *Claim*: The `start.create-scope` transaction of a plan started with a lesson among its sources sets `claim{plan_id, claimed_at}` in its own transaction after it wrote the plan scope, and a lesson already claimed, retired, not resolved `found`, or in a breach is refused `lesson_unavailable` naming its state (link:../state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc[State Catalogue Specification, Bootstrap & Lessons]); a claim of a `present_unreadable` lesson is a *stale claim*, listed as such (the `stalled_lessons` harvester population) and released only by an operator act, and how a claim ends is <<lesson-claim>>.
* *arch-constraint Recurrence*: An add whose `rule` matches an active `arch-constraint` lesson reinforces it (increments `recurrence_count`, refreshes `last_seen`) instead of allocating a new lesson. The quiet-window retirement, an act of `pm-operator sweep --apply` beside the pass over technical residue (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]), never of the automatic sweep, retires every active `arch-constraint` lesson whose `last_seen` is older than the window (`retention.arch_constraint_quiet_days` of the operator's `local-config.json`, link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#retention-days[Timeouts & Budgets Specification]) with verdict `obsolete`.
* *Retirement*: A retirement sets `retired{at, by, verdict, reason, …}` and `status: retired` in the front matter in one transaction, the file staying in place; every retirement other than a claim outcome requires a `verdict` of the closed set with no default, and `completely_covered` also requires `covering_clause` and a concrete `covering_input` that the clause's worked example resolves, a whitespace-only value counting as missing, while `into` is set exactly for `merged` and `superseded`. It acts only on a lesson resolved `found` or, by an explicit operator act, on a `present_unreadable` one, which replaces the unparseable front matter by a minimal valid one (`lesson_id` from the file name, `title` and `component` `unknown`, `retired.lesson_state: present_unreadable`) and keeps the original file bytes as the body (link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-store.adoc#_the_lesson_store[`core.store` Developer Guide § The Lesson Store], link:../../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-store/079-the-lesson-store-refuses-a-blank-body-escapes-del-and-trusts-what-it-reads.adoc[ADR core-store/079]); in every other state it changes nothing and reports the state. The periodic sweep removes only the body of a retired lesson, whatever the verdict, after `retention.retired_lesson_bodies_days` (link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#retention-days[Timeouts & Budgets Specification]).
* *Lesson Maintenance*: Only a recipe the operator starts maintains the lesson store (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/05-starting-work.adoc#UC-STA-5[UC-STA-5]): its `lessons.review` task submits merge, supersede, and retire judgments through the server, each one transaction under the lesson store lock on unclaimed lessons resolved `found` (link:../domain-extensions/05-recipes-and-extension-points.adoc[Domain Extensions Specification, Recipes & Extension Points]). `submit merge (ids, into)` appends the merged lessons to `merged_from[]` of `into`, replaces its body as a new revision and retires each merged lesson with verdict `merged` and `into`; `submit supersede (id, by)` retires `id` with verdict `superseded` and `into: by`; `submit retire (id, verdict)` retires with the given verdict. A claimed lesson is refused `lesson_unavailable`.
* *No Bulk Mutation*: The lesson store is never wiped or bulk-mutated; no bulk-mutation verb exists. A removal answering `absent` is not proof that the lesson never existed and is never retried automatically; it is reported together with the store-resolution discriminator.
* *Version Provenance*: The runtime stamps `tool_version` from its own release version (`PM-DIST-1`) at creation; it is never a `submit` parameter.
* *Provenance*: Every new lesson records `provenance` (`run_artifact`, `tool_return`, or `operator_action`); the server sets it where the creating transition determines it (a recurring-waiver lesson of `lessons-capture` is `run_artifact`) and otherwise requires it as a `submit` parameter.
* *Derived from Untrusted Text (`PM-SEC-1`)*: The server sets `derived_from_untrusted` to `true`, never back to `false`, on a lesson or revision whose writing task was given text marked untrusted, whose plan counts as having read third-party content (link:../model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#third-party-content[Model Work Specification, Third-Party Content on This Machine]), or which cites a lesson that is untrusted or whose trust cannot be established (a cited lesson that does not exist is read as no evidence and marks nothing), and such a lesson is not trusted (link:../model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[Model Work Specification, What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]; the citation states: link:../../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-store/079-the-lesson-store-refuses-a-blank-body-escapes-del-and-trusts-what-it-reads.adoc[ADR core-store/079]). The mark is carried by every later reader: chosen as the source of a start it is screened as a source like any external text and stays marked untrusted (link:../state-catalogue/09-bootstrap-and-lessons.adoc#state-catalogue-source[State Catalogue Specification, Source Units]), the lesson review reads it marked untrusted (link:../model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[Model Work Specification, What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]), and the clients show the mark (link:../state-catalogue.adoc#chat-context[State Catalogue Specification, What a Chat Knows]).
* *Remedy*: Every lesson states what would have prevented its mistake in `remedy`, a parameter of `submit:lesson`. A mistake a machine can detect (a fixed pattern, a banned API, an import or file-location rule, a format) takes `check`, and `wire_check` when a check that would have caught it already exists but ran later than the task or not at all, naming that check; a `wire_check` lesson never adds a written rule beside the check. A judgment call takes `review_standard`, a rule for the review roles rather than for the writer, and missing information takes `pointer`. A plan started with the lesson among its sources carries the remedy in its intent as the change to make.
+
The front matter refuses a `wire_check` without `check` and a `check` set for any other kind (detail `remedy_check_mismatch`).
* *Audience Classification*: A lesson's `audience` is `upstream` iff its `component` names a component shipped by the `PM-MCP` distribution (a built-in skill, recipe, or finalize-step identifier from the compiled-in catalogue); every other component, including a project-prefixed one (`api-sheriff:maven-build`) and one without a bundle prefix (`integration-tests`), is `local`. The classification never inspects the repository layout, so a consumer's own prefixed component stays `local`.
* *Upstream Release (`PM-ARCH-2`)*: An `upstream` lesson is created `upstream_pending` and stays in the local store; nothing transmits it automatically. It leaves the workstation only through the operator action `pm-operator lesson release --lesson <lesson_id>`, which renders the issue body for review and hands it to the operator, who files it on the vendor's tracker; the runtime writes to no tracker, and the lesson stays `upstream_pending` (filing by the runtime: link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/later-improvements.adoc#L-87[Later Improvements, L-87]). A `local` lesson is never offered this action; naming one is refused with `audience_mismatch`, which states the lesson's audience and leaves it unchanged.
** *Rendering*: The issue body is rendered from the stored record only: body, `component`, producing `plan_id`, and the recorded `tool_version`, never values sampled at release time.
** *Duplicates*: Duplicate detection keys on (`component`, normalized `rule`) for `arch-constraint` lessons and on (`component`, normalized `title`) for every other category, never on `component` alone (two lessons on the same component are usually different defects); a `no_duplicate` verdict carries the compared population (count and ids).
** *Corpus Routing Campaign*: A routing run over an existing lesson corpus (the lesson maintenance recipe) records one disposition per lesson (`routed`, `reclassified`, or `retired` through the in-file retirement with its mandatory `reason` and `verdict`, *Retirement*); re-running on unchanged input is a no-op keyed on the duplicate key (*Duplicates*); when the population differs between derivation and action, the run reports the delta (added and removed ids) and never adopts it silently.
* *Promotion Provenance*: `promotions[]` records every destination that absorbed the lesson. A routing sweep or the `lesson-cleanup` harvester reports a lesson with a matching destination as `already_promoted` and never routes it again as new.

[#lesson-claim]
=== A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-31[PM-WF-31: Lessons]_
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-IMPL-7[PM-IMPL-7: Data Store Invariants, Footprint Reconciliation & Lock Ordering]_

The claim of a lesson (<<lesson-record>>, Claim) ends by whether its plan landed its changes, never by the outcome's name alone:

* *Retired when the plan lands its changes*: the merge of `landing.pull-request`, the push of `landing.main` on the base branch, or the write of `landing.files` by `finalize.land-files` into the main checkout (link:../workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL Specification, Plan Behaviour Axes]); the lesson is retired with verdict `applied`, `by` the `plan_id`, and the claim is cleared: the link to the plan stays in `retired.by`, which is what reach is computed from.
* *Released on every other end*: `returned`, `handed_back` (whatever its cause, except a `landing.files` plan whose `finalize.land-files` wrote its changes), `no_change`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released` with its project, or `aborted`: the claim is released (`claim: null`) and the lesson stays `active` (link:../state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#plan-end[State Catalogue Specification, Cancelling and Abandoning Archive]).
* The end of a claim is a lesson store transaction after the plan's terminal transaction, and recovery retires or releases a claim whose plan is terminal, archived, or no longer resolvable per that plan's recorded outcome and landing (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal (`mcp/transaction-journal.json`)], Writes outside the scope, Plan end). The end of a claim and its release are authorized by the durable `claim.plan_id` alone; a repeated call changes nothing and reports the state.
