[#job-record]
== Job Record (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/jobs/<job_id>.json`)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/job-runtime.adoc#PM-WATCH-JOB-23[PM-WATCH-JOB-23], link:../../implementation-watch/job-runtime.adoc#PM-WATCH-JOB-41[PM-WATCH-JOB-41]_

A job that must wait for a build slot or a worker that must wait for a model slot (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#machine-lease-stores[Machine Lease Stores]) is recorded `queued` first and becomes `running` in the transaction that acquires its slot; the write-ahead record of an in-process `git` or `ci` operation of the native variant is written before the operation starts (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#in-process-operations[Job Runtime Specification, In-Process Operations]); and a job enters `active_jobs` only once its post-spawn record is written.

*Write order at job end*: The fixed order in which a settling job writes its fate, a worker's metrics row of the attempt that ended with it, the change-ledger row, the timing observation, and the slot release, and what a crash in between loses, is owned by link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#outcome-derivation[Job Runtime Specification, Outcome Derivation] (Write order at job end). For a worker, the fate, the fence of its generation, and the revocation of its token are one write of its job record; the release of the lease of a task it held follows as a write of the queue task record (xref:16-task-queue.adoc#queue-task-record[Queue Task Record]), in the fixed order of xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal] (Writes outside the scope). The usage of every other attempt of a task is a metrics row of the task's scope, written when that attempt ends (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store (`metrics.json`)]).

*Recovery and log*: The project's recovery settles every record a previous runtime instance left `queued` or `running`; the raw log `<job_id>.log` lies next to the record in the `jobs/` of its store. The log of an ended job is work history: only the cleanup deletes it, once the job ended longer ago than `cleanup.logs_after_days` (the project's, else the machine's; link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#cleanup[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, One Cleanup]). The record stays after its log: the record of a job of a plan or epic goes when the cleanup deletes the archived plan or epic, the record of an ended job of the workspace scope or of a worker is technical residue that the periodic sweep removes after `retention.task_records_days` (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]).

*Workers*: A job of kind `llm` is a worker (link:../model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc[Model Work Specification, Supervision & Recycling]): the headless harness CLI of the enrolled harness of its role's effective configuration (xref:11-enrolment-store.adoc#harness-enrolment-store[Harness Enrolment Store]), launched by the supervisor with the launch contract recorded in `worker` (its composed argument vector is digested in `argv_digest` like every job's), in the working directory fixed at its launch (a plan worktree or the project checkout), and a `zero_tool` worker (the screener, the judge) or a graded worker of a qualification item whose corpus context travels in-band in the empty subdirectory `cwd/` of its job-private directory `<PM_MCP_BASE>/run/jobs/<job_id>/` (link:../filesystem-layout/02-machine-runtime.adoc#job-private-directories[Filesystem Layout Specification, Job-Private Directories]). Its raw output is the job log; the server parses the per-turn usage and the run totals from it, and, while the worker runs, the activity events its profile maps (link:../harness-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#worker-activity[Harness Profiles Specification, What a Worker Does]) into the task's activity journal (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store], `tasks/activity/`). The worker block holds the protocol state of the worker (its state, the task it holds, the skills its generation has received, its recycling counters); the usage of each attempt of a task is a metrics row of the task's scope (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store (`metrics.json`)]). A worker's generation is its `job_id`. The job token is valid only while `status` is `running` and is revoked in the fate transaction; a call over a revoked token is refused `job_ended`, and a call of a fenced generation `generation_fenced`.

== Lesson Record (project lesson store)
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-31[PM-WF-31: Lessons]_
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/credentials-and-enrolment.adoc#PM-WATCH-CLI-4[PM-WATCH-CLI-4], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-14[PM-WATCH-STORE-14], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-15[PM-WATCH-STORE-15], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-16[PM-WATCH-STORE-16], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-19[PM-WATCH-STORE-19], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-20[PM-WATCH-STORE-20], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-34[PM-WATCH-STORE-34], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-58[PM-WATCH-STORE-58]_

Lessons live in the project's lesson store in the per-project machine store and outlive the plans that create them. Each lesson is one Markdown file: YAML front matter carrying the fields below, followed by its Markdown body. The narrative stays Markdown text (`PM-IMPL-2`); only the front matter is structured. The lesson `submit` transition (link:../phase-workflows.adoc[Phase Workflows Specification], Lesson Creation & Body) writes both atomically. The store is a Ported store (`PM-TEST-1`; its legacy mapping lives in test code only, `PM-TEST-2`).

* *Placement*: The store is `<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/lessons/` (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store]): machine-local, outside the repository, resolved identically from the main checkout and every worktree, one file `<lesson_id>.md` per lesson and the lesson-store digest sidecar `.digests.json`. A lesson file is never moved and never deleted by a lifecycle transition: applying a lesson claims it, and retiring it records the retirement in its front matter. Legacy lessons never enter it: `PM-MCP` never reads the project's legacy `.plan/local/lessons-learned/` (`PM-MIG-2`), and every project starts with an empty lesson store. A lesson therefore stays on the machine that recorded it; a second machine or clone does not see it. This trade-off is accepted: lessons reach other machines only through their promotion destinations (`promotions[]`).
* *Lock*: Lesson id allocation and every write, claim, release, or retirement in the lesson store run under the lesson store lock `locks/lessons.lock` of the per-project machine store (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#lock-free-snapshot-reads[Lock-Free Snapshot Reads, Transaction Locks & Leases (`PM-IMPL-7`)]), each as one journaled transaction (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal (`mcp/transaction-journal.json`)]). The store opens no transaction and takes no lock itself: every write is a target of the caller's transaction, so two concurrent writers never allocate the same `lesson_id` and never claim the same lesson.
* *File Format*: A lesson file starts with a line `---`, followed by the front matter, a line `---`, and the Markdown body. The front matter is read through a YAML 1.2 library with anchors, aliases, tags and multiple documents refused, written as one JSON object in the order of the schema — a YAML 1.2 flow mapping — with every non-ASCII character escaped, and validated against the `LessonFrontMatter` schema below, including `format_version`, on every read and write. An instant is accepted only in the shape `Instant.toString` writes (RFC 3339, UTC, suffix `Z`), so what is read is written again byte for byte. A file without parseable front matter resolves to `present_unreadable`. The body is free Markdown; the `title` lives only in the front matter.
* *Revisions*: A change of the body replaces it wholesale and increments `revision`; the new body carries its own `sources[]` (the evidence it was regenerated from), and `revisions[]` appends `{n, at, sources, body_sha256}` for the new revision. Old bodies are not kept; `revisions[]` keeps their digests and sources, so the derivation of every revision stays stated. A body is never edited in part.
* *Lesson-Store Digest*: Every server write, claim, release, or retirement in the lesson store records the size and SHA-256 of the lesson file in `.digests.json` (the sidecar format of xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#store-digest-verification[Store Digest Verification Against Out-of-Band Edits]) in the same transaction as the lesson write. On read, a lesson whose file differs from its entry raises `integrity_breach` (kind `store_tampered`) for that lesson only; a lesson file that disappeared while its entry remains is reported as `deleted_out_of_band`, and a file without an entry is reported as `untracked`. None of these states is ever read as `absent`, and an out-of-band edit is accepted only by the operator's act `accept-edit` of the lesson in either client (`pm-operator accept-edit lesson <lesson_id>` in the terminal), which shows the path and the recorded and current size and SHA-256 before confirmation (no restore, no diff), re-baselines that lesson's entry, and records the action in `audit.jsonl`. The breach of a lesson ends only by that `accept-edit` (no abort applies to the lesson store); until then the lesson is not listed as a candidate, `source-lesson` fails for it with `lesson_unavailable` (state `store_tampered`), and no claim, release, or retirement touches it.

* *One Resolver, Four States*: Listing and lookup use one resolver over the lesson store. Every listed lesson resolves by the exact id it is listed under to one of `found`, `absent`, `present_unreadable` (front matter missing or invalid; listed with its path, never omitted), or `empty_shell` (valid front matter, a body without a non-whitespace character), and a lesson whose file differs from its sidecar entry is `store_tampered`, `deleted_out_of_band` or `untracked`. `present_unreadable` is never reported as `absent`, and `empty_shell` is never a content verdict. A lesson write without valid front matter is rejected.
* *Lifecycle*: `status` is `active`, `upstream_pending`, or `retired`; the lifecycle is binary (live or retired) and carries no moving confidence value, mirroring `plan-marshall`. Whether a lesson is being applied is its `claim`, never the location of its file.
* *Claim*: The `start.create-scope` transaction of a plan started with a lesson among its sources claims the lesson after it has written the new plan scope, as its own transaction under the lesson store lock (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal (`mcp/transaction-journal.json`)], Writes outside the scope; startup recovery claims the lesson of a non-terminal plan created from it whose claim is missing): it sets `claim{plan_id, claimed_at}`; a lesson that is already claimed, retired, not resolved `found`, or in an integrity breach is refused (`lesson_unavailable`, naming the state and, for a claim, the claiming plan), and the start returns to `start.ready` with the reason. How the claim ends is <<lesson-claim>>. An obsolete lesson never reaches a plan: the premise state of `source-lesson` retires it with verdict `obsolete` (`by`: the `entry_id`) before any plan exists, adds no source, and returns to `start.sources`; the entry goes on. A claim of a lesson resolved `present_unreadable`, which neither recovery nor an end event can read or change, is a *stale claim*: listed as such (the `stalled_lessons` harvester population), never released without an operator act.
* *arch-constraint Recurrence*: An add whose `rule` matches an active `arch-constraint` lesson reinforces it (increments `recurrence_count`, refreshes `last_seen`) instead of allocating a new lesson. The quiet-window retirement, an act of `pm-operator sweep --apply` beside the pass over technical residue (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]), never of the automatic sweep, retires every active `arch-constraint` lesson whose `last_seen` is older than the window (`retention.arch_constraint_quiet_days` of the operator's `local-config.json`, link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#retention-days[Timeouts & Budgets Specification]) with verdict `obsolete`.
* *Retirement*: A retirement (supersede, merge, remove, quiet-window retirement, the premise `retire` of a lesson source, or the end of a claim) sets `retired{at, by, verdict, reason, …}` and `status: retired` in the front matter in one transaction; the file stays in place, so no separate tombstone exists. Every retirement other than the claim outcomes requires a `verdict` from the closed set, with no default. `completely_covered` also requires `covering_clause` and a concrete `covering_input` that the clause's worked example resolves; a whitespace-only value counts as missing; `into` is set exactly for `merged` and `superseded`. A retirement acts only on a lesson resolved `found`, or on a `present_unreadable` lesson through an explicit operator action, which replaces the unparseable front matter by a minimal valid one (`lesson_id` from the file name, `title` and `component` `unknown`, `retired.lesson_state: present_unreadable`) and keeps the original file bytes as the body; in every other state it changes nothing and reports the state. The periodic sweep removes only the body of a retired lesson after `retention.retired_lesson_bodies_days`, as technical residue whatever the retirement's verdict, and keeps its front matter.
* *Lesson Maintenance*: The lesson store is maintained by a recipe the operator starts like any recipe (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/use-cases/05-starting-work.adoc#UC-STA-5[UC-STA-5]); no state of the project setup or of a start maintains it. The recipe's workflow calls the sub-workflow `lessons.pmwf`, which writes the judgments of its `lessons.review` task (role `lessons`) only through the server. The task's entity runs the multi-submit entity unit `entity-multi.pmwf`: each of the following submits is validated and persisted on its own while the entity stays active, and the closing `submit:review` ends it (link:../model-work.adoc[Model Work Specification], Entity Units). `submit merge (ids, into)` appends the merged lessons to `merged_from[]` of `into`, replaces its body as a new revision, and retires each merged lesson (verdict `merged`, `into`); `submit supersede (id, by)` retires `id` with verdict `superseded` and `into: by`; `submit retire (id, verdict)` retires with the given verdict. Each is one transaction under the lesson store lock and acts only on lessons resolved `found` and unclaimed; a claimed lesson is refused `lesson_unavailable`.
* *No Bulk Mutation*: The lesson store is never wiped or bulk-mutated; no bulk-mutation verb exists. A removal answering `absent` is not proof that the lesson never existed and is never retried automatically; it is reported together with the store-resolution discriminator.
* *Version Provenance*: The runtime stamps `tool_version` from its own release version (`PM-DIST-1`) at creation; it is never a `submit` parameter.
* *Provenance*: Every new lesson records `provenance` (`run_artifact`, `tool_return`, or `operator_action`); the server sets it where the creating transition determines it (a recurring-waiver lesson of `lessons-capture` is `run_artifact`) and otherwise requires it as a `submit` parameter.
* *Derived from Untrusted Text (`PM-SEC-1`)*: The server sets `derived_from_untrusted` when it writes a lesson or a new revision: `true` when the facts it composed for the writing task (the `lessons` task of `lessons-capture` or of the lesson review) delivered any text marked untrusted (a review comment, a bot finding, an analysis-provider message, an issue or pull request of the plan's sources, an accepted flagged text), or when a lesson in `merged_from[]` or among its `sources[]` is `true`; it never turns back to `false`, whatever a later revision reads. Such a lesson is not trusted (link:../model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[Model Work Specification, What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]): a start screens it as a source, the lesson review reads it marked untrusted, and the clients show the mark beside the lesson.
* *Remedy*: Every lesson states what would have prevented its mistake in `remedy`, a parameter of `submit:lesson`. A mistake a machine can detect (a fixed pattern, a banned API, an import or file-location rule, a format) takes `check`, and `wire_check` when a check that would have caught it already exists but ran later than the task or not at all, naming that check; a `wire_check` lesson never adds a written rule beside the check. A judgment call takes `review_standard`, a rule for the review roles rather than for the writer, and missing information takes `pointer`. A plan started with the lesson among its sources carries the remedy in its intent as the change to make.
+
The front matter refuses a `wire_check` without `check` and a `check` set for any other kind (detail `remedy_check_mismatch`).
* *Audience Classification*: A lesson's `audience` is `upstream` iff its `component` names a component shipped by the `PM-MCP` distribution (a built-in skill, recipe, or finalize-step identifier from the compiled-in catalogue); every other component, including a project-prefixed one (`api-sheriff:maven-build`) and one without a bundle prefix (`integration-tests`), is `local`. The classification never inspects the repository layout. *Deliberate change vs. `plan-marshall`*: `manage-lessons add` decides ownership by the existence of `marketplace/bundles/{prefix}`, which misfiles a consumer's own prefixed component as foreign.
* *Upstream Release (`PM-ARCH-2`)*: An `upstream` lesson is created `upstream_pending` and stays in the local store; nothing transmits it automatically. It leaves the workstation only through the operator action `pm-operator lesson release --lesson <lesson_id>`, which renders the issue body for review and either hands it to the operator to file or, on the operator's explicit confirmation, runs a filing job through the enrolled CI provider. A `local` lesson is never offered this action; naming one is refused with `audience_mismatch`, which states the lesson's audience and leaves it unchanged. A successful filing records the issue in `promotions[]` (`kind: issue`) and returns the lesson to `active`.
** *Rendering*: The issue body is rendered from the stored record only: body, `component`, producing `plan_id`, and the recorded `tool_version`, never values sampled at filing time.
** *Duplicates*: Duplicate detection keys on (`component`, normalized `rule`) for `arch-constraint` lessons and on (`component`, normalized `title`) for every other category, never on `component` alone (two lessons on the same component are usually different defects); a `no_duplicate` verdict carries the compared population (count and ids).
** *Loud, Lossless Failure*: A failed filing leaves the lesson `upstream_pending` with `last_filing_failure` (`offline`, `forbidden`, `rate_limited`, or `refused`) and the unchanged content; it never falls back to `local`.
** *Corpus Routing Campaign*: A routing run over an existing lesson corpus (the lesson maintenance recipe) records one disposition per lesson (`routed`, `reclassified`, or `retired` through the in-file retirement with its mandatory `reason` and `verdict`, *Retirement*); re-running on unchanged input is a no-op keyed on the duplicate key (*Duplicates*); when the population differs between derivation and action, the run reports the delta (added and removed ids) and never adopts it silently.
* *Promotion Provenance*: `promotions[]` records every destination that absorbed the lesson. A routing sweep or the `lesson-cleanup` harvester reports a lesson with a matching destination as `already_promoted` and never routes it again as new.

[#lesson-claim]
=== A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-31[PM-WF-31: Lessons]_
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-IMPL-7[PM-IMPL-7: Data Store Invariants, Footprint Reconciliation & Lock Ordering]_

The claim of a lesson (<<lesson-record>>, Claim) ends by whether its plan landed its changes, never by the outcome's name alone:

* *Retired when the plan lands its changes*: the merge of `landing.pull-request`, the push of `landing.main` on the base branch, or the write of `landing.files` by `finalize.land-files` into the main checkout (link:../workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL Specification, Plan Behaviour Axes]); the lesson is retired with verdict `applied`, `by` the `plan_id`, and the claim is cleared: the link to the plan stays in `retired.by`, which is what reach is computed from.
* *Released on every other end*: `returned`, `handed_back` (whatever its cause, except a `landing.files` plan whose `finalize.land-files` wrote its changes), `no_change`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `released` with its project, `aborted`, or the plan's deletion (`delete-plan`): the claim is released (`claim: null`) and the lesson stays `active` (link:../state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#plan-end[State Catalogue Specification, Cancelling and Abandoning Archive]).
* The end of a claim is a lesson store transaction after the plan's terminal transaction, and recovery retires or releases a claim whose plan is terminal, archived, or no longer resolvable per that plan's recorded outcome and landing (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal (`mcp/transaction-journal.json`)], Writes outside the scope, Plan end). The end of a claim and its release are authorized by the durable `claim.plan_id` alone; a repeated call changes nothing and reports the state.
