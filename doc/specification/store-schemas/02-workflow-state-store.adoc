== Workflow State Store (`mcp/status.json`)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-1[PM-WATCH-PHASE-1], link:../../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-40[PM-WATCH-PHASE-40], link:../../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-69[PM-WATCH-PHASE-69], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-3[PM-WATCH-STORE-3], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-48[PM-WATCH-STORE-48], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-51[PM-WATCH-STORE-51]_

Tracks the authoritative state machine position, phase, persisted sub-workflow `call_stack`, the plan's title, creation instant, and the start of its current phase, the operator's pause, active jobs and the job bound to the current job state (`active_job`), the queue tasks of the current model state (`active_tasks`), the pending condition overlay, the plan instance version in force, the remediation policy counters, the identifier counters of the scope (`counters`), the attempts of the server-rule retries and of the condition budgets counted outside a condition (`retry_counters`), pending waiver requests, recorded gaps, per-step outcome records, the plan outcome, and composite wait facts for a plan, an epic, the project's workspace scope, or the machine scope (`PM-ARCH-6`, `PM-WF-7`, `PM-WF-13`, `PM-WF-16`, `PM-WF-26`, `PM-IMPL-7`); the machine scope's store is `<PM_MCP_BASE>/machine/mcp/status.json` (link:../workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#machine-scope[Workflow DSL Specification, Machine Scope]). The plan's history (<<plan-history>>), instance files, overrides, waivers, questions, and evidence are separate stores (xref:14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#plan-instance[Plan Instance, Waivers, Questions & Evidence]). Store digests live in the sidecar `mcp/digests.json` (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#store-digest-verification[Store Digest Verification Against Out-of-Band Edits]). The values of the `state` field are catalogued in the link:../state-catalogue.adoc[State Catalogue]; a plan's `phase` is the phase sub-workflow on its call stack (xref:03-findings-and-review-records.adoc#phase-sub-workflows[Phases as Sub-Workflows]).

The schema below is the closed contract of the whole document; it is the file `status.schema.json` in `pm-core/src/main/resources/de/planmarshall/core/store/`, beside its codec `WorkflowStatusCodec`.

* *Step Records Count Firings*: Every firing of a step appends to its `firings[]`; a re-fire never replaces the history. When `VerdictCurrencyEvaluator` decides to re-fire, the step's `outcome` is set to `unverified` before the step runs and is then replaced by the new firing's terminal outcome, so a prior `done` never survives a re-fire. The current `outcome` and `head_at_completion` are always those of the last firing; no force flag exists. A requested outcome change that the engine refuses appends a firing with `refused` set and leaves no stale outcome silently current. Re-fire counts per cause are `firings` minus one, grouped by `cause`, never counted from log lines. A record without `assurance_verdict` reads `unadjudicated`, never as a pass.
* [[retry-counters]]*Retry Counters*: Every retry whose count must outlive the transaction that takes it, and that no other member counts, is counted in `retry_counters`, so a plan that crashes the runtime or meets a drain never retries beyond its budget (`PM-WF-7`). The keys and the states that count them: `sync_failed` at `finalize.merge-sync`, `merge_barrier_indeterminate` at `finalize.merge-barrier`, `merge_refused_re_enter` at `finalize.merge-refused`, `merge_queue_re_enter` at `finalize.merge-queue-exit`, `push_failed` at `git.push-retry-wait`, and `open_pr` at `finalize.open-pr` (each within the server-rule retry budget, link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#server-rule-retry[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Server-Rule Retry Budget]); `retry_once` at `remediation-<class>.conservative` and `remediation-qgate.conservative` (the conservative retry once per unit instance, link:../state-catalogue/08-remediation-units.adoc#state-remediation.conservative[State Catalogue, `remediation-<class>.conservative`]); and `condition_budget` at `finalize.adopt-blocked`, `finalize.merge-lease-interrupted`, and `finalize.archive-plan` (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#condition-budget[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Condition Budget]). The two `re_enter` rules have keys of their own because a merge can pass both states in turn, and one shared entry would be replaced at every alternation. No key exists for the budgets another member already counts: the condition overlays and the task re-offers (`pending_condition.remedy_attempts`), the watch restarts of a wait-region firing (`watch_restarts`), the failed-step re-fire (`steps.<step_id>.firings`), the outline loop-back (`outline_loop_backs`), and the remediation budgets (`policy_counters`); nor for the retries a primitive runs immediately inside one transaction (the `.gitignore` oracle of `git.artifact-escalate`, the landing delivery of `finalize.retry-emit-landing`, `fetch_failed` of `replan.integrate-base`), which a restart aborts before their outcome is written.
* [[epic-linkage]]*Origin and Epic Linkage*: Epic membership has one carrier, `origin.epic_id`: the server writes `from_epic` into the pending entry record of a plan it starts for a launchable epic row (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Pending Entry Record]), `source-epic-row` verifies the queue row, and the `start.create-scope` transaction of the plan bootstrap copies it into `origin.epic_id`; no tool parameter carries epic membership. Every consumer (landing emission, outbox link offering, epic observation) reads epic membership from `origin.epic_id` through one resolver, which reads the active epic root and the archived epic root (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout Specification, Plan & Epic Archives]) and derives `epic_linkage`: `not_orchestrated` iff `origin.epic_id` is null; `detached` iff it names a terminal epic (archived or aborted, its directory in the archived root); `indeterminate` iff it is set but the epic's header `epic.json` (xref:04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Epic Header (`epic.json`)]) is found readable in neither root; otherwise `orchestrated`. Every consumer treats `detached` like `not_orchestrated`: no landing emission to an inbox, late findings seeded as lessons, no outbox link; the plan runs to its own outcome. `indeterminate` is never read as `not_orchestrated`.
* *Amendments*: Every intent-changing answer after refinement appends an `amendments[]` entry with its `question_id`; every later representation that renders the request (outline review, `create-pr`, landing) includes the amendments in order.
* *Operator Acts in This Store*: An operator wait is a state of the scope like any other (link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Workflow DSL Specification, Waiting for the Operator]); its decision lives in the question store (xref:14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Question Record]) and a waiver request in `waiver_requests`. The operator's pause is `pause`; the operator's other acts on a plan write their own stores (waivers, xref:14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#waiver-record[Waiver Record]; overrides, xref:14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#plan-overrides-store[Plan Overrides]; cross-project access grants, xref:15-screening-and-grants.adoc#grant-record[Access Grant Record]) or end the plan (cancel, abandon, the integrity resolutions, `delete-plan`). The store holds no merge authorization.
* *What a Plan Is Doing*: A plan's activity is derived on read, never stored: `paused` while `pause.reached_at` is set, `pausing` while `pause` is set without it, else `waiting_for_operator` while its current state is an operator wait, `stalled` as the runtime derives it (link:../runtime-lifecycle/02-plan-pause-and-stalled-plans.adoc#stalled-plans[Runtime Lifecycle Specification, Stalled Plans]), and `running` otherwise. A plan in a terminal phase (`completed`, `aborted`) is not active, wherever its directory lies (link:../state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[State Catalogue, Running Work]).

[#plan-history]
=== The Plan's History (`mcp/history.jsonl`)
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11: The Operator's Two Clients & Their Contract]_

Each plan keeps an append-only history of its process in `plans/<plan_id>/mcp/history.jsonl`: one line per state entered and left, per sub-workflow called and returned, and per instance version taking force, each with its time. `mcp/status.json` records where the plan is; the history records where it has been, which branch it took, and with which outcome. The runtime derives the state of every node of the plan's process structure from it (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-resource[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, The Plan's Process as a Structure]) and announces each line as an event of the plan (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-events[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Events of the Process]).

* *Written with the transition*: every line is a target of the transaction of the transition it records (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal]), under the plan lock, following the JSONL append discipline (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#format-versioning[Format Versioning & Atomic Write Discipline], item 6), and covered by the plan's digest sidecar. A transition that leaves a state and enters the next writes both lines in one transaction; a call writes `called` and the callee's first `entered`, a return the callee's last `left` and `returned`. A condition overlay, which changes neither state nor call stack, writes no line.
* *Never rewritten, never derived elsewhere*: no line is changed or removed. The step records of `mcp/status.json` (`steps.<step_id>.firings`) stay the record of a step's firings; the history names the states, never the steps.
* *Retained and archived with the plan*: the history moves with the plan into its archive and is deleted only with it, by `delete-plan` or by the cleanup (link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#cleanup[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, One Cleanup]); the process of an ended plan is read from it.

[#titles]
=== Titles and Names
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11: The Operator's Two Clients & Their Contract]_

A client names a plan, an epic, and a project by a line a person reads, apart from the identifiers that address them.

* *A plan's title*: `title` of `mcp/status.json`, one line of at most 200 characters. The plan's creation transaction writes it from the title `start.ready` shows, proposed from the plan's sources and changeable by the operator there (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Pending Entry]); the refine phase or the light outline may replace it once by a better one in the result its task submits. No other writer exists, and the title never changes the `plan_id`. The pull request's title stays its own field (`metadata.pr_title`).
* *An epic's title*: `title` of the epic header (xref:04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Epic Header]), written at the epic's creation from `start-epic.ready`. The runtime status carries each live epic's title and `created_at` from its header, so that a client reads no ledger.
* *A project's display name*: `display_name` of the project's store identity `store.json` (xref:11-enrolment-store.adoc#project-store-identity[Project Store Identity]), derived at enrolment from the remote's owner and name and changeable by the operator in either client; the `project_id` stays the identifier.

[#phase-start]
=== The Start of the Current Phase
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11: The Operator's Two Clients & Their Contract]_

`mcp/status.json` records when its scope was created (`created_at`, written by the creating transaction and never rewritten) and when it entered its current phase value (`phase_entered_at`), so that a client shows since when a plan runs and since when it is in its phase without reading the metrics. `phase_entered_at` is set by every transition that changes `phase`: the call of a phase sub-workflow, also the call of a phase again after a loop-back and the re-entry from finalize into execute, and the entry into a terminal phase. A re-entered phase thus starts anew; the metrics keep the phase's earlier closes (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store], Re-Entry). An epic, the workspace, and the machine set it at their creation and at their end. A plan's creation instant is `created_at`, never `origin.stamped_at`.

[#merge-outcome-mapping]
=== Merge Outcome Mapping
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-19[PM-WATCH-DISP-19], link:../../implementation-watch/phase-workflows.adoc#PM-WATCH-PHASE-82[PM-WATCH-PHASE-82], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-50[PM-WATCH-STORE-50]_

The plan's `merge_outcome` (this store) and the landing's `merge_state` (link:../phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#_landing_envelope_emit_landing[Phase Workflows Specification, Landing Envelope]) are two enumerations with different consumers, derived from *one* observation through *one* table.
