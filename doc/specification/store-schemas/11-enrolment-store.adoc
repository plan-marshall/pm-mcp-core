[#enrolment-store]
== Project Enrolment Store (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/enrolments/<project_id>.json`)

The project enrolment store is implemented: one file per enrolled project with its closed, typed schema, its strict reading and canonical writing, the integrity sidecar record with the HMAC under the operator-lane key, the per-use checks, the enrolment check whose refusal blocks only its project, and the runtime's writes of `enrol`, `unenrol` and the re-baseline (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-2[PM-EXEC-2], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/concepts/enrolment.adoc[Concepts § Enrolment], link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/developer/enrolment-store.adoc[Developer Guide § Enrolment Store]). The schema is the type `ProjectEnrolment` and its codec `EnrolmentCodec` (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/developer/enrolment-store.adoc#schema-and-codec[Developer Guide § Enrolment Store, Schema and Codec]). This section keeps what is not implemented yet.

* *Operator Channel*: The file is written only by the runtime on an act of the operator: the state `project.enrol` of a project's onboarding, which either client drives (link:../credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#project-onboarding[Credentials & Enrolment Specification, Onboarding a Project as One Process]), and the direct verb `pm-operator enrol`, a later run of the onboarding's identity and enrolment states with its arguments as the answers (link:../credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#enrolment-lifecycle-release[Credentials & Enrolment Specification]); no client process writes a store. What the enrolment records was found by the onboarding's detection after the operator's consent `inspect_checkout`, the scripts' facts only after `run_checkout_scripts` (xref:12-machine-stores.adoc#consent[Consent Before Acting]); no remembered consent is held in the enrolment.
* *Verification Points*: The runtime verifies a project's file before serving the project for the first time after start and before every verify-not-resolve check (link:../credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#enrolment-integrity[Credentials & Enrolment Specification, Enrolment Integrity]). The project's recovery runs the check as its first step, which is implemented (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/concepts/runtime-lifecycle.adoc#per-project-recovery[Concepts § Per-Project Recovery]), and so does the verify-not-resolve check, as its stage 0 (link:../../../pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/job/VerifyNotResolvePipeline.java[VerifyNotResolvePipeline]); the other verification points are not wired yet. The re-baseline verb is `pm-operator accept-edit enrolment <project_id>`, which shows the recorded and current size and SHA-256 from the sidecar record before confirmation (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-accept-edit[CLI, Coordination & Security Specification, Accept-Edit]).
* *Audit Replay at Recovery*: An enrolment write owes its audit record in `enrolments/<project_id>.json.audit-intent` before the write, as a record of outcome `failed` with the reason `not_confirmed` that the completed write replaces by the record of outcome `succeeded`, and hands it over after it (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/concepts/store-integrity.adoc#audit-delivery[Concepts § Store Integrity, Audit Records of a Store Change]); a record a refused or stopped hand-over left owed is handed over by the project's recovery, as the outcome it had reached: a write that failed or stopped before its completion was recorded is never handed over as `succeeded`. The replay is implemented and wired into the recovery: step 2 of the project's recovery hands over what the project owes (`Enrolments.replayOwedAudit(projectId)`), and its background pass what projects no longer enrolled owe (`Enrolments.replayOwedAuditOfUnenrolledProjects()`): the records of a project without an enrolment file, including one that an interrupted `unenrol` left with a sidecar, are handed over by the background pass, which also removes that sidecar; it continues past a project whose intent file is refused (unreadable, another format version) or fails on I/O (it cannot be read, rewritten or removed), keeps that file and reports the first failure after the last project.
* *Refused at Enrolment*: Of the values refused at enrolment (<<enrolment-field-semantics>>), the runtime checks before it signs an enrolment, at `enrol` and at the re-baseline, and refuses as `enrolment_invalid`: a placeholder other than `{module}` in an argument vector, or `{module}` outside its module-argument group; an absolute write path that is, lies below or contains `<PM_MCP_BASE>`, judged by the real path of its longest existing ancestor with the names that do not exist yet appended, so a symbolic link into `<PM_MCP_BASE>` is caught for a path that does not exist yet, and refused as well when it holds a `..` below that ancestor or cannot be resolved; a credential-pattern build environment name (`*_TOKEN`, `*_PASSWORD`, `*_SECRET`, `*_KEY`) and `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS`, `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, `MVND_DAEMON_STORAGE` (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/concepts/enrolment.adoc#policy[Concepts § Enrolment, Policy Before Signing]); an argument schema that matches a string by a `pattern` and admits a `maxLength` above 256 code points, at its root or in one of its properties, so the string handed to the backtracking engine is bounded by the schema's admission and not only by the enrolling project's choice (link:../job-runtime/03-parsers-and-gates.adoc#ingestion-validator[Job Runtime Specification, Ingestion Validator]). Not checked yet, because this specification does not define them concretely: the shell metacharacters of an argument vector, whether the forbidden free flags apply to enrolled templates, the credential patterns beyond the four listed, a write path that is not absolute, and the unique root below.
* *Pair Write*: A write stages the enrolment file and its sidecar completely before it renames them into place, and a stopped write is completed or discarded by the project's recovery or the next write of the project (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/developer/enrolment-store.adoc#store[Developer Guide § Enrolment Store, The Store]).
* *Unique Root*: `project_root`, the canonical absolute path of the repository main checkout resolved at enrolment, is unique across all enrolment files; the operator CLI checks it at every enrolment write and the runtime at start, and a duplicate root blocks both projects with `enrolment_root_conflict` until the operator resolves it.
* *Templates Decide What Runs*: A build job runs the canonical template of its build system and verb (`build.canonical_templates`), or the compiled default when none is enrolled; there is no goal allowlist per wrapper. The typed placeholder `{module}`, the only one, is validated at job time; a verb that needs a build profile names it literally in its template (for example `"integration-tests": ["verify", "-Pintegration-tests", "-pl", "{module}"]`) (link:../domain-extensions/06-configuration.adoc#enrolled-argument-templates[Domain Extensions Specification, Tier 2]).

[#enrolment-field-semantics]
=== Field Semantics Beyond the Schema

What the enrolment verb and the job runtime do with the fields of an enrolment:

* `pilot`: set and cleared only by the operator's enrolment act (`pm-operator enrol` with or without `--pilot`); clearing it affects only later `start.create-scope` calls, live plans and epics continue (link:../credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#enrolment-lifecycle-release[Credentials & Enrolment Specification, Lifecycle gate]).
* `container_engine`: set only by `pm-operator enrol --container-engine docker` after the operator confirmed that the project's jobs then hold the authority of the Docker group (audited `container_engine_enabled`), and cleared by re-running enrol without the option (`container_engine_disabled`). `null`: no job receives `DOCKER_HOST`, `DOCKER_CONFIG`, or `TESTCONTAINERS_*`, and the engine's socket paths are outside the read set. `docker`: build jobs receive the captured container variables and every process job of the project records `confinement: partial(container_engine)` (link:../job-runtime/02-confinement-and-environment.adoc#container-engine[Job Runtime Specification, Container Engine]).
* `released_at`: written by the release of the project, an operator act of either client (`pm-operator project release <id>` in the terminal; audited), and cleared by a new enrolment of a released project (audited). While set, a request that opens a plan's or an epic's start refuses with `lifecycle_closed`, and so does `start.create-scope` (link:../credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#project-release[Credentials & Enrolment Specification, Releasing Ends Work as the Operator Ends It]).
* Argument vectors (`build`, `providers`): shell metacharacters and the forbidden free flags are refused at enrolment; the placeholder `{module}`, only inside the module-argument group, which a whole-tree job omits, is validated at job time (link:../domain-extensions.adoc[Domain Extensions Specification, Optional Module-Argument Group]). An absent entry of `build.canonical_templates` or `build.discovery_templates` means the compiled default template (link:../job-runtime.adoc[Job Runtime Specification, Canonical Build Verb Set]; link:../domain-extensions.adoc[Domain Extensions Specification, Server-Owned Module Discovery]); an absent entry of `providers` means the compiled provider verification.
* `wrappers[].pinned_files`: the files that decide which tool runs and with which flags (wrapper jar and properties, `.mvn/maven.config`, `.mvn/jvm.config`, `gradle.properties`, where present at enrolment); a listed path with `sha256: null` must stay absent.
* `host_tools[]`: the host tools of this project; a provider's CLI tool not listed here is taken from the machine's `host-tools.json` (xref:12-machine-stores.adoc#provider-defaults[Machine Provider Defaults]). `executable_path` is resolved at enrolment from the absolute directories of the operator's login `PATH`, with no `PATH` lookup at runtime; for a host tool that is a script, the job starts its `interpreter` (`ruby` for `asciidoctor`) with the script as its first argument, never the script's shebang; `version` is the version printed at enrolment and informational.
* `project_scripts`: the `interpreter`'s own digest is verified from `host_tools`; the `argument_schema` (draft 2020-12 subset: `type`, `properties`, `required`, `additionalProperties` false, `enum`, `pattern`, `minimum`, `maximum`, `maxLength`) is validated as a schema at enrolment and applied to every job request; `max_timeout_seconds` is the budget of every run of the script, with no learning and no escalation (link:../timeouts.adoc[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Job Budgets]).
* `command_budgets`: operator budgets in seconds per budget key (`<build_system>:<verb>[@<module_id>]`, `git:<operation>`, `ci:<operation>`, `llm:<role>`), clamped up to the engine or role floor, may exceed the escalation cap, recorded as `budget_derivation` `operator` (link:../timeouts.adoc[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, Job Budgets]).
* `write_paths`: build toolchain cache directories added to the Linux Landlock write set of this project's jobs (link:../job-runtime.adoc[Job Runtime Specification, Linux Filesystem Confinement]); never under `<PM_MCP_BASE>`.
* `build_environment`: the operator's login environment as the terminal client captured and sent it, never the runtime's own environment; an enrolment from the web app takes the environment the terminal client last captured on the machine and says so (link:../job-runtime.adoc[Job Runtime Specification, Job Environment]); credential-pattern variable names, `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS`, `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, and `MVND_DAEMON_STORAGE` are refused; the container variables are passed only while `container_engine` is `docker`.

[#project-store-identity]
=== Project Store Identity (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/store.json`)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/credentials-and-enrolment.adoc#PM-WATCH-CLI-23[PM-WATCH-CLI-23]_

The identity record is implemented: `store.json` with its closed, typed schema and its strict reading, written once when the store is created, under the project's workspace lock (`locks/workspace.lock`), and never rewritten, so `project_id` and `project_root` never change afterwards; a missing and an unreadable record are outcomes of their own (link:../../../pm-core/doc/developer/core-store.adoc#_project_store[Scope Stores Developer Guide § The Per-Project Machine Store], link:../../../pm-core/doc/adr/core-store/038-per-project-machine-store-with-its-identity-record.adoc[ADR core-store/038]). The schema is the type link:../../../pm-core/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/core/store/ProjectStoreIdentity.java[`ProjectStoreIdentity`] and its codec `ProjectStoreIdentityCodec`. This section keeps what is not implemented yet: the acts of enrolment, rename and unenrolment that write it.

The per-project machine store records the main checkout it belongs to, the project's display name, and its unenrolment. The enrolment creates the store and writes `store.json`. The operator renames the project in either client (`display_name`); `pm-operator project unenrol <id>` and the unenrol act of either client remove only the enrolment file and its sidecar, leave the store in place, and record `unenrolled_at`; from then on the store is an item of the cleanup, deleted whole once it has been unenrolled longer than `cleanup.unenrolled_after_days` (link:../timeouts/03-retention-host-config.adoc#cleanup[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, One Cleanup]), and never a candidate of the periodic sweep. Enrolling the same `project_id` again removes `unenrolled_at` and reuses the store only when the new `project_root` equals the recorded one; otherwise the enrolment is refused (`project_store_root_mismatch`), naming both roots. After the cleanup deleted the store, an enrolment starts a new one.

*Display name* (xref:02-workflow-state-store.adoc#titles[Titles and Names]): proposed at enrolment from the remote's owner and name (`<owner>/<name>`), changed by the operator before the enrolment or later; display only, never an identifier. The `project_id` is proposed from the remote's name, valid by the identifier grammar and unique on the machine, and confirmed by the operator.

[#harness-enrolment-store]
== Harness Enrolment Store (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/harnesses.json`)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-33[PM-WATCH-DISP-33]_

The verification of `harnesses.json` against its sidecar under a given integrity key is implemented, and the credential-store move uses it to verify the file under the moved key (link:../../../pm-runtime/src/main/java/de/planmarshall/runtime/enrolment/HarnessesSidecarCheck.java[HarnessesSidecarCheck], link:../../../pm-runtime/doc/developer/runtime-enrolment.adoc[Developer Guide § Integrity Key and Sidecars]). This section keeps what is not implemented yet: the file's schema, its writers, its verification before every worker spawn, the self-update and the tampered file.

One machine-wide file holds the harness CLIs that may run workers (job kind `llm`, xref:10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#job-record[Job Record]). A harness is enrolled per machine, not per project, by `harness.enrol` of the machine's onboarding, which records a candidate of the harness scan or a harness the operator added with a verified profile (link:../client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#harness-scan[Client Profiles Specification, Harness Scan]), or by `pm-operator enrol-harness <client_id>` (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-enrol-harness[CLI, Coordination & Security Specification, Harness Enrolment]): the runtime resolves the executable path once (no `PATH` lookup at job time), runs the profile's version probe, and records path, digest, and version, the usage report of its profile, and, where its profile declares a weights probe, the weights identity of each allowed model, and nothing else: the file holds no environment value, no variable name, and no credential, because `PM-MCP` never stores or exports a harness's authentication (the harness uses its own login). Written only by the runtime on those acts and on an accepted self-update, under `locks/harnesses.lock`, with an HMAC sidecar `harnesses.json.hmac` in the format of the sidecar of a project enrolment: a closed, versioned record with exactly `format_version` (`1`), `size` (the byte length of the canonical file), `sha256` (its lowercase-hex SHA-256) and `hmac` (the lowercase-hex HMAC-SHA-256 over its exact canonical bytes), under the same enrolment integrity key, verified before every worker spawn (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/concepts/enrolment.adoc#integrity[Concepts § Enrolment, Integrity]).

* *Self-Update Within Range*: Harness CLIs update themselves. At every spawn the runtime re-digests the executable; a changed digest is checked first against the update evidence of the client profile without running the file (link:../client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#update-evidence[Client Profiles Specification, Update Evidence]), and failed or missing evidence refuses the launch before spawn (fate `refused`, reason `harness_update_unverified`) until the operator enrols the harness again. After passed evidence the runtime probes the version; a version inside the client profile's supported version range (`headless.supported_versions`, link:../client-profiles.adoc[Client Profiles Specification]) is accepted: in the accepting transaction the runtime, the file's only writer, rewrites `harnesses.json` and its HMAC sidecar under `locks/harnesses.lock` with the new version and digest, and an `operator` audit record `harness_updated` names the old and new version and digest. A version outside the range, or a harness not enrolled, refuses the worker launch before spawn (fate `refused`, reason `harness_version_unsupported` or `harness_not_enrolled`); the refusal counts as a start failure of the role on that harness, and at the start-failure guard the role is held for the operator with the reason and the act that resolves it, a new enrolment of the harness (link:../model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#start-failure-guard[Model Work Specification, Start-Failure Guard]).
* *Tampered File*: An HMAC mismatch refuses every worker launch of that file before spawn (fate `refused`, reason `harness_tampered`, with a `security` audit record `enrolment_tampered`); each refusal counts as a start failure of the role on that harness, so the start-failure guard holds the role for the operator with `harness_tampered` and the act of a new enrolment (link:../model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#start-failure-guard[Model Work Specification, Start-Failure Guard]). A new enrolment is the re-baseline: after showing path, version, and digest of the named client for confirmation it rewrites the file and its sidecar with the named client only; entries of other clients in a file that failed its check are not carried over, and each of those clients must be enrolled again. There is no pause and no `accept-edit` form for this file; at the guard the queued tasks of the affected roles close `lost` with `role_unavailable` and take their conservative fallback (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-enrol-harness[CLI, Coordination & Security Specification, Harness Enrolment]).
