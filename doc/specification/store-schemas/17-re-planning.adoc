== Re-Planning Record (`mcp/status.json` → `replan_pending`, `replans[]`, `follow_ups[]`)
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-17[PM-WF-17: Dynamic Re-Planning of a Running Plan]_

_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-24[PM-WF-24: Chatting with the Planning Role]_

The plan's `mcp/status.json` (xref:02-workflow-state-store.adoc#_workflow_state_store_mcpstatus_json[Workflow State Store]) carries the state of its re-plannings (link:../phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#dynamic-re-planning[Phase Workflows Specification, Dynamic Re-Planning]): `replan_pending`, the trigger a checkpoint raised and the unit has not yet judged (`null` otherwise); `replans[]`, append-only, one record per judged trigger, whose evidence keys decide whether a checkpoint raises a trigger again and whose task count is charged against `replan_budget`, and one record per revision the operator accepted in a chat with the planning role (link:../state-catalogue.adoc#chat[State Catalogue, Chatting with the Planning Role]), which has no trigger and is never charged; `follow_ups[]`, append-only, the follow-up plans the re-plannings recorded; and `queued_revision`, the revision the operator accepted in a chat while it waits for the plan's next task boundary (`{chat_id, turn_id, subject: {kind, id}, chat_open, applies}`: the chat by its id, the subject that chat belongs to, the plan or a decision of it, whether that chat is still open, and the point it waits for; `null` otherwise; link:../state-catalogue.adoc#chat-proposal-states[State Catalogue, A Proposal's States]), set by `chat.apply` and cleared in the transaction that applies the revision or records it `refused` at its boundary or `lapsed`. All four are written only by the server, in the transactions of the trigger points, of the `replan` unit, and of `chat.apply`.

* *A chat's origin*: the `chat` of a record with `decided_by: operator` names the chat's task (`queue_task_id`), the chat (`chat_id`) and the turn of the accepted proposal (`turn_id`), and the operator's channel and device: the origin every change of a chat records (link:../state-catalogue.adoc#chat-origin[State Catalogue, Origin]).
* *Budget*: `replan_budget` is charged per record with `decided_by: task` or with a `queue_task_id` (a task that closed `lost` counts); a fallback taken without a task and a revision the operator accepted in a chat are not charged.
* *Rendering*: The pull-request body, the landing record, and the plan's completion report (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#reports[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Reports to the Operator]) list every record with its action and rationale, a revision of the operator's chat naming the operator as who decided, the moved deliverables, and the follow-ups with their scope and preserved ref; the epic's queue row of a follow-up carries its origin (xref:04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-queue-row[Queue Row], `follow_up`).
