[#decision-record]
== Decision-Log Record (`decision-log.jsonl`)
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-19[PM-WF-19: Uniform Decision Log & Corpus Export]_
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-46[PM-WATCH-DISP-46]_

Every decision a scope takes at a decision point is one `decided` line of the scope's decision log; a later departure from it is one `corrected` line that names it. The semantics of every field, the decision points, the causes of a correction, and the export are owned by link:../model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#decision-records[Model Work Specification, Decision Log]; this section fixes the store. The store is append-only, one file per scope: `plans/<plan_id>/decision-log.jsonl`, `epics/<epic_id>/decision-log.jsonl`, `workspace/decision-log.jsonl`, and an entry's `pending/<entry_id>/decision-log.jsonl`, which moves into the new scope at its creation (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store]; xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#pending-entry-record[Pending Entry]). A line is appended in the transaction that takes the decision or the correction, under the scope lock and covered by the scope's digest sidecar like every scope store; no line is ever rewritten or removed, except by the periodic sweep's removal of the workspace scope's technical rows, which removes the workspace scope's decisions whose facts are no longer referenced (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]).

The schema is the file `pm-core/src/main/resources/de/planmarshall/core/store/decision-record.schema.json`.

* *Identifier*: `decision_id` is the next value of a scope-wide counter, zero-padded to four digits, never reused (link:../identifiers/01-general-rules-and-registry.adoc#id-decision-id[Identifier Registry, `decision_id`]). A correction carries the `decision_id` of the decision it corrects and has no identifier of its own.
* *Question link*: A question record's `adjudicated` and `answered` lines name their decision-log records by `decision_id` (xref:14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Question Record]).
* *Interpretation*: every result of the interpreting role on an operator's own words is one `decided` line of the decision point `decision.interpret` (subject `question` with the `question_id`, decider `role` `interpret`, `choice` one of `option`, `option_with_condition`, `own_option`, `delegation`, `unclear`, with the server's checks among the measurements), appended in the transaction that ends the interpreting task, whether or not the operator confirms it at the read-back (link:../model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#decision-points[Model Work Specification, Decision Points]).
* *Untrusted text*: `rationale` of a role or a vote is model-authored text, and the operator's words of an answer are the operator's own and never screened; each is shown as text, never as markup, and reaches a model only inside the facts of a later task, quoted as data; the `rationale` of a role whose facts snapshot (<<facts-snapshot-store>>) holds an item marked untrusted reaches it also in the spotlighting delimiters and marked untrusted, as a lesson derived from untrusted text does (link:../model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[Model Work Specification, What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]).
* *Shown to the operator*: the decisions taken in a plan are listed in its completion report (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#reports[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Reports to the Operator]).
* *Retention*: The decision log and the facts snapshots are part of the scope's store: a plan's move to the archive with `archive-plan`, its deletion by the cleanup the operator confirms, and `delete-plan` take them along (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout Specification, Plan & Epic Archives]; link:../timeouts/03-retention-harness-config.adoc#cleanup[Timeouts & Budgets Specification, One Cleanup]). An epic's stores go with its machine directory `epics/<epic_id>/`, deleted by the cleanup once the epic is archived; in the workspace scope, decisions whose facts are no longer referenced are removed by the periodic sweep with their facts snapshots after `retention.workspace_rows_days` (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]).

[#decider-kind]
=== Decider Kind

Who took a choice is named by one closed set, defined here and used by every record that names a decider: the decision log's `decider.kind`, the `decider` of an intent record's `choices[]` (xref:06-task-and-references.adoc#intent-record-store[Intent Record]), and the `source` of an intent amendment (xref:02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Workflow State Store], `amendments[]`). Each record admits the subset its own schema lists.

[cols="1,4,2"]
|===
|Kind |Who took the choice |Used by

|`operator`
|The operator: an answer in either client (channel `web` or `cli`), or on a corrected line the waiver that overrode a gate.
|decision log, choice, amendment

|`quorum`
|A quorum of roles whose votes agreed in the adjudication (channel `quorum`).
|decision log, choice, amendment

|`role`
|A task of a role, by its own judgment: a role decision at a decision point, a role's own call, a model judgment such as `continue_narrowed`.
|decision log, choice, amendment

|`server_rule`
|A server rule that compares a measured value with a threshold or applies a fixed rule.
|decision log

|`fact`
|Evidence, not a judgment: what exploring found (a path or `pm://` URI), an in-triage refutation, or the premise evidence of a lesson source.
|choice, amendment
|===

[#facts-snapshot-store]
=== Facts Snapshot (`decision-facts/<facts_digest>.toon`)

The facts of a judgment decision (link:../model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#facts-snapshot[Model Work Specification, Facts Snapshot]) are written once per distinct content to `decision-facts/<facts_digest>.toon` of the scope's store, where `facts_digest` is the SHA-256 of the canonical TOON text the file holds; the file is immutable, an existing file is never written again, and the digest sidecar covers it. A snapshot whose task closed without a decision (a task `lost`) is kept with the others; snapshots go only with their scope's store, or in the workspace scope with their decisions (<<decision-record>>, Retention).
