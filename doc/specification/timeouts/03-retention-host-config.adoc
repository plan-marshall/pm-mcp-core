[#cleanup]
== One Cleanup
_See Requirement link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-10[PM-ARCH-10: One Cleanup of Work History & Automatic Removal of Residue]_

Work history is the operator's and goes only on the operator's word, through one cleanup the operator confirms in either client; technical residue needs no judgement and goes by itself (<<retention-days>>; link:../runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#periodic-sweep[Runtime Lifecycle, Periodic Sweep]).

* *Work history*: an archived plan with its whole archive entry (records, history, decisions, transcripts, metrics rows, and the workspace's metrics rows of the plan); an archived epic's machine store `epics/<epic_id>/`; the logs of ended work (the logs of ended jobs and the worker logs of archived plans; a job record stays with its plan); read reports (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#reports[Runtime Model, Reports to the Operator]); and the machine store of an unenrolled project, whole. No sweep and no retention period ever removes it.

[cols="2,3,2,2,2,3,2"]
|===
|Name |Value / derivation |Configurable? |Floor / cap |Applies to |Behaviour on expiry |Defined for

|[[cleanup-durations]]Cleanup durations
|In days, each `30` by default: `cleanup.plans_after_days` (archived plans), `cleanup.epics_after_days` (archived epics' machine stores), `cleanup.logs_after_days` (logs of ended work), `cleanup.reports_after_days` (read reports), `cleanup.unenrolled_after_days` (stores of unenrolled projects). An item is due when its end (its archiving, its reading, the project's unenrolment) is older than its duration.
|machine values in `machine-config.json` `cleanup`; a project's own value of the first three in its `local-config.json` `cleanup`, which wins for its items; reports and unenrolled projects belong to the machine only (link:../store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#project-levels[Store Schemas, Project Values over Machine Values])
|an integer of at least `7`, or `never`; the floor keeps every window of the system view (at most seven days) untouched by a deletion
|the work history above
|Not an expiry: a due item joins the next cleanup job, and nothing is deleted before the operator decides it.
|this section

|[[cleanup-remind]]Cleanup reminder
|`3` days after a _remind_.
|`cleanup.remind_after_days` in `machine-config.json`
|at least `1`
|a postponed cleanup job

|this section
|===

* *Never due*: live work, a pending entry, an unread report, an archived plan with an undischarged owed item (a retained capture ref, link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-drop-capture[CLI & Security, Capture Ref Removal]), a kept item, and an item of an open or postponed job.
* *The check*: the first pass of the periodic sweep after the start and the first of each new UTC day compute the items due. When there are items not yet in a job and no job is open or postponed, they form a new job, fixed from then on, and the runtime writes one report `system.cleanup_due` (`from` the machine) with the counts per kind, whose detail is the job; one report per job, never one per item. Items that become due while a job is open or postponed wait for the next job.
* *The journal*: one append-only journal of the machine scope, `<PM_MCP_BASE>/state/cleanup.jsonl` (link:../filesystem-layout/02-machine-runtime.adoc#machine-layout[Filesystem Layout, Machine-Wide Runtime Layout]), with its own leaf lock like `state/reports.jsonl`. Its events: `job` (the job with its items: kind, id, project, title, outcome, ended at, size, the duration and its source), `decided` (`delete` with the unchecked items, `remind` with when it comes back, or `ignore`; the channel, the device, and when), `deleted` and `delete_failed` per item (with the reason), and `kept` and `released` per item. A job's state (`open`, `postponed`, `deleting`, `done`) and the kept list are the fold of its events. A job is identified by its `job_id`, store-assigned `cln-NNNN` and never reused (link:../identifiers/02-registry-stores-to-web.adoc#id-cleanup-job-id[Identifier Registry, Cleanup Job Identifier]); it is no job of the job runtime: it starts no process and has no job record.
* *The decisions*, operator acts of either client: _delete_ deletes the checked items and keeps the unchecked ones for good; _remind_ deletes nothing and, after the reminder, opens a new job of the same items with a new report; _ignore_ deletes nothing and keeps every item for good. The first decision of a job is the one taken; a later one on a decided job is answered with the decision taken, unchanged. A released kept item is due again at the next check.
* *Deleting*: item by item, each in its own journaled transaction with one audit record `cleanup_deleted` (category `store`; the kind, the id, the project, `job_id`, the channel, the size, link:../store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Store Schemas, Audit Log]). An archived plan's directory `archive/<plan_id>/` is replaced by a tombstone `archive/<plan_id>/deleted.json` (its `plan_id`, title, outcome, when it ended, `deleted_at`, `job_id`), so its id is never allocated again, ended work leaves it out, and its reports' detail resolves to "deleted" with the date; an archived epic's `epics/<epic_id>/` likewise by `epics/<epic_id>/deleted.json` (link:../filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout, Archives]). Read reports leave `state/reports.jsonl` by a journaled rewrite under its lock; their ids are never reused. An unenrolled project's store goes as a whole; enrolling the project again starts a new store. Before a plan's metrics rows go, what it used is added to the machine's usage aggregate `<PM_MCP_BASE>/state/usage-aggregate.jsonl` (per day, project, model, and role, in tokens with their coverage), from which the system view's figures are filled at the start beside the metrics stores, so no figure goes down (link:../runtime-lifecycle/03-runtime-metrics.adoc#runtime-metrics[Runtime Lifecycle, The Runtime's Metrics]). An item no longer due (a project enrolled again) is skipped and named; a failure keeps the item with its reason for the next job. A deletion cut by a stop goes on after the next start from the journal.
* *Unenrolment*: `pm-operator project unenrol <project_id>` (or the same act of the web app) records `unenrolled_at` in the store's `store.json` (link:../store-schemas/11-enrolment-store.adoc#project-store-identity[Store Schemas, Project Store Identity]); enrolling again removes it. The store is a cleanup item from then on, never residue.
* *Local API*: `GET /api/v1/system/cleanup` answers the open or postponed job (its summary with the counts per kind and the space it frees, its items by project, each linked read-only to its ended view), the durations with their sources, the count of kept items, and what the automatic pass of the periodic sweep removed and when; `POST /api/v1/system/cleanup/{job_id}/delete` with the unchecked items, `…/remind`, and `…/ignore` decide the job; `GET /api/v1/system/cleanup/kept` lists the kept items and `PUT /api/v1/system/cleanup/kept` writes the list, an item left out being released. Each write is privileged (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/cli-and-security/02-operator-channel.adoc#operator-channel-isolation[CLI & Security, Operator Channel Isolation]), accepted from `pm-operator` (`pm-operator cleanup [--delete|--remind|--ignore] [--yes]`) and from a paired device, and audited with its channel. The runtime-wide stream announces `cleanup.job_opened`, `cleanup.decided`, `cleanup.progress`, and `cleanup.done` (link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/runtime-model/06-event-catalogue.adoc[Runtime Model, Event Catalogue]).
* *Recurring waivers* are counted over the `waiver_waived` records of the audit log of the last 90 days, whatever has been deleted since (link:../phase-workflows/02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc[Phase Workflows, Planning Lanes & Phases 3–4], Recurring Waivers).

[#size-limits]
== Worker Input Size Limits

Two compiled size bounds and one per-harness limit shape what a worker receives in-band. The two compiled bounds are _provisional_ until calibrated against the context windows of the supported harnesses; the measured basis so far is a task input of 128 000 characters completed on `claude` and of 32 000 characters on `opencode`. This table is the one place the calibrated values are recorded. The table uses the columns above; a size bound has no expiry, so that column states what happens above the bound.

[cols="2,3,2,2,2,3,2"]
|===
|Name |Value / derivation |Configurable? |Floor / cap |Applies to |Behaviour above the bound |Defined for

|[[prompt-in-band-bound]]Prompt in-band skill bound
|Provisional `262144` bytes of rendered skill content per prompt file (UTF-8), counted in composition order.
|fixed
|—
|The launch prompt of every worker except the screener, and the skills an offer carries
|The remaining skills are rendered as their section index with the URIs that `pm_skill` serves; the worker is never refused for size, and a submit before it has loaded every required skill is rejected `skills_not_loaded` with the missing URIs (link:../model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc#skill-delivery[Model Work Specification, Skill Delivery Record]).
|link:../model-work/04-worker-launch.adoc[Model Work Specification, Worker Launch]

|[[screening-split-bound]]Screening split bound
|Provisional `65536` bytes of level-1-cleaned item text per screener part (UTF-8, delimiters included); a single item above the bound forms a part of its own.
|fixed
|—
|Screener tasks (role `screener`) over a `batch-v1` batch
|The batch's uncached items are split into consecutive parts in item order, one screener task each: the screener state is a fan-out `job llm` state (`job llm screen role screener each screen.batch-parts`, over the server-computed list of parts), which completes when every part has its verdict.
|link:../model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc[Model Work Specification, Screener]

|[[tool-result-limit]]Harness tool-result limit
|`headless.tool_result_limit_chars` of the worker's client profile: the largest tool result the harness passes to its model whole. `opencode`: `32000` characters (the harness cuts a larger result and keeps the full text in a file a restricted worker cannot read; its inline settings set its own limit to 128 000 bytes, of which a quarter is the character count no UTF-8 result can exceed, link:../client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#headless-inline-settings[Client Profiles, Inline Settings]); `claude`: `null` (no limit; 128 000 characters completed).
|client profile (compiled)
|minimum `4000`
|Every tool result the server returns to a worker of that harness: the offer answering `pm_wait`, entity representations, skills, and resource reads

[#configuration-keys]
== Configuration Keys

The configurable values above, by store:

* `machine-config.json` (the operator's acts in either client, link:../store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#machine-config[Store Schemas, Machine Configuration Store]): `drain_timeout_seconds`, `escalation_cap_seconds`, `model_slots`, `progress_wait_max_seconds`, `lsp_idle_timeout_seconds`, `lsp_start_timeout_seconds`, `lsp_request_timeout_seconds`, `audit_rotation.max_days`, `audit_rotation.retain_days`, `client_profiles.<client_id>.connection.{bounded_wait_seconds, idle_timeout_seconds, progress_resets_timeout}` (machine-wide overrides of the compiled client profile values), `project_defaults.retention.*` (machine-wide defaults of the per-project retention keys), and `cleanup.*` (the cleanup durations and the reminder, <<cleanup>>). The `proxy` and `web` objects of the same file hold no timeout.
* `<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/local-config.json` (the operator's acts in either client, link:../store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#local-config[Store Schemas, Operator Local Configuration]): `retention.*`, the project's own `cleanup.plans_after_days`, `cleanup.epics_after_days`, and `cleanup.logs_after_days`, and, per upstream server, `upstream_mcp.servers.<server_id>.connect_timeout_seconds` and `read_timeout_seconds` (written by the operator's act `pm-operator upstream allow` or its form in the web app, link:../../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/mcp-tools.adoc[MCP Tools Specification, Upstream MCP Proxy]; <<host-integration>>, Outbound HTTPS and upstream MCP call timeouts).
* Project-owned phase units `.marshall/config/*.pmwf` (team, link:../workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#project-owned-units[Workflow DSL Specification, Project-Owned Units]): the remediation call arguments `retry_budget` and `fix_budget`, within the compiled hard caps (xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#remediation-budgets[Remediation Budgets]); a plan's effective ceiling is raised only by a `raise` waiver. The declarations of `replan.pmwf` (re-planning thresholds, budget, prerequisite wait) and `adjudicate-decisions.pmwf` (the decision declarations, `ask` or a quorum per decision kind, the vote wait, and the calibration level), within their compiled bounds (link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#unit-declarations[Workflow DSL Specification, Unit Declarations]).
* `enrolments/<project_id>.json` (the operator's acts, link:../store-schemas/11-enrolment-store.adoc#enrolment-store[Store Schemas, Project Enrolment Store]): `command_budgets` (operator budgets per budget key, `llm:<role>` of workers included), `project_scripts.<project_script_id>.max_timeout_seconds`.
* Client profile (compiled; the operator override is the `client_profiles` object of `machine-config.json` above, for the `connection` keys only): `connection.bounded_wait_seconds`, `connection.idle_timeout_seconds`, `connection.progress_resets_timeout`, and the worker values `worker.ack_deadline_seconds`, `worker.ack_informational`, `worker.silence_grace_seconds`, `worker.idle_wakeups` (xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#worker-supervision[Worker Supervision & Recycling]).
* Role artifacts (operator files of the role sets, link:../model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc[Model Work Specification, Roles & Configuration]): `budget_floor_seconds` (xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#model-job-budget[Worker budget]), `recycling.idle_wakeups`, `recycling.token_budget`, `recycling.tasks_per_generation`, and the lowered thresholds of the off-track signals (xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#off-track-thresholds[Off-track signal thresholds]).

No team configuration file (`.marshall/config/*.json`) holds a timeout, wait, threshold, or retention value; the remediation budgets are unit call arguments, not configuration keys. Tuning values that calibrate server algorithms are compiled constants (`fixed` above, and in the owning specification: the lane prune thresholds, the refine confidence threshold `95`, the iteration caps, the step wait timeouts, the preference recurrence threshold, the untrusted raw-input byte cap, the review rate-window floor `3600`, the re-review completion allowance `600`, the default review attempt ceiling `6`, the provider re-poll cadence `30`, the merge-queue stall fallback `3600`, the native request timeouts, the attempt caps of `git.commit_and_push` (xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#commit-attempt-caps[Commit sub-process attempt caps], xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#commit-fix-change-set-cap[`git.commit-fix` change-set cap]), the language server restart cap (xref:02-runtime-leases-operator-web.adoc#lsp-restart-cap[Language server restart cap]), the compiled minimum of the role floors (xref:01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#model-job-budget[Worker budget]), the start-failure guard, the task re-offer budget, the hard caps of the remediation budgets, the condition budget, the watch restart budget, the failed-step re-fire, the release landing wait, the off-track thresholds as caps, the event buffer, the metrics event interval, and the update check interval); per-project tuning would make metrics incomparable across projects (`PM-MIG-5` triggers).

A value outside its floor or cap is never used: the runtime clamps it to the bound and reports the configured value, the used value, and the source (`machine_config`, `local_config`, `enrolment`, `profile_override`, `compiled_default`, `invalid`, or `unreadable`) in the runtime status and in the record of the affected job (`clamped_values`, link:../store-schemas/10-job-and-lesson-records.adoc#job-record[Store Schemas, Job Record]) or wait, never in its facts: the model cannot change an operator value.
