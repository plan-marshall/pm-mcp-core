== Overview

This document specifies how *plan-marshall-mcp* (`PM-MCP`) gets model work done inside the plans and epics it drives. Every state that needs a model creates a task of a role; a worker of that role does it through the task protocol. A worker is a headless harness process that the runtime launches as a job of kind `llm`, supervises, watches for the off-track signals, recycles, and replaces; a harness is a worker only and never a channel of the operator, who acts only in the two clients.

The parts specify the role artifacts, the role sets, the role assignment and the default assignment, the one model caller, role qualification and the conservative fallback; the task protocol (`pm_wait`, offer, acknowledgement, submit, leases per generation, fencing, the `worker-task` sub-workflow); supervision, the off-track signals, and recycling; the launch of a worker and the output read while it runs; consultation between roles through the server; task envelopes, evidence in facts, and the decisions the operator answers, in own words where allowed; the recommendation score of questions and their adjudication by declarations per decision kind (`ask` or a quorum of roles); the uniform decision records of every decision point and their export as corpus items; the trust of local content and the screening of untrusted text; epic coordination with its review, its actions, and its registers; and task completion, usage in tokens, and spend classes. The measurements behind the values are recorded in the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/evaluation.adoc[Evaluation Reference]; the principles in the link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/concepts/harness-as-worker/README.adoc[Harness as Worker] concept.

== Modules

Implemented in (link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc[Module Structure Specification]):

* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc#module-pm-core[`pm-core`]: part 01 (the role artifact schema, the shipped, machine, and project role sets with their override, the role assignments and the default assignment, validation against the client profiles and the model catalogue, and the qualification records) and part 02 (the task queue store: queue task records, task leases)
* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc#module-pm-workflow[`pm-workflow`]: parts 01 (the one model caller, the composition check, and the conservative fallback), 02 (the task protocol unit `worker-task`: offers, acknowledgement, leases and fencing, task outcomes and re-offers), 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, and 11: the model states, their tasks and return evaluation, question records and their adjudication, decision records, consultation, screening and triage states, the decision and epic sub-workflows (`decision`, `decomposition`, `review`), epic coordination, and usage reporting with its spend classes
* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc#module-pm-runtime[`pm-runtime`]: the supervisor of part 03 with the off-track signals, and the worker launch of part 04 beside the job runner (harness templates, job token and generation, launch prompt, model slots, the output read while a worker runs)
* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc#module-pm-mcp-server[`pm-mcp-server`]: the serving of `pm_wait` of part 02 and the per-request binding of the job token and generation of part 04
* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc#module-pm-relay[`pm-relay`]: parts 02 and 04: the job relay `pm-mcp serve --job` a worker connects back through, which carries the worker's generation
* link:../../../plan-marshall-documentation/doc/specification/module-structure.adoc#module-pm-operator[`pm-operator`]: part 01: the verbs `pm-operator roles list`, `show`, and `qualify`; part 11: the verb `pm-operator decisions export`
