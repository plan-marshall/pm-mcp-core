## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/role-qualification.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated by cleanup at 99971f3 from the package file).

1. Grading a role's configuration against the model verification corpus, its record bound to the qualification digest, its invalidation, and the conservative fallback of an unqualified or unavailable role.
2. The digest over the effective `harness`, `model`, and `effort` (from `role-sets-and-model-caller`) with `tools`, `class`, the skill digests, and the harness major.minor, so a changed assignment or plan override makes the record stale per project; every record the operator makes written to the project set. A plan override without a current record is shown unqualified and its tasks take the conservative fallback. The `judge` and the `steward` are exempt.
3. The record: `total_tokens` per item and per form, the open `harness` pattern, and the required `missed_sources`. A grading starts only behind the operator's consent with the expected tokens shown, a declined one recorded as owed; the offer and the consent are `project-onboarding-unit`'s and `pm-operator roles qualify`'s.
4. The corpus is completed from its sources before grading, through `corpus-sources`: on a failed import no role is qualified; a grading on a partial corpus records the missed sources, and a role counts as qualified only on what was graded.
5. The corpus itself and its validation by the build are `plan-marshall-mcp` / `qualification-corpus-in-the-build`.
6. Agreement per topic: the record keeps per form and per topic (`own` and `generic`, defined by the corpus items' `role` field) the agreement and the item count in `topics` (`topicResult`), never a pooled agreement; `warm_fresh_gap` is the largest over the topics of the fresh minus the warm agreement, and the own items of the review roles include planted defects at their correct severity, among them a defect that every criterion and every test accepts but that a constraint `CN-<n>` of the intent record rules out. A record is valid, in the fresh form, only when every topic of the record reaches the project's `qualification.agreement_level` (read at every check, so a changed level applies to existing records) and the record holds a result for each of those topics; a role whose `generic` topic reaches it and whose `own` topic does not is not valid, and neither is the reverse; a role without items of its own is valid on the `generic` topic alone, and a `zero_tool` role without items of its own has no topics and cannot qualify (Model Work § Corpus Topics of a Role; § Qualification Records; Store Schemas § Role Qualification Record; § Operator Local Configuration; Implementation Watch § Model Work).

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): A changed member of the digest invalidates the record, a changed role assignment or plan override included; an unqualified role takes its conservative fallback; a grading run over a fixture corpus with a scripted harness writes the specified record with `total_tokens`; a grading over a failed import writes no valid record, and one over a partial import names its missed sources. A record whose `generic` topic reaches the level and whose `own` topic does not is not valid, and neither is the reverse; a record that lacks a topic of the corpus is not valid; a record whose every topic reaches the level is valid; a role without items of its own is valid on the `generic` topic alone, and a `zero_tool` role without items of its own cannot qualify; `warm_fresh_gap` is the largest over the topics of the fresh minus the warm agreement; each topic's item count is recorded beside its agreement, and a changed agreement level applies to an existing record at its next check without re-grading; the review roles' own items include a defect that every criterion and every test accepts but a constraint `CN-<n>` rules out, and an answer that grades such a plan clean disagrees with the reference.
