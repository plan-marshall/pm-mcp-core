## Objective

Implement the work package `worker-activity` of `doc/plans/worker-activity.adoc`: what a worker does, and a worker off track. Serves: Roadmap Milestone 4 (the worker activity and the off-track signals); the activity of a task below a plan or an epic (Use Cases, UC-PLN-1) in both clients of `pm-mcp-clients`. Where: pm-core (the activity kinds of the profile schema and the mapping `headless.events`, the activity journal in the store of the task's scope); pm-runtime (the mapping of a worker's output stream while it runs, the off-track signals and the server's actions, the activity endpoints and the stream of one task). The package file is the brief; this spec adds what the orchestrator observed and the constraints of the epic.

## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/worker-activity.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated by cleanup at 99971f3 from the package file).

1. Implements Client Profiles § What a Worker Does, Model Work § A Worker Off Track, and Store Schemas § Activity Journal (`PM-WORK-7`).
2. Events, mapped per harness: `headless.events` maps a line of the worker's output stream onto the closed, harness-neutral activity kinds `text` (cut to the compiled bound), `tool_call` (the tool's name and its target, never its whole arguments), `tool_result` (its size and whether it failed, never its content), `file_changed`, and `turn` (its usage through the usage pointers of `token-usage`); a line that matches no mapping is counted, not shown; the server adds the entries it knows itself (the links the worker followed, its questions and answer acknowledgements, the off-track signals and actions).
3. The activity journal: per task, append-only in the store of the task's scope, written while the worker runs, masked, bounded, archived with the plan and deleted with it; untrusted data, shown as plain text and never read by a model as instructions.
4. The off-track signals: computed by the runtime from the activity journal and the task's records, never by a model (`repeating`, `no_progress`, `over_size`, `outside_footprint`), each against its compiled threshold, which a role artifact may lower in its `off_track` member and never raise, evaluated at every activity entry in the transaction that appends it, at most once per attempt and plan task; a signal is no limit and ends no plan; the action the role artifact chooses from the closed list (`show`, the default, `nudge`, `yield`, `restart`), recorded in the queue task's `signals[]` and as a `signal` entry of the activity journal, a `yield` or `restart` reported `task.off_track` (`operator-reports`); a running plan's `off_track` with its signal in the project's resource (`process-and-overview`).
5. The `agent_started` signal: the four signals this package computes from the activity journal and the task's records stay the closed list of activity-derived signals; `agent_started` is appended by the supervisor from the worker's recorded process tree, and this package records it in the journal's `signal` entry and the queue task's `signals[]` like any other signal (`supervisor`).
6. Served to the clients: a task's activity from a cursor and as a live stream a client opens only while it shows the task; the plan's process names per leaf its tasks with a link to their activity.
7. Verified per harness: the stage `events` of the profile verification checks that every mapping resolves and every expected kind appears, recording per kind `reported` or `missing`; a recorded output of each shipped harness version is a parsing fixture, written for the test, never copied from the content of the product.
8. Navigation figures: beside the tokens, the server derives from a task's records what the work cost to navigate, each figure with its population and `indeterminate` where that population cannot be read: the cache-read share (the cache-read tokens over the input, cache-read and cache-creation tokens of the task's turns, `unmeasured` where the harness reports no cache reads); the revisits (the reads of a file the worker had already read in the same plan task, over all such reads of its activity); the language-server use (the `pm_lsp` read operations over the `pm_lsp` reads plus the calls of the harness's own search tools, `indeterminate` where the task made no lookup); and the fallback-to-read rate (the `pm_lsp` lookups followed, in the same plan task, by a whole-file read of the file the lookup answered with, over all lookups, a read of a part of that file not counting). The completion report's section _tokens_ shows them per task, per role and per plan; they are evidence for a comparison, never a limit (Model Work § Navigation Figures).
9. Skill residency: per skill of a task's required set, the figure records the bytes delivered in-band at launch and the sections the worker read later through `pm_skill` with a `section` (or a `resources/read` of a section anchor), read from the generation's delivery record and the activity's reads, so the skill content resident without being needed and the sections read on demand are visible per role and per skill; a generation's record starts empty, so a successor's residency is measured anew (Model Work § Navigation Figures; § Skill Delivery Record & Re-Delivery).

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): A recorded output of each shipped harness version maps onto its expected activity entries, an unmatched line counted and not shown, no tool result's content and no whole argument list recorded; the journal is masked and bounded, archived with its plan, and served from a cursor and as a live stream; each off-track signal holds on a fixture at its threshold and not below it, a lowered threshold of a role artifact applies and a raised one is refused, each action is recorded in `signals[]` and the journal, a signal takes its action at most once per attempt and plan task, and a `yield` or `restart` is reported once; no signal ends a plan; the stage `events` records each kind `reported` or `missing` and fails for a mapping that does not resolve. Each navigation figure is derived with its population and `indeterminate` (or `unmeasured`) where its population cannot be read, a read of a part of a file never counts as a fallback-to-read, the completion report's Tokens section shows them per task, per role and per plan, and the skill residency of a task names the bytes delivered in-band and the sections read on demand, measured anew for each generation.

(9) A test that asserts a file location derives the expected path from the production layout independently, never from the object under test.
