## Objective

Implement the work package `chat` of `doc/plans/chat.adoc`: chatting with the planning role. Serves: roadmap Milestone 8 (the chat with the planning role); State Catalogue § Chatting with the Planning Role; Chat on a plan and on an epic (UC-CTL-1, UC-CTL-2), in the review of an epic's registers (UC-CTL-3) and of its decomposition (UC-STA-6); Chat inside a decision (UC-PLN-2), while starting a plan or an epic (UC-STA-1, UC-STA-2), and about ended work (Runtime Model § Ended Work); the chat in both clients of `pm-mcp-clients`. Where: pm-workflow (the compiled unit `chat`, its routing, its call from a plan, an epic, a decision, and a start, and over the archive); pm-core (the transcript kept with its subject: the plan, the epic, the decision, the entry, or the archive entry); pm-runtime (the endpoints and operator verbs of Chat on a plan, an epic, a decision, a start, and ended work). The package file is the brief; this spec adds what the orchestrator observed and the constraints of the epic.

**Parked:** waits on packages that are not done: `operator-wait-and-machine-scope`, `phase-sub-workflows`, `task-queue-and-protocol`, `role-sets-and-model-caller`, `role-qualification`, `decision-engine`, `start-from-clients`, `process-and-overview`. Re-ground this spec before emitting it.

## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/chat.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated at 710da61, after the specification moved into this repository).

1. Implements State Catalogue § Chatting with the Planning Role: `chat`, with the chat over ended work of Runtime Model § Ended Work (`PM-WF-24`), except the revision of a plan: a plan's revision proposed in a chat and applied through the re-planning path is `chat-plan-revision`'s, which adds it on top of this package once the unit `replan` exists.
2. The unit `chat`: compiled only (`chat.pmwf`, never designable), its states `chat.<state>`, of no phase of its own, in two ways: called by a state that offers it (`decision.chat`, `start.chat`, `start-epic.chat`, `decomposition.chat`, `review.chat`), on the call stack with the caller's phase (`phase-sub-workflows`) and the exits `ended` and `proposed`, the caller's own path applying an accepted proposal; or hosted by its subject scope (a plan or an epic in every state, an ended plan or epic), an instance with its own state that never moves the subject's current state or stands on its call stack, one chat at a time per subject, a chat about ended work in the project's workspace scope.
3. Routing: a plan's chat goes to its planning role `replan`, whose facts carry the plan's outline, its tasks with their status, the done deliverables and the footprint; an epic's chat, also in its decomposition and its review, goes to the epic's planning role `decompose`. Chat is offered disabled, with the reason, while no configuration of the routed role qualifies.
4. The states: `chat.open` resolves the routed role and assembles the facts of the scope as data, untrusted items still marked untrusted; `chat.turn` waits for the operator's message (an operator wait), which is not interpreted; `chat.reply` runs one task `chat-reply` of the routed role that answers and may propose changes, back to `chat.turn`, again until the operator ends the chat; `chat.read-back` shows each proposed change as a read-back to accept or reject, nothing changing without accept; `chat.apply` applies an accepted change of a hosted chat through the subject's own path and its checks; `chat.end` ends the chat.
5. Apply by subject: an epic through staged rows under the epic's checks (disjoint surfaces, dependencies without a cycle). The decomposition draft and a register item are applied by the path their callers pass in (`epic-decomposition`, `epic-findings-review`).
6. A plan's chat: answers and proposes no revision of the plan, as outside the window of re-planning; nothing in this package applies a plan's revision (`chat-plan-revision`). A question never touches the plan, and the server never starts a chat itself.
7. Chat as an action: on a plan and on an epic in every state that has not ended (the states as the actions by state of UC-CTL-1 and UC-CTL-2 name them), each an operator endpoint of both clients; on an ended plan or epic read-only (below).
8. Chat inside a decision (UC-PLN-2): at `decision.answer` the choice Chat beside own words, "think again", and later, so the operator can ask back before answering. Scope: the decision (question, why now, evidence, what it blocks, reversibility, parts and options, the recommendation and the quorum's votes, which the chat may explain). Routing: a decision a worker asked goes to the role of the task that asked; one a server rule raised or a quorum escalated goes to the subject's planning role (`replan` for a plan, `decompose` for an epic, `refine` for a plan's entry and `decompose` for an epic's entry); a workspace decision a worker asked goes to that role, while one the setup's workflow raised has no planning role, and Chat is offered there disabled with that reason. The chat never answers: when it ends, the operator answers as before, and an answer the chat proposed is offered as the operator's own words and passes `decision.interpret` and `decision.read-back` unchanged (the interpretation still never sees the recommendation or the votes). The chat proposes no revision of the plan here either (`chat-plan-revision`). The plan keeps waiting during the chat with no time limit; the transcript is kept with the decision, whose record says the answer followed a chat. A waiver request offers no chat; an answer in another channel during the chat closes the decision and ends the chat.
9. Chat while starting (UC-STA-1, UC-STA-2): at `start.sources` and `start-epic.sources`, once at least one source is stored and every source from outside the machine is screened, about the request before it starts. Scope: the entry (its stored sources, untrusted ones marked, and the description); nothing of a plan exists yet. Routing: a plan entry goes to `refine`, an epic entry to `decompose`; disabled with the reason while the role is not qualified; not offered for a recipe. The only change it may propose is the description, a rewrite or an addition, read back and stored as a new source (`description-<n>.md` in the source directory) through `start.store-description` (`start-epic.store-description` for an epic) by the main workflow; it never changes, adds, or removes another source. Optional: starting never waits for a chat. The transcript stays in the entry's `pending/<entry_id>/chat/`, moves into the plan's or the epic's machine store (never into a ledger) at `start.create` or `start-epic.create` (kept for the audit, not a source the plan reads), and goes with the entry when it is abandoned. A chat still open at `start.create`, `start-epic.create`, the abandonment, or the deletion of the entry with the release of its project (`plan-and-epic-control`) is ended first in the same step: a running turn's task is withdrawn, a proposal not yet accepted dropped, and the transcript written complete before the entry's directory moves or is deleted; a deleted entry's transcript goes with it, as an abandoned one's.
10. Chat about ended work (Ended Work): read-only, on an ended plan or epic, at project scope over the archive. Scope: the archived record (outcome, reason, history, decisions, re-planning records, events), read from the archive, which the chat never changes. Routing: an ended plan goes to `replan`, an ended epic to `decompose`. The one change it may propose is a follow-up plan: a description drafted in the chat, read back, which opens the start of a new plan (UC-STA-1) with that description stored and the ended subject named in it (the ended plan; for an ended epic the epic, and the plan of it the chat was about where there was one), the operator going on from `start.sources`. No lesson is offered: the specification has no operator path to record one. The transcript is kept beside the archive entry, outside the archived record, which stays as it ended.
11. The transcript: kept with its subject for the audit, never sent elsewhere; the operator's messages answered by the role, never interpreted, only a change the role proposes passing a read-back; the role reading the content of the plan or the epic as data.

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): A test per subject and scope shows the routing to its role, a turn, a read-back accepted and one rejected, and the end; a chat called from a plan reports the phase it is called in; nothing changes without an accept; a plan's chat proposes no revision; an accepted change to an epic is a staged row that passed its checks; Chat is disabled with its reason while the routed role is not qualified; the transcript is kept with its subject. In a decision the chat never answers: a proposed answer reaches the decision only as own words through its interpretation and read-back, a waiver request offers no chat, and an answer in another channel ends the chat; a decision of a start's entry that a server rule raised routes to `refine` for a plan's entry and to `decompose` for an epic's, and a workspace decision a worker asked to that worker's role; in a start the only change applied is the description through `start.store-description`, no other source changes, the transcript moves from the entry's `chat/` at `start.create` and at `start-epic.create` and goes with an abandoned entry and with an entry the release of its project deletes, a chat open then ended first with its running turn's task withdrawn, and no recipe offers it; an ended plan's or epic's chat changes nothing in the archive and its follow-up opens a new start with the drafted description stored and the ended plan named.
