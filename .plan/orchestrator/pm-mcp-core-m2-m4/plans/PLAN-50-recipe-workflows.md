## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/recipe-workflows.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated by cleanup at 99971f3 from the package file).

1. Implements Domain Extensions § A Recipe Is a Workflow with its descriptor schema, and State Catalogue § Recipe Choice `recipe` (`PM-EXT-4`).
2. A recipe is a unit of type `recipe` without entries and exits (the unit types and the frontmatter are implemented: Workflow AST Developer Guide § Unit Types, Workflow DSL Developer Guide § The Unit Frontmatter) beside its declarative descriptor: a workflow of phase sub-workflows, its own where it differs and the plan's shared ones (finalize, the landing) elsewhere, its parameters, and whether it takes sources; starting it creates a plan whose process is that workflow; the recipe states of Phases 1 and 3 go. Recipes come from the domains' bundles (built in at build time, a duplicate key failing the build), from the machine (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/recipes/<recipe_id>.json` with its `.pmwf`, offered in every project), and from the project (`.marshall/recipes/`), a project's recipe taking the place of one of the same key; an empty harvest ends the recipe's plan with the composition event and prune reason `empty_harvest`. The simplify recipe is its own workflow, so no composition rule keys on `recipe_id`: the shipped rule `simplify-recipe` and its reason `recipe_override` are removed (ADR workflow-plan 008).
3. Through `start.ready`: a recipe's start passes `start.ready` of `start-from-clients` like a plan's (the recipe and its parameters, the title and id proposed from the recipe and the time, both changeable); `recipe.create` re-checks what creation needs and returns to `start.ready` with the cause where the recipe, the id, or a gate changed meanwhile, creating nothing. A parameter without an option is shown with its reason and the recipe cannot run; a request-scoped recipe gathers its sources as a plan does, without a chat; leaving keeps the entry with the recipe and its parameters.

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): A recipe runs as its own workflow from its parameters to its landing, an empty harvest ends it without a pull request, and no recipe state is left in the plan's phases; the simplify recipe runs as its own workflow, its steps not pruned by a rule on `recipe_id`; a recipe's start passes `start.ready`, and a change found at `recipe.create` returns there with its cause. **Sonar:** the SonarCloud search of the pull request (`pullRequest=<N>&resolved=false`) lists no open issue other than one dismissed with its reason, and its quality gate is green, before the pull request is reported ready.
