## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/decision-engine.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated by cleanup at 99971f3 from the package file).

1. Implements the engine side of UC-PLN-2 (`PM-WF-12`, `PM-WF-18`, `PM-WF-20`): Model Work § The Decision Structure, § Delivery to the Operator, § Answers in the Operator's Own Words, § Answers & Amendments; Store Schemas § Answers from the Terminal Client, § Question Record; State Catalogue § Unit `decision`. The client side is `decision-contract` of `pm-mcp-clients`.
2. The decision as a structure with its id (`<scope_id>/<question_id>`) and revisions; what it blocks and how reversible it is; the support per option derived by the runtime from the quorum's votes.
3. The interpreting role `interpret` (`read_only`, its corpus topic `own_words`), the read-back (`decision.read-back`: _confirm_, _edit_ with new words interpreted again, _choose_ an option instead) and the clarification; the confirmation `decision.confirm` before `decision.record` for a decision of more than one part or one not `reversible`, showing the whole answer and what follows from it, with _confirm_ and _back_.
4. None fits and later: _none fits_ (with a note; offered only where the decision names a role that can ask anew or a quorum is declared that can vote again) goes to `decision.withdraw`: a worker's question closes with the answer `none_fits`, which the continuing task carries in `facts.answers` with its `ask` link offered again within the question bound, and a quorum's decision is voted again on a new revision; _later_ answers nothing, the decision stays open and announced (`decision.announce`), the client leaves it, and its subject (the plan, the epic, the setup, or the entry) waits.
5. A waiver request is announced like a decision and answered by `waive` or `decline` (the outcome `declined`, the remediation unit going on; the wait is `operator-wait-and-machine-scope`'s), both operator overrides: no preselection, no interpretation, no chat.
6. A project's setup asks: a decision of a project's workspace scope (a question of its setup) is delivered, announced with its project, and answered like a plan's, through the same states and record; it holds the setup only, never a plan; Chat over it is offered as for a plan's, disabled with its reason where the setup's workflow raised the question and no planning role exists (the routing is `chat`'s).
7. A start's entry asks: a decision of a start's entry is announced with its entry, held in the entry's `questions.jsonl` and `decisions.jsonl`, answered through the same states and record, and holds only the entry; the stores and their move into the new scope at `start.create-scope` are `start-source-intake`'s, the routing of its chat to `refine` or `decompose` `chat`'s.
8. The open decisions across scopes, the workspace scope of a project included, with since when each waits; the channel `cli`, the second channel of content answers beside `web` (and `quorum` for a decision a quorum took); the answer endpoint (pm-runtime), one scope-qualified endpoint for both clients, whose validation and recording of the answer are one transaction of the decision's scope, so the first valid answer of either channel wins: an answer to an older revision refused with `conflict` and the current revision, a second answer or an answer to a closed record with `already_answered`, an answer the part does not allow (an unknown option, a value failing the part's `value_schema`, own words, a note, a delegation, or _none fits_ where not allowed) with `invalid_answer`, and an answer to a part whose dependency is unanswered with `dependency_unanswered`.
9. Chat before answering: `decision.answer` and the decision record leave room for the choice Chat that `chat` adds on top of this package (the record saying that an answer followed a chat, the transcript kept with the decision, a decision closed as withdrawn with its reason); the chat itself, its routing, and its rules are that package's.

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): Every scenario of `decision-contract` replays against the runtime; an answer in own words reaches the decision only after its read-back, _edit_ interpreting the new words again and _choose_ taking an option instead; a decision of several parts or not `reversible` is recorded only after `decision.confirm`, and _back_ returns to the answer; _none fits_ closes a worker's question with `none_fits` and offers its `ask` link again, and has a quorum vote again on a new revision, _later_ leaves the decision open and changes nothing; the first valid answer of either channel wins, also when two answers arrive at once, and a test per refusal shows `conflict` with the current revision, `already_answered`, `invalid_answer`, and `dependency_unanswered`, none of them changing the record; a decision of a project's setup is announced, answered, and recorded like a plan's, holds no plan, and offers its chat disabled where the setup's workflow raised it; a decision of a start's entry is announced with the entry and holds only it. **Sonar:** the SonarCloud search of the pull request (`pullRequest=<N>&resolved=false`) lists no open issue other than one dismissed with its reason, and its quality gate is green, before the pull request is reported ready.

## Specification Added Since The Package File

Merged outside the ledger on 2026-10-21 by the operator, specification only: #133 `ab1826f` (from the analysis `first-level-thinker` of `plan-marshall-documentation`). The package file does not name it; this plan carries it (carried by the cleanup of 2026-10-22). The executing plan reads the named parts at its branch point and builds what they now say; the package file is deleted with the package as usual.

- **Two members of a vote in the decision record.** The vote of an adjudication record carries `rationale_origin` (`model`, or `server` where the server composed the rationale from a judgment role's probabilities, never a model's reasoning) and `probabilities` (`null` for every vote but a judgment role's; for that, the objects `sorted` and `reversed`, each the probability of every option in one of the two requests, between 0 and 1); both are required members of the vote — `doc/specification/store-schemas/18-decision-records.adoc`, the vote of `question.adjudicate`, and the sentence of `doc/specification/model-work/11-decision-records.adoc` on the adjudication record (the rationale with its origin; a judgment role's probabilities of both requests).
- **A server-composed rationale is shown as composed.** An escalated set shows the dissent with its reason; a rationale the server composed for a judgment role is shown as composed, and a role of another kind never reads it as reasoning — `doc/specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc`, *Delivered with the votes* and § Judgment Votes (the rationale and the record).

Folded into the deliverable on the decision record and the support per option; no new deliverable. NOT carried here: a judgment role as a listed voter (the endpoint of the protocol `system_one`, the two requests, the class rule of a declaration, the calibration of `form`) — nothing in the work packages builds the judgment endpoint (see the Open Defect of the epic), so this plan builds the record members and their rendering only. A test: a vote of a model carries `rationale_origin: model` and `probabilities: null`; a record written with a `server` origin and both distributions validates and is shown as composed.
