## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/chat-plan-revision.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated at 710da61, after the specification moved into this repository).

1. Implements Phase Workflows § Revision from a Chat and the plan's part of _Apply by subject_ of State Catalogue § Chatting with the Planning Role: `chat`, on top of `chat`, whose plan chat until then proposes no revision.
2. The proposal: a plan's chat proposes a revision only while the plan runs in Phase 5 or in finalize before the merge, the window of every re-planning, and otherwise answers and proposes none.
3. Apply: an accepted revision passes the guards of a first plan (outline validation, derivation dry-run, task graph, Q-Gate), is applied at the plan's next task boundary as `replan.apply` applies one (`replan.apply-revision`), what runs finishing first, a paused plan applying it when started again, and composes a plan instance version of event `replan`; its re-planning record names the operator's chat as the decider, and the report `plan.revised` is written. A chat's revision is the operator's own act: it spends nothing of the plan's re-planning budget. A change to a plan an epic started reaches the epic's inbox like every act.
4. Inside a decision: a revision the chat proposes goes through the same path; the decision stays open unless the applied revision removes the task or state that asked it, which closes it as withdrawn, with the reason.

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): A plan's chat proposes a revision in Phase 5 and in finalize before the merge and none outside that window; an accepted revision passes the guards of a first plan, takes effect at the next task boundary (a paused plan's when started again), composes an instance version of event `replan` whose record names the chat as the decider, writes `plan.revised`, and leaves the re-planning budget untouched; a change to a plan of an epic reaches the epic's inbox; in a decision, an applied revision that removes what asked it closes the decision as withdrawn with the reason, and one that does not leaves it open.
