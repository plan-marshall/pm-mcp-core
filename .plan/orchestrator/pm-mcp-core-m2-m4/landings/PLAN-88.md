## Follow-Ups

- **Wiring.** The CDI producer of the adapter and the end-to-end `question` state path are `plan-marshall-mcp`'s (`pm-workflow` may not depend on `pm-runtime`). Nothing in this repository calls the adapter.
- **The port cannot address a pending entry.** `ReadRequest(Optional<String> scopeId, String questionId)` names a plan, an epic or the workspace and nothing else, so a `question` state of a pending entry (an entity of the workspace scope) cannot be resolved through this adapter. Plan-reported by the lane and corroborated by the record's two components. No package owns it; it is recorded as an Open Defect.
- **`PM-IMPL-5` in `doc/Traceability.adoc`** was not given a line for the adapter although the spec's Expected Surface names it. Either the id in the port Javadoc and the spec is the wrong one or the entry is owed. Fold it into the next plan that edits `QuestionRecordReader` or the PM-ARCH-4 block.
