## Objective

Second half of the package `lesson-store` (the first, PLAN-V2-11 (`lesson-record-and-codec`), created the record types, the file codec, the parity test and the package file). Build the store of the project's lessons (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/lessons/`, Store Schemas § Lesson Record) on the shipped `core.store` substrate: lesson id allocation and every write as one journaled transaction under the lesson store lock with the digest sidecar; the one resolver (four states plus the three integrity states); the revision of a body; the retirement with its closed verdicts; and the claim of a lesson by a plan, its release, and its retirement `applied` when the plan lands (Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing). It is what PLAN-V2-24 (`source-lesson.resolve`, the premise `retire`, the Remedy), PLAN-V2-22 (the claim at creation and its end) and PLAN-V2-21 (the release on every end without a landing) read and write; they wait for it. Then close the package: delete the package file and the row, and hand the remainder to a package of its own.

**Which lesson store.** The lesson store of the PM-MCP engine, per project on the machine. Not `plan-marshall`'s `manage-lessons` store (external, never read by the engine, `PM-MIG-2`); the ported test data (`PM-TEST-2`) is derived once from its `test/plan-marshall/manage-lessons` suite (`test_add_collision_safe_allocation.py`, `test_add_get_next_id.py`, `test_lesson_id_resolution.py`, `test_lessons_crud_cmd.py` and the `remove` fixtures) and from the cases of PM-WATCH-STORE-14 and -20, committed as plain fixtures; no test reads that checkout.

## Deliverables

1. **The store, allocation and creation.** `LessonStore` for a project (built from the project's `JournalScope.lessons`), after the pattern of `PendingEntryStore` (reads take no lock; every write is a target of the caller's `ScopeTransaction` of the lesson scope, which holds `locks/lessons.lock`, so it is journaled and the digest sidecar `lessons/.digests.json` covers it). `allocate(transaction, clock)` draws the id of the current UTC hour with the next per-hour sequence (at least three digits), counting the lesson files present and the lesson targets the same transaction already holds, so two concurrent writers never share an id (an id is never reused: a lesson file is never deleted). `create(transaction, …)` adds a create target (`mustBeAbsent`) for a new lesson whose server-set members the store derives itself: `format_version` 1, `lesson_id`, `created_at`, `status` (`upstream_pending` for the audience `upstream`, else `active`), `revision` 1 with its first `revisions` entry (`n`, `at`, `sources`, the SHA-256 of the UTF-8 body), `claim` and `retired` null. The caller passes what the specification names as given: `title`, `component`, `category`, `bundle`, `rule`, `remedy`, `provenance`, `audience` (derived by the audience predicate, which is NOT built here), `derived_from_untrusted` (set by the layer that composed the writing task's facts), `tool_version` (the runtime's release version), `sources` (not empty) and the body. Done when: `LessonStoreTest` shows a created lesson on disk as the bytes of `LessonCodec`, its sidecar entry, no journal left, ids unique over 200 concurrent creations of two threads on one hour, an id drawn for the next hour after the clock moves, and a create over an existing id refused.
2. **The resolver.** `list()` and `get(LessonId)` resolve a lesson to one value of a sealed `LessonResolution`: `Found`, `Absent`, `PresentUnreadable` (listed with its path, never omitted), `EmptyShell` (valid front matter, body without a non-whitespace character), and, from the digest sidecar, `StoreTampered` (size or SHA-256 differs), `DeletedOutOfBand` (an entry whose file is gone) and `Untracked` (a file without an entry). Two flavours: the lock-free read reports a difference from the sidecar only as unverified (`StoreSnapshot`, `StoreIntegrity.DIGEST_UNVERIFIED`; it never decides an edit, PM-IMPL-7), and the locked read, which any decision (claim, retire, revise) uses, recovers the scope's journal and decides through `ScopeIntegrity.verify` (`Mismatch` with a current digest is `StoreTampered`, without one `DeletedOutOfBand`). The listing names only files whose name parses as `<lesson_id>.md`; the staged files `<store>.tmp.<transaction id>` and the sidecar are never lessons. Provide `unclaimed()` (`status` `active`, `claim` null, resolved `Found`, newest id first; a lesson with `derived_from_untrusted` is listed and carries the mark) for the `source-lesson.choose` facts, and `claimedBy(planId)` and `claims()` (read from the front matter, `PresentUnreadable` listed separately by id) for the recovery of the plan end. No path turns `Absent` into a retry. Done when: `LessonResolverTest` shows every listed lesson resolving by its listed id (the nine YAML-front-matter fixtures and the six headerless fixtures of PM-WATCH-STORE-14 as committed plain files under `pm-core/src/test/resources/ported/lessons/`), a headerless file `PresentUnreadable` and never `Absent`, an empty body `EmptyShell`, a changed byte `StoreTampered`, a deleted file `DeletedOutOfBand`, a stray file `Untracked`, a lock-free read of a tampered file `DIGEST_UNVERIFIED` and not a decision, and a property test over generated store states that no listed state is reported `Absent`.
3. **Revision and retirement.** `revise(transaction, id, body, sources, derivedFromUntrusted, now)` replaces the body wholesale as the next revision (`revision` + 1, `revisions` appends `{n, at, sources, body_sha256}`, the new `sources` not empty, old bodies not kept); `derived_from_untrusted` is monotonic: a lesson that is `true` stays `true` whatever the argument, and a revision whose `sources` name a lesson of kind `lesson` that is `true` becomes `true` (PM-WATCH-STORE-17 and -58, store half). `retire(transaction, id, retirement)` sets `retired{at, by, verdict, into, reason, covering_clause, covering_input, lesson_state}` and `status: retired` in one transaction, the file staying in place: the verdict from the closed set (`applied`, `completely_covered`, `redundant`, `superseded`, `merged`, `obsolete`) with no default; `completely_covered` requires a `covering_clause` and a `covering_input` that are not blank; `into` required for `merged` and `superseded`, null otherwise; acts only on a lesson resolved `Found` and unclaimed (the end of a claim is Deliverable 4's), and in every other state changes nothing and returns the state. The premise `retire` of a start is this call with verdict `obsolete` and `by` the `entry_id`. The retirement of a `PresentUnreadable` lesson, `golden_label`, `audience_override`, `tool_version_override` and the release of a stale claim are NOT built: they are operator acts that no verb performs yet (PM-WATCH-STORE-56, a decision; see Standing Constraints). Done when: `LessonRevisionTest` and `LessonRetirementTest` show each rule above with a refusing case and a passing control, a revision without sources refused, the flag never turning back, each verdict's required members, a retired lesson not revisable, and a claimed lesson not retirable through `retire`.
4. **Claim, release and the end of a claim.** `claim(transaction, id, planId, now)` sets `claim{plan_id, claimed_at}` and returns `Claimed`, or `Unavailable(state, claimingPlan)` for a lesson already claimed (naming the claiming plan), retired, or not resolved `Found` (the states of Deliverable 2): the `lesson_unavailable` of `source-lesson` and of the `start.create-scope` re-check. `release(transaction, id, planId)` sets `claim` to null and leaves the lesson `active`; `endClaimApplied(transaction, id, planId, now)` retires it with verdict `applied`, `by` the `plan_id`, and clears the claim (the link to the plan stays in `retired.by`, which is what PM-WATCH-STORE-18 computes reach from; the ADR states it). Both act only when the durable `claim.plan_id` equals `planId` (an authorization verified against durable state, R14 (2)); a second call after the end is a no-op that reports the state, so the recovery of the plan end can repeat it. Done when: `LessonClaimTest` shows the table of the next deliverable's cells for claim, release and end, and the verdicts and members of the retired record.
5. **The crash windows and the concurrency, by table (R14).** Before the first review, write the table (lesson state: unclaimed, claimed by A, claimed by B, retired, `PresentUnreadable`, `StoreTampered`, absent) x (operation: create, revise, retire, claim by A, claim by B, release by A, release by B, end applied by A, end applied by B) x (writer: the operation alone, two concurrent writers), and a test per cell in `LessonClaimTest` and `LessonStoreTest`. In `LessonStoreCrashTest` (in `core.store`, with the package-private `ScopeTransaction.StepHook`, the pattern of `PendingEntryStoreTest.StopWrite`): a write stopped after each step (stage, journal, apply, digests) of a create, a revise, a claim and an end applied leaves, after `JournalRecovery`, the old or the new lesson and a sidecar entry that matches, never a torn one; two concurrent claims of one lesson by two plans end with exactly one claimant; a claim racing a retirement ends in one of the two orders, never both; a repeated release or end is a no-op. The staged namespace is disjoint from the legal names (R14 (6)): `<lesson_id>.md.tmp.<transaction id>` is no lesson id and is never listed. Expected paths are derived independently of the store (R14 (9)). Done when: the table is in the pull request body with the test of each cell and every cell passes.

## Standing Constraints

Plan-specific constraints (the epic-wide rules are in `standing-rules.md`, named in the First Instruction above):

- **R14 binds in full** (the claim is a durable protocol of `core.store`): (1) the decision of every operation is taken from the pair (the front matter actually recorded, the operation), never from the operation alone; a guard added to one branch gets its symmetric guard on every other (Deliverable 5's table); (2) while a journal of the lesson scope exists no other writer touches the scope, which `ScopeTransaction` and the lesson store lock already give; the authorization of a release or an end is the durable `claim.plan_id`, never an argument; (3) the journal is the record that drives recovery and is deleted last, by the shipped transaction; (4) the store decides nothing from a probe that can flap; (5)(6)(8)(9) as written in Deliverable 5.
- **The transactions are the caller's.** The store adds targets to a `ScopeTransaction` the caller opened under the lesson store lock, as `PendingEntryStore` does; it opens none and takes no lock itself. A transition that also writes outside the lesson store (the claim after the new plan scope, the end after the plan's terminal transaction) is ordered by the caller (Store Schemas § Transaction Journal, Writes outside the scope); that ordering is PLAN-V2-22's and PLAN-V2-21's.
- **PM-WATCH-STORE-56 is decided (operator walk, 2026-10-29, option 1) and deleted by PLAN-V2-11.** `golden_label`/`labelled_at`, `audience_override` and `tool_version_override` are cut from the record by PLAN-V2-11; this plan builds no operator act. The two recovery acts (retiring a `PresentUnreadable` lesson, releasing a stale claim) are the first items of the package `lesson-lifecycle` that Deliverable 6 writes, so that no lesson stays forever; Deliverable 6 does NOT delete or keep PM-WATCH-STORE-56 (it is already gone).
- **No audience predicate, no `submit` transition, no upstream release.** `audience` and `derived_from_untrusted` are given by the caller; the compiled-in catalogue of the distribution that the predicate reads is content of `plan-marshall-mcp`.
- **Log messages: none expected.** If the trace matrix shows one, it goes into `CoreLogMessages` at the next free identifier of its level and the plan says so in its inbox message.
- **Format versions stay 1 (R13)** and `LessonFrontMatter` is not changed in shape by this plan; if the store needs a member the schema lacks, stop and report, do not add it.
