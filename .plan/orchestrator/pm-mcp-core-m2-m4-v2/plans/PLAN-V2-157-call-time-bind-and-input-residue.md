## Objective

PLAN-V2-15 shipped on 2026-11-19 (#513) with two of its six deliverables met. The engine fills slots but never binds them: a call that binds `slot.x` pushes the literal text `slot.x` into the frame and the callee runs on it; `InputValidator`, `PathRules` and `InputRefusal` have no production caller; a stored frame's provenance is rewritten to empty at every advance; a pruned or composed plan instance loses its fills and slots; the caps the specification states are not enforced; and the guides, two ADRs and the watch state this behaviour in the present tense. The lane's blocker for the bind ("no text carrier for the in-memory call frame") does not hold: the carrier types exist. The first eight commits of #513 were written by a free model the operator had selected by mistake, and the pull request merged with the review gate overridden and ten of eleven review threads open. This plan finishes the bind, the slots' passage through composition and pruning, the caps, the provider's contract and the documentation, and records, for every carried item, the outline's adopt, split-off or drop decision.

## Standing Constraints

- **Log messages: none expected.** A bind refusal is a condition and a `Violation`, not a log line.
- **Format versions stay 1** (R13): the frame and the status member change in place; no reader for the old frame.
- **No command line from a value.** A bound text or path is never concatenated into a command, a path of the filesystem outside `PathConfinement`, a log message beyond 80 characters, or an audit field unbounded; the commit message goes to the repository over the API or `stdin`, as `PM-EXEC-5` says.
- **No generic `Exception`** is caught or thrown in production code; the refusal values of `InputValidator` and `ConfinementOutcome` are returned.
- **Add no dependency and no plugin** (CLAUDE.md).
- **The existing call sites keep working unchanged**: every `call git.commit_and_push (message_mode=…, push_mode=…)` of the specification (about a dozen sites in `phase-workflows/03`, `04`, `05`, `state-catalogue/04`, `08`, `17`, `18`, `workflow-dsl/03`, `08`) still verifies.
- **Fail closed.** A bind that cannot resolve, validate or confine a value refuses; it never pushes the unresolved text, and a refusal reason never carries more than the 80 characters the constraint above allows.

The lane of PLAN-V2-04 reported the resolution as "left to PLAN-V2-15's call-time channel"; neither the ADR nor the Javadoc says so, and the lane's claim is the lane's. This plan's one binder (Decision 6, `InputBindingContext` with the configuration value by key, which was written for exactly this `input_fallback`) takes the whole resolution in the order of the ADR, and its test per step (call site, fallback present, fallback absent with a default, default, `?` absent, required and omitted); say in the landing whether the ADR text changed.
