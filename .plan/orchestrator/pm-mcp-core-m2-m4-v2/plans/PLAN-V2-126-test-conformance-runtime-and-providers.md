## Standing Constraints

Plan-specific constraints (the epic-wide rules are in `standing-rules.md`, named in the First Instruction above):

- **Substance, not form.** The Done-when of PLAN-V2-89 to 93 was met by "a controller plus one `Generators` call", by a grep that came back empty, or by a one-line statement, and every analysis then found the weakness. A conversion here is accepted when a scratch mutation of the thing the test names fails it. The pull request body carries the per-class table (Deliverable 4), the counts before and after with their counting commands, and one failing mutation run per restored test.
- **A boundary keeps its edge; a failure keeps its reason.** A test that pins the value just beyond a limit (a status code, a revision, a version, a time before the boot, a page size) keeps that literal or a draw pinned to `limit + 1`. An assertion keeps reporting the detail, reason, diagnostic or measured value it reported before, within 20 to 60 characters. A test that pins an order keeps data unsorted by construction. A repeated expression goes into the module's artifact, not into a private method per class. Two values a test must tell apart are drawn so they cannot be equal.
- **Assertion messages belong to PLAN-V2-103, 104 and 105.** This plan does not re-open them. A line it touches for another reason carries a message of 20 to 60 characters that says what is checked.
- **The artifacts are consumed outside this repository (R16).** `pm-runtime:tests` is consumed by the assembly `plan-marshall-mcp`; `RuntimeGenerators` keeps every existing method and signature, and `TestBases`, `FakeBus` and `FakeSecretService` are not changed. The POM change of Deliverable 2(b) declares artifacts `CLAUDE.md` names; it adds no third-party dependency.
- **Not here:** the assertion messages (PLAN-V2-103, 104, 105), the fakes (`FakePmExec`, `RecordingAuditSink`, `LockHolderProcess`, `RecordingProcessCall`, `FakeParticipant`, the provider fakes: PLAN-V2-106), the `Optional` conversions (PLAN-V2-99), the record builders (PLAN-V2-108), the generic catches (PLAN-V2-94), the runtime parity tests (PLAN-V2-123 D4), the lock tests (`FileLockManagerTest`, `FileLockManagerTwoProcessIT` ordering and OS-lock cases: PLAN-V2-124), the CI-contract cases (`ForgeUrlTest`, the comment cases of `GitHubCiProviderTest` and `GitLabCiProviderTest`: PLAN-V2-128), the judgment and system-one classes with their tests (PLAN-V2-129), the pm-core and pm-workflow tests (PLAN-V2-125).
