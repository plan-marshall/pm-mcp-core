## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/plan-and-epic-control.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated by cleanup at 99971f3 from the package file).

1. Implements Runtime Lifecycle § Pausing and Starting a Plan, State Catalogue § Cancelling and Abandoning Archive, § Holds and Their Acts, Workflow DSL § Plan Overrides (a plan's settings), and Model Work § The Epic Is Told (`PM-WF-23`). The step names the use cases give these flows (`plan.*`, `plan-settings.*`, `hold.*`) are operator acts over the local API, never workflow states (State Catalogue § Operator Flows That Are Not Workflow Units).
2. A plan's activity (UC-CTL-1): beside its terminal states a plan is `running`, `pausing`, or `paused`, part of its persisted state and never a kind of `needs_operator`. Pause (`POST /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/pause`) with its point: `after_task` (the default: what runs finishes, no new task or job starts), `after_phase` (the phase sub-workflow on the call stack runs to its return), or `now` (running tasks withdrawn uncounted, their workers ended and fenced, running jobs cancelled, a retry's backoff clock stopped); a plan inside the rebase-to-merge section holds when it leaves it. A paused plan keeps its operator waits, holds, and leases but no slot, is offered no task, is never `stalled`, and keeps its place in its epic's `parallelization_scope`. Start (`POST /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/start`) resumes from the current state, and on a pausing plan withdraws the pause; an act the activity does not offer is refused `act_not_available`, on an ended plan `plan_ended`. A plan of an epic is created and started in one act, never waiting to be started. Every pause and start is audited with its channel and announced (`activity.changed`).
3. The ends of a plan: cancel (a running, pausing, waiting, or held plan, outcome `cancelled`) and abandon (a paused one, outcome `abandoned`) through `plan.aborted` with the operator's required reason, confirmed with what they end, `pm-operator abort plan` their shell form; the open pull request closed with a comment naming the reason or left open, its branch kept; the archive under `archive/<plan_id>/` like a completed plan; the report `plan.ended`. Only the cancel of a plan held by an integrity pause is that pause's resolution _abort_, outcome `aborted`. The closed list of plan outcomes of the terminal-state catalogue gains `cancelled`, `abandoned`, and `released`. A claimed lesson is released on every end without a landing (Store Schemas § A Claimed Lesson Ends with the Landing, built by `start-source-intake`).
4. A plan's settings (UC-CTL-5): the plan's overrides of `machine-onboarding-unit` read with each value's origin (the plan, the project, the machine) and written by an operator act into `overrides.json`, validated like the machine's values (an enrolled harness, an allowed model or `model_not_allowed`, an available provider, a landing variant validated as for the project), audited, composing a new instance version through the composition event `override`, implemented in the composer (Plan Instance Developer Guide § The Plan Behaviour Axes), under the position rule; a change whose new version would not contain the plan's position is refused `position_not_contained` and not written; a change made against an older revision is refused `conflict` with the current value (CLI & Security § Concurrent Changes of a Setting); what runs keeps its value, the new one applies where the plan next reads it; an override to an unqualified role configuration is shown unqualified and takes the qualification fallback.
5. Its epic told: an inbox record in the act's transaction per operator act on a plan of a live epic (`paused`, `started`, `cancelled`, `abandoned`, `revised`, `settings_changed`), with the reason and channel; the queue row following the plan (its status gains `pausing`, `paused`, and `cancelled`; `abandoned` also for a paused plan the operator abandoned); `coordinate` deciding on each record; a pausing or paused plan's row counting toward `parallelization_scope`.
6. A restart: a paused plan stays paused, a pausing one holds at its point.
7. Holds and their acts (UC-CTL-4): a hold is a record derived from the scope's records and kept on read, never a workflow state or an operator wait, with its `kind` from the closed list `stalled` (acts `retry_now`, `recycle_worker`), `condition` (the acts its reason names), `integrity_pause` (the act `accept`, which shows the tampered file with its recorded and current size and digest and accepts it as it is; no diff and no restore), `held` (`retry` and the item's own acts), `landing_failed` (`retry`, and `give_up` for the removal of moved documents), its `cause`, `since`, what was tried, and its `acts` as operator endpoints of both clients, each act audited with its channel and device and answered with the hold as it stands then; beside them the scope's cancel, for an integrity pause its resolution _abort_ (a plan `aborted`, an epic `cancelled`); a hold that clears by itself ends without an act and writes the report `plan.hold_released`; `paused` is no hold; no hold carries an operator command as relay text (`facts.operator_command` goes). The cancel of an epic this bullet offers as the integrity pause's resolution _abort_ (an epic `cancelled`) is built by `epic-resource-and-release` (its bullet _Cancel_); this package offers it as the hold's act.

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): A test per act and activity of a plan shows what it does and what it refuses (`act_not_available`, `plan_ended`), also waiting for the operator and held; each pause point holds where it says, also at an operator wait, at a loop-back, and leaving the rebase-to-merge section; a paused plan holds no slot, is offered no task, and resumes where it stopped; an ended plan is archived with each of its outcomes and reason, the cancel of an integrity pause with `aborted`; a settings change applies from the plan's next read, is refused with its reason for an invalid value, with `position_not_contained` where the new version would not contain the plan's position, and with `conflict` and the current value where another change came first, and composes an instance version of event `override`; every act on a plan of an epic, a settings change included, appends its inbox record in the act's transaction and moves the queue row; every hold of the closed list offers its acts and cancel as endpoints, the integrity pause's `accept` showing sizes and digests and offering no diff or restore, an act answering with the hold released or still held with its cause, and no representation carrying `facts.operator_command`. **Sonar:** the SonarCloud search of the pull request (`pullRequest=<N>&resolved=false`) lists no open issue other than one dismissed with its reason, and its quality gate is green, before the pull request is reported ready.
