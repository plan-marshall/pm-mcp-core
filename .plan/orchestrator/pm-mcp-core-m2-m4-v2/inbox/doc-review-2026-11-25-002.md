## The principle behind the decisions
A mechanism that predicts, infers or pre-declares something the process later observes as a fact is replaced by the observation, also where code exists. This round:
- whether a CI check is required, and whether a merge must go through the queue, are read at the act from the pull request, not from cached forge facts;
- the landing commit's subject on `landing.main` is written by a `commit-message` task over the actual change, not taken from the planned `pr_title`;
- the per-deliverable build and the self-review surfacers follow the realized change, not the declared module or the plan's recorded domains;
- the sibling collision check at init goes: a conflict is met at integration like every plan's;
- worker-declared fields the server already knows go: `consultation_ids`, `blocks` in the decision envelope, `evidence_class` on `submit:refine`;
- a triage `FIX` is `fixed` on the recorded commit, never by matching its free-text `population`;
- the questions a refine submission carries open the clarification round, not the self-score.
