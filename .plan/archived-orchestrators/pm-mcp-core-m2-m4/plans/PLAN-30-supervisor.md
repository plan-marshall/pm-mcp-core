## Deliverables

The bullets of `doc/plans/supervisor.adoc` § What, in its words (regenerated by cleanup at 99971f3 from the package file).

1. One supervisor per project and role: starting fresh and warm workers within the role's count and the model slots, acknowledgement, silence and exit detection, idle and token-budget recycling, ending and fencing, the start-failure guard.
2. Changed configuration: a warm worker is recycled after its task when the role's configuration digest over the effective values differs from its generation's (an edited artifact, a changed role assignment, or a plan override); the start-failure guard clears on such a change.
3. Pause at once: a worker whose plan the operator pauses at once is ended by force at once, its task queued again without counting a re-offer.
4. Idle: a `wait_again` answer to a generation holding a lease (consulting, or holding for an answer) is no idle wake-up.
5. The missing answer acknowledgement as a loss of a continuing worker, and the token budget reached during a hold (the hold ends, the task suspends or falls back, the worker is recycled), are `question-continuation`'s; the off-track signals and their actions on live workers are `worker-activity`'s. Reports of the supervisor go to both clients and the audit log.
6. `agent_started`: this is the one off-track signal the supervisor raises rather than `worker-activity` (the four activity-derived signals stay that package's): a process of the worker's recorded process tree (the launcher's record of the tree) that runs an enrolled harness executable or the operator binary `pm-operator`, compared by path and digest with the harness enrolment and the installation, raises `agent_started`; its threshold is `1` and fixed, its action is always `restart` whatever the role artifact names, and it appends a `security` audit record `agent_started` naming the executable, because a second agent started by a worker would run outside every task, tool set and usage record (Model Work § A Worker Off Track; Timeouts § Worker Supervision & Recycling; Job Runtime § Process Termination & Cancellation Protocol, step 3a).

**Done when** (the package's own acceptance): With a fixture harness and a virtual clock: each loss is detected at its deadline and never before; a start failure repeated to the guard's count ends the role's starts, and a changed role configuration clears the guard; a pause at once ends the plan's worker by force and re-queues its task without counting a re-offer; a changed role assignment recycles a warm worker after its task; a consulting worker's `wait_again` answers do not recycle it as idle. A worker whose recorded process tree starts an enrolled harness executable or `pm-operator` is detected at the first such process — the comparison by path and digest, so an impostor binary is not the enrolled one —, ended with the action `restart`, its task re-offered, and a `security` audit record `agent_started` names the executable; the threshold is `1` and fixed, and a role artifact that names another action for it or another threshold does not change it. **Sonar:** the SonarCloud search of the pull request (`pullRequest=<N>&resolved=false`) lists no open issue other than one dismissed with its reason, and its quality gate is green, before the pull request is reported ready.
